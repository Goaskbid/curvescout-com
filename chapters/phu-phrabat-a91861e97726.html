Phu Phrabat

This is an existing place description, not a separately verified motorbike route. Seasonal, access and service information may have changed.

Imagine a forest of sandstone mushrooms, boulders balanced on pillars and arches sculpted by wind and rain, and you are close to the setting of Phu Phra Bat. The place lies on a low plateau in Udon Thani province, in north-east Thailand's Isan region, about eighteen kilometres from the town of Nam Som. Over millions of years, erosion has carved the rock into strange shapes, and people have used the overhangs and caves for shelter and worship since prehistoric times.

The name means Mountain of the Buddha's Footprint, and it refers to a hollow in the rock venerated as a footprint of the Buddha. The legend most visitors hear concerns the princess Usa and the prince Barot, a love story in which the princess's father, a ruler, set impossible tasks to separate them. Several rock formations carry their names, including a tower said to be her home, and the whole park feels like the stage set for a fairy tale.

History runs deep. Rock shelters are decorated with prehistoric paintings of hands and animals, while later peoples, in the first millennium AD, carved sema, boundary stones that mark sacred precincts, and set them around natural rock shrines. Statues of the Buddha were carved or placed in natural niches. In 2024 UNESCO inscribed the park as a World Heritage site, recognising it as part of the sema stone tradition of the Dvaravati culture.

The ride across Isan is flat to gently rolling, with rice fields, sugar cane, eucalyptus and small villages. Roads are good and quiet, with long straight sections, though trucks and motorbikes carry loads of every kind. Heat is intense from March to May, and the rains from June to October turn the fields green and the dirt tracks to mud. Cool dry weather from November to February is best. The park has a modest entry fee, paths and signs, and a small cafe; fuel is easy in Nam Som and Ban Phue, where you can also buy sticky rice and grilled chicken for lunch.
