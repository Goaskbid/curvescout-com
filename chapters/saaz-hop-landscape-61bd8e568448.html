Saaz Hop Landscape

This is an existing place description, not a separately verified motorbike route. Seasonal, access and service information may have changed.

Smell comes first here. Late in summer the air around Žatec turns green and faintly bitter, because this is the heart of the Saaz hop country and the harvest fills the lanes with the scent of cones being picked and dried. The hops grown on these slopes and flats along the Ohře river are the ones that give Czech lager, and Pilsner above all, its clean, spicy bite. The cultural landscape of the town and its fields earned UNESCO status in 2023, and the old hop-drying houses and brewery buildings in Žatec are the reason.

The setting is gentle and a little open. The Ohře runs through a broad basin, with the Ore Mountains hanging on the northern horizon and the Doupov hills to the south. Between them lie fields of tall poles and wires, pale in winter and a dense wall of green by July. The roads are mostly modest and quiet, small asphalt strips linking villages, with the occasional tractor and, in harvest season, lorries full of cones. It is not twisty riding; it is slow, scenic looping through farmland, best on a bright morning with the poles throwing long shadows.

Žatec itself is a proper town of about twenty thousand people, so fuel, food and a bank machine are straightforward. Postoloprty, a few kilometres away, is smaller but has services too. In town the place to look for is the huge brick warehouse district where hops were once stored, graded and sealed for export. This was an international trade, and the old storehouses carried the stamps that told Hamburg and Vienna buyers the cones were genuine. There is a hop museum, and it is worth the time if you have even a passing interest in beer.

Ride through in late August or September if you can bear a slightly slower pace, since that is when the scent and the activity peak. In winter the place is stark and quiet, with bare wires against grey sky, and it has a different kind of beauty. Whichever season you choose, bring a hat for the sun on the flat sections, because there is little shade between the poles and the villages.
