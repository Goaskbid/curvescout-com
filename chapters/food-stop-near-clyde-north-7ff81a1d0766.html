Food stop near Clyde North

This is an existing place description, not a separately verified motorbike route. Seasonal, access and service information may have changed.

Clyde North is about as new as a Melbourne suburb gets. Not long ago this was flat farmland south-east of Berwick; now it is a sea of fresh rooftops, wide boulevards and shopping centres built almost overnight. As a place to end a ride, it has one big advantage: it is easy. Plenty of food options, ample parking, and a short run to the Monash Freeway and the South Gippsland Highway.

The landscape around it still tells its old story if you look. To the south lie the flats that run down towards Western Port, once the Koo Wee Rup Swamp, a vast wetland drained in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries to create farmland. Asparagus, potatoes and dairy took over, and Koo Wee Rup is still known for its asparagus. To the north rise the green hills of Harkaway and the Dandenongs.

The riding nearby is mostly flat and practical, but the back roads south towards Tooradin and the coast have a quiet, open charm, with long straights across the drained plains and big skies overhead. Watch out for strong crosswinds, trucks and farm vehicles. In wet weather, roads can be slippery with mud near farm entrances.

For food, you have a wide choice in the shopping centres, from cafés to family restaurants and takeaway. Fuel is readily available too. After a long day on the Great Ocean Road or the Otways, Clyde North offers a calm, unremarkable finish where you can sit down, refuel and reflect on the miles behind you.
