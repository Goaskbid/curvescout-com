Royal Mound 16

This is an existing place description, not a separately verified motorbike route. Seasonal, access and service information may have changed.

Nobody knows who was buried in Royal Mound 16, and that is one of the nicest things about it. The big tombs at A'ali have been called royal for decades, because of their size and their position on the highest ground, but there is no inscription, no king's name, nothing that settles the matter. The tombs were looted long ago, probably within a few centuries of their construction, and what the robbers did not carry off was scattered by time. What remains is architecture: a vast stack of limestone, a chamber, a ramp and a silence.

Part of the pleasure of visiting lies in guessing. You can walk around the base and imagine the labour: the quarrying, the hauling, the setting of slabs weighing several tonnes each, with no iron tools and no wheeled carts that we know of. Then you notice the mound's neighbours, all within a short walk, and begin to see a landscape designed to impress. People coming up the island from the old harbour towns would have seen the tombs on the skyline and understood who ruled here.

For the rider, the site is a calm place. Roads around A'ali are straight and flat, with some low speed bumps and plenty of roundabouts, and the approach from Madinat Isa takes only a few minutes. The surface is generally good, but the gravel verge is soft and the sand on the tarmac can surprise you on corners. There is little shade and no water at the site itself, so bring a bottle. If the sun is high, wear a light scarf or cooling neck tube under your helmet.

Bahrain's population is dense, and this part of the island is surrounded by housing, so help is never far away. Madinat Isa has petrol stations, supermarkets and small restaurants, and its market is lively in the evening.

When you leave, ride slowly along the edge of the cemetery and look at the field. Hundreds of smaller mounds sit in the gravel, many opened and collapsed, each a ruined doorway to a life no one has recorded.
