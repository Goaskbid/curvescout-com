Sossusvlei Desert Route point 1

This is an existing place description, not a separately verified motorbike route. Seasonal, access and service information may have changed.

Everything about the approach teaches you scale. The plains run out toward a horizon that looks too far away, mountains stand like cut-outs on the east side, and the road from the main gravel arteries delivers you here with a steady rumble of corrugation.

The name is older than any lodge. Sesriem means 'six belts' in Afrikaans, and the story goes that early settlers had to knot six lengths of oryx hide together to lower a bucket into the narrow gorge of the Tsauchab River to reach water. That gorge still exists, a short walk from the gate, a slot a few dozen metres deep, cut through conglomerate and cool in the shade. After rain the Tsauchab can flow a long way toward the sand sea, but in most years it never gets there, which is why the vleis downstream stay dry.

The tar road that leads from Sesriem deep into the dune sea is the reason the route exists. It runs for around sixty kilometres between red ridges that grow taller as you go, and the gate is the control on when you can use it, because the park gates follow sunrise and sunset rather than the clock on your wrist. Be at the gate early. Dawn colours the dunes, the temperature is kind, and the oryx and springbok walk the road verges before the heat sends them into the shade. By late morning the sand radiates and the road shimmers.

It helps to remember that Sesriem is a small cluster of campsite and lodging rather than a town, so food, water and anything you forgot are handled in the little complex here, and larger supplies come from the bigger settlements to the north and east. The sky here is among the darkest on any ride in the country, so an evening spent at a camp with a thermos and nothing but stars overhead is as good as the riding.

Wind is the thing to respect in the Namib. It can shift sand across the tar in a few hours, and a rider coming around a bend on a dune-crested stretch should expect a thin carpet of grit.
