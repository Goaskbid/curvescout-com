Longhzou County rock art

This is an existing place description, not a separately verified motorbike route. Seasonal, access and service information may have changed.

The great cliff painting here is known as Huashan, which means Flower Mountain, and it is part of the Zuojiang Huashan Rock Art Cultural Landscape, inscribed by UNESCO in 2016. It lies along the Ming River, a tributary of the Zuo, in the karst country of southwest Guangxi near Ningming. On a limestone cliff about two hundred meters wide and rising high above the water, thousands of red figures are painted in iron-rich pigment, showing people with raised arms, bent knees, swords and drums, and dogs among them. The shapes have lasted for roughly two thousand years, protected by an overhang and the dryness of the rock.

The painters are thought to be ancestors of the Zhuang, the Luoyue people, who lived along these rivers between about the fifth century BC and the second century AD. They made the paintings during rituals that probably involved bronze drums, which are still an important symbol in the region. Large figures, with their arms raised and knees bent, appear to be leaders, with smaller ones around them. Seeing them from a boat, as most visitors do, gives a feeling of looking at a crowd frozen in a dance.

The landscape is the second half of the experience. The river is wide, green and slow, bordered by steep karst hills with bamboo along the banks and the occasional water buffalo. Mornings are often misty, and the best light for photographs comes before the sun gets high. In the rainy season, the water rises and the colors of the painting change, with the rock appearing wetter and the figures a darker red.

For riders, a caution is necessary. China does not allow foreign visitors to ride their own motorcycles or use a foreign license on public roads, so independent touring requires special arrangements with a local operator. The nearest large town on the map, Baihe, is a good thirty kilometers away, and for a visit most people reach the site from Ningming or Chongzuo, where there are fuel stations, hotels and restaurants.

The boat trip takes a few hours, and it is wise to bring water and sun protection. The humidity is high in summer. Winter is clearer and cooler, and the paintings are easier to see in the soft light.
