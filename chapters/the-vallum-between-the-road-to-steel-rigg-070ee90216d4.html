The vallum between the road to Steel Rigg

This is an existing place description, not a separately verified motorbike route. Seasonal, access and service information may have changed.

Steel Rigg is the place everyone photographs. The crag rises steeply from the road, with the Wall running along its top and dipping into a gap known as Sycamore Gap, where a single tree stood for generations in a perfect V of the hills, until it was felled one night in September 2023. The loss caused shock across the country, and the stump now sprouts new shoots. The vallum, the great ditch and mounds south of the Wall, runs beside the road at the foot of the crags, and here it is clearly visible, a long straight hollow in the field, with the mounds standing on either side.

The road to Steel Rigg leaves the Military Road at the Once Brewed visitor centre, near a pub with the delightful name of the Twice Brewed Inn. The Military Road itself is a gem for riders, undulating over the ridge with wide views, long curves and little protection from the weather. On a clear day it is glorious, and on a wet day it is bleak and exposed. The area is also a designated International Dark Sky Park, one of the best places in England to see the stars, so a night stay brings a bonus.

Haltwhistle, about four kilometres away, has the nearest fuel and a range of shops, and Haydon Bridge, with fewer than sixteen hundred people, is smaller and farther. Hexham, twenty kilometres east, is a handsome market town with a fine abbey. Houses along the Military Road are few, so plan on carrying water and a snack, because you may not meet a cafe for miles. A short walk from the road to Steel Rigg leads along the crag to Crag Lough, a dark lake beneath cliffs.

Walk along the top of the crags in the late afternoon, when the shadows lengthen and the light turns gold. The Wall rises and falls over the ridge like a stone serpent, and the views north stretch to the horizon. It is easy to feel small here. The wind, the open sky and the sense of two thousand years of footsteps give you a feeling that few places can match.
