CURVESCOUT / PLACE TO SEE

Elze

Opening times, access and services can change - check before you go.

That is not meant as an insult. Pumps sit along the main roads, a supermarket and a few bakeries are in the centre, and you can be on your way again in ten minutes.

The surroundings are more interesting than the town. The Leine valley here is a broad, flat trough of fertile land, bordered to the west by the long wooded ridge of the Sieben Berge, a line of hills that gives the area its horizon, and to the east by the low plateau around Hildesheim. In the fields you see sugar beet, wheat and rape, with the occasional church spire or farm cluster. The river itself is slow and tree-lined, and a footpath follows it for a long way. A short walk along the bank clears the head and gives a view of ducks and herons.

They climb through beech woods in gentle bends, with the surface mostly smooth and the traffic light. In autumn the leaves lie thick on the corners, and in winter frost lingers in shaded sections. Weather comes from the west and can change fast, so keep a layer handy. The region is not famous for dramatic scenery, but it is a quietly attractive stretch of countryside, with small villages, old churches and half-timbered farms.

Do the checks on tyres and chain while the pump runs, and take the chance to stow the jacket liner. The morning light on the river is a decent consolation prize for such a practical stop.
