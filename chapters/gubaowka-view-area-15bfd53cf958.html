Gubałówka view area

This is an existing place description, not a separately verified motorbike route. Seasonal, access and service information may have changed.

Gubałówka is the ridge that stands between Zakopane and the open Podhale plain, and it exists for one reason, which is to look at the Tatras. From the top, at a little over eleven hundred metres, the whole range spreads along the southern horizon in one go: Giewont with its cross, the long saw-edge of the High Tatras behind, and the town laid out in the valley like a model. It is the view that people on postcards mean when they say Zakopane.

Most visitors ride up on the funicular, which has been hauling people up from the edge of town since the 1930s. At the top there is a market of wool socks, sheepskin and smoked cheese, a handful of restaurants, and a long ridge walk with every bench facing south. It is commercial, but it is also cheerful, and on a clear morning the commerce hardly matters because the view does all the work.

For riders, the ridge is more about the approach than the summit. The roads around the north side of Zakopane and toward Poronin and Kościelisko link villages of wooden farmhouses with steep shingled roofs, small chapels and hay meadows. The surface varies, and in the hills the corners can be tight and sudden. Take it slowly. In the foothills you get a feel for the old highland life, with shepherds still driving flocks in summer, and the smell of woodsmoke in the cooler months.

The light here changes everything. Early morning gives you clean air and sharp peaks, while afternoon haze flattens the distance. Clouds often gather on the Tatras by midday, so if a clear view matters, go early. In summer, thunderstorms build over the mountains in the afternoon and can sweep across the valley in a matter of minutes.

Fuel, food and repairs are all in Zakopane, a few minutes away, and Poronin and Kościelisko have smaller services. There is no need to carry spare fuel. But do bring a warm layer, because the ridge catches the wind, and in shoulder seasons the temperature at the top can be several degrees below the town.

Photographers should walk a little along the ridge away from the lift station. The crowds thin out within a few minutes, and the angle to the Tatras gets cleaner.
