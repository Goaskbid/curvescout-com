CURVESCOUT / PLACE TO SEE

Voiron

Opening times, access and services can change - check before you go.

The town is also where the Rossignol ski company began in 1907, a reminder that these valleys feed the Alps with both hardware and thirst.

Grenoble's traffic is close enough to cause delays on the roads at commuting time, so leave early or wait until after nine. From here the roads head toward the Vercors balcony roads and the Ardèche gorges, and the first hour is a gradual shift from industrial valley to mountain air.

To the east, the Chartreuse massif rises in vertical limestone cliffs, grey and cream, with forest on the shelves and meadows above. To the north lies the Lac de Paladru, a glacial lake with clear water and a long public beach, which makes a good swim stop in summer.

A coffee on the main street before setting off is the right way to begin, and the same coffee in the evening is a fair reward for coming back. Winter mornings can be icy on the valley roads, so check the temperature before you leave.
