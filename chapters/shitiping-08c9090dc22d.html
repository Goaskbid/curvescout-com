Shitiping

This is an existing place description, not a separately verified motorbike route. Seasonal, access and service information may have changed.

Shitiping means roughly stone steps terrace, and that already tells you a lot about the place. It sits on Taiwan's east coast, where the Coastal Mountain Range drops almost straight into the Pacific, and the highway squeezes itself between rock and sea. The area belongs to the long, wild stretch of Provincial Highway 11, the road that runs south from Hualien towards Taitung with the ocean on one side nearly all the way.

This is among the great motorcycle roads of the island. The surface is generally good, the bends are open and flowing, and the sea view is constant: a deep Pacific blue in the morning, turning silver-grey under cloud. There are tunnels and cuttings, a few tight spots at the headlands and a lot of rolling coastal tarmac where you can settle into a rhythm. Traffic is mostly local trucks, scooters and cyclists, the last of whom come here for exactly the same reason you do. Be careful in typhoon season, from roughly July to October, when rain and rockfall can close sections, and take extra care on wet days, as the paint on the road can be slick.

The geology deserves a look. The rocks along this coast are the result of the Philippine Sea plate colliding with the Eurasian plate, and the cliffs and terraced stone platforms at Shitiping show it, with layered rock shaped by waves and the long push of the tectonic squeeze. People walk the stepped rock shelf at low tide, watching crabs and small tide pools, and the roar of surf on black stone is a good reason to stop and switch off the engine. The coast was also home to Amis communities, an Indigenous people whose fishing and harvest culture is still alive in the villages here, and in summer you might come across festival preparations.

Fuel stations are spaced out along the coast, mostly in the small towns, and it is wise never to push your range. Convenience stores, found in nearly every village, sell cold drinks and simple food, and they are a good place for a rest.
