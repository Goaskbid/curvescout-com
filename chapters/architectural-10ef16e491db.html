Architectural

This is an existing place description, not a separately verified motorbike route. Seasonal, access and service information may have changed.

Nyasvizh is a small town of about thirteen thousand people in the flat, wooded country south-west of Minsk, and for four centuries it was the seat of one of the great noble families of the old Grand Duchy of Lithuania, the Radziwiłłs. They were wealthy enough to maintain private armies, print books, found schools and build, and what they built here is the reason Nyasvizh appears on the UNESCO list, together with the castle at nearby Mir.

The complex is a castle, a palace and a park surrounded by a moat and ponds, with bastions and walls going back to the sixteenth century. The Radziwiłłs made it their main residence, and it passed through war, siege and fire, being rebuilt and extended again and again. Across the water stands the Corpus Christi church, built in the 1580s and among the earliest Baroque churches in this part of Europe, with a facade that was a model for later buildings across the region. The family crypt beneath holds members of the dynasty, and the church is still in use.

The setting is quiet and lovely, with swans on the ponds, old lime and oak trees along the paths and a long avenue of poplars. Mist often hangs over the water in the morning, and the light on the red brick walls in the late afternoon is soft and golden. A small town grew up around the castle, with a market square, a few older houses, and a calm, provincial feeling.

The ride here is on gentle country roads through birch and pine, with long straight stretches between villages and very little traffic outside the main roads. Surfaces are generally good, but in spring after thaw, frost heave and patching can catch out an inattentive rider. Police checks and speed cameras exist, so keep to the limits. Nyasvizh itself has fuel and shops, and the larger towns along the highway have more. For a meal, order machanka, a rich stew of pork and sausage eaten with pancakes, and cold kvass. Opening times at the complex change with the season, so ask locally. Late May and September are the most comfortable months, with warm days and cool nights.
