Campion

Opening times, access and services can change - check before you go.

Campion is a small, quiet community; Berthoud, about six kilometres away, is a small town of some six thousand people with a historic main street.

The surrounding land is irrigated cropland, with fields of corn, wheat and sugar beets, and the Little Thompson River winds quietly through cottonwoods. Loveland, a bit farther north, is known for its sculpture and for its Valentine's Day postmark.

For a rider, this is the doorstep to the mountains. US 34 runs from Loveland up the Big Thompson Canyon to Estes Park, a beautiful, winding road squeezed between granite cliffs and a rushing river, which was devastated by a flash flood in 1976 and again in 2013. It is a road for caution: tight, shaded, and prone to rockfalls. The plains roads here are dead straight, grid-like, and fast, with the wind blowing hard across the open country, often from the west.

Fuel is easy in Berthoud and Loveland. The weather here can change rapidly: summer afternoons bring thunderstorms off the mountains, often with hail, while spring may bring sudden snow. Early mornings are the best time, with the sun low and the peaks sharp, and the air cold and clear. Set out before the traffic begins, fill the tank, and head west toward the canyons, with the whole range ahead of you.
