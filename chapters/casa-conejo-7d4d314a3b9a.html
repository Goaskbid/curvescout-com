Casa Conejo

This is an existing place description, not a separately verified motorbike route. Seasonal, access and service information may have changed.

Casa Conejo is a small unincorporated community in Ventura County, tucked among the suburbs of Newbury Park on the western edge of Thousand Oaks. Conejo means rabbit in Spanish, and the whole area, the Conejo Valley, takes its name from the old Rancho El Conejo land grant of the Spanish and Mexican era. Today it is houses, shopping centres, schools and freeways, set in a basin ringed by chaparral-covered hills that turn green in winter and golden by summer.

As a fuel stop, it does exactly what you need. Stations, coffee and fast food are all close to the US 101, and Camarillo and Thousand Oaks, both large towns within ten kilometres, have everything else. What makes it interesting is what lies around it. Just to the west, the 101 drops down the Conejo Grade, a long, steep descent into the Oxnard Plain, with sudden views of farmland stretching to the ocean and the Channel Islands floating offshore on clear days. Trucks crawl, and the grade has a reputation for brake fires and accidents, so ride it with care and keep your distance.

South of here lie the Santa Monica Mountains, and that is where the riding becomes something special. Roads such as Potrero Road, Yerba Buena Road, Decker Canyon and Mulholland Highway wind through canyons, oak woodland and rocky ridges before dropping steeply to the Pacific Coast Highway near Malibu. These are famous motorcycle roads, technical and narrow, with blind corners, sheer drops and plenty of sport bikes on weekends. The Rock Store on Mulholland has been a gathering point for riders for decades. Speed enforcement is common and the canyons are unforgiving, so this is a place to ride well rather than fast.

The weather is usually kind: warm, dry and bright for much of the year, with cool marine air in the mornings near the coast and occasional fierce Santa Ana winds in autumn, when hot, dry gusts sweep down from the desert and raise the fire danger. Wildfires have scarred these hills many times, and after big fires the roads can be closed or damaged.

For the Pacific Coast ride toward Santa Barbara, Casa Conejo is the practical last top-up before the coastal stretch through Oxnard and Ventura. Fill the tank, get a cold drink, and enjoy the knowledge that the best views are still ahead.
