Primary reference directory

These links identify source scope. The named organizations have not endorsed CurveScout. References do not grant rights to ingest or republish their data feeds.

  • Swiss Alpine passes: pass-specific information
    Swiss Alpine pass information. An industry association, not a national road authority. A seasonal pattern is not a current observation.
  • Grossglockner operator: opening times
    Road operating hours, admission and possible motorcycle-specific closures. Opening-time page is not an all-road live status feed.
  • Nockalm operator: opening hours
    Motorcycle admission and operating hours for the Nockalm road only.
  • Norwegian Public Roads Administration: traffic information
    Road messages, mountain-pass information, cameras and ferry planning links in Norway.
  • US National Park Service: Yellowstone park roads
    Park road status and seasonal information. Adjacent state roads require the relevant state road authority.
  • US National Park Service: Tioga Road
    Seasonal Tioga Road information. Not a forecast of a future reopening.
  • US National Park Service: Blue Ridge Parkway road closures
    Parkway section closures, not all connecting roads. Reference carried from the source-backed calendar package; not a live status statement.
  • DriveBC: delays and road closures
    British Columbia events, directions and schedules. Not all Canada and not a positive open-road assertion.
  • Australian Bureau of Meteorology: monsoon
    Northern Australia wet-season context. Not applicable as a single national riding calendar.
  • NZ Transport Agency: Journey Planner
    State-highway disruptions and travel context. Local roads may need a local council check.
  • Japan Meteorological Agency: climate regions
    Regional climate context, not a road-opening forecast.
  • Vialidad Nacional: national-road information
    National-road condition resources; provincial and cross-border sections may use other authorities.
  • Chile Direccion de Vialidad: road management
    Road-network management reference. Does not provide a verified opening status for a CurveScout route.
  • Western Cape Government: public road-closure layer
    Western Cape only. Distinguishes no access, stop-and-go, proceed-when-safe and local-access restrictions.
  • UK government: traffic-information services
    Major-road information and links for England, Scotland and Wales. Not a complete rural-road status feed.
  • Switzerland Tourism: Furka Pass
    Furka Pass elevation and named road identity. Tourism season text is not a current opening observation.
  • Switzerland Tourism: Swiss mountain passes
    Distinguishes Nufenen as the highest pass entirely within Switzerland. Does not establish live access or a new road measurement.
  • Bison Fute: current traffic
    National traffic context. Check the road owner and exact section rather than treating the national display as every local pass.
  • Hautes-Alpes: Info Route 05
    Departmental road events, planned works and named high-pass categories in Hautes-Alpes. Not a France-wide opening guarantee.
  • South Tyrol Provincial Traffic Information Centre
    Provincial traffic, roadworks and pass-road information. Verify each side of a cross-border or cross-province itinerary.
  • Autobahn GmbH: traffic reports
    Federal motorway notices and transfers, not clearance of scenic state or local roads.
  • DGT: traffic information and incidents
    Official incident information, including identified regional inputs. The authority states that the map does not replace road signs.
