Why this ride

Alpine scenery with border-road discipline. Out of Chamonix-Mont-Blanc and back again: 235 km, roughly 4 h 31 in the saddle. The climbs: Col des Montets and Grand St Bernard approach. It tops out at about 2,450 m. It crosses France, Switzerland and Italy - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. The passes are the reason to go. The plan has 1 food stop and 1 fuel stop marked.
