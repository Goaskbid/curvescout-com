Why this ride

Scenery first, traffic last, with checkpoints that make sense on a bike. Out of Nice and back again: 918 km, roughly 16 h 42 in the saddle. The stops in between: Torre Pellice, Saint-Gervais-les-Bains and Sainte-Croix. The highest point is about 2,100 m. It crosses France, Italy and Switzerland - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. Long stretches are under trees. The plan has 1 fuel stop marked.
