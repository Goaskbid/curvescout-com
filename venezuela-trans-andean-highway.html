Why this ride

From Barinas it runs 257 km to Mérida; allow about 3 h 30 of riding. The stops in between: Santo Domingo and Pico El Aguila Pass. The highest point is about 4,100 m. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

Roadbook

  1. Barinas start

    Barinas is where the day begins. Next is Santo Domingo.

  2. Santo Domingo scenic

    Santo Domingo comes up 86 km in. Next is Pico El Aguila Pass.

  3. Pico El Aguila Pass scenic · 4118 m

    171 km in you reach Pico El Aguila Pass. The road climbs 4118 m on the way up to it, hard. Reckon on about 27 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. Nothing on this route is higher. Next is Merida, mostly downhill.

  4. Merida finish

    Merida closes the day.
