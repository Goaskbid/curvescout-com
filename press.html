Press, citation and reuse

CurveScout is a free motorcycle route planner and ride library, made in Meilen, Switzerland. Every ride page gives the distance and riding time measured on a stored road built from OpenStreetMap data, the stops, the height profile and plain notes on the roads. Nothing on the site needs an account.

Quoting and linking

You are welcome to quote a ride, a few sentences of its notes or a figure from it, with a link to the page you took it from. Please do not copy whole pages or long passages. Photos on CurveScout belong to their photographers (mostly Wikimedia Commons) and keep their own licences; the credit is next to each photo.

How to cite a ride

Every ride page ends with a ready-made line. The pattern:

“Susten · Grimsel · Furka Triple Crown”, CurveScout, https://curvescout.com/rides/susten-grimsel-furka-triple-crown.html (accessed 18 November 2026).

For the site as a whole: CurveScout, free motorcycle route planner, https://curvescout.com/.

The best rides as a table

curvescout-best-rides.csv lists the 1,172 rides we currently rate best, in 93 countries: name, country, page, high point, first and last stop. You may read it and cite single rides with a link; copying the collection or reusing it elsewhere needs our written permission. Distances and riding times are on each ride page. The read-me says where each column comes from.

How the numbers are made

How the evidence works explains what is measured, what is recorded and what is an estimate. Sources and coverage lists every data source and its licence, and Suggest a correction is the fastest way to fix a mistake.
