Red Mountain Pass

Red Mountain Pass is a road pass at 3,358 m on the US-550. The summit lies about 10 km south-east of Telluride and about 11 km north-west of Silverton.

Before you ride it

At 3,358 m the air is about 22 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: winter controls possible.

3 of the 7 other passes within 80 km whose height we hold are higher.
