Torre

Torre is a road pass at 1,993 m. The summit lies about 7 km east of Loriga and about 10 km north-west of Covilhã.

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: winter weather restrictions possible.

2 of the 3 other passes within 80 km whose height we hold are higher.
