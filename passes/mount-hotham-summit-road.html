Mount Hotham Summit Road

Mount Hotham Summit Road is a road pass at 1,845 m on the B500. The summit lies about 2 km north-west of Hotham Heights and about 11 km north-west of Dinner Plain.

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: winter snow controls.
