Col des Aravis

Col des Aravis is a road pass at 1,486 m. The summit lies about 5 km south-east of La Clusaz and about 7 km south-east of Saint-Jean-de-Sixt.

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: several passes summer only.

1 of the 2 other passes within 80 km whose height we hold is higher.
