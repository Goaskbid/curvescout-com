Valdresflye

Valdresflye is a road pass at 1,389 m. The summit lies about 28 km north-west of Heggenes and about 30 km north-east of Vang.

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: winter closure possible.
