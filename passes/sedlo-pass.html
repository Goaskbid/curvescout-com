Sedlo Pass

Sedlo Pass is a road pass at 1,907 m. The summit lies about 10 km south-west of Žabljak and about 15 km east of Plužine.

Before you ride it

At 1,907 m the air is about 12 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: winter closure possible.
