Lizard Head Pass

Lizard Head Pass is a road pass at 3,116 m. The summit lies about 15 km south of Mountain Village and about 17 km south-west of Telluride.

Before you ride it

At 3,116 m the air is about 20 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.

6 of the 7 other passes within 80 km whose height we hold are higher.
