Cuesta del Obispo

Cuesta del Obispo is a road pass at 3,348 m on the RP 33, RN 40. The summit lies about 31 km west of Chicoana and about 33 km south-west of Rosario de Lerma.

Before you ride it

At 3,348 m the air is about 22 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.
