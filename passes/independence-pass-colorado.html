Independence Pass Colorado

Independence Pass Colorado is a road pass at 3,687 m on the CO-82. The summit lies about 24 km south-east of Aspen and about 28 km south-west of Leadville North.

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: seasonal winter closure.

It is the highest of the 7 passes within 80 km whose height we hold.
