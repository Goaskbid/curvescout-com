Mangart Saddle

Mangart Saddle is a road pass at 2,055 m. The summit lies about 8 km south-east of Tarvisio and about 11 km west of Kranjska Gora.

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: summer opening only; toll and vehicle restrictions.

It is the highest of the 5 passes within 80 km whose height we hold.
