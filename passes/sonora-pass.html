Sonora Pass

Sonora Pass is a road pass at 2,933 m on the CA 108. The summit lies about 36 km west of Bridgeport.

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: summer opening only.
