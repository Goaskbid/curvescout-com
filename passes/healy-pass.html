Healy Pass

Healy Pass is a road pass at 334 m. The summit lies about 20 km south-west of Kenmare and about 22 km west of Bantry.

Before you ride it

At 334 m the air is about 2 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.

It is the highest of the 2 passes within 80 km whose height we hold.
