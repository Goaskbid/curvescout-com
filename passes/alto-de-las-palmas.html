Alto de Las Palmas

Alto de Las Palmas is a road pass at 2,550 m. The summit lies about 8 km east of Envigado and about 9 km east of Itagüí.

Before you ride it

At 2,550 m the air is about 17 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.
