Nathu La

Nathu La is a road pass at 4,310 m on the Nathu La road. The summit lies about 17 km south-west of Xarsingma and about 23 km east of Gangtok.

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: permit and border restrictions.

It is the highest of the 2 passes within 80 km whose height we hold.
