Puerto de la Ragua

Puerto de la Ragua is a road pass at 2,041 m. The summit lies about 6 km south-east of Aldeire and about 6 km south of Ferreira.

Before you ride it

At 2,041 m the air is about 13 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.
