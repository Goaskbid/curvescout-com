Jvari Cross Pass

Jvari Cross Pass is a road pass at 2,379 m on the S3, E117. The summit lies about 3 km north-west of Gudauri and about 23 km south-west of St’epants’minda.

Before you ride it

At 2,379 m the air is about 15 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.
