Mortirolo Pass

Mortirolo Pass is a road pass at 1,852 m. The summit lies about 4 km east of Mazzo di Valtellina and about 4 km east of Tovo di Sant'Agata.

Before you ride it

At 1,852 m the air is about 12 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.

2 of the 2 other passes within 80 km whose height we hold are higher.
