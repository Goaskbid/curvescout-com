Guanella Pass

Guanella Pass is a road pass at 3,557 m. The summit lies about 12 km south of Georgetown and about 23 km south-west of Idaho Springs.

Before you ride it

At 3,557 m the air is about 23 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: winter closure possible.
