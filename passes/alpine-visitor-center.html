Alpine Visitor Center

Alpine Visitor Center is a road pass at 3,595 m on the US 34. The summit lies about 21 km west of Estes Park and about 42 km north of Granby.

Before you ride it

At 3,595 m the air is about 23 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: summer opening only.

It is the highest of the 4 passes within 80 km whose height we hold.
