Kenosha Pass

Kenosha Pass is a road pass at 3,057 m on the US-285. The summit lies about 25 km east of Breckenridge and about 29 km south-east of Keystone.

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: winter controls possible.
