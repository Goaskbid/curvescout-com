Grimsel Pass

Before you ride it

At 2,164 m the air is about 14 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: alpine; seasonal closure possible.

3 of the 6 other passes within 80 km whose height we hold are higher.
