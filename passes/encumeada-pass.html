Encumeada Pass

Encumeada Pass is a road pass at 1,007 m. The summit lies about 5 km south-east of São Vicente and about 6 km north-west of Curral das Freiras.

Before you ride it

At 1,007 m the air is about 7 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.
