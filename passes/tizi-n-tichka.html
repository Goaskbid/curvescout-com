Tizi n Tichka

Tizi n Tichka is a road pass at 2,260 m on the N9. The summit lies about 14 km west of Telouet and about 18 km south of Zerkten.

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: winter snow and closure possible.

It is the highest of the 5 passes within 80 km whose height we hold.
