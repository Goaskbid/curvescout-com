How to read it

"Closed" and "Restricted" are what the open traffic feed of South Tyrol reported for a pass at the checked time above. This is a dated snapshot, not a continuous live display; open the pass page for an updated check. "Scheduled" starts later. "Report ended" means the last report has run out - that is not a confirmation that the road is open. Outside South Tyrol CurveScout has no live source yet, so those passes are not listed here; their pages give height, season and the rides that cross them. The signs on the road always decide.
