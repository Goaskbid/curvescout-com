Glaubenberg Pass

Glaubenberg Pass is a road pass at 1,543 m. The summit lies about 8 km east of Flühli and about 9 km north-west of Giswil.

Before you ride it

At 1,543 m the air is about 10 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.

6 of the 7 other passes within 80 km whose height we hold are higher.
