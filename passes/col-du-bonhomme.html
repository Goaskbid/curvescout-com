Col du Bonhomme

Col du Bonhomme is a road pass at 949 m. The summit lies about 4 km north-west of Lapoutroie and about 6 km west of Fréland.

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: winter closures possible.
