Lassen Peak trailhead

Lassen Peak trailhead is a road pass at 2,594 m on the CA 89. The summit lies about 30 km north-west of Chester and about 33 km east of Shingletown.

Before you ride it

At 2,594 m the air is about 17 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: summer opening only.
