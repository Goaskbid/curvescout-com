Franconia Notch

Franconia Notch is a road pass at 590 m. The summit lies about 9 km south-west of Deerfield and about 16 km south-east of Littleton.

Before you ride it

At 590 m the air is about 4 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.

1 of the 2 other passes within 80 km whose height we hold is higher.
