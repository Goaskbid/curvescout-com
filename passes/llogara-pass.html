Llogara Pass

Llogara Pass is a road pass at 1,027 m on the SH8. The summit lies about 9 km west of Vranisht and about 10 km south-west of Brataj.

Before you ride it

At 1,027 m the air is about 7 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.
