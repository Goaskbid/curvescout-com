Snake Pass

Snake Pass is a road pass at 512 m on the A57. The summit lies about 5 km east of Glossop and about 7 km north of High Peak.

Before you ride it

At 512 m the air is about 3 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.
