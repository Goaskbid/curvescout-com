Before you ride it

At 971 m the air is about 6 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: winter snow and ice possible.

1 of the 2 other passes within 80 km whose height we hold is higher.
