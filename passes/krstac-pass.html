Krstac Pass

Krstac Pass is a road pass at 926 m on the P1. The summit lies about 5 km south-east of Kotor and about 8 km south-east of Dobrota.

Before you ride it

At 926 m the air is about 6 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.

It is the highest of the 2 passes within 80 km whose height we hold.
