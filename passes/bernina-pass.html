Bernina Pass

Bernina Pass is a road pass at 2,330 m. The summit lies about 9 km north of Poschiavo and about 16 km south of Livigno.

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: alpine; seasonal closure possible.

It is the highest of the 4 passes within 80 km whose height we hold.
