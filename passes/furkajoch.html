Furkajoch

Furkajoch is a road pass. The summit lies about 5 km west of Damüls and about 5 km north of Blons.

Before you ride it

CurveScout does not hold a surveyed summit height for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: alpine; seasonal closure possible.
