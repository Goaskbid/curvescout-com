Shipka Pass

Shipka Pass is a road pass at 1,190 m on the E85. The summit lies about 4 km north of Shipka and about 13 km south of Gabrovo.

  • RoadE85

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.
