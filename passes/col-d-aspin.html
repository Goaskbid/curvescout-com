Col d Aspin

Col d Aspin is a road pass at 1,489 m. The summit lies about 14 km north of Saint-Lary-Soulan and about 15 km south-east of Campan.

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: high passes winter closure possible.
