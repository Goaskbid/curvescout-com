      How to read this list

      Heights are summit heights. A ride counts as crossing a pass when its stored road passes within 300 m of the summit - 190 passes are crossed by at least one ride. Season notes are general; whether a pass is open today is a separate question, answered on each pass page only where a live source exists (currently South Tyrol). Everywhere else, check the road authority before you ride.
