Abra del Acay

Abra del Acay is a road pass at 4,895 m on the RN 40. The summit lies about 26 km south of San Antonio de los Cobres and about 26 km west of Santa Rosa de Tastil.

Before you ride it

At 4,895 m the air is about 32 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: weather dependent.
