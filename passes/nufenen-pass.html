Nufenen Pass

Nufenen Pass is a road pass at 2,478 m. The summit lies about 11 km north of Formazza and about 14 km south-west of Airolo.

Before you ride it

At 2,478 m the air is about 16 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: alpine; seasonal closure possible.

It is the highest of the 7 passes within 80 km whose height we hold.
