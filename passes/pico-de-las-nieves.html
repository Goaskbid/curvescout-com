Pico de las Nieves

Pico de las Nieves is a road pass at 1,949 m. The summit lies about 4 km north of San Bartolomé de Tirajana and about 6 km south-east of Tejeda.

Before you ride it

At 1,949 m the air is about 13 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.
