Engineer Pass

Engineer Pass is a road pass at 3,901 m. The summit lies about 9 km south-east of Ouray and about 19 km north of Silverton.

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: short summer season only.

It is the highest of the 8 passes within 80 km whose height we hold.
