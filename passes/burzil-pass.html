Burzil Pass

Burzil Pass is a road pass at 4,100 m on the Astore Valley road. The summit lies about 33 km south-east of Eidgah.

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: restricted and seasonal access.
