Piano Battaglia

Piano Battaglia is a road pass at 1,572 m. The summit lies about 7 km south-east of Collesano and about 7 km south of Isnello.

Before you ride it

At 1,572 m the air is about 10 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.
