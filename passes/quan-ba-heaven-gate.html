Quan Ba Heaven Gate

Quan Ba Heaven Gate is a road pass at 1,500 m. The summit is at Thị Trấn Tam Sơn and about 17 km west of Thị Trấn Yên Minh.

Before you ride it

At 1,500 m the air is about 10 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.

It is the highest of the 2 passes within 80 km whose height we hold.
