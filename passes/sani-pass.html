Sani Pass

Sani Pass is a road pass at 2,876 m. The summit lies about 28 km north-west of Himeville and about 39 km south-east of Mokhotlong.

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: weather dependent.
