Zoji La

Zoji La is a road pass at 3,528 m on the NH1. The summit lies about 33 km north-east of Pahlgām.

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: weather and security controls apply.
