Kancamagus Pass

Kancamagus Pass is a road pass at 863 m on the NH 112. The summit lies about 16 km north-east of Woodstock and about 21 km north-east of Thornton.

Before you ride it

At 863 m the air is about 6 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.

It is the highest of the 3 passes within 80 km whose height we hold.
