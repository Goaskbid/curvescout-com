Trollstigen

Trollstigen is a road pass at 858 m. The summit lies about 13 km south of Åndalsnes and about 27 km north-east of Valldal.

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: seasonal closures and maintenance restrictions.
