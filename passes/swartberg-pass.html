Swartberg Pass

Swartberg Pass is a road pass at 1,583 m. The summit lies about 15 km south of Prince Albert and about 31 km north-west of Oudtshoorn.

Before you ride it

At 1,583 m the air is about 10 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.
