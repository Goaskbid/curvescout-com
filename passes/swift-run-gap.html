Swift Run Gap

Swift Run Gap is a road pass at 720 m. The summit lies about 8 km south-east of Elkton and about 12 km north-west of Stanardsville.

Before you ride it

At 720 m the air is about 5 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.
