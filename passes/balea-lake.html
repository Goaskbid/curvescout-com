Balea Lake

Balea Lake is a road pass at 2,042 m on the DN7C. The summit lies about 13 km south of Cârţişoara and about 14 km south of Arpașu de Sus.

Before you ride it

At 2,042 m the air is about 13 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: summer opening only.
