Sognefjellshytta

Sognefjellshytta is a road pass at 1,434 m. The summit lies about 30 km north of Farnes and about 38 km south of Bismo.

Before you ride it

At 1,434 m the air is about 9 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: summer opening only.

It is the highest of the 3 passes within 80 km whose height we hold.
