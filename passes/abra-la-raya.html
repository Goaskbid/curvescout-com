Abra La Raya

Abra La Raya is a road pass at 4,338 m on the PE-3S. The summit lies about 18 km east of Layo and about 22 km east of Conchopata.

Before you ride it

At 4,338 m the air is about 28 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: high-altitude weather risk.
