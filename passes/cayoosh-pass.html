Cayoosh Pass

Cayoosh Pass is a road pass at 1,275 m on the BC 99. The summit lies about 28 km east of Pemberton.

Before you ride it

At 1,275 m the air is about 8 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.
