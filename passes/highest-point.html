Highest Point

Highest Point is a road pass at 2,255 m on the N204. The summit lies about 2 km north-west of Baculongan and about 2 km south-west of Bangao.

Before you ride it

At 2,255 m the air is about 15 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.
