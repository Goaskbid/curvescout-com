Before you ride it

At 5,328 m the air is about 35 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: summer opening only; permits and altitude risks.

It is the highest of the 3 passes within 80 km whose height we hold.
