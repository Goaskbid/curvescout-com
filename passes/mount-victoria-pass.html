Mount Victoria Pass

Mount Victoria Pass is a road pass at 1,040 m on the Great Western Highway. The summit lies about 6 km north-west of Blackheath and about 15 km south-east of South Bowenfels.

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: winter weather possible.
