Yellowhead Pass

Yellowhead Pass is a road pass at 1,131 m on the Yellowhead Highway. The summit lies about 25 km west of Jasper and about 27 km west of Jasper Park Lodge.

Before you ride it

At 1,131 m the air is about 7 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: winter controls possible.
