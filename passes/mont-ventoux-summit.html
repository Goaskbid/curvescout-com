Mont Ventoux summit

Mont Ventoux summit is a road pass at 1,909 m. The summit lies about 10 km north-east of Bédoin and about 11 km south of Buis-les-Baronnies.

Before you ride it

At 1,909 m the air is about 12 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: summit road seasonal.
