Hardknott Pass

Hardknott Pass is a road pass at 393 m. The summit lies about 16 km west of Ambleside and about 18 km east of Seascale.

Before you ride it

At 393 m the air is about 3 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.

1 of the 2 other passes within 80 km whose height we hold is higher.
