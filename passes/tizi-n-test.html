Tizi n Test

Tizi n Test is a road pass at 2,100 m on the R203. The summit lies about 29 km north-west of Aoulouz and about 29 km south of Adassil.

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: weather and road-condition restrictions possible.
