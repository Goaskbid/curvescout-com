Sela Pass

Sela Pass is a road pass at 4,170 m. The summit lies about 19 km south-east of Tawang and about 41 km north-west of Bomdila.

Before you ride it

At 4,170 m the air is about 27 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.
