Katschberg Pass

Katschberg Pass is a road pass at 1,641 m. The summit lies about 5 km north of Rennweg am Katschberg and about 5 km south of Sankt Michael im Lungau.

Before you ride it

At 1,641 m the air is about 11 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: generally open; local closures possible.

5 of the 5 other passes within 80 km whose height we hold are higher.
