Tioga Pass

Tioga Pass is a road pass at 3,031 m on the CA 120. The summit lies about 34 km north-east of Yosemite Valley and about 38 km north-west of Mammoth Lakes.

Before you ride it

At 3,031 m the air is about 20 °C cooler than at sea level on the same day (standard atmosphere, 6.5 °C per 1,000 m) - wind on the summit makes it feel colder still, so pack a warm layer even in summer. Season note: summer opening only.

It is the highest of the 2 passes within 80 km whose height we hold.
