Passo Giovo

Passo Giovo is a road pass at 2,094 m. The summit lies about 4 km south-east of Racines and about 6 km south-west of Stanghe.

Before you ride it

Summit weather can differ sharply from conditions at your start. The standard-atmosphere lapse rate is a reference model, not a forecast for this pass. Check the summit forecast, wind and current road conditions; carry a warm layer even in summer. Season note: Timmelsjoch summer opening only.
