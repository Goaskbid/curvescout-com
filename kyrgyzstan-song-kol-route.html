Why this ride

A one-way ride of 270 km, Naryn to Kochkor - roughly 3 h 48 in the saddle. The stops in between: Moldo Ashuu Pass, Song Kol and Kalmak Ashuu Pass. The highest point is about 3,450 m. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

Roadbook

  1. Naryn start

    Naryn is where the day begins. Next is Moldo Ashuu Pass.

  2. Moldo Ashuu Pass scenic · 3346 m

    Moldo Ashuu Pass comes up 68 km in. The road climbs 3346 m on the way up to it, hard. Reckon on about 22 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. Next is Song Kol, mostly downhill.

  3. Song Kol scenic

    135 km in you reach Song Kol. Next is Kalmak Ashuu Pass.

  4. Kalmak Ashuu Pass scenic · 3447 m

    Kalmak Ashuu Pass, late in the day at 3447 m. The road climbs 3447 m on the way up to it, hard. Nothing on this route is higher. Next is Kochkor, mostly downhill.

  5. Kochkor finish

    Kochkor is where it ends.
