Why this ride

Mountain switchbacks with a sea-air finish. A 304 km loop that starts and ends near Pavia, about 5 h 51 of riding. It goes by way of Bobbio Trebbia road and Portofino coast. The climb of the day is Passo del Penice. It is a high-mountain day. The plan has 1 fuel stop marked.
