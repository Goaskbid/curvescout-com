Why this ride

Remote Patagonia, ferries and a route that demands planning. On the way it passes Pumalín forest, Chaitén overnight and Futaleufú river roads. The highest point is about 1,000 m. Back roads rather than main roads, where the map allows. The plan has 1 fuel stop marked.
