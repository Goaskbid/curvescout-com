Why this ride

Scenery first, traffic last, with checkpoints that make sense on a bike. A 198 km loop that starts and ends near Castelldefels, about 3 h 36 of riding. It goes by way of Santa Coloma de Cervelló, Cap de Creus Coastal Ride, Canet de Mar and Vilassar de Mar. Castelldefels is the high point. Much of it runs through woodland. The plan has 1 food stop and 1 fuel stop marked.
