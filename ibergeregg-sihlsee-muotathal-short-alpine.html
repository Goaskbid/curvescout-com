Why this ride

Close, punchy and better than its distance suggests. 96 km round from Unterägeri - plan on about 1 h 50 of riding. On the way it passes Einsiedeln, Sihlsee water line and Muotathal valley. The climbs: Ratenpass warm-up and Ibergeregg pass road. It tops out at 1,406 m. The passes are the reason to go. The plan has 1 food stop marked.

Roadbook

  1. Ratenpass warm-up pass · 1077 m

    Ratenpass warm-up is the summit of this stretch at 1077 m. Reckon on about 3 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. Next is Einsiedeln coffee stop, mostly downhill.

  2. Einsiedeln coffee stop food · 882 m

    Einsiedeln coffee stop is where this day stops to eat. Next is Sihlsee water line.

  3. Sihlsee water line swim · 889 m

    29 km in you reach Sihlsee water line. Next is Muotathal valley, mostly downhill.

  4. Muotathal valley reset scenic · 610 m

    Muotathal valley, about halfway at 610 m. You lose 279 m on the way down to it, steeply. Next is Ibergeregg pass road, climbing 796 m.

  5. Ibergeregg pass road pass · 1406 m

    Ibergeregg pass road tops the climb at 1406 m. The road climbs 796 m on the way up to it, hard. Reckon on about 5 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. Nothing on this route is higher. High enough to hold ice in the shaded corners early and late in the year. Next is Ratenpass warm-up, mostly downhill.

  6. Back to Ratenpass warm-up finish · 1077 m

    The route ends here after 96 km. You lose 329 m on the way down to it, hard.
