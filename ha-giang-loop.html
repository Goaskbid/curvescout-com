Why this ride

350 km round from Hà Giang - plan on about 5 h 39 of riding. It goes by way of Quan Ba Heaven, Dong Van, Ma Pi Leng Pass and Meo Vac. The highest point is about 1,500 m. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

Roadbook

  1. Ha Giang start

    Ha Giang is where the day begins. Next is Quan Ba Heaven.

  2. Quan Ba Heaven Gate scenic · 1500 m

    Quan Ba Heaven comes up 58 km in. The road climbs 1500 m on the way up to it, steadily. Reckon on about 10 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. Nothing on this route is higher. Next is Dong Van, mostly downhill.

  3. Dong Van scenic

    117 km in you reach Dong Van. Next is Ma Pi Leng Pass.

  4. Ma Pi Leng Pass scenic · 1500 m

    Ma Pi Leng Pass, about halfway at 1500 m. Next is Meo Vac, mostly downhill.

  5. Meo Vac scenic

    Meo Vac sits at 233 km, about halfway. Next is Du Gia.

  6. Du Gia scenic

    Du Gia comes up 292 km in. Next is Ha Giang.

  7. Ha Giang finish

    Ha Giang closes the day.
