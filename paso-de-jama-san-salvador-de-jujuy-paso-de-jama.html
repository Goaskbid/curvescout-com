Why this ride

541 km from San Salvador de Jujuy to the finish, about 7 h 27 of riding. It goes by way of Purmamarca and Susques. The highest point is about 4,200 m. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

  4. Paso de Jama finish · 4200 m

    Paso de Jama closes the day. The road climbs 4200 m on the way up to it, steadily. Reckon on about 27 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. Nothing on this route is higher.
