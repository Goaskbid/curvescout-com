Why this ride

A 82 km loop that starts and ends near Landeck, about 1 h 25 of riding. On the way it passes St Anton am Arlberg, Arlberg Pass and Flexen Pass. The highest point is about 2,150 m. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

Roadbook

  1. Landeck start

    Landeck is where the day begins. Next is St Anton am Arlberg.

  2. St Anton am Arlberg scenic

    St Anton am Arlberg comes up 21 km in. Next is Arlberg Pass.

  3. Arlberg Pass scenic · 1793 m

    41 km in you reach Arlberg Pass. The road climbs 1793 m on the way up to it, steeply. Reckon on about 12 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. Nothing on this route is higher. Next is Flexen Pass.

  4. Flexen Pass scenic · 1773 m

    Flexen Pass, late in the day at 1773 m. Next is Lech, mostly downhill.

  5. Lech finish

    Back at Lech. That is the ride.
