Why this ride

Coast mountains, fast weather and a proper Canadian day. Out of Squamish and back again: 321 km, roughly 5 h 32 in the saddle. The stops in between: Whistler, Pemberton and Duffey Lake Road. The highest point is about 1,600 m. Back roads rather than main roads, where the map allows. The plan has 1 food stop and 1 fuel stop marked.
