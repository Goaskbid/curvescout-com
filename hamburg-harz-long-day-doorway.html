Why this ride

When the north needs altitude, this is the serious long-day option. A 494 km loop that starts and ends near Uelzen, about 9 h 29 of riding. On the way it passes Rappbode dam, Goslar old town and Goslar overnight option. The climb of the day is Torfhaus high road. It is a high-mountain day. The plan has 1 food stop and 1 fuel stop marked.
