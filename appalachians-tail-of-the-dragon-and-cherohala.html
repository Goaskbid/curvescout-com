Why this ride

Corners, discipline and zero interest in showing off. Out of Robbinsville and back again: 202 km, roughly 3 h 53 in the saddle. The stops in between: Cherohala Skyway and Tellico Plains. Tail of the Dragon is the high point. The passes are the reason to go. The plan has 1 food stop and 1 fuel stop marked.
