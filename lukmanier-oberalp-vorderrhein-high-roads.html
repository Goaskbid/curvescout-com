Why this ride

Two passes, one river valley and a proper touring spine. Out of Chur and back again: 333 km, roughly 6 h 24 in the saddle. On the way it passes Andermatt and Disentis. The climbs: Lukmanier pass road and Oberalppass high road. It tops out at 2,044 m. The passes are the reason to go. The plan has 2 food stops and 1 fuel stop marked.

Roadbook

  1. Chur fuel gate fuel · 593 m

    Fuel at 0 km. Next is Lukmanier pass road, climbing 1322 m.

  2. Lukmanier pass road pass · 1915 m

    You top out at 1915 m here. The road climbs 1322 m on the way up to it, steadily. Reckon on about 9 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. High enough to hold ice in the shaded corners early and late in the year. Next is Andermatt, mostly downhill.

  3. Andermatt base reset food · 1444 m

    A break here at 164 km. You lose 471 m on the way down to it, steadily. Reckon on about 6 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. Next is Oberalppass high road, climbing 600 m.

  4. Oberalppass high road pass · 2044 m

    The high point of this section, 2044 m up. The road climbs 600 m on the way up to it, steadily. Nothing on this route is higher. At this height the road is a summer one: snow gates can stay shut into June and shut again in October. Next is Disentis food stop, mostly downhill.

  5. Disentis food stop food · 1130 m

    Food at 203 km, roughly 4 hours into the riding. You lose 914 m on the way down to it, hard. Reckon on about 3 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. Next is Vorderrhein valley flow, mostly downhill.

  6. Vorderrhein valley flow scenic · 650 m

    Vorderrhein valley flow comes up 210 km in. You lose 480 m on the way down to it, hard. Next is Chur.

  7. Back to Chur finish · 593 m

    Back to Chur closes the day.
