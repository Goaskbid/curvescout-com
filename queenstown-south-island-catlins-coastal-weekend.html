Why this ride

Scenery first, traffic last, with checkpoints that make sense on a bike. 257 km round from Queenstown - plan on about 5 h 21 of riding. The climb of the day is Catlins Coastal Weekend. It tops out at about 1,250 m. Long stretches are under trees. The plan has 1 food stop and 1 fuel stop marked.
