Why this ride

Water, forest and Highland atmosphere without chasing a postcard queue. This road trip is shaped as a ride, not as a shortcut: leave the start cleanly, use the first checkpoint for fuel and weather discipline, then let the middle 53 km be the part you remember. The best part sits around Loch Ness view, Glen Affric forest core, while Drumnadrochit fuel gate and Beauly food stop keep the day practical instead of turning it into a fuel hunt. Check the selected map before departure: scenic sections, transfer sections, speed/control reminders, risk points and the GPX checkpoint order are deliberately visible so the route is understandable before the engine starts. Ride it early if the area is popular, keep the photo stops legal, and shorten the route if weather, daylight or energy stops agreeing with the plan.
