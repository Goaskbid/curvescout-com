Why this ride

Escarpments, cool air and proper overlook stops. 301 km round from Kurrajong Heights - plan on about 5 h 47 of riding. It goes by way of Mount Victoria and Three Sisters lookout. Oberon high country is the high point. It tops out at about 1,050 m. The passes are the reason to go. The plan has 1 food stop marked.
