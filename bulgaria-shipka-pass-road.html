Roadbook

  1. Gabrovo start

    Gabrovo is where the day begins. Next is Shipka Pass.

  2. Shipka Pass scenic · 1190 m

    Shipka Pass comes up 22 km in. The road climbs 1190 m on the way up to it, hard. Reckon on about 8 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. Nothing on this route is higher. Next is Shipka Memorial, mostly downhill.

  3. Shipka Memorial scenic

    45 km in you reach Shipka Memorial. Next is Kazanlak.

  4. Kazanlak finish

    Kazanlak closes the day.
