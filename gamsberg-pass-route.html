Roadbook

  1. Walvis Bay start

    Walvis Bay is where the day begins. Next is Kuiseb Pass.

  2. Kuiseb Pass scenic · 905 m

    Kuiseb Pass comes up 216 km in. The road climbs 905 m on the way up to it, steadily. Reckon on about 6 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. Next is Gamsberg Pass, climbing 760 m.

  3. Gamsberg Pass scenic · 1665 m

    433 km in you reach Gamsberg Pass. The road climbs 760 m on the way up to it, steadily. Reckon on about 11 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. Nothing on this route is higher. Next is Windhoek, mostly downhill.

  4. Windhoek finish

    Windhoek closes the day.
