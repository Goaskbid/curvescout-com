Why this ride

Scenery first, traffic last, with checkpoints that make sense on a bike. This road trip is shaped as a ride, not as a shortcut: leave the start cleanly, use the first checkpoint for fuel and weather discipline, then let the middle 134 km be the part you remember. The best part sits around Costa Brava and Tossa de Mar scenic road, Costa Brava and Tossa de Mar high point, Costa Brava and Tossa de Mar photo pull-off, while Costa Brava and Tossa de Mar fuel gate and Costa Brava and Tossa de Mar food stop keep the day practical instead of turning it into a fuel hunt. Check the selected map before departure: scenic sections, transfer sections, speed/control reminders, risk points and the GPX checkpoint order are deliberately visible so the route is understandable before the engine starts. Ride it early if the area is popular, keep the photo stops legal, and shorten the route if weather, daylight or energy stops agreeing with the plan.
