Why this ride

The stops in between: San Alfonso and El Volcán. The highest point is 1,800 m. The line favours views over speed.

Roadbook

  1. San José de Maipo start · 970 m

    San José de Maipo is where the day begins. Next is San Alfonso.

  2. San Alfonso scenic · 1100 m

    San Alfonso comes up 39 km in. Next is El Volcán, climbing 300 m.

  3. El Volcán scenic · 1400 m

    79 km in you reach El Volcán. The road climbs 300 m on the way up to it, steadily. Reckon on about 3 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. Next is Baños Morales, climbing 400 m.

  4. Baños Morales finish · 1800 m

    Baños Morales closes the day. The road climbs 400 m on the way up to it, steadily. Reckon on about 5 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. Nothing on this route is higher.
