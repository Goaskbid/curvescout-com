Why this ride

From Mendoza it runs 412 km to Los Andes; allow about 5 h 38 of riding. It goes by way of Potrerillos, Uspallata, Puente del Inca and Cristo Redentor Pass. The highest point is about 4,050 m. It crosses from Argentina into Chile - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

Roadbook

  1. Mendoza start

    Mendoza is where the day begins. Next is Potrerillos.

  2. Potrerillos scenic

    Potrerillos comes up 82 km in. Next is Uspallata.

  3. Uspallata scenic

    165 km in you reach Uspallata. Next is Puente del Inca.

  4. Puente del Inca scenic

    Puente del Inca, about halfway. Next is Cristo Redentor Pass.

  5. Cristo Redentor Pass scenic · 3832 m

    Cristo Redentor Pass sits at 330 km, late in the day. The road climbs 3832 m on the way up to it, hard. Reckon on about 25 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. Nothing on this route is higher. Next is Los Andes Chile, mostly downhill.

  6. Los Andes Chile finish

    The route ends here after 412 km.
