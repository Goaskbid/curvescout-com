Everything CurveScout does for a motorcycle ride

Find a ride near you

Type a town, a pass or your position. CurveScout suggests rides that start nearby, sorted by what you want: passes, coast, forest or quiet backroads.

22,781 motorcycle rides

Itinerary records across more than 130 country contexts. Recorded distances and modelled riding times support planning; a catalogue record is not a field inspection or confirmation of current access.

Roadbooks for every ride

Each ride has its own page: the map, every stop, leg-by-leg notes and what to see on the way.

Multi-day trips

Join rides into a trip of several days with overnight stops, and see each day on the map.

CurveScout Ride navigator

Turn-by-turn directions on your phone, a guide that tells you about the places you pass, and a new route if you leave the planned one.

Mountain passes and seasons

Pass heights, historical opening patterns and seasonal context. Current opening observations have separate coverage and dates; a usual season does not establish that a pass is open.

Road rules by country

A selection of dated motorcycle rules and authority references. Check the rule coverage, relevant road and vehicle, effective dates and official source before travel.

Guides for planning

How to choose a route, check a GPX file and budget a riding day.

Places to see

Thousands of short guides to the villages, castles, lakes and viewpoints along the rides.
