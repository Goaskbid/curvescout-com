Global riding season calendar

Qualitative operating and climate patterns for planning. They are not forecasts, opening guarantees or measurements of road-surface conditions.

Grossglockner operating season

Operator describes a usual early-May to early-November season. Boundary months vary. This is an operating pattern, not live status.

Grossglockner road operator. Source reviewed 2026-11-12.

Nockalm operating season

Operator describes beginning of May to end of October, with separate motorcycle night restrictions.

Nockalm road operator. Source reviewed 2026-11-12.

Tioga Road seasonal access

NPS describes late May or June until sometime in November. Snow clearance and repairs determine actual dates.

National Park Service Tioga and Glacier Point. Source reviewed 2026-11-12.

Going-to-the-Sun alpine section

NPS describes whole-road opening usually in late June or early July. Lower sections have different access.

National Park Service Going-to-the-Sun Road. Source reviewed 2026-11-12.

Upper Thailand seasonal context

Cooler period roughly mid-October to mid-February; heat before the rains; rainy season roughly mid-May to mid-October. Road closures, smoke and storms need separate current observations.

Thai Meteorological Department seasons. Source reviewed 2026-11-12.

Southern Thailand east-coast contrast

The east coast can remain wet late in the year. Other months stay unrated here rather than borrowing the north's seasonal model.

Thailand Department of Land Development climate. Source reviewed 2026-11-12.

Northern Australia wet-season context

Far-northern wet-season context about October to April. Dry-season labels do not confirm road access, temperature comfort or absence of cyclones.

Australian Bureau of Meteorology monsoon. Source reviewed 2026-11-12.

New Zealand seasonal context

Official broad seasons only. The South Island alpine roads need separate snow, wind, rainfall and road-status checks.

Tourism New Zealand climate. Source reviewed 2026-11-12.

Patagonian Andes seasonal context

Editorial mapping of the official regional description to southern seasons: snowy mountain winters and stronger summer winds. Not a measured monthly climate normal.

Official Patagonia tourism climate. Source reviewed 2026-11-12.

Southwestern Cape climate contrast

Winter rainfall is an important contrast with summer-rainfall parts of South Africa. Only core winter context is mapped; remaining months are unrated.

South Africa government weather reporting. Source reviewed 2026-11-12.

Japan main islands: regional seasonal context

Japan has distinct regional climates. Early-summer rainfall and late-summer/autumn storms matter; snowy mountain roads need their own closure sources. This overview is not applied to all Japanese road surfaces.

Japan Meteorological Agency climate regions. Source reviewed 2026-11-12.

Northern Japan: snow-sensitive context

Northern Japan has very cold winters, with heavy snow on the Sea of Japan side and in mountains. Summer temperatures and rain vary by region. These monthly groupings are editorial context, not pass-opening dates.

Japan Meteorological Agency climate regions. Source reviewed 2026-11-12.

Singapore: monsoons and thunderstorm context

Rain occurs throughout the year. Northeast-monsoon surges can bring prolonged rain, while inter-monsoon thunderstorms can be intense. The calendar does not identify a guaranteed dry riding month.

Meteorological Service Singapore climate. Source reviewed 2026-11-12.
