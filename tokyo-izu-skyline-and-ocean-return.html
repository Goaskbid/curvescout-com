Why this ride

Skyline curves, hot-spring towns and Pacific air if the weather behaves. This road trip is shaped as a ride, not as a shortcut: leave the start cleanly, use the first checkpoint for fuel and weather discipline, then let the middle 105 km be the part you remember. The best part sits around Izu Skyline high road, Ito ocean stop, while Atami fuel and coast gate and Shuzenji onsen food keep the day practical instead of turning it into a fuel hunt. Check the selected map before departure: scenic sections, transfer sections, speed/control reminders, risk points and the GPX checkpoint order are deliberately visible so the route is understandable before the engine starts. Ride it early if the area is popular, keep the photo stops legal, and shorten the route if weather, daylight or energy stops agreeing with the plan.
