Why this ride

Scenery first, traffic last, with checkpoints that make sense on a bike. 346 km round from Anmore - plan on about 6 h 18 of riding. The stops in between: Sechelt, Duffy Lake Road Loop and Richmond. Vancouver / British Columbia is the high point. It tops out at about 1,100 m. Much of it runs through woodland. The plan has 1 food stop and 1 fuel stop marked. Highlight: a ferry crossing to Ganges, about 19 km of water - check the timetable and book ahead in summer.
