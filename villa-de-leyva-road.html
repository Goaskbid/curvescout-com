Villa de Leyva Road

North from Bogotá into Boyacá: dry hills, a colonial town of cobbles and the desert valley behind it.

Why this ride

From Tunja it runs 172 km to Ráquira; allow about 3 h 18 of riding. It goes by way of Arcabuco and Villa de Leyva. The highest point is 2,820 m. The line favours views over speed.

Roadbook

  1. Tunja start · 2820 m

    Tunja is where the day begins. Reckon on about 4 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. Nothing on this route is higher. Next is Arcabuco, mostly downhill.

  2. Arcabuco scenic · 2600 m

    Arcabuco comes up 57 km in. Reckon on about 3 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. Next is Villa de Leyva, mostly downhill.

  3. Villa de Leyva scenic · 2140 m

    115 km in you reach Villa de Leyva. You lose 460 m on the way down to it, steadily. Next is Ráquira.

  4. Ráquira finish · 2150 m

    Ráquira closes the day.
