Why this ride

Leave the city behind and spend the day on roads with texture. 474 km round from Walnut Grove - plan on about 8 h 37 of riding. The stops in between: Sunshine Coast Ferry Ride, Richmond and Point Roberts. The climbs: Vancouver / British Columbia and Birch Bay. It tops out at about 1,400 m. It crosses from Canada into United States - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. The plan has 1 food stop and 1 fuel stop marked. Highlight: a ferry crossing to Nanaimo, about 4 km of water - check the timetable and book ahead in summer.
