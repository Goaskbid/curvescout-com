Why this ride

Scenery first, traffic last, with checkpoints that make sense on a bike. A 218 km loop that starts and ends near Constantia, about 3 h 58 of riding. On the way it passes Cape Town, Atlantis, Stellenbosch and Bainskloof and Du Toitskloof. The climb of the day is Constantia. Long stretches are under trees. The plan has 1 food stop and 1 fuel stop marked.
