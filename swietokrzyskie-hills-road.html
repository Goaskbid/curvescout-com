Roadbook

    Kielce is where the day begins. Next is Nowa Słupia, mostly downhill.

  2. Nowa Słupia scenic

    Nowa Słupia comes up 37 km in. Next is Święty Krzyż.

  3. Święty Krzyż pass · 590 m

    590 m, and the road stops climbing. The road climbs 590 m on the way up to it, steadily. Reckon on about 4 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. Nothing on this route is higher. Next is Opatów, mostly downhill.

  4. Opatów finish

    Opatów closes the day.
