Why this ride

Forest, passes and sea towns stitched into a touring week. A one-way ride of 634 km, George to George - roughly 12 h 12 in the saddle. On the way it passes Wilderness beach, Knysna lagoon, Tsitsikamma forest and back through Knysna lagoon. It is a high-mountain day. The plan has 1 food stop marked.
