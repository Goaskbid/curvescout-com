Why this ride

Jura curves and a lake return with big views. A 111 km loop that starts and ends near Gex, about 2 h 07 of riding. On the way it passes Jura balcony road, Nyon lakeside and La Dôle view area. The highest point is about 950 m. It crosses from France into Switzerland - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. The passes are the reason to go. The plan has 1 food stop marked.
