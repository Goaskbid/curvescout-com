Why this ride

Marble canyon, high road and weather risk. It goes by way of Tianxiang and Qixingtan beach. Hehuanshan high road is the high point. It tops out at about 3,300 m. It is a high-mountain day. The plan has 1 food stop marked.

Roadbook

  2. Tianxiang food

    Tianxiang is where this day stops to eat. Next is Hehuanshan high road.

  3. Hehuanshan high road pass · 3275 m

    This is where the road stops climbing. Next is Qixingtan beach.

  4. Qixingtan beach stop

    Qixingtan beach, late in the day. Next is back to Taroko Gorge.

  5. Back to Taroko Gorge finish

    Back at Taroko Gorge. That is the ride.
