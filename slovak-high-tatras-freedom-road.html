Why this ride

Out of Poprad and back again: 78 km, roughly 1 h 19 in the saddle. The stops in between: Strbske Pleso, Stary Smokovec and Tatranska Lomnica. The highest point is about 1,100 m. It suits an unhurried touring pace.
