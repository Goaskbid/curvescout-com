Why this ride

Vorarlberg balcony roads, high meadows and compact pass rhythm. This road trip is shaped as a ride, not as a shortcut: leave the start cleanly, use the first checkpoint for fuel and weather discipline, then ride Faschinajoch high road with something in reserve. The best part sits around Bregenzerwald villages, Furkajoch pass road, Faschinajoch high road, while Dornbirn exit gate and Rankweil food reset keep the day practical instead of turning it into a fuel hunt. Check the selected map before departure: scenic sections, transfer sections, speed/control reminders, risk points and the GPX checkpoint order are deliberately visible so the route is understandable before the engine starts. Ride it early if the area is popular, keep the photo stops legal, and shorten the route if weather, daylight or energy stops agreeing with the plan.
