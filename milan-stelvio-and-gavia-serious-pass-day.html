Why this ride

Hairpin mythology with no room for fantasy planning. 374 km round from Bormio - plan on about 7 h 12 of riding. It goes by way of Santa Maria and Tirano. The climbs: Stelvio Pass and Gavia Pass. It tops out at about 2,750 m. It crosses from Italy into Switzerland - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. It is a high-mountain day. The plan has 1 food stop and 1 fuel stop marked.
