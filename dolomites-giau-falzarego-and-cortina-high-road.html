Why this ride

Sharper, higher, wilder: a Dolomite day with status checks mandatory. A 54 km loop that starts and ends near Selva di Cadore, about 1 h 02 of riding. The climbs: Passo Giau, Falzarego Pass and Valparola high road. It tops out at about 2,250 m. It is a high-mountain day. The plan has 1 food stop marked.
