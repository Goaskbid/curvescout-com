Why this ride

Leave the city behind and spend the day on roads with texture. A 346 km loop that starts and ends near Stellenbosch, about 6 h 17 of riding. On the way it passes Moorreesburg, Cederberg Mountain Weekend and Constantia. The climb of the day is Retreat. The plan has 1 food stop and 1 fuel stop marked.
