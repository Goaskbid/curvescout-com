Why this ride

A good northern day: fields, bay air, lake roads and no motorway boredom. This road trip is shaped as a ride, not as a shortcut: leave the start cleanly, use Probstei villages to settle fuel, weather and timing, then let the middle 74 km be the part you remember. The best part sits around Probstei villages, Hohwacht bay, Lütjenburg reset, while the most useful town stop keeps the day practical instead of turning it into a fuel or food hunt. Check the OSM map, Google preview and GPX checkpoint order before departure; they use the same waypoint sequence. Ride it with legal pull-offs, patience through villages and enough daylight to stop without rushing.
