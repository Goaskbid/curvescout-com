Why this ride

The classic hill-road taste without pretending it is a two-day tour. 228 km round from Chōfu - plan on about 5 h of riding. The stops in between: Odawara, Hakone Turnpike, Lake Ashi view stop and Gotemba. The highest point is 850 m. The plan has 1 food stop and 1 fuel stop marked.

Roadbook

  1. Tokyo south-west exit start · 40 m

    Tokyo south-west is where the day begins. Next is Odawara.

  2. Odawara fuel gate fuel · 15 m

    Odawara is the fuel stop for this leg. Next is Hakone Turnpike, climbing 835 m.

  3. Hakone Turnpike serpentine · 850 m

    90 km in you reach Hakone Turnpike. The road climbs 835 m on the way up to it, hard. Reckon on about 5 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. Nothing on this route is higher. Next is Lake Ashi view stop.

  4. Lake Ashi view stop photo · 720 m

    Lake Ashi view stop, about halfway at 720 m. Next is Gotemba return check, mostly downhill.

  5. Gotemba return check food · 450 m

    Food at 119 km, roughly 3 hours into the riding. You lose 270 m on the way down to it, steadily. Reckon on about 3 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. Next is Tokyo, mostly downhill.

  6. Back to Tokyo finish · 40 m

    The route ends here after 228 km. You lose 410 m on the way down to it, steadily.
