Campos do Jordão Climb

Up the Mantiqueira to the highest town in Brazil: araucaria pine, cold nights and a road that climbs sixteen hundred metres.

Roadbook

  1. Taubaté start · 570 m

    Taubaté is where the day begins. Next is Tremembé.

  2. Tremembé scenic · 590 m

    Tremembé comes up 31 km in. Next is Campos do Jordão, climbing 1038 m.

  3. Campos do Jordão scenic · 1628 m

    61 km in you reach Campos do Jordão. The road climbs 1038 m on the way up to it, steadily. Reckon on about 7 °C colder than the valley, and more in the wind. Nothing on this route is higher. Next is São Bento do Sapucaí, mostly downhill.

  4. São Bento do Sapucaí finish · 780 m

    São Bento do Sapucaí closes the day. You lose 848 m on the way down to it, steadily.
