Why this ride

Scenery first, traffic last, with checkpoints that make sense on a bike. 297 km round from Samora Correia - plan on about 5 h 24 of riding. It goes by way of Silveira, Colares, Sesimbra and Charneca de Caparica. The climb of the day is Arrábida and Setúbal. Long stretches are under trees. The plan has 1 food stop and 1 fuel stop marked.
