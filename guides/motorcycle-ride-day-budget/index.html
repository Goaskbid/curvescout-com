Motorcycle ride-day budget: include stops and delays

A riding-time estimate is only one part of the day. This calculator adds the stops and delay allowance you choose. It does not select a safe riding duration, forecast traffic or change your navigation route.

Use time you can explain

Enter the riding estimate shown for the actual journey, not an estimate from a shorter variant. Add fuel, meals, photo stops and planned visits separately. Use a delay allowance as a planning assumption, not as a prediction.

Compare before adding another pass

Try a larger stop budget or a later departure and compare the resulting finish time. The difference shows the consequence of your inputs; it is not an assessment of road safety or a live arrival estimate.

Keep the plan private and revisit it

The optional Save button stores only this budget in your browser. The budget inputs stay in this browser unless you export and share them. Export provides a portable copy, and Clear removes the saved budget.

Calendar road checks remain a separate step. A budget that fits your available time can still include a closed road or a restriction that applies to your motorcycle.

Your day, in hours

Example inputs. Set your own estimates. Elapsed-time arithmetic, not a safety or daylight assessment.

Example: 4 hours riding + 90 minutes stops + 45 minutes delay = 6 hours 15 minutes total. A 09:00 departure finishes at 15:15 on the same clock, before any time-zone or clock change.

Uses a single unchanged local clock. It does not model time-zone changes, daylight-saving changes, fatigue, daylight or road closures. This calculator does not transmit your budget inputs.
