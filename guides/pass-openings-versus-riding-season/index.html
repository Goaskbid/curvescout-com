Pass opening status, operating hours and riding season

An operating season answers a different question from an open-road observation. Keep both, plus your actual visit time, when planning a pass crossing.

Five signals, five different meanings

Current status concerns a particular road, scope and observation time. Published hours describe an operating schedule. A planned reopening is an intention. Historical opening dates describe previous years. Seasonal climate provides broader context.

None can silently replace another. A history of June openings cannot confirm a June crossing this year, and a forecast of sunshine cannot override a road closure.

Admission and transit are not the same

Road operators can publish a last-admission time as well as the end of operation. Grossglockner and Nockalm publish road-specific hours. Use the planned arrival at the relevant gate rather than treating hotel departure as admission.

Check the whole visit

Where evidence expires or changes during a planned visit, retain that gap. Do not average two open observations into a continuous all-day permission. The same road can need another assessment when you cross it again on the return journey.

Use the calendar as a workspace

Watch the named road, save the intended timing and return before departure. Check reminders are prompts to review information, not promises that the road will open. The coverage page distinguishes researched sources from connected live observations.
