Why this ride

A serious northbound day into Pyrenean bend country. 258 km round from Vic - plan on about 4 h 58 of riding. On the way it passes Ripoll mountain and La Molina overnight option. The climb of the day is Collada de Toses. It tops out at about 1,850 m. The passes are the reason to go. The plan has 1 food stop and 1 fuel stop marked.
