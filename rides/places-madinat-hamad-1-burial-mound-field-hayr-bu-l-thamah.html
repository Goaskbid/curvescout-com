Why this ride

A 909 km round trip from Madīnat Ḩamad - roughly 16 h 30 in the saddle. On the way it passes Al Zubarah Archaeological Site and back through Al Zubarah Archaeological Site. It crosses from Bahrain into Qatar - carry ID and check the toll rules. The line favours views over speed.

The stops

  1. 1. Madinat Hamad 1 Burial Mound Field

    Start here.

    Madinat Hamad is a young town built on very old ground. It was developed in the 1980s as a planned settlement, a grid of housing blocks, mosques, schools and shopping areas laid out on the desert west of A'ali, and its growth has pushed the suburbs right up to the edge of a burial field that is thousands of years old. The contrast is vivid: on one side, apartment blocks and satellite dishes; on the other, a field of rubble domes and silence.

    Read the complete Madinat Hamad 1 Burial Mound Field write-up.

  2. 2. Al Zubarah Archaeological Site

    About 455 km in.

    Out on the north-west coast of Qatar, where the desert slides into a shallow turquoise sea, stand the low ruins of Al Zubarah, once one of the busiest pearling and trading towns in the Gulf. In the late eighteenth and nineteenth centuries the town thrived on pearls dived from the oyster beds offshore, traded on to India, Persia and beyond. Then it declined, was abandoned in the early twentieth century, and the sand covered it. Archaeologists have since uncovered streets, houses, a souk, mosques and a long town wall running in an arc around the old harbour. UNESCO inscribed the site in 2013.
