Why this ride

Covered bridges and village greens rather than headline mountain roads. The road: NH-127, NH-103 and local roads. Distance and riding time are estimates from the checkpoints until the road is measured on this page.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Henniker

    Henniker looks like the Currier and Ives print it was made to resemble, and it does so without effort. The town lies on the Contoocook River in the hills of central New Hampshire, with a white-steepled church, a long common and a New England College campus that gives it a pleasant undercurrent of students. The surrounding countryside is a patchwork of old farms, stone walls, covered bridges and small villages, and the lanes between them are among the best in the state for a quiet ride.
