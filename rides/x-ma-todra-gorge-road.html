Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Tinghir

    Tinghir lies on the southern side of the High Atlas in Morocco, where the river Todra runs out of the mountains and across a hard, red desert, and the contrast is startling. A long ribbon of palm trees follows the river for kilometres, with small terraced fields of barley, alfalfa, almonds and vegetables underneath, all watered by channels that have been in use for centuries. From a viewpoint on the edge of town, the green oasis stretches away between bare ochre hills, a strip of life in a landscape of stone.

  2. 2. Todra Gorge

    Todra Gorge is a slot in the High Atlas where the Todgha River has cut through red limestone, leaving walls that rise sheer, close to three hundred metres, on either side of a gap just wide enough for the road and the stream. The narrowest part is only a few paces across, and in the morning the walls glow orange while the bottom stays cool and shaded. Tinghir, a town of nearly forty thousand people, lies at the mouth of the valley, and it is the place for fuel, food and a bed.
