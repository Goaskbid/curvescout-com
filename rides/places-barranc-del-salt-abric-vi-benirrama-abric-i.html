Why this ride

A 65 km round trip from Benasau - roughly 1 h 07 in the saddle. The stops in between: Barranc de la Penya Blanca, Benirrama- Abric I and back through Barranc de la Penya Blanca.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Barranc del Salt- Abric VI

    Alcoi is the capital of this mountain district, a big industrial town in a basin between ranges, and its reputation rests on paper, textiles and fiesta. The town was a centre of paper-making for centuries, drawing on the water of its rivers, and in the nineteenth century, it became one of the first industrial cities in Valencia, with factories in the valley bottom and fine modernist buildings on the hill. Every April, it stages the most famous of the Moors and Christians festivals in Spain, with hundreds of costumed participants, mock battles, firecrackers and processions that last for days. The festival is dedicated to Saint George, and it commemorates the legendary victory of Christians over Muslims in the thirteenth century.

  2. 2. Barranc de la Penya Blanca

    Planes is a small village in the hills of the Comtat in northern Alicante province, crowned by a castle and looking out over a valley of olive and cherry orchards. The Barranc de la Penya Blanca, the ravine of the white rock, lies close by, among the folded limestone ridges between Planes, Benimarfull and the reservoir at Beniarrés.

  3. 3. Benirrama- Abric I

    In the Vall de Gallinera, a long, narrow valley in the mountains of northern Alicante, the Benirrama shelter is one of a cluster of rock art sites, part of the Rock Art of the Mediterranean Basin World Heritage listing. The valley is particularly rich in macro-schematic art, a distinctive style found only in this region, with large, abstract human figures and symbols painted in red, and the nearby Pla de Petracos is the most famous site of this style. Benirrama itself is also the name of a ruined Moorish castle on a hill above the valley.
