Why this ride

A 248 km round trip from Mirşid - roughly 4 h 17 in the saddle. The stops in between: Muncel - Comorița, Groșii Țibleșului - Izvorul Șurii and back through Muncel - Comorița. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Popeni - Dealul Racova

    Timber is the economy of the forest villages of Sălaj, and the roads show it. Popeni, in the north-west of the county, is among the hills that supply beech, oak and spruce to sawmills in the valleys, and the lane to Dealul Racova, about three kilometres from Mirșid, is one of the routes along which logging trucks run. You will see stacks of cut trunks beside the road waiting for collection, with bark and sawdust smells and big tyre marks in the mud.

  2. 2. Muncel - Comorița

    On the hills between the valley of the Someș and the Meseș mountains, in the north-west of Transylvania, the ground rises in gentle ridges, and this is the highest point in a small stretch of country near the villages of Vad, Gâlgău and Poiana Blenchii. The label is Muncel, meaning little hill, and Comorița, which carries the sense of a small treasure, and both names tell you about the country: low, open, rounded hills, used for generations as pasture and orchard land. A lot of local folklore has treasure buried in hills like this, and many an old shepherd has a tale of a hoard or a lost mine.

  3. 3. Groșii Țibleșului - Izvorul Șurii

    The Țibleș mountains rise like a dark wall between the Lăpuș country and the valleys of Maramureș, a volcanic range of dense spruce and beech forest topping out at nearly two thousand metres. On their slopes, near the source known as Izvorul Șurii above the village of Groșii Țibleșului, the forest feels almost untouched: springs bubbling out of mossy banks, fallen trunks covered in fungi, and a silence broken only by woodpeckers and the wind. This is bear country, and wolves and lynx roam these woods too. The area lies on the edge of the Maramureș region, famous for its wooden churches, carved gates and deeply traditional village life.
