Why this ride

65 km from Pego to Pego, about 1 h 07 of riding. The stops in between: Barranc de la Penya Blanca, Barranc de frainos- Abric I and back through Barranc de la Penya Blanca. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Benirrama- Abric II

    The Vall de Gallinera is a narrow valley in the mountains of northern Alicante, where eight small villages hang on the slopes among cherry trees, and Benirrama is one of them. The valley lies inland from the Costa Blanca, only an hour or so from the tourist beaches, yet it feels like another century. Terraced slopes, stone walls, irrigation channels and a thin, quiet road follow the Gallinera river, and in late winter and spring the cherry blossom covers the hillsides in white.

  2. 2. Barranc de la Penya Blanca

    Planes is a small village in the hills of the Comtat in northern Alicante province, crowned by a castle and looking out over a valley of olive and cherry orchards. The Barranc de la Penya Blanca, the ravine of the white rock, lies close by, among the folded limestone ridges between Planes, Benimarfull and the reservoir at Beniarrés.

  3. 3. Barranc de frainos- Abric I

    The mountains behind Benidorm are a world away from the high-rise hotels and the beaches. Inland from the Costa Blanca, the land rises quickly into a jumble of limestone ridges, the highest of which, the Serra d'Aitana, reaches over fifteen hundred metres, the roof of the province of Alicante. Between Sella, Benilloba and Relleu, deep in this rugged country, the Barranc de Frainos holds a rock shelter with prehistoric paintings, part of the UNESCO listing of Rock Art of the Mediterranean Basin on the Iberian Peninsula. The sites here are small and fragile, and access is usually limited to guided visits, but the landscape is worth the journey.
