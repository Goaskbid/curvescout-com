  3. 3. Jelenia Góra

    The Karkonosze, the Giant Mountains, wall off the south of Lower Silesia, and Jelenia Góra sits in the broad basin at their feet like a market town at the foot of a castle wall. Until 1945 it was Hirschberg, a German linen and glass town, and the arcaded burghers' houses around the rectangular market square still show that prosperity, with Baroque gables and a town hall in the middle. About eighty-seven thousand people live here now, which is enough for a proper centre and not enough to be a headache.
