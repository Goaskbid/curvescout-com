Why this ride

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. George

    George lies at the foot of the Outeniqua Mountains, a long blue ridge that shuts off the sea breezes to the south and gives the town a warm, sheltered feel. It is the largest town on the Garden Route, and it sits in a strip of green country between the mountains and the Indian Ocean, with forest, farmland and rivers close by. The Dutch founded the settlement in 1811 and named it for King George III, and the old oak tree planted in the early days still stands in the centre of town, protected as a national monument.

  5. 5. Knysna

    Knysna lies around a tidal lagoon on the Garden Route, with a pair of sandstone cliffs known as the Heads guarding the narrow channel to the sea. Forest runs down to the water, and the whole town has a green, relaxed look, with a population around sixty-nine thousand and a waterfront full of small shops and restaurants. The lagoon is an estuary, home to rare seahorses and a rich oyster industry, and every year there is an oyster festival in the winter that fills the town.
