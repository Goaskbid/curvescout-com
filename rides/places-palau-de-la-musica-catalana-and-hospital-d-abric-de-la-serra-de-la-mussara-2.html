Why this ride

From Barri Gòtic it runs 278 km to Barri Gòtic; allow about 4 h 48 of riding. On the way it passes Roca Roja, Abric de la Serra de la mussara and back through Roca Roja. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Palau de la Música Catalana and Hospital d

    Lluís Domènech i Montaner built hospitals the way other people build palaces. The Hospital de Sant Pau, which stands about a kilometre from the Sagrada Família along the broad Avinguda de Gaudí, is a campus of brick pavilions covered in coloured tiles, domes and mosaics, set in gardens, and linked by underground tunnels so that patients could be moved without going out into the weather. It was paid for by a banker's bequest, begun in 1902 and finished, with his son Pere taking over, in 1930, and it continued as a working hospital for decades before the patients moved to a new building and the old one became a museum and cultural centre. Together with the Palau de la Música Catalana, it was inscribed on the UNESCO list in 1997.

  2. 2. Roca Roja

    Roca Roja, the red rock, is a fitting name for this corner of inland Catalonia, where sandstone and conglomerate cliffs glow rust-coloured in the evening light above pine forest and small terraced fields. The point lies in the hills between Querol and Mediona, where the comarques of Alt Camp, Alt Penedès and Anoia meet, a rugged, thinly populated upland that most people speed past on the motorway between Barcelona and Lleida without realising it exists.

  3. 3. Abric de la Serra de la mussara

    Climb out of Reus on a clear morning and the Serra de la Mussara rises ahead like a wall, a limestone escarpment that holds up the edge of the Prades plateau and drops steeply to the plain of the Camp de Tarragona. The rock shelters along this cliff line, close to the village of Vilaplana, are part of the prehistoric painted landscape of eastern Spain. The best reason to stop, though, is the view: on a good day you look straight down over hazelnut orchards and olive groves to Reus, Tarragona and the sea.
