Ride storyWaypoints

Why this ride

Sustained elevation change, linked bends and open summit views. It also links naturally to Mounts Iglit-Baco Natural Park. Highlight: a ferry crossing to San Jose, about 13 km of water - check the timetable and book ahead in summer.

Access context

Verify ferries, typhoon effects, road condition and local access. Specific flag: rough sections; ferries; weather.
