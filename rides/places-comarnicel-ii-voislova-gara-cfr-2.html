Why this ride

316 km from Petrila to Petrila, about 5 h 27 of riding. On the way it passes Geoagiu-Băi - Germisara / Dâmbul Romanilor, Voislova - Gara CFR and back through Geoagiu-Băi - Germisara / Dâmbul Romanilor. The highest point is about 1,450 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

  2. 2. Geoagiu-Băi - Germisara / Dâmbul Romanilor

    The Romans never met a hot spring they did not want to bathe in, and at Germisara, now Geoagiu-Băi, they found warm water bubbling from the ground in a valley at the edge of the Apuseni mountains. They built baths, carved pools into the rock and dedicated offerings to the nymphs and to the healing gods, including small gold plaques found by archaeologists in one of the pools. The site, called Dâmbul Romanilor, the Romans' hillock, sits on the World Heritage list as part of the Dacian frontier, together with the military fort of Germisara in the Mureș valley below. Today, Geoagiu-Băi is again a spa resort, with modern pools fed by the same warm springs.

  3. 3. Voislova - Gara CFR

    The Bistra valley is a natural corridor, and armies have known it for millennia. Running east from Caransebeș toward the Iron Gate of Transylvania and the Hațeg basin, it was the route by which Trajan's legions advanced on the Dacian heartland in the wars of AD 101 to 106, and later the road that linked Roman forts and towns along the way. Near the village of Voislova, by the railway station, the remains of a Roman site have been identified as counted within the UNESCO serial site of the Dacian frontier, a reminder of how important this valley was to the Roman presence in the province.
