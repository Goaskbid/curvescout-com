Why this ride

133 km from Albocàsser to Albocàsser, about 2 h 18 of riding. On the way it passes Abric del.mas Blanc, Roca del Senallo, molí Darrer- Abric I and mas d'en Badenes. The highest point is about 1,250 m.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Cingle de l'Ermità -Abric IV

    Cingle means a band of cliff in Valencian, and the Cingle de l'Ermità is exactly that: a rim of pale limestone above a ravine in the dry highlands around Albocàsser, with a hermitage somewhere in its name and its history. The shelter here, Abric IV, is one of a cluster along the cliff, painted in the Levantine style that UNESCO protects as the Rock Art of the Mediterranean Basin on the Iberian Peninsula. These were made by hunting peoples who used the overhangs as a canvas, and the paintings are small and mostly red.

  2. 2. Abric del.mas Blanc

    Benassal is a stone village on a high plateau in the Alt Maestrat, in the interior of Castellón, at around eight hundred metres above sea level, and it is known throughout the region for its spring water, the Font d'en Segures, which is bottled and sold across Spain. The Abric del Mas Blanc, the shelter of the white farmhouse, is one of the many rock-art sites in the area, part of the UNESCO listing of Mediterranean prehistoric art. The nearby Valltorta gorge, about twenty kilometres to the east, has some of the most famous Levantine paintings, including dramatic hunting scenes, and the Valltorta museum in Tírig is the best place to learn about them.

  3. 3. Roca del Senallo

    The Maestrat of inland Castellón is a highland of stone: stone villages on hilltops, stone walls running for kilometres across the slopes, stone farmhouses called masos scattered over the plateau. Roca del Senallo is a rock shelter near Culla, Vilafranca and Benassal, one of many painted shelters in this region, and it belongs to the World Heritage Site of Rock Art of the Mediterranean Basin. The Maestrat is one of the richest areas for Levantine art, with famous sites in the Valltorta gorge and around Ares and Albocàsser.

  4. 4. molí Darrer- Abric I

    The Alt Maestrat is the high, rugged interior of Castellón province, a country of limestone plateaus, deep ravines and stone villages perched on ridges, and it is crossed by a web of dry-stone walls that farmers have been building and rebuilding for centuries. Near the villages of Benassal, Catí and Villafranca del Cid, under a rock overhang beside an old mill site, the shelter called Molí Darrer holds prehistoric paintings, part of the scattered Levantine rock art that UNESCO inscribed as one site across eastern Spain.
