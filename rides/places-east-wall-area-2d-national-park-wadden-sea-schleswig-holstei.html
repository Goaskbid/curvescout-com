Why this ride

From Eckernförde it runs 48 km to Eckernförde; allow about 52 min of riding. On the way it passes Main Wall Areas 2 to 3 and back through Main Wall Areas 2 to 3. It keeps close to the shore for long stretches.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. East Wall Area 2D

    For anyone who likes to see how a frontier was really made, the East Wall of the Danevirke is a good place to stand. It is a long rampart running north-south across the base of the Jutland peninsula, a bank of earth and clay, with traces of timber and later brick, and in the section by Windeby you can see it as a low ridge across the fields. The Danes built and rebuilt it over several centuries, and it was one of the largest construction projects of the early medieval north.

  2. 2. Main Wall Areas 2 to 3

    Start with the ground, because the ground is the whole reason this wall exists. Between the Baltic inlet of the Schlei and the North Sea marshes, the Jutland peninsula pinches to a corridor of firm, sandy land, and for more than a thousand years every army, trader and pilgrim travelling between Scandinavia and the rest of Europe had to squeeze through it.
