Plan a motorcycle trip in New Zealand

Read the complete route and the relevant highway conditions together. A map can suggest a connection while an incident, work zone or local-road closure still changes what is possible.

Use the correct coverage

NZTA Journey Planner covers highway conditions and travel disruptions. Its own guidance directs local-road checks to local or regional councils.

Compare the day, not only the kilometres

Include stops and any planned waits in the ride-day budget. Recalculate the arrival plan when you change a departure or add a detour.

Save a departure recheck

Keep the roadbook and the authority link together. Revisit the saved trip before departure; a previous successful route calculation is not a current closure check.
