Why this ride

From Strel'na it runs 420 km to Strel'na; allow about 6 h 24 of riding. On the way it passes Primorskoe Highway, The Road of Life and back through Primorskoe Highway. The line favours views over speed.

The roads

Saint Petersburg Imperial Shore

Primorskoe Highway is 87 km from A.F.Orlov's Datcha, mostly on ЗСД. An easy profile, never above 32 m. Best in the early light, before the traffic.

The stops

  1. 1. A.F.Orlov's Datcha

    Start here.

    Strelna lies on the south shore of the Gulf of Finland, on the road to Peterhof, and it is where Peter the Great first thought of building his seaside palace before he settled on the site a few kilometres to the west.

    Read the complete A.F.Orlov's Datcha write-up.

  2. 2. Primorskoe Highway

    87 km in.

    Zelenogorsk used to be Terijoki, a Finnish resort town on the Gulf of Finland, and it belonged to Finland until the Winter War of 1939 to 1940, when this stretch of the Karelian Isthmus passed to the Soviet Union. Before that, Terijoki was a place where Russian aristocrats and artists kept summer houses, and the wooden villas that survive among the pines still have some of that air. The Primorskoe highway runs north-west from Saint Petersburg along the shore to Vyborg, and this is the part of it where the road threads a sandy, forested coastal strip with dunes on one side and wet pinewoods on the other.

    Read the complete Primorskoe Highway write-up.

  3. 3. The Road of Life

    218 km in.

    On the south-eastern shore of Lake Ladoga, east of Saint Petersburg, a road ends at the water. This is the start of the Road of Life, the supply route across the ice that kept Leningrad alive during the siege of 1941 to 1944. The siege lasted 872 days, from September 1941 to January 1944, and it cut the city off from nearly everything except the lake. In the first winter, when Ladoga froze, trucks and horse-drawn sledges began to cross the ice with food and fuel, and on the return journey they carried out women, children and the sick. Lorries broke through the ice, and bombing and artillery attacked the route, but it kept going.

    Read the complete The Road of Life write-up.
