Why this ride

142 km from Kronstadt to Kronstadt, about 2 h 25 of riding. On the way it passes Zelenogorsky, Terioki and back through Zelenogorsky. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The roads

Saint Petersburg Imperial Shore

The 10 km between Fort «Paul I» and Zelenogorsky, mostly on КАД. An easy profile, never above 9 m. Water alongside for much of it - pick one good pull-out and stop.

The stops

  1. 1. Fort «Paul I»

    Start here.

    To the Russian navy, Kronstadt is the fortress island of Kotlin in the eastern Gulf of Finland, and the forts scattered across the water around it were built to stop any enemy from approaching St Petersburg by sea.

    Read the complete Fort «Paul I» write-up.

  2. 2. Zelenogorsky

    9.8 km in. The ride passes about 6 km from it.

    The northern shore of the Gulf of Finland, west of Saint Petersburg, was for centuries the summer playground of the capital, and Zelenogorsk, which the Finns called Terijoki, was the best known of its resort towns.

    Read the complete Zelenogorsky write-up.

  3. 3. Terioki

    71 km in.

    Terioki, or Terijoki in Finnish, is the old name of Zelenogorsk, a resort town on the northern shore of the Gulf of Finland, about fifty kilometres from St Petersburg. Until the Winter War, it was part of Finland, and before the revolution it was one of the favourite dacha destinations for the wealthy of the imperial capital, who came by train to spend summers among the pine trees and sandy beaches. After the Soviet Union took the Karelian Isthmus in 1940 and again in 1944, the Finnish population left, and in 1948 the town was renamed Zelenogorsk, the green hill.

    Read the complete Terioki write-up.
