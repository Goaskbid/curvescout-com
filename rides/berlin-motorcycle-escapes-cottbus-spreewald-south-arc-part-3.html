Why this ride

A 275 km loop that starts and ends near Berlin, about 5 h of riding. The plan has 1 food stop marked.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Berlin

    The tower, finished in 1969 and 368 metres high, is the tallest structure in Germany and can be seen from nearly everywhere in the city. It was built by the East German government as a showpiece of socialist engineering, and when the sun hits its steel sphere, it throws a cross of light that Berliners cheekily called the Pope's revenge.

    Read the complete Berlin write-up.

  2. 2. Cottbus

    Cottbus is the capital of Lower Lusatia, a city on the Spree about 120 kilometres south-east of Berlin, and it is a better lunch stop than its reputation outside Brandenburg suggests. The street signs are bilingual, German and Lower Sorbian, a reminder that the Slavic Wends have lived in this region for well over a thousand years. The old market square is handsome after careful restoration, and the Art Nouveau state theatre ranks among the finest buildings of its kind in eastern Germany.

    Read the complete Cottbus write-up.
