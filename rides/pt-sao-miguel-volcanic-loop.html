  1. 1. Ponta Delgada

    Ponta Delgada is the main town of the Azores, on the south coast of São Miguel, a green volcanic island in the middle of the Atlantic. The town is laid out along the sea, with black basalt and white limestone pavements in patterns that make the streets look like a drawing, and three big arches, the Portas da Cidade, mark the old entrance from the harbour. Fishing boats, whale-watching boats and ferries come and go, and the island's wealth once came from oranges, then pineapples, which are still grown in glasshouses and are sold in the market with a sweetness you will not forget.
