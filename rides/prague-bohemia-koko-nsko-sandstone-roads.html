Why this ride

A proper escape from Prague with roads worth waking up for. 694 km round from Votice - plan on about 14 h 28 of riding. On the way it passes Scenic stretch near Jesenice, near Litvínov and Dvůr Králové nad Labem. near Čimelice is the high point. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Fuel stop near Votice

    The hills around Votice belong to the Central Bohemian uplands, a rumpled country of fields, fishponds and small forests between Prague and Tábor. Votice itself is a modest market town with a square, a church and a few thousand inhabitants, a quiet place where the main event of the day might be the arrival of the bus. It has a bakery, shops and fuel, which is all a rider really needs from it.

  4. 4. Photo stop near Litvínov

    Litvínov lies at the foot of the Ore Mountains, the Krušné hory, in north-western Bohemia, and it is a town of contrasts. Below it stretches the Most basin, scarred by decades of open-cast brown coal mining and dominated by the chimneys of a large chemical works. Above it, the mountains rise steeply in a wall of forest, and a few kilometres up you are in a different world of beech and spruce woods, high meadows and small villages near the German border.

  5. 5. Near Dvůr Králové nad Labem, on the way back

    In 1817 a manuscript was found in the church tower of Dvůr Králové, a supposedly medieval collection of Czech epic poems that seemed to prove the antiquity of Czech literature. It became a national treasure, until scholars proved it a forgery decades later, sparking one of the great intellectual battles of the Czech national revival. The town, on the upper Elbe at the foot of the Krkonoše mountains, has never quite lived it down.

  8. 8. Back to Kokořínsko

    This corner of Central Bohemia is farmland, ponds and gentle hills, the kind of country that Prague residents drive through without noticing and riders learn to love. The point lies between Votice, Sedlčany and the village of Kosova Hora, roughly halfway between the capital and the Šumava forests to the south. The sandstone towers of Kokořínsko are far to the north, so think of this as the quiet middle of the country, the part that gets less attention.
