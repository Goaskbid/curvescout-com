Why this ride

A proper escape from Paris with roads worth waking up for. Out of Villiers-le-Bel and back again: 245 km, roughly 5 h 07 in the saddle. The stops in between: Scenic stretch in Mitry-Mory, in Mennecy and Rungis. The climb of the day is near Ozoir-la-Ferrière. The line favours views over speed.

Scenic stretch in Mitry-Mory to High point near Ozoir-la-Ferrière

This Scenic stretch in Mitry-Mory → High point near Ozoir-la-Ferrière stretch is for rhythm and scenery. Keep your head up, let the view breathe, and resist turning every open bend into a target. Stop once properly rather than half-stopping everywhere.

Photo stop in Mennecy to Rungis

Photo stop in Mennecy → Rungis is the technical section. Hairpins, gradients and mixed traffic can stack up quickly; use low drama inputs, stay wide of centre lines and expect buses or riders appearing exactly where optimism says they will not.

Food stop near Les Essarts-le-Roi to Vigny, on the way back

Food stop near Les Essarts-le-Roi → Vigny, on the way back can flow quickly, but only if you keep village limits and junction sightlines sacred. Fast connectors are where rides get untidy when fatigue creeps in.
