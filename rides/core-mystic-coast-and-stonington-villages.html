Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Mystic

    Mystic is a name that travels far beyond its size, and it earns it with ships and pizza in roughly equal measure. The town sits where the Mystic River broadens into a tidal estuary just before Fishers Island Sound, and for much of the nineteenth century its shipyards turned out fast clippers and whalers. That history is preserved at Mystic Seaport, an open-air museum of a whole waterfront village, with tall ships, old workshops and the Charles W. Morgan, a whaler launched in 1841 that is the last wooden whaling ship in the world.
