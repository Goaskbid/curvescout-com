Why this ride

302 km from Nashville to Nashville, about 3 h 41 of riding. It goes by way of Mammoth Cave National Park and back through Mammoth Cave National Park. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

The stops

  1. 1. Nashville

    Start here.

    Nashville is where the Natchez Trace begins in spirit, although the parkway itself starts on the southwestern side of the city, near the town of Pasquo, a short ride from downtown. The city sits on the Cumberland River, in a bowl of rolling Tennessee hills, and has been a centre of music since the Grand Ole Opry began broadcasting from here in the 1920s. Broadway, the strip of honky-tonks in the middle of town, plays live music from morning till late, and the Ryman Auditorium, a former church with pews that still serve as seating, is one of the most respected small concert halls in the country.

    Read the complete Nashville write-up.

  2. 2. Mammoth Cave National Park

    152 km in.

    Under the rolling hills of south-central Kentucky lies the longest known cave system in the world, with more than four hundred miles of mapped passages and surveyors still finding more. Mammoth Cave was carved by the Green River and its tributaries over millions of years, working through a thick layer of limestone capped by a protective layer of sandstone. At the surface it is a karst landscape of sinkholes, springs and disappearing streams, with oak and hickory forest covering the ridges and a quiet green river winding below.
