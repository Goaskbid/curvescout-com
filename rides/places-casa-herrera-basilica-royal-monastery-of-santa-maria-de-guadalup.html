Why this ride

429 km from Mérida to Mérida, about 6 h 28 of riding. It goes by way of Old Town of Cáceres, Royal Monastery of Santa María de Guadalup and back through Old Town of Cáceres. The line favours views over speed.

The stops

  1. 1. Casa Herrera Basilica

    Start here.

    Just north of Mérida, among cereal fields and holm oak, lie the quiet remains of the Casa Herrera basilica, an early Christian and Visigothic church that most visitors to the city never see. It is a modest site, low walls and foundations rather than soaring arches, and that is part of its charm. The surrounding countryside is typical of Extremadura: wide, open, lightly farmed, with big skies, storks nesting on pylons and the smell of dry grass and sheep.

    Read the complete Casa Herrera Basilica write-up.

  2. 2. Old Town of Cáceres

    77 km in.

    Cáceres has an old town so complete that film crews treat it as a ready-made set, and walking its streets at dusk, when the stone glows amber and the crowds have gone, you can see why. Inside the walls, built largely by the Almohads in the twelfth century, are dozens of fortified mansions, towers, churches and palaces, many built by noble families who made their fortunes in the conquest of the Americas. The towers once had battlements, but Queen Isabella ordered most of them cut down to curb the nobles' power. UNESCO listed the old town in 1986.
