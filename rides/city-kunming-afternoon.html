Why this ride

A 132 km round trip from Kunming - roughly 2 h 17 in the saddle. On the way it passes Chengjiang Fossil Site and back through Chengjiang Fossil Site. The highest point is about 2,150 m.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Kunming

    Kunming is called the Spring City, and the title is earned. Set at about nineteen hundred metres on the Yunnan plateau, it has mild weather almost all year, with warm days, cool nights and a long season of flowers, so that you can ride in a light jacket in January. Dian Lake, the big freshwater lake on the southern edge, spreads out under hills hazy with distance, and gulls from Siberia arrive in winter to feed on bread thrown by the crowds on the lakeside. The city itself, with more than three and a half million people, is a lively and rather modern place of tower blocks, tree-lined avenues and parks.

  2. 2. Chengjiang Fossil Site

    About 518 million years ago, in a shallow sea where Yunnan now stands on a high plateau, a sudden mud slide buried a community of soft-bodied animals so quickly that their outlines survived as fossils. Those fossils, found in the Maotianshan Shale near Chengjiang, are among the most important ever found, because they capture the Cambrian explosion, the rapid appearance of most major animal groups. They include early arthropods, worms with spiny legs and some of the oldest known relatives of vertebrates, preserved with details like eyes, guts and gills. The site is a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and a small museum and viewing area explain what scientists have found.
