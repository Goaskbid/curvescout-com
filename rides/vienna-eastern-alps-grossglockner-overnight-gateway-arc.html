Why this ride

Out of Alland, one way: 292 km, roughly 5 h 59 in the saddle. On the way it passes Purkersdorf, Scenic stretch near Ernstbrunn and in Asparn an der Zaya. near Kaumberg is the high point.

  3. 3. Purkersdorf

    Purkersdorf sits at the eastern edge of the Wienerwald, where the Vienna Woods roll down into the city, and it is the last stop before the motorway delivers you back to Vienna. The town follows the narrow valley of the Wien river, and the hills around are covered with beech forest, meadows and small villages, a landscape of gentle curves and green shade.

  5. 5. Photo stop in Asparn an der Zaya

    Asparn an der Zaya is the kind of Weinviertel village you could ride through in forty seconds and never suspect it holds one of Austria's best windows onto the deep past. The old castle in the middle of the village houses MAMUZ, the museum of prehistory for Lower Austria, and behind it an open-air area of reconstructed houses from the Stone Age to the Iron Age. If you have ever wondered who lived on these loess hills before the vineyards, this is where to find out.

  6. 6. Near Pama, on the way back

    Wind is the first thing you notice here, and the last. This stop lies on the Parndorf plateau in the far north-east of Burgenland, close to the point where Austria, Slovakia and Hungary meet, and the land is flat, open and swept by a near-constant wind that turns hundreds of turbines on the horizon. On a bike it pushes you around, especially on the open roads between villages, so expect to ride with a lean even on the straights.
