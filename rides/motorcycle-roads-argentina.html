Choose the specific road corridor and identify each authority involved. A national-road reference alone cannot confirm a provincial connection or an international crossing.

Use the national-road reference precisely

Vialidad Nacional publishes road information and links to route-condition resources. Match the road number and section to your plan.

Keep borders and local roads separate

A roadbook does not establish immigration, customs or cross-border opening requirements. Verify those with their responsible authorities before relying on a connected route line.

Compare realistic time budgets

Count the complete day, including stops and uncertainty, rather than adopting a longer itinerary because its distance fits a headline target.
