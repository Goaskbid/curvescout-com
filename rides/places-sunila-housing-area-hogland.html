Why this ride

A 119 km round trip from Karhula - roughly 2 h 10 in the saddle. On the way it passes HOGLAND and Sunila Housing Area. It crosses from Finland into Russia - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Sunila Housing Area

    Alvar Aalto designed this place before the Second World War, and you can still read the plan on the ground. Sunila is a pulp mill on a rocky island near Kotka, on the Gulf of Finland, and Aalto designed both the mill buildings and the housing for the workers, in the late 1930s. The houses step down the slope in terraces among the pines, with an eye for views and shared space, which was unusual for an industrial settlement then. The mill still runs, with smoke rising over the pines on cold mornings, so the place is a working one, not a museum, and the housing area is lived in by residents who deserve some quiet.

  2. 2. HOGLAND

    Hogland, or Gogland to the Russians who now own it, is a lump of granite in the middle of the Gulf of Finland, a long boat ride from the Finnish coast near Kotka. There is no road to ride to it, so on a motorcycle route this point is a marker for the Gulf of Finland and the island itself rather than somewhere you will actually park.
