Why this ride

The roads

Chile Lake District Route

The 16 km between Ensenada Chile and Petrohue, mostly on Ruta 225 CH. Hardly any climbing; it runs between 61 and 223 m. Best in the early light, before the traffic.

The stops

  1. 1. Puerto Varas

    Start here.

    Puerto Varas looks across Lake Llanquihue at a volcano that appears to have been drawn with a ruler. Osorno, at about 2,650 metres, is a near-perfect snow-capped cone, and from the lakeshore at sunset, when the cone turns pink and the water goes flat and glassy, it is one of the great views of southern Chile. Beside it, the more jagged Calbuco, which erupted dramatically in April 2015 and sent a column of ash high into the sky, looks darker and more menacing.

    Read the complete Puerto Varas write-up.

  2. 2. Ensenada Chile

    45 km in.

    Ensenada is a small village on the southeastern shore of Lake Llanquihue, in the Chilean Lake District, and it stands in the shadow of one of the most beautiful volcanoes in the Andes. Osorno, a near-perfect cone of snow rising to about 2,650 metres, fills the sky behind the village on clear days, while the jagged Calbuco, which erupted dramatically in 2015, stands to the south. The mix of blue lake, black volcanic sand, dark forest and white cone gives the place a unique, almost theatrical setting, and many travellers say it is the best view in southern Chile.

    Read the complete Ensenada Chile write-up.

  3. 3. Petrohue

    The end: 61 km.

    Petrohue sits at the western end of Lake Todos los Santos, a deep green lake in the shadow of Osorno volcano, and the small settlement is the end of the road for anyone driving up from Puerto Varas. Osorno, a near-perfect snow-capped cone of about 2,650 metres, dominates the horizon, and on a clear morning it is reflected in the lake's still water. La Ensenada, a hamlet on the shore of Lake Llanquihue, is the last real settlement before the final stretch.

    Read the complete Petrohue write-up.
