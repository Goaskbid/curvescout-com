Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Bouillon

    Bouillon is squeezed into a tight loop of the river Semois, with a castle that seems to grow out of the rock above the town. The crags force the river into a hairpin, and the town huddles on the neck of land below. It is a small place, barely five thousand people, yet it is one of the most memorable in the Belgian Ardennes, and the bend of the Semois here is the kind of scene that makes people take out their phones without being asked.
