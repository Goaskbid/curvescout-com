Why this ride

From Varna it runs 397 km to Varna; allow about 6 h 51 of riding. It goes by way of Ancient City of Nessebar, Madara Rider, back through Madara Rider and back through Ancient City of Nessebar. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Varna

    Varna is Bulgaria's summer capital, a big, confident Black Sea city of three hundred thousand with a long beach, a grand seaside garden and a surprisingly deep past. In 1972, workers digging near the shore found a cemetery that has since become famous, the Varna Necropolis, which contained some of the oldest worked gold ever found, made around 4500 BC, long before the pharaohs. The treasures, which include tiny gold ornaments buried with the dead, are in the city's archaeological museum, and they show that a sophisticated society lived here thousands of years before Rome or Athens.

  2. 2. Ancient City of Nessebar

    Nessebar sits on a small rocky peninsula that sticks out into the Black Sea, joined to the mainland by a narrow causeway, and the whole of the old town is an open-air museum. Greek colonists from Megara founded it in the sixth century BC, calling it Mesembria, and it passed through Thracian, Roman, Byzantine, Bulgarian and Ottoman hands. Its position, with a good harbour and a defensible rock, made it a valued port for two and a half thousand years. UNESCO listed the old town in 1983.

  4. 4. Madara Rider

    Above the village of Madara, on a vertical cliff about a hundred metres up, a horseman is carved into the rock, thrusting a spear at a lion that lies under his horse, while a dog runs behind. The Madara Rider is the only relief of its kind in Europe, made in the early eighth century by the first Bulgarian state, probably to celebrate a victory by Khan Tervel, and it is a rare relic of the pre-Christian era of the country. UNESCO listed it in 1979. Beside it are Greek inscriptions recording the deeds of Bulgarian khans, among the oldest written documents in the country.
