The stops

  1. 1. Sao Roque de Minas

    Start here.

    Sao Roque de Minas is a small town in the highlands of Minas Gerais, at the gateway to the Serra da Canastra National Park, a high plateau of grassland, rocky ridges and waterfalls that is the source of the Sao Francisco River, one of the longest rivers in Brazil. The town is quiet, with simple houses, a church and a few pousadas, and there is no larger settlement for some distance, so fuel and supplies are worth sorting out before the ride.

    Read the complete Sao Roque de Minas write-up.

  2. 2. Capitólio

    86 km in.

    Capitólio owes its fame to a lake that was built by accident, in a sense. In the 1960s the Furnas dam flooded the valley of the Rio Grande in south-west Minas Gerais, and the water rose up between the old canyons, leaving a huge reservoir with sheer rock walls, small islands and hidden coves. Today it is Brazil's inland playground, and the small town of Capitólio on its shore is the base for boat trips that run along the canyon, past waterfalls pouring straight from the cliffs into green water.
