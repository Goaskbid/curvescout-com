Why this ride

A 85 km loop that starts and ends near Como, about 1 h 22 of riding. The stops in between: Bellagio, Varenna, Colico and Menaggio. The highest point is about 1,650 m. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Como

    Lake Como is shaped like an upside-down Y, with the city of Como at the end of one leg, and it is the deepest lake in Italy, plunging to over four hundred metres. The Alps rise straight from the water, with villas and gardens on the shores, and ferries zigzagging between the villages. Como itself is a small and elegant city, with a Duomo that took centuries to build and mixes Gothic and Renaissance styles, a walled old town and a lakefront with a funicular climbing to the hill village of Brunate. It was the birthplace of Alessandro Volta, the inventor of the battery, and the unit of electrical potential carries his name.

  2. 2. Bellagio

    Bellagio sits at the end of a long peninsula where Lake Como splits into two arms, in a spot so well placed that the Romans and then the Milanese noble families all wanted a villa here. It is a town of steep stone lanes, stepped alleys lined with shops and cafes, and houses in warm yellow and ochre with green shutters. The waterfront is a line of arcades and terraces, with boats coming and going and a view across the water to the mountains. It is often called the pearl of the lake, and for once the cliche is not too far off.

  3. 3. Varenna

    Varenna is a small, tidy village on the eastern shore of Lake Como that looks like a set designer's idea of an Italian lake town. Houses in orange, yellow and rose crowd down to a little harbour, a lakeside path runs along the water, and steep stepped lanes climb between them with flowers on every windowsill. Above the village, the ruins of Castello di Vezio look down from a hill, and the best views across the lake to Bellagio are from its walls.

  4. 4. Colico

    Colico lies at the very top of Lake Como, on the flat plain where the Adda river arrives from the Valtellina, and it is a place that works for a living rather than for the camera. The mountains here are higher and closer than anywhere else on the lake, with Monte Legnone rising to almost 2,600 metres directly above the town, and the lake itself narrows and turns north into a wider, windier stretch. The wind is the local gift. In summer, a steady breeze blows up the lake from the south, the Breva, and the northern end is one of the best places in Italy for windsurfers and kitesurfers, who fill the beaches on warm afternoons.

  5. 5. Menaggio

    Menaggio is the western door of the lake, a handsome small town with a ferry quay, a lakefront of cafes and a population that doubles with visitors in summer. It sits at the foot of the mountains on the western shore of Lake Como, opposite Bellagio and Varenna, and its position is the reason for its life: the road from Switzerland through Porlezza and Lake Lugano reaches the water here, and so does the old route along the shore from Como to Colico. Behind the town, the steep slopes rise to a ridge where the mountain paths of the Via dei Monti Lariani run along the top, giving views of the lake from above.
