Why this ride

From Ares del Maestre it runs 248 km to Ares del Maestre; allow about 4 h 17 of riding. The stops in between: Valmayor V, Abrigo del Plano del Pulido and back through Valmayor V. The highest point is about 1,100 m. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Cova Remigia- Abric V

    Imagine a visit to a museum where the curator has not turned on the lights and the exhibits are painted on the walls of a cliff. That is Cova Remigia in the Gasulla ravine, where the shelter is a wide hollow in the limestone, a rock-roof high enough to stand under and deep enough to keep out most of the rain. Five kilometres from Benassal, on a plateau that is cold in winter and bright in summer, it lies on one of the great hunting grounds of the prehistoric past, and the paintings are among the clearest in Spain.

  2. 2. Valmayor V

    The Matarraña, in the far east of Teruel province, is often called the Tuscany of Aragon, and for once the comparison holds. Stone villages crown hilltops above olive groves and vineyards, rivers run clear through limestone gorges, and the light in the evening is honey-coloured. Valmayor is a ravine in the hills here, between Valderrobres, Fuentespalda and the Catalan border, and its shelters hold prehistoric paintings, part of the UNESCO-listed Rock Art of the Mediterranean Basin. As elsewhere, they are protected, and visits are best arranged through local tourist offices.

  3. 3. Abrigo del Plano del Pulido

    South of Caspe, the lower Aragón country is a dry, sculpted landscape of ochre hills, flat-topped mesas and gullies cut into soft sandstone, with almond and olive groves clinging to the valley floors. In these sandstone outcrops, prehistoric people found shelter and left paintings, and the Abrigo del Plano del Pulido is one of the rock shelters of the area included in the World Heritage site of the rock art of the Mediterranean Basin on the Iberian Peninsula. The painted figures, in the reddish tones of Levantine art, are small and faded, and as at most of these sites, access is controlled and visits may need to be arranged in advance.
