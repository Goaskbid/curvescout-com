Why this ride

From Tanta it runs 402 km to Tanta; allow about 9 h 28 of riding. The stops in between: Segment Casila Alto - Chillaco, Segment Quebrada Verde - Pachacamac and back through Segment Casila Alto - Chillaco. It tops out around 4,700 m. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

The roads

Segment Masho - Piticocha → Segment Casila Alto - Chillaco

Segment Masho - Piticocha to Segment Casila Alto - Chillaco: 134 km, mostly on LM-117. It climbs 900 m to the top at 4,585 m and drops 3820 m again. Above 2,000 m the weather turns fast; have a layer and a plan B.

The stops

  1. 1. Segment Masho - Piticocha

    Start here.

    High above Lima, where the desert coast gives way to the bare, windswept grasslands of the Andean puna, an old stone path crosses the mountains of Huarochirí. This is a segment of the Qhapaq Ñan, the great Inca road network that UNESCO listed in 2014, and it is one of the many trails that linked the Pacific coast with the central highlands. The region has a particular literary fame: a Quechua manuscript from around 1600, known as the Huarochirí Manuscript, records the myths and rituals of the local communities, with stories of mountain gods, floods and the great shrine of Pachacamac on the coast. It is among the few surviving texts that tell the pre-Hispanic stories in their own words.

  2. 2. Segment Casila Alto - Chillaco

    134 km in.

    In the mountains east of Lima, high above the desert coast, a stone path clings to the slopes of the province of Huarochirí. It is a section of the Qhapaq Ñan, the great Inca road system, which in this region linked the sacred coastal sanctuary of Pachacamac with Jauja and the central highlands. The segment between Casila Alto and Chillaco is one of the stretches listed by UNESCO in 2014 as part of the transnational Andean Road System. The Inca engineers built it with retaining walls, steps and drainage channels to cope with the steep terrain and seasonal rains, and parts are still used by local farmers and their animals.

    Read the complete Segment Casila Alto - Chillaco write-up.

  3. 3. Segment Quebrada Verde - Pachacamac

    203 km in.

    Lima is one of the driest capitals on earth, and yet just south of it, in the hills above the Lurín valley, the desert turns green for a few months every year. The Lomas de Quebrada Verde are fog oases, where the winter mist called garúa, rolling in from the cold Pacific between roughly June and October, condenses on the hillsides and brings to life a brief explosion of grass, flowers, cacti and shrubs, attracting birds, butterflies and the people of Lima, who come here to walk. The segment between Quebrada Verde and Pachacamac is part of the Qhapaq Ñan, the Inca road system, which linked the coast with the highlands.
