  1. 1. Chino

    Chino is a town of fifty-six thousand people on the eastern side of Lake Suwa's basin, in Nagano Prefecture, and it has two secrets. One is that the town gave Japan one of its most famous prehistoric treasures: the Jomon Venus, a small, rounded clay figure found at the Tanabatake site and designated a national treasure, which is perhaps five thousand years old. The other is agar, or kanten, a seaweed jelly made in the cold dry winters here, with long frames of it set out to freeze and dry in the open air. Neither is on the usual tourist list, which is part of the appeal.
