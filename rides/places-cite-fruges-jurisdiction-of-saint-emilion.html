Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Cité Frugès

    Pessac is better known for wine than for modern architecture, so it is a surprise to find a small colony of Le Corbusier houses in its southern suburbs. In 1924, the industrialist Henry Frugès, a sugar and timber magnate with a social conscience, commissioned the young architect to build cheap, comfortable homes for his workers, and Le Corbusier designed rows of cubic houses with flat roofs, long ribbon windows and bright colours, inside and out. It was one of his first large housing experiments, an attempt to apply factory methods to building, and it took a few years to finish. The result was a village of around fifty houses.

  2. 2. La citadelle et le fort Paté et Médoc de B

    The Gironde estuary is wide enough here to feel like an inland sea, and Vauban saw it as the gateway to Bordeaux that needed a lock. His answer, at the end of the seventeenth century, was a triangle of fortifications: the citadel at Blaye on the right bank, Fort Médoc on the left bank at Cussac, and between them Fort Paté, a squat stone tower built on an island in the middle of the river. Together they could cross their fire over the shipping channel, and today they share a place on the UNESCO list of Vauban's works. The citadel of Blaye is the most impressive of the three, a fortified town in its own right, with ramparts, gates and old barracks set high on a bluff above the water.

  3. 3. Jurisdiction of Saint-Emilion

    Saint-Émilion is a wine village that looks like a stage set, with steep, cobbled lanes tumbling down a limestone hillside, golden stone houses, and a bell tower rising above the rooftops. The UNESCO listing covers not just the village but its whole jurisdiction, eight parishes of vineyards around it, recognising a landscape in which vines have been cultivated since Roman times. The village grew around the hermitage of Émilion, a Breton monk who lived in a cave here in the eighth century, and its most remarkable monument is the monolithic church, carved out of the rock beneath the bell tower.
