Why this ride

Out of South Lake Tahoe, one way: 80 km, roughly 1 h 23 in the saddle. On the way it passes Cave Rock and Sand Harbor. The highest point is about 2,250 m. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

Lake Tahoe Loop

Water arrives beside the road and stays for 27 km. The line follows the shore because there is nowhere else for it to go — slow enough to look. The surface follows the shoreline, so the corners arrive where the water decided, not where an engineer did.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. South Lake Tahoe

    Lake Tahoe sits at 6,225 feet, which makes it one of the highest large lakes in the country, and the water is so clear that on a calm day you can see objects more than sixty feet down. It is also very deep, about 1,645 feet at its deepest, making it the second deepest lake in the United States after Crater Lake. The basin formed as the Sierra Nevada rose along fault lines and glaciers later carved the valleys, and the Washoe people spent their summers on its shores for thousands of years, long before Europeans arrived. Their name for the lake, Da ow a ga, means edge of the lake.

  2. 2. Cave Rock

    Cave Rock is a lump of volcanic rock on the Nevada shore of Lake Tahoe, and US 50 goes straight through it. Two tunnels bore through the stone, the older from 1931 and a second added in the 1950s, and when you ride through you are literally passing through a mountain that has been sacred to the Washoe people for thousands of years. The Washoe consider it a place of power and healing, and rock climbing was banned there after a long legal fight. Visitors are asked to treat the site with respect, and the park around the base has a boat ramp and picnic area.

  3. 3. Sand Harbor

    Sand Harbor is where Lake Tahoe shows off. A cluster of smooth granite boulders sits in water so clear that it looks like glass, with pale sand coves between them, and the colour of the lake runs from bright aqua in the shallows to deep blue just beyond. The setting is a Nevada state park on the east shore, and in high summer it is among the busiest beaches on the lake, with the car park often full by mid-morning. Come early or late, and it is very different.

  4. 4. Incline Village

    Incline Village sits on the north-east shore of Lake Tahoe, on the Nevada side, a community of about 8,800 people with some of the most expensive lake-view houses in the country, though from the road the mood is quiet pine woods and low buildings. It is the sort of place where a casual cafe has a view of blue water, and where the boulders on the nearby beaches are so large and smooth that they seem to have been placed by a sculptor.
