Why this ride

351 km from Montealegre del Castillo to Montealegre del Castillo, about 6 h 36 of riding. The stops in between: Abrigo del Canto Blanco, Abrigo de las Enredaderas, Abrigo de los Cortijos and El Laberinto. It tops out around 800 m. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

The stops

  1. 1. Abrigo del mediodía I

    Start here.

    North of Yecla, where the Murcia region meets Albacete, the land rises into a series of isolated limestone hills above a wide, dry plain of vineyards and almond groves. The rock shelters scattered through this country, including the Abrigo del Mediodía, are part of the UNESCO listing of Mediterranean Iberian rock art. The best known cluster is on Monte Arabí, a striking, isolated rock mountain north of the town, whose Cantos de la Visera shelters hold both Levantine and schematic paintings.

    Read the complete Abrigo del mediodía I write-up.

  2. 2. Abrigo del Canto Blanco

    39 km in.

    Jumilla is wine country, and you know it as soon as you arrive. The town sits in the north of the Murcia region, surrounded by a high, dry plateau of vineyards growing the Monastrell grape, which produces powerful, dark red wines. A castle tops the hill above the old town, and the bodegas on its outskirts welcome visitors. The Abrigo del Canto Blanco, the shelter of the white stone, lies in the hills about five kilometres away.

    Read the complete Abrigo del Canto Blanco write-up.

  3. 3. Abrigo de las Enredaderas

    83 km in.

    Where the Río Segura squeezes through the sandstone and limestone ridges of northern Murcia, between Calasparra and Cieza, the valley turns into a canyon of orange and grey rock with narrow green strips of reeds and orchard along the water. The Abrigo de las Enredaderas, a rock shelter, lies twelve kilometres or so from both towns, in dry hills of pine, rosemary and esparto grass that smell sharply of resin on a hot day.

    Read the complete Abrigo de las Enredaderas write-up.

  4. 4. Abrigo de los Cortijos

    133 km in.

    Hellín is a town in the south-east of Albacete province, on the edge of the dry plains of La Mancha, where the landscape breaks into hills and ravines on its way down to the Segura river. Its old town climbs a hill crowned by a castle, and its streets are lined with houses from the seventeenth and eighteenth centuries. The Abrigo de los Cortijos lies in the hills about nine kilometres from the town, toward Albatana and Tobarra.

    Read the complete Abrigo de los Cortijos write-up.

  5. 5. El Laberinto

    177 km in.

    Calasparra is a rice town, which sounds odd until you see the terraces beside the Segura river, flooded and green in summer, with herons wading between the plants. The rice here has its own protected designation, and it is the base for paella in much of Spain. The river has carved a deep gorge, the Almadenes canyon, on its way through the hills, and in the rock above the water, hidden among limestone cliffs, there are shelters decorated with paintings made thousands of years ago. El Laberinto is one of them, part of the rock art tradition of the Mediterranean basin, listed by UNESCO.

    Read the complete El Laberinto write-up.
