Why this ride

From Schleswig it runs 264 km to Schleswig; allow about 4 h 48 of riding. On the way it passes National Park Wadden Sea Niedersachsen and North Wall Areas 1 to 2. The coast is the point of it.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. North Wall Areas 1 to 2

    The Danevirke is a wall that has been in the news for a thousand years. It stretches across the base of the Jutland peninsula, where the land narrows between the Baltic inlet of the Schlei and the marshes of the Treene and Rheide rivers, and it was built, extended and rebuilt from about the eighth century by Danish kings. It protected the southern border of the Danes against the Franks and later the Saxons, and its earthen banks, once topped by timber palisades, ran for some thirty kilometres. UNESCO listed it together with the Viking trading town of Hedeby in 2018.

  2. 2. National Park Wadden Sea Niedersachsen

    Here the Wadden Sea comes right up against the dyke. This is the coast of the Wurster Nordseeküste, between the Weser and Elbe estuaries, a country of low sea walls, drained farmland and wide skies, and the mudflats outside the dyke are part of the Lower Saxony Wadden Sea National Park. Together with the Dutch and Danish parts of the Wadden Sea, the area is a UNESCO World Heritage site, valued above all for the sheer scale of the tidal flats and the millions of birds that pass through on migration. When the tide goes out, it goes a very long way.
