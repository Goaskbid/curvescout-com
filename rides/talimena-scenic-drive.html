Why this ride

From Talihina it runs 178 km to Mena; allow about 2 h 24 of riding. On the way it passes Winding Stair Mountain and Queen Wilhelmina State Park. The highest point is about 800 m. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

Scenic road chapters

Talihina → Winding Stair Mountain

The ground tilts up and stays tilted for 79 km. 825 m of it, taken steadily rather than in one wall. A steady rhythm suits this better than bursts. The height arrives whether you hurry or not.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Talihina

    Talihina is a very small town in the Ouachita Mountains of southeastern Oklahoma, with just over a thousand people, and it is the western gate of one of the best ridge roads in the middle of the country. The name comes from the Choctaw language, and is often translated as iron road, a reference to the railroad that arrived here in the 1880s. This is Choctaw Nation land, and the tribe's presence is visible in the town signs, the language and the cultural centres. The Choctaw were forced here from Mississippi on the Trail of Tears in the 1830s, a history that gives the landscape a sombre depth.

  2. 2. Winding Stair Mountain

    Winding Stair Mountain is a long, forested ridge in southeastern Oklahoma, and the Talimena Scenic Drive follows its crest like a thread along a hem. The Ouachita Mountains are unusual in the United States because they run east to west, not north to south, folded into long parallel ridges with broad valleys between them, and from the top you can see row after row of blue ridges receding into Arkansas. The elevation is only around 800 metres, but in this flat part of the country it feels like a proper mountain range.

  3. 3. Queen Wilhelmina State Park

    A mountaintop state park named after a Dutch queen is the sort of fact that needs explaining, and the explanation is a railway. In the 1890s Dutch investors in the Kansas City Southern line, which ran through the nearby town of Mena, built a grand hotel on the crest of Rich Mountain and named it for their young queen, Wilhelmina. The original hotel did not last, but the name did, and the present lodge, rebuilt in the 1960s, sits on the same ridge with a view that spreads over Arkansas and, on a clear day, a long way beyond.

  4. 4. Mena

    Mena is a small Arkansas town at the foot of the Ouachita Mountains, near the Oklahoma border, with a little over five thousand people and an old railroad main street. The story goes that Dutch investors who backed the railway named the town after the wife of one of their number, and the Dutch connection has stuck, so that the state park on the mountain above is named for Queen Wilhelmina of the Netherlands.

Talihina to Winding Stair Mountain

Talihina → Winding Stair Mountain climbs about 825 m in 44 km. This is the part worth slowing down for - look through the bends and use the pull-offs.

Winding Stair Mountain → Queen Wilhelmina State Park drops about 825 m over 34 km. Brakes and eyes forward; the views are behind the corners, not in them.
