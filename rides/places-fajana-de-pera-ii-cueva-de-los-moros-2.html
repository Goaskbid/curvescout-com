Why this ride

315 km from Bárcabo to Bárcabo, about 5 h 26 of riding. It goes by way of Pyrénées - Mont Perdu, Cueva de los moros. and back through Pyrénées - Mont Perdu. The highest point is about 1,950 m. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Fajana de Pera II

    Barbastro is the gateway to this country, and most riders pass through it without a second look, which is a mistake. The town, with some seventeen thousand people, sits on the Vero where it leaves the hills, at the edge of the Somontano wine region, and it has a handsome old centre with arcaded streets, a Gothic cathedral begun in the sixteenth century and a long tradition of markets and trade. It is the practical centre of the district, with the fuel, supermarkets, banks and restaurants that the small villages of the valley lack.

  2. 2. Pyrénées - Mont Perdu

    Monte Perdido, the lost mountain, at 3,355 metres, is the highest limestone massif in Europe and the centrepiece of a cross-border World Heritage site shared by Spain and France. On the Spanish side, it gives its name to the Ordesa y Monte Perdido National Park, with deep canyons radiating out from the summit; on the French side, it looks down on the great cirques of Gavarnie and Troumouse.

  3. 3. Cueva de los moros.

    There is a Cueva de los Moros in half the valleys of Spain. Every unexplained cave, ruin or rock shelter used to be credited to the Moors, and this one, in the hills of the western Aragonese Pyrenees between the valleys of Hecho and Ansó, is a rock shelter with ancient traces. You should approach it as an excuse to spend time in two of the most beautiful and least spoiled valleys of the Pyrenees, rather than as a monument.
