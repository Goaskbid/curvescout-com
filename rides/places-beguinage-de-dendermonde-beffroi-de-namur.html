Why this ride

209 km round trip from Béguinage de Dendermonde, about 4 h 29 of riding. It runs out through Grand Béguinage of Leuven and Beffroi de Namur and comes back by another road.

The stops

  1. 1. Béguinage de Dendermonde

    Start here.

    Push open the gate of the Dendermonde béguinage and the traffic noise of the ring road simply drops away. Inside is a quiet triangle of grass, whitewashed houses with green shutters and a modest church, where for centuries communities of beguines lived. These were laywomen who chose a shared religious life without taking permanent vows, earning their keep with lacework, nursing and teaching. The béguinage is one of thirteen in Flanders listed together by UNESCO, and it is among the most intimate of them.

    Read the complete Béguinage de Dendermonde write-up.

  2. 2. Grand Béguinage of Leuven

    54 km in.

    Step through the gate of the Groot Begijnhof in Leuven and the city noise drops away. Cobbled lanes, small brick houses with white window frames and stepped gables, little bridges over the arms of the river Dijle, and a Gothic church in the middle: this is one of the largest béguinages in the Low Countries, part of the Flemish Béguinages group listed by UNESCO in 1998.

    Read the complete Grand Béguinage of Leuven write-up.

  3. 3. Beffroi de Namur

    110 km in.

    The belfry of Namur is a stout, round tower with a bulbous slate roof, not at all the slender Gothic spire people imagine when they hear the word. It started life in the fourteenth century as part of the town walls, the Tour Saint-Jacques, and later became the belfry, holding the bell that rang the hours and the curfew. It is one of the many towers in the World Heritage Site of the Belfries of Belgium and France.
