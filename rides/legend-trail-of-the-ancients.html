Why this ride

National Scenic Byway. archaeology, desert, mesa landscapes. A one-way ride of 207 km, Cortez to the finish - roughly 3 h 46 in the saddle. On the way it passes Trail of the Ancients and Blanding. The highest point is about 2,450 m. The line favours views over speed.

  4. 4. Blanding

    Blanding is a quiet town of under four thousand people in the far southeast of Utah, at around 1,800 metres, and it serves as a gateway to some of the least visited and most extraordinary country in the American Southwest. It was settled by Mormon pioneers in the early twentieth century, and it still feels like a ranching and farming town, with wide streets, neat houses and mesas on the horizon. For travellers, it is a base for exploring the canyons and ruins of Cedar Mesa and the Bears Ears region.
