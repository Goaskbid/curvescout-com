Why this ride

A 493 km one-way ride from near Kyneton, about 10 h 07 of riding. It goes by way of Melbourne / Victoria, near Alexandra and Altona. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Photo stop near Trentham

    Trentham is a small town on the Great Dividing Range, set high and cool among tall forest and potato paddocks between Daylesford and Woodend. Its main street has a handful of heritage buildings, a historic bakery and pub, and a quiet, unhurried charm that makes it a favourite stop for riders and weekenders alike.

  5. 5. Back to Mornington

    Mount Martha is a bayside town on the Mornington Peninsula, a leafy cluster of beaches, coastal scrub and big houses that rises on the slopes of a low hill between Mornington and Dromana. For a ride described as easy and scenic, it is a fitting place to end: the road runs close to the water, and the sea is rarely out of view.
