Why this ride

From Lomonosov it runs 246 km to Lomonosov; allow about 4 h of riding. On the way it passes Park «The Menagerie», Koltushskoe Highway and back through Park «The Menagerie». It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The roads

Saint Petersburg Imperial Shore

59 km from S.K.Grieg's Estate «Sans Ennui» to Park «The Menagerie», mostly on КАД. An easy profile, never above 117 m. Watch for sand and spray near the water.

The stops

  1. 1. S.K.Grieg's Estate «Sans Ennui»

    Start here.

    Samuel Greig was a Scottish naval officer from Inverkeithing who rose to become an admiral in Catherine the Great's navy, and in 1788 he fought the Battle of Hogland against the Swedes in the Gulf of Finland, a few weeks before dying of fever at Reval. He is the S.K. Greig of the label, Samuil Karlovich in Russian style, and an estate on this coast called Sans Ennui is associated with the family, French for without boredom, a charming name for a country house. The site lies near Bolshaya Izhora, west of Lomonosov, where the land is flat, wooded and close to the water, and the Izhora river flows toward the gulf.

    Read the complete S.K.Grieg's Estate «Sans Ennui» write-up.

  2. 2. Park «The Menagerie»

    59 km in.

    Gatchina is one of the great imperial estates ringing St Petersburg, and the Menagerie, the Zverinets, is its wild back garden: a large walled tract of forest north of the palace that was laid out in the eighteenth century as a hunting reserve, with deer and other game kept for the court. Today it is mostly woods, ponds and paths, and on a hot day it is the coolest place within reach.

    Read the complete Park «The Menagerie» write-up.

  3. 3. Koltushskoe Highway

    125 km in.

    East of Saint Petersburg, the Koltushskoe highway runs through suburban sprawl and countryside toward the village of Koltushi, and it belongs to a memorial landscape that ties the whole region together: the Green Belt of Glory, a chain of monuments marking the defensive line around Leningrad during the siege of 1941 to 1944, when nearly nine hundred days of blockade cost the lives of hundreds of thousands of civilians. Monuments, steles and memorials stand along the roads where the front lines and supply routes ran, including the Road of Life across Lake Ladoga.

    Read the complete Koltushskoe Highway write-up.
