Why this ride

29 km from Ormond Beach to Ormond Beach, about 28 min of riding. On the way it passes Tomoka State Park, Highbridge Road and A1A Ocean Shore. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Ormond Beach

    Ormond Beach is a small city with a big place in the history of going fast. Early in the twentieth century the hard-packed sand here, together with its neighbour Daytona to the south, was where the world's first speed records on wheels were chased, long before anyone thought of building a speedway. Henry Flagler's railway brought the winter crowds, and his grand hotel in town drew millionaires; John D. Rockefeller wintered a short walk from the shore in a house called The Casements. A motorcyclist rolling in today is following a very old tradition.

  2. 2. Tomoka State Park

    Tomoka State Park sits where the Tomoka and Halifax rivers meet on the Florida coast just north of Daytona, and it is a surprise after the busy beach towns: a quiet, shaded patch of old Florida where live oaks arch over the road, hung with Spanish moss, and the tidal creeks run slow and tea-coloured through salt marsh. The air smells of brackish water and warm pine needles, and the only traffic is often a car or two and a cyclist.

  3. 3. Highbridge Road

    Highbridge Road is a narrow ribbon of tarmac that runs across the low, wet country behind the Florida coast, and it is one of the pleasures of the Ormond Scenic Loop. The land is flat, the sky is huge, and on either side of the road the marsh stretches away in green and gold, with tidal creeks winding through the cordgrass and ibises and egrets picking at the mud. Where the road lifts onto a bridge, a gentle rise that counts as a hill in this part of Florida, you get a wide view across the water to the trees on the far bank.

  4. 4. A1A Ocean Shore

    A1A is the road that follows Florida's Atlantic edge, and between Ormond Beach and Flagler Beach it does so with unusual honesty: there is the ocean on one side and the dunes and low houses on the other, and almost nothing in between. The road runs a few metres from the sand, with sea oats waving along the dunes, pelicans skimming the waves and the smell of salt and sunscreen in the air. The sea is a warm green-grey, the horizon is perfectly flat, and the light, especially in the early morning, is luminous.
