  4. 4. Foothills Parkway point 4

    Most people never see this end of the Foothills Parkway, which is exactly why you should. It lies on the north-eastern edge of the Great Smoky Mountains near Cosby, a sleepy community that has a reputation as an old moonshine country and still feels somewhat apart from the tourist bustle on the Tennessee side. The short eastern section of the parkway runs along the ridge above the valley, with long views across farmland to the high peaks.
