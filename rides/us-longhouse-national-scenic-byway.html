  1. 1. Warren

    Warren sits where the Conewango Creek meets the Allegheny River, in the far northwest of Pennsylvania, and the Allegheny National Forest rolls away on every side. It is a small old city of about nine thousand, a county seat with a courthouse, a downtown of brick and stone buildings built on oil and lumber money, and a long river park. The place was named for General Joseph Warren, a hero of the Revolution who died at Bunker Hill.
