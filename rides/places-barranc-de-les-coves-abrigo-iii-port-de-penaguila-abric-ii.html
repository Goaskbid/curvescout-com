Why this ride

From Rugat it runs 68 km to Rugat; allow about 1 h 10 of riding. On the way it passes Abric de la Paella, Port de Penaguila- Abric II and back through Abric de la Paella. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Barranc de les Coves- Abrigo III

    The Benicadell range is the thing that dominates every view here, a pale limestone ridge that rises above 1,100 metres between the Vall d'Albaida and the Comtat. The shelter marked here lies at the base of it, among villages that most Valencians have heard of but few have visited: Ráfol de Salem, Castelló de Rugat and Beniarrés, all within four kilometres of one another. This is quiet country of orchards, olive terraces and little hamlets with a church and a bar.

  2. 2. Abric de la Paella

    Muro de Alcoy and Cocentaina sit side by side in the valley of the Serpis river, in the Comtat, a district of northern Alicante surrounded by mountains. Cocentaina is crowned by a castle with a tall tower, and below it lies an old town with a palace of the Counts of Cocentaina and a Franciscan convent. The Abric de la Paella, named after the paella pan perhaps because of its shape, lies in the hills between the two towns.

  3. 3. Port de Penaguila- Abric II

    The Port de Penàguila is a mountain pass in the interior of Alicante province, on the road between Alcoy and the Vall de Guadalest, where the hills of the Comtat rise toward the Serra d'Aitana, the highest range in the province. The village of Penàguila, below the pass, is a small, ancient place huddled beneath a castle on a rocky crest, and Benilloba, Cocentaina and Alcoy are all close. The second shelter of the Port de Penàguila is one of several painted sites in the area.
