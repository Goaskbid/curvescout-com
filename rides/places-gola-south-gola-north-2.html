Why this ride

290 km from Gorahun to Gorahun, about 5 h of riding. It goes by way of Gola-Tiwai Complex, Gola Central, Gola North and back through Gola Central. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Gola South

    At the eastern edge of Sierra Leone, on the border with Liberia, there is one of the last big tracts of rainforest in West Africa. The Gola forest, which is split into several blocks, is part of the Upper Guinean forest, a hotspot of biodiversity, and its trees tower up to fifty metres, forming a dense canopy that blocks out the sun. It is home to the pygmy hippopotamus, a shy, forest-dwelling relative of the river hippo, the western chimpanzee, the Diana monkey, the red colobus, the forest elephant and the white-breasted guineafowl, and it has an astonishing array of birds, butterflies and frogs. Gola Rainforest National Park was created in 2011 to protect it.

  2. 2. Gola-Tiwai Complex

    Sierra Leone's south-east holds some of the last big pieces of the Upper Guinean rainforest, and the Gola-Tiwai area is where the forest feels most generous. The Gola Rainforest National Park covers a broad sweep of hills and valleys, while Tiwai Island, a small wooded island in the Moa river, is a sanctuary where you can see more primates per hectare than almost anywhere in West Africa. Diana monkeys, red colobus and several other species live here, and the island is home to a population of pygmy hippos, shy animals that are rarely seen by day.

  3. 3. Gola Central

    Gola is the last big block of the Upper Guinean rainforest in Sierra Leone, and the central part of it lies close to the border with Liberia in the south-east of the country. Sierra Leone made it a national park in 2011, after decades of logging and the shock of a civil war that ran from 1991 to 2002, and it is run with the help of local communities, who receive a share of the benefits and who have a role in protecting the trees. The forest is split into three main blocks, North, Central and South, and the centre is the heart of the system.

  4. 4. Gola North

    Forest this tall and this old is rare in West Africa now, and the Gola rainforest in eastern Sierra Leone is one of the largest pieces left of the Upper Guinean belt that once ran from Guinea to Ghana. Gola North is the northern block of the national park, which was formally established in 2011 and runs along the border with Liberia, where the forest continues into a matching protected area on the other side. It is a place where conservation has been built on top of a very hard recent history, because the civil war of 1991 to 2002 passed through this region and left deep scars.
