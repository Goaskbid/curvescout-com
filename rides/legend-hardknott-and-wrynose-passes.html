  2. 2. Hardknott and Wrynose Passes point 2

    Hardknott Pass tops out at about 393 metres, and the Romans got here first. Just below the summit on the Eskdale side stand the ruins of Mediobogdum, a fort built in the second century to guard the road from Ravenglass to Ambleside, and its stone walls, gateways and parade ground are laid out on a shelf with a view down the valley to the sea. Soldiers were posted here from as far away as Dalmatia, which makes the place feel even more remote. It is free to visit, and the walk from the road is short.
