Why this ride

318 km from Tashkent to Tashkent, about 5 h 29 of riding. It goes by way of The Chatkal State Biosphere Nature Reserve, Besh-Aral State Nature Reserve - main part and back through The Chatkal State Biosphere Nature Reserve. The highest point is about 2,700 m. It crosses from Uzbekistan into Kyrgyzstan - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Zhemchug Residential Building

    Zhemchug means pearl in Russian, and it is the name given to a residential building in Tashkent that looks nothing like an ordinary apartment block. In the years after the 1966 earthquake, when the city was rebuilt, Soviet architects used housing as a stage for their ideas, and some of the results are strange and rather wonderful: curved facades, great circular forms, concrete screens that echo Islamic patterns, and apartments arranged around internal courtyards. The Pearl is one of the well-known examples, and photographers of Soviet modernism have made it a favourite subject.

  2. 2. The Chatkal State Biosphere Nature Reserve

    East of Tashkent, the land rises steeply into the western ranges of the Tian Shan, and the Chatkal range forms the border with Kyrgyzstan. The Chatkal State Biosphere Reserve, established in the Soviet era, protects a stretch of these mountains, with juniper forests, walnut and wild fruit trees, alpine meadows and rocky peaks.

  3. 3. Besh-Aral State Nature Reserve - main part

    Far up the Chatkal valley in western Kyrgyzstan, where the river runs between the Chatkal and Sandalash ranges of the Western Tien Shan, lies the Besh-Aral State Nature Reserve. It protects mountain steppe, juniper woodland, walnut and apple groves and rocky high country, and it is part of the transnational World Heritage Site of the Western Tien Shan, inscribed in 2016 and shared with Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan. The reserve was created in particular to protect Menzbier's marmot, a small marmot found only in this part of Central Asia.
