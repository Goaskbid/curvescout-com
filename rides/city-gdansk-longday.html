Why this ride

339 km from Gdańsk to Gdańsk, about 3 h 45 of riding. The stops in between: Medieval Town of Toruń and back through Medieval Town of Toruń. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

The stops

  1. 1. Gdansk

    Start here.

    Gdansk has been a trading port for a thousand years, and the front of its Long Market, the Dluga Targ, shows what the money bought. Tall, narrow merchant houses stand shoulder to shoulder, their facades painted and gilded and carved with figures, around the Neptune Fountain and the Artus Court. In the Second World War, the city was ninety percent destroyed, and what you see today is a painstaking reconstruction from old paintings and photographs, which makes it more remarkable. The Gothic crane on the quay, the Zuraw, was once the largest in Europe, and it hoisted masts and cargo from ships in the Middle Ages.

  2. 2. Medieval Town of Toruń

    171 km in.

    Toruń is a Gothic brick town that survived the twentieth century almost untouched, which is rare for Poland, and that makes it the best place in the country to see what a medieval trading city looked like. The Teutonic Knights founded it in 1231 on the Vistula, and it grew rich as a member of the Hanseatic League, with merchants shipping grain, timber and goods down the river towards the Baltic. UNESCO listed the old town in 1997, and the red-brick churches, granaries, town hall and city walls are still there.
