Why this ride

curated scenic road. tropical mountains, waterfalls, ocean views. A one-way ride of 41 km, Petite Case Noyale to Curepipe - roughly 45 min in the saddle. On the way it passes Chemin Grenier and Grand Bois. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Mauritius Black River Gorges Route

Slow enough to look. The surface follows the shoreline, so the corners arrive where the water decided, not where an engineer did. Water arrives beside the road and stays for 13 km. The line follows the shore because there is nowhere else for it to go.

  5. 5. Curepipe

    Curepipe is the highest of the big towns on Mauritius, at about 550 metres on the central plateau, and it has a climate to match. It is cooler and wetter than the coast, often misty, with drizzle that locals take for granted, and the town has a slightly faded, colonial air, with old wooden houses, verandas and tall trees. The name possibly comes from soldiers cleaning their pipes here on the march. Curepipe has about eighty-four thousand people and runs into Vacoas and Midlands, so services are plentiful, and the end of the Black River Gorges route is a comfortable finish.
