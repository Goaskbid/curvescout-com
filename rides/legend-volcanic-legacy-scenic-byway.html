Why this ride

All-American Road. volcanoes, lava landscapes, mountain lakes. The stops in between: Klamath Falls, Volcanic Legacy Scenic Byway and Mount Shasta. The highest point is about 1,950 m. The line favours views over speed.

  2. 2. Klamath Falls

    Klamath Falls sits on the south end of Upper Klamath Lake, a broad shallow sheet of water that is the largest lake by surface area in Oregon, and the whole town faces it. Tucked in a high basin of the Cascades' eastern flank, at over a thousand metres, the city of twenty-odd thousand has a dry, sunny, big-sky character, more like the high desert than the green coastal Oregon of postcards. The Link River tumbles through town, connecting the lake to the Klamath River, which flows west to the Pacific through a wild, deep canyon.

  3. 3. Volcanic Legacy Scenic Byway point 3

    Up here on the California and Oregon border the land looks burnt, and in a sense it is. Beneath your tyres the ground is honeycombed with lava tubes, hundreds of them, formed when the outer skin of a flow cooled and the molten rock inside drained away.

  4. 4. Mount Shasta

    Mount Shasta appears long before you reach it, and it never seems to get smaller. This is a stratovolcano rising 4,322 metres from a plain of lava flows and pine forest, its twin summits dazzling with snow and glacier even in August, and it is visible from over a hundred miles away. The town that takes its name sits at the western foot, at about 1,100 metres, and everything in it, from the streets to the cafes, is built around the view. When the mountain is clear it dominates; when cloud wraps it, which happens often, the whole town feels deflated.

  5. 5. Volcanic Legacy Scenic Byway point 5

    Lassen Peak is the southernmost of the big Cascade volcanoes, and it is the one that most recently reminded California it was alive. Between 1914 and 1917 it erupted again and again, and in May 1915 a blast and a mudflow sent ash far to the east and stripped a long swathe of forest from its northeastern side. The scar is still visible as the Devastated Area, now slowly greening.
