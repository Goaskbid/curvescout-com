Why this ride

A 329 km round trip from Kropp - roughly 6 h 03 in the saddle. It goes by way of National Park Wadden Sea Niedersachsen and Kovirke Area 2. The coast is the point of it.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Kovirke Area 2

    The village of Dannewerk gave its name to the great monument that surrounds it, and it is a small place with a big story. About a thousand people live here, in a scatter of farms and houses along the road, and a museum on the edge of the village does what a museum should: it explains why the grass banks in the fields matter. Inside you find a model of the whole system, tools and weapons from the excavations, and a clear account of the many phases of building, from the timber palisade of the eighth century to the brick wall of the twelfth.

  2. 2. National Park Wadden Sea Niedersachsen

    Here the Wadden Sea comes right up against the dyke. This is the coast of the Wurster Nordseeküste, between the Weser and Elbe estuaries, a country of low sea walls, drained farmland and wide skies, and the mudflats outside the dyke are part of the Lower Saxony Wadden Sea National Park. Together with the Dutch and Danish parts of the Wadden Sea, the area is a UNESCO World Heritage site, valued above all for the sheer scale of the tidal flats and the millions of birds that pass through on migration. When the tide goes out, it goes a very long way.
