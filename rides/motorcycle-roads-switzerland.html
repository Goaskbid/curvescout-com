Plan a motorcycle trip in Switzerland

Start with the particular passes and the start town, not a country-wide best month. Use the ride cards below to compare recorded distances, then open the roadbook to check which climbs and return legs are actually included.

Plan the whole circuit

Write down every named pass and the road used to return to your start. A pass page describes that crossing, not the remaining circuit. Keep a lower-level alternative as a separate plan rather than assuming a closed crossing can be bypassed.

Check each pass separately

The Alpine Passes Interest Group publishes pass-specific information. Check the applicable road and date before departure. Do not infer access from the calendar month, a nearby tunnel, or a photograph of a clear summit.

Leave time for the stops

The displayed riding estimate is not the total time away. Add fuel, meals, viewpoints and a delay allowance in the ride-day budget. Save the named roads in the calendar workspace for a later recheck.
