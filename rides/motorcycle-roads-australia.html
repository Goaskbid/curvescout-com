Plan a motorcycle trip in Australia

Choose the region before the month. A touring plan in the tropical north and one on a southern coastal road need different seasonal context and separate road-status checks.

Use regional climate context

The Bureau of Meteorology describes northern Australia's wet season and monsoon. That context does not apply as one national calendar, and a seasonal pattern is not a forecast for your ride.

Review the actual surface and stops

Read the recorded route and waypoint notes, then verify current access and services using the authority responsible for the roads in your plan. The country page is not a surface survey.

Keep an achievable day

Add stops and delay allowance to the riding estimate before adopting a longer option. Save the chosen roads, rather than treating a country-level score as a complete trip check.
