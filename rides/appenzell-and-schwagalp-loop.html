Why this ride

52 km round from Sankt Gallen - plan on about 50 min of riding. It goes by way of Appenzell, Wildhaus and Schwagalp. The highest point is about 1,300 m. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

Scenic road chapters

Grand Tour of Switzerland

The valley floor is behind you and the road starts working for its height at about 16.0%. Corners stack above one another where the ground allows nothing straighter — first and second gear more often than not. Look through each corner to the next one and let the engine do the holding.

Rider note

Check the pass is open before you commit to it.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. St Gallen

    St Gallen grew up around a hermit's cell. In the early seventh century an Irish monk called Gallus settled in a forest valley here, and a monastery grew up that became one of the great centres of learning in medieval Europe. Today the abbey district is a UNESCO site, with a baroque cathedral, an abbey library, and a plan of the cloister, a famous ninth-century drawing that shows what an ideal monastery should look like.

  2. 2. Appenzell

    Every year on the last Sunday in April, the people of Appenzell Innerrhoden gather in the main square, men and women, many with a ceremonial sword or a bayonet at the hip, and vote with raised hands. The Landsgemeinde is one of the last open-air assemblies of its kind in Switzerland, and it takes place in the middle of this small town, which has been the capital of the half-canton for centuries. Women only gained a vote at the cantonal level here in 1990, after a federal court ruling, which tells you something about how conservative the valley is, and also how proud.

  3. 3. Wildhaus

    Wildhaus is the village where Huldrych Zwingli was born, and that makes it, quietly, one of the cradles of the Swiss Reformation. Zwingli, who became the leader of the Protestant movement in Zürich and died in battle in 1531, was born in 1484 in a farmhouse here, and the small timber building still stands, now a museum. It is a modest place, a wooden house with low ceilings and small windows, and it gives a feel for the mountain farming community from which the reformer came.

  4. 4. Schwagalp

    Schwägalp is a pass road, a mountain pasture and a cable car station all at once, and the third of those is the reason most people stop. At 1,278 metres, on a high saddle between canton Appenzell and the Toggenburg, it is the lower station for the Säntis cable car, which climbs in a few minutes to the summit of the Säntis at 2,502 metres. The mountain is the highest of the Alpstein range, a compact, craggy massif that rises abruptly out of the Swiss plateau, and on a clear day the view from the top extends across six countries.

St Gallen to Appenzell

St Gallen → Appenzell is 13 km of rhythm rather than effort. Let it flow and let the view breathe.

Appenzell to Wildhaus

Appenzell → Wildhaus is 18 km of rhythm rather than effort. Let it flow and let the view breathe.

Wildhaus to Schwagalp

Wildhaus → Schwagalp climbs about 1278 m in 8 km. This is the part worth slowing down for - look through the bends and use the pull-offs.

Schwagalp to St Gallen

Schwagalp → St Gallen drops about 1278 m over 24 km. Brakes and eyes forward; the views are behind the corners, not in them.
