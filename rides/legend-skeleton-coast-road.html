Why this ride

A desert coastal road from Swakopmund, Namibia to Torra Bay, Namibia, via Henties Bay. Key character: fog desert; shipwreck coast; seal colonies. Surface: gravel.

  1. 1. Swakopmund

    Swakopmund is a German seaside town that someone dropped into the Namib Desert, and it is still a little startled to be there. Palm-lined streets, half-timbered houses, a lighthouse and a pier would suit the Baltic, yet the sand dunes begin at the edge of town and the sea is the cold, grey Atlantic, chilled by the Benguela Current. The German colonial administration founded it in 1892 as a landing point, since the lagoon harbour at Walvis Bay lay in British hands, and the old buildings, bakeries and the Kaffee und Kuchen habit are what remains.

  3. 3. Skeleton Coast Road point 3

    Cape Cross is the place on this coast where history, wildlife and smell all arrive at once. The seal reserve here hosts one of the largest colonies of Cape fur seals in the world, with tens of thousands of animals crowded on the rocks and beaches, barking, squabbling and basking. You will notice the colony long before you see it, because the odour is strong, a mix of fish, ammonia and salt that carries on the wind, and the sound is a constant chorus of grunts and bleats.
