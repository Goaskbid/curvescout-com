Why this ride

From Ares del Maestre it runs 256 km to Ares del Maestre; allow about 4 h 25 of riding. It goes by way of La fenellosa, Cova del Ramat and back through La fenellosa. The highest point is about 1,100 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. molí Darrer- Abric III

    High up in the interior of Castellón, where the Maestrat hills fold into the mountains of Els Ports, there is a country of limestone ravines and old farms that few foreign riders ever see. The shelter called Molí Darrer, Abric III, lies in that country, about nine kilometres from Benassal. The name means last mill, a reminder that these ravines once powered small mills on seasonal streams, and that the people who farmed here worked hard with little water.

  2. 2. La fenellosa

    People in Aragón like to call the Matarraña their Tuscany, and for once the comparison is not entirely marketing. It is a comarca of honey-coloured stone villages on hilltops, olive groves on terraces, almond trees and vineyards, with the limestone wall of the Ports de Beseit rising behind. La Fenellosa lies in this country, between Valderrobres, Fuentespalda and Mas de Barberans across the Catalan line, in the rough hills where the farmland gives way to the mountains.

  3. 3. Cova del Ramat

    The Ebro makes a great loop through southern Catalonia, and the Cova del Ramat lies in the dry hills just west of it, above the villages of Ginestar and Rasquera. The river here is broad and slow, lined with orchards and reed beds, and in 1938 it became the site of the Battle of the Ebro, the longest and bloodiest battle of the Spanish Civil War, which raged for nearly four months in the summer and autumn and left the hills riddled with trenches. Their traces survive in the landscape, and several memorial sites and museums, notably around Gandesa, tell the story.
