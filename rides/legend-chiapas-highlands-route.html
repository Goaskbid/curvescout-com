Why this ride

curated scenic road. highlands, canyon, Maya communities. From Tuxtla it runs 186 km to Miguel Hidalgo; allow about 3 h 23 of riding. The stops in between: San Cristóbal de las Casas and Comitán. The highest point is about 2,000 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

  3. 3. San Cristóbal de las Casas

    San Cristóbal de las Casas sits at about 2,200 metres in a bowl of pine and oak forest in the Chiapas highlands, and the altitude shapes everything. The mornings are cold, the afternoons bright and the evenings smoky with wood fires, and the old streets, cobbled and low, are lined with houses painted in ochre and red. Diego de Mazariegos founded the town in 1528, and it was later renamed for Bartolomé de las Casas, the Dominican bishop who spoke up for the Indigenous population. On New Year's Day 1994 the Zapatista rebels briefly took over the town hall, and the uprising still shapes the politics of the region.

  4. 4. Comitán

    Comitán sits at around 1,600 metres in the highlands of eastern Chiapas, closer to Guatemala than to any large Mexican city, and it has the slightly reserved, self-contained air of a town that has been far from things for a long time. Its full name honours Belisario Domínguez, the local doctor and senator who stood up in the Senate in 1913 and denounced the dictator Victoriano Huerta, and was murdered for it. His statue, his museum, and the main square named for him tell visitors how the town sees itself. Another famous daughter is the writer Rosario Castellanos, whose novels and poems of Chiapas are still read widely.
