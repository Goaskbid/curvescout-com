Why this ride

A 259 km round trip from Nerpio - roughly 4 h 28 in the saddle. The stops in between: Izquierda E. de Santonje, Cueva de la Plata and back through Izquierda E. de Santonje. The highest point is about 1,400 m. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. mingarnao II

    Nerpio is a high village in the far southwest of Albacete province, about eleven hundred metres above sea level, and Mingarnao is one of the names scattered across the countryside around it. The shelter, Mingarnao II, is one of dozens in the Nerpio area that make up a notable concentration of rock paintings. Several have been found here, and some show Levantine figures and others show later schematic signs, a mixture that makes the area unusual.

  2. 2. Izquierda E. de Santonje

    The Indalo is everywhere in Almería: a little stick figure holding an arc over its head, painted on houses, sold as a souvenir and used as the symbol of the province. It comes from a rock shelter near Vélez-Blanco, the Cueva de los Letreros, where it was painted in prehistoric times, and the area around the village is rich in rock art, part of the World Heritage rock art of the Mediterranean. The shelters around Santonje, near the villages of María and Vélez-Blanco, are among them.

  3. 3. Cueva de la Plata

    Aledo is a tiny village on a rocky spur in the hills of Murcia, a place that punches well above its weight in history. In the eleventh century, it was a fortress on the frontier between Christian Castile and the Muslim taifa kingdoms, and the siege of Aledo in 1088 was a major episode in the struggle for the peninsula. A tower still stands above the village. The Cueva de la Plata, the silver cave, lies in the hills nearby, between Aledo, Totana and Alhama de Murcia.
