Why this ride

A 251 km round trip from Puçol - roughly 4 h 20 in the saddle. On the way it passes La Joquera, Abric de la mostela and back through La Joquera.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Covacha Picayo- abrigo II

    The Sierra Calderona is the wooded ridge behind Valencia's northern suburbs, and it is the best surprise on this coast. It is a natural park of pine, holm oak and rosemary, with low summits, deep ravines and a sense of space that is hard to believe so close to a large city. Wild boar wander the slopes, along with foxes, genets and a growing population of birds of prey, and in spring the slopes are full of orchids and cistus flowers.

  2. 2. La Joquera

    Just behind Castelló de la Plana, where the coastal plain meets the first hills, the ravine of La Joquera cuts into the limestone near the village of Borriol. Its shelters hold prehistoric paintings, part of the UNESCO-listed rock art of the Mediterranean basin, and the area has been studied for its archaeology for decades. It is unusual to find such ancient art so close to a large modern city, and the contrast between the busy plain below, with its orange groves, factories and motorways, and the quiet hills above is striking.

    The hill country around Albocàsser and Catí, in the north of Castellón province, is a landscape of terraced slopes, almond trees and limestone ravines that few visitors cross on their way from the coast to Morella.
