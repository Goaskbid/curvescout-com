Why this ride

68 km from Erriadh to Erriadh, about 1 h 10 of riding. On the way it passes Mosquée El Bessi, Zone Temlel and back through Mosquée El Bessi. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Mosquée Sidi Jmour

    Sidi Jmour is a mosque near the north coast of Djerba, in an area of dunes, olive groves and sandy tracks, and it is named, as many Tunisian shrines are, for a holy man. The mosque has the family look of the island's buildings: thick white walls, strong buttresses and a small dome, and it stands in open country, so you see it from a distance, bright against the dark green of the olives. Most mosques on Djerba have a sacred feeling of austere simplicity, and the whitewashed walls reflect the sun in a way that is almost dazzling at midday.

  2. 2. Mosquée El Bessi

    Djerba is an island that Odysseus is supposed to have visited. Homer's lotus-eaters, who fed him and his men a fruit that made them forget home, are traditionally placed here, and it is easy to see how a visitor might think so, with its white houses, palms, olive groves and slow warm air. The island lies off the south-eastern coast of Tunisia in the Gulf of Gabès, flat, sandy and joined to the mainland by a Roman causeway, and it has long been home to a mix of peoples: Berber-speaking Ibadi Muslims, Jews and Arabs. UNESCO listed the island's pattern of settlement in 2023.

  3. 3. Zone Temlel

    Midoun is the second town of Djerba, and Temlel lies just beside it, a rural zone of walled plots, olive trees, palms and whitewashed houses in the east of the island. It is the kind of place that a guidebook would skip, and yet it is exactly what the island's World Heritage listing is about. In 2023 UNESCO recognised Djerba as an outstanding example of how a community lived on an island with no rivers, by dividing the land into small family holdings, each with its well, its cistern, its olive press or oven, and its orchard, grouped in neighbourhoods called houmas, around a mosque and a market.
