Why this ride

National Scenic Byway. high alpine, summit views, wildlife. A one-way ride of 20 km, Idaho Springs to Georgetown - roughly 22 min in the saddle. The highest point is about 3,350 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Scenic road chapters

Mount Blue Sky Scenic Byway

Expect company on a summer weekend. Early is better than fast. For 9 km this is Mount Blue Sky Scenic Byway, a road with a name of its own and a reason for it.

  2. 2. Near Georgetown

    Silver built Georgetown. In the 1860s, prospectors working up Clear Creek found rich silver ore in the mountains above this narrow valley west of Denver, and within a few years the town became one of the most important mining centres in Colorado, nicknamed the Silver Queen of the Rockies. When silver prices crashed in 1893, the boom ended, but the town survived, and today its streets of Victorian houses, brick storefronts and wooden churches form one of the best-preserved historic mining districts in the American West.
