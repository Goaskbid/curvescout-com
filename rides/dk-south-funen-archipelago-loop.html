  1. 1. Odense

    Odense is best known as the birthplace of Hans Christian Andersen, born in 1805 in a poor part of town, and his story is the thread that runs through the place. The museum dedicated to him occupies several old houses in the centre of the old quarter, with yellow walls and cobbled lanes that have been tidied to look like a fairy-tale street. The tales, which include The Little Mermaid and The Ugly Duckling, were written long after he left, but the city has made a very good job of claiming him. The town's name goes back to the Viking age, and it means Odin's shrine, which tells you how old the settlement is.
