Why this ride

A 265 km round trip from Schleswig - roughly 4 h 49 in the saddle. On the way it passes National Park Wadden Sea Niedersachsen and Kovirke Area 7. The coast is the point of it.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Kovirke Area 7

    South of Schleswig, in the narrow neck of land where the Jutland peninsula pinches itself between the Baltic and the North Sea marshes, lies one of the longest ancient monuments in Europe. This is the Danevirke, a system of earth ramparts, ditches and walls that runs for about thirty kilometres across the country, and the label here, Kovirke Area 7, refers to one of its sections, the Kovirke, whose name is usually translated as the Cow Work. Today the walls are grassy banks in fields, easy to miss if you are riding fast, but they once barred the way into Denmark.

  2. 2. National Park Wadden Sea Niedersachsen

    Here the Wadden Sea comes right up against the dyke. This is the coast of the Wurster Nordseeküste, between the Weser and Elbe estuaries, a country of low sea walls, drained farmland and wide skies, and the mudflats outside the dyke are part of the Lower Saxony Wadden Sea National Park. Together with the Dutch and Danish parts of the Wadden Sea, the area is a UNESCO World Heritage site, valued above all for the sheer scale of the tidal flats and the millions of birds that pass through on migration. When the tide goes out, it goes a very long way.
