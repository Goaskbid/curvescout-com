Why this ride

From New Delhi it runs 443 km to New Delhi; allow about 7 h 38 of riding. The stops in between: Keoladeo National Park and back through Keoladeo National Park. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Delhi

    Delhi is a city of layers, and every dynasty that wanted to rule northern India seems to have built its own version here. The Qutub Minar, a 73-metre tower of red sandstone, was begun in the late twelfth century; the great Mughal emperor Shah Jahan built the Red Fort and the Jama Masjid in the seventeenth century; and in the twentieth century the British laid out New Delhi with its wide avenues, India Gate and the long processional road up to the Presidential Palace. Old Delhi, around Chandni Chowk, is a maze of lanes, spice markets and food stalls where paratha shops have been frying bread in ghee for generations.

  2. 2. Keoladeo National Park

    For decades the Maharajas of Bharatpur kept this marshland as a duck-shooting estate, and the scale of the sport is hard to believe. On a single day in 1938 the viceroy Lord Linlithgow and his party shot more than four thousand birds, a record that still shocks people today. The shoots ended after independence, and in 1971 the area became a protected sanctuary. It is now Keoladeo National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage site since 1985 and one of the finest bird sanctuaries in Asia.
