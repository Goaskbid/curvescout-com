Why this ride

A 426 km one-way ride from near Horsted Keynes, about 7 h 45 of riding. It goes by way of Scenic stretch near Reading and near Buckingham. The climb of the day is near Storrington. The line favours views over speed.

Slow enough to look. The surface follows the shoreline, so the corners arrive where the water decided, not where an engineer did. Water arrives beside the road and stays for 67 km. The line follows the shore because there is nowhere else for it to go.

  4. 4. Photo stop near Buckingham

    Buckingham is a small, handsome market town in the north of the county that bears its name, wrapped in a loop of the river Great Ouse. Its centre is a pleasing jumble of Georgian brick, coaching inns and a castellated old gaol that now houses a little museum. The town also has the distinction of being home to the University of Buckingham, the first independent university in Britain, founded in the 1970s.
