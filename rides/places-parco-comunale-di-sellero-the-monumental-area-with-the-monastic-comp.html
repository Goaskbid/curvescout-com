Why this ride

A 179 km round trip from Sellero - roughly 3 h in the saddle. The stops in between: Parco Comunale di Luine di Darfo-Boario Te, The monumental area with the monastic comp and back through Parco Comunale di Luine di Darfo-Boario Te. It tops out around 2,000 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The roads

Passo di Vivione

The 30 km between Parco Comunale di Sellero and Parco Comunale di Luine di Darfo-Boario Te, mostly on SS42. Mostly downhill - about 430 m down, from a high point of 656 m.

The stops

  1. 1. Parco Comunale di Sellero

    Start here.

    Valcamonica is the valley of the rock carvings, and Sellero is one of the places where you can walk among them. For about eight thousand years, from the Stone Age to Roman times, the people who lived here, especially the Camuni of the Iron Age, hammered and scratched pictures onto the smooth glacier-polished rock: hunters, deer, warriors, farmers, ploughs, huts and strange symbols. The valley holds hundreds of thousands of them, and in 1979 it became Italy's first UNESCO World Heritage Site. The municipal park at Sellero preserves a group of engraved rocks in woodland above the Oglio river, so you walk on a path and find the carvings on bare sheets of stone among the trees.

    Read the complete Parco Comunale di Sellero write-up.

  2. 2. Parco Comunale di Luine di Darfo-Boario Te

    30 km in.

    Valcamonica, the long valley of the Oglio river north of Lake Iseo, holds one of the largest collections of prehistoric rock carvings in the world, with hundreds of thousands of figures pecked into smooth glacier-polished rocks over thousands of years. It was the first site in Italy to be listed by UNESCO, in 1979. The Luine park, on a wooded hill above Darfo Boario Terme at the southern end of the valley, is one of the places where you can see these engravings up close, with paths leading between rock surfaces covered in figures of animals, people, weapons and mysterious symbols.

    Read the complete Parco Comunale di Luine di Darfo-Boario Te write-up.

  3. 3. The monumental area with the monastic comp

    90 km in.

    The centre of Brescia holds two thousand years of layered history within a few hundred metres. Along the old Roman main street, the Capitolium temple raised under Vespasian still lifts its columns above the forum, with a Roman theatre beside it, and a short walk away the monastery of San Salvatore and Santa Giulia spreads its cloisters and churches over the site of Roman houses. The monastery was founded in the eighth century by Desiderius, the last king of the Lombards, and his wife Ansa; their daughter Ermengarda, rejected by Charlemagne, is said to have ended her days here.
