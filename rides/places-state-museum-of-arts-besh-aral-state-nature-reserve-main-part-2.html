Why this ride

From Tashkent it runs 315 km to Tashkent; allow about 5 h 26 of riding. The stops in between: The Chatkal State Biosphere Nature Reserve, Besh-Aral State Nature Reserve - main part and back through The Chatkal State Biosphere Nature Reserve. The highest point is about 2,700 m. It crosses from Uzbekistan into Kyrgyzstan - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. State Museum of Arts

    The best Orientalist painter of Turkestan was a Russian who hated war. Vasily Vereshchagin came to Tashkent in the 1860s as an artist attached to the Russian army, and he painted the bazaars, the beggars and the mosques of Central Asia with a precision that shocked his patrons, and then went on to paint scenes of battle that showed the dead as they were. His canvases are among the treasures of Russian art, and their subject was the world that lies around Tashkent today. The State Museum of Arts of Uzbekistan holds paintings of this kind, along with works by Russian and European artists, and a good collection of Uzbek applied art.

  2. 2. The Chatkal State Biosphere Nature Reserve

    East of Tashkent, the land rises steeply into the western ranges of the Tian Shan, and the Chatkal range forms the border with Kyrgyzstan. The Chatkal State Biosphere Reserve, established in the Soviet era, protects a stretch of these mountains, with juniper forests, walnut and wild fruit trees, alpine meadows and rocky peaks.

  3. 3. Besh-Aral State Nature Reserve - main part

    Far up the Chatkal valley in western Kyrgyzstan, where the river runs between the Chatkal and Sandalash ranges of the Western Tien Shan, lies the Besh-Aral State Nature Reserve. It protects mountain steppe, juniper woodland, walnut and apple groves and rocky high country, and it is part of the transnational World Heritage Site of the Western Tien Shan, inscribed in 2016 and shared with Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan. The reserve was created in particular to protect Menzbier's marmot, a small marmot found only in this part of Central Asia.
