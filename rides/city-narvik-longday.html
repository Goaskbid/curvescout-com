Why this ride

From Narvik it runs 333 km to Narvik; allow about 5 h 44 of riding. The stops in between: Laponian Area and back through Laponian Area. The highest point is about 1,150 m. It crosses from Norway into Sweden - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Narvik

    Narvik exists because of iron. In the late nineteenth century the huge ore deposits at Kiruna in Swedish Lapland needed an ice-free port, and the Norwegian coast, warmed by the Gulf Stream, offered one. The railway, the Ofotbanen, opened in 1902 and still carries ore trains down the mountains to the quay, where it is loaded onto ships, and the great ore jetty, lit at night, is the most characteristic sight in town.

  2. 2. Laponian Area

    They fall in the heart of the Laponian Area, the huge World Heritage landscape of northern Sweden that takes in Sarek, Padjelanta, Stora Sjöfallet and Muddus, and most of it is roadless wilderness of glaciers, braided rivers, birch forest and bare peaks above two thousand metres. The Sami have herded reindeer through it for thousands of years, following the seasons from the high pastures down to the forests, and the protection of the area is as much about that living culture as about the nature.
