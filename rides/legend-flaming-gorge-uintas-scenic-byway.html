Why this ride

National Scenic Byway. reservoir, red canyon, Uinta Mountains. 78 km from Vernal to Manila, about 1 h 25 of riding. The highest point is about 2,700 m. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Vernal

    The town has about eleven thousand people, with a long main street, a few motels and diners, and a museum of natural history with a collection of dinosaur skeletons, including a life-size model garden with creatures that appear to lurk beside the road. The surrounding country is red and tan rock, sagebrush, and dry river valleys, with the high, snowy Uinta Mountains standing to the north.

  4. 4. Manila

    Manila is a very small town at a very big view. It sits on the Utah side of the state line, only a few hundred metres south of the Wyoming border, on the western rim of Flaming Gorge Reservoir, and if you blink on the highway you might miss it. A few hundred people live here, a handful of motels and cafés serve the visitors, and the real attraction lies just beyond the last building, where the land drops into a long blue arm of water between red cliffs.
