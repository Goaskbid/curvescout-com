  1. 1. Gospić

    Gospić is the small capital of Lika, a high, cold plateau tucked behind the Velebit mountains, and the town sits at about five hundred and sixty metres above the sea in a broad valley of meadows and pine woods. Winters here are famously harsh, with deep snow and temperatures that can drop far below freezing, and people sometimes call the region Croatia's Siberia. The summers are mild and fresh, and the long evenings are good for sitting outside.

  2. 2. Otočac

    Otočac lies in the Gacka valley, a green strip of meadow in the middle of Lika, the sparsely populated highland behind the Velebit mountains. The river Gacka is the town's treasure. It rises from limestone springs, runs cold and so clear that every stone shows on the bottom, and holds trout and grayling that draw fly fishermen from across Europe. In the town itself, the river passes between old stone houses and mills, with willows and ducks along the banks.
