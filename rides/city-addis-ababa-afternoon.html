Why this ride

From Addis Ababa it runs 127 km to Addis Ababa; allow about 2 h 11 of riding. The stops in between: Balchit, Atebella, Kella and Wofi. The highest point is about 2,700 m.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Addis Ababa

    At about 2,350 metres, Addis Ababa is one of the highest capitals on Earth, and you feel it in the first hour: the sun is strong, the air is thin and cool, and a flight of stairs is a small event. The city sits in a bowl of eucalyptus-covered hills, founded in the 1880s by Emperor Menelik II and his wife Taytu, whose name for it, the new flower, stuck. It is a sprawling, restless, cheerful place, and the pace of building is astonishing.

  2. 2. Balchit

    Southwest of Addis Ababa the high plateau starts to roll, and the names on the map begin to belong to farms and villages rather than to cities. Balchit is one of those names, a small place in the Oromia highlands beyond Sebeta, and I will be straight with you that it is not a destination in its own right. What it offers is a sample of the country that surrounds the capital: red-brown fields, eucalyptus groves, round huts and squared houses with tin roofs, and a sky of enormous size at around two thousand metres above the sea.

  3. 3. Atebella

    Atebella lies among the farmlands that stretch south-west of Sebeta, and what there is to say about it is really a description of the countryside. The plateau here is a patchwork of small plots, divided by hedges of cactus and euphorbia, and dotted with trees whose silhouettes look like umbrellas. It is a landscape shaped by generations of smallholders, and in the early morning, with the sun low and the mist lying in the hollows, it looks as though it has hardly changed in a century. Cattle with long horns are driven along the road by boys with sticks.

  4. 4. Kella

    Kella is another small name on the plateau south-west of the capital, and its interest lies in what you pass on the way. The old road leads out of Addis Ababa through Sebeta, a town that grew around a railway and market, and beyond it climbs gently into high farmland, with slopes of green and tan, groves of eucalyptus and, in the distance, the shape of the mountains that rim the Rift. At this latitude and height the sun is strong, but the air is cool, and the clarity of the light is memorable.
