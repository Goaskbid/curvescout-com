The stops

  1. 1. Fort Erie

    Start here.

    Across a fast, deep stretch of river, the Buffalo skyline sits so close you can almost read the signs. Fort Erie faces it from the Canadian side at the point where Lake Erie pours into the Niagara River, and the Peace Bridge, opened in 1927, connects the two countries in a long curve of arches over the current. This is a border town of about sixteen thousand people, and for riders it is often the first or last taste of Canada on a trip around the Great Lakes.

    Read the complete Fort Erie write-up.

  2. 2. Niagara Falls

    The end: 34 km.

    The noise reaches you first, a deep, continuous roar, and then a column of mist rising above the trees. Riding up the Niagara Parkway from the south, the Horseshoe Falls appear suddenly: a vast curved wall of water, about fifty metres high, pouring over the edge of the escarpment into the gorge below. From the railing at Table Rock you stand almost at the lip, close enough to feel the spray on your face.
