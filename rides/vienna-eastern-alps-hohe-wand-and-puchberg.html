Why this ride

A proper escape from Vienna with roads worth waking up for. A 633 km loop that starts and ends near Drassburg, about 11 h 31 of riding. The stops in between: Scenic stretch in Prinzersdorf, near Japons and near Brezová pod Bradlom. The climb of the day is near Otterthal. It tops out at about 850 m. It crosses from Austria into Slovakia - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Wachau Danube Road

Expect company on a summer weekend. Early is better than fast. For 83 km this is Wachau Danube Road, a road with a name of its own and a reason for it.

  4. 4. Photo stop near Japons

    Japons lies on the high granite plateau of the Waldviertel, the forest quarter of Lower Austria, north-west of Vienna, a land of rolling hills, fields, forests and scattered villages, where the climate is harsher and the pace slower than in the Danube valley below. The village is small and quiet, with a church and farms, and the surrounding landscape is open and airy, with long views over the plateau.
