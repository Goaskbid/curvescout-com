Why this ride

curated scenic road. rock cuts, escarpment, mountain views. 213 km from São José to the finish, about 4 h 18 of riding. The highest point is about 1,505 m. The line favours views over speed.

The roads

Serra do Corvo Branco

Serra do Corvo Branco point 2 is 183 km from Near São José, mostly on SC-390. A proper up-and-over: 2300 m of climbing, 1,505 m at the top, 1300 m down. Fill the tank before the climb.

Rider note

Check the pass is open before you commit to it.
