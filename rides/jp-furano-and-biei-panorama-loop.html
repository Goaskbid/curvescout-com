  2. 2. Furano

    Furano lies in the middle of Hokkaido, in a basin ringed by the Tokachi mountains and the Yubari range, and it is known for lavender. The fields of Farm Tomita and the surrounding farms turn purple in July, and visitors come from across Japan to see them. The climate here is continental, with hot summers and cold, snowy winters, and the long hours of summer light let farmers grow lavender, melons, potatoes and onions. The town is small, and the settlement of Shimo-furano is right beside it, while Ashibetsu, a former coal town, lies thirty kilometres or so away.
