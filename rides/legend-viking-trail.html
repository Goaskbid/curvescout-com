Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Deer Lake

    Deer Lake is where western Newfoundland begins for most visitors, because it has the airport, a handful of motels, and the junction where the Viking Trail leaves the Trans-Canada Highway and heads north. About 4,600 people live here, there is fuel in town, and it is the last place with real choice before the long empty miles on Route 430. Treat it as the point to fill the tank, eat something warm, and check the sky.

  4. 4. St. Anthony

    At St Anthony the road runs out of Newfoundland, or nearly. This is the northern capital of the Great Northern Peninsula, a fishing port of about two thousand people facing the cold Atlantic and the Labrador current, and it has been a lifeline for the whole coast since the English doctor Wilfred Grenfell set up a hospital here in the early twentieth century. The Grenfell historic properties still stand, and the story of the doctor who sailed from cove to cove to treat fishing families is one of the best told in the province.
