Why this ride

65 km round from Žabljak - plan on about 1 h 03 of riding. On the way it passes Sedlo Pass, Trsa and Tara Canyon. The highest point is about 1,900 m. Sedlo Pass is subject to seasonal closure - check it is open before you set off. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

Scenic road chapters

Montenegro Durmitor Ring

The last of the trees go and the road runs out onto open ground. The summit sits at 1907 m, and what surrounds it is rock, weather and distance — cold comes early up here and stays late. Ride it with something in reserve: the summit is not the end of the work, only the middle of it.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Zabljak

    Žabljak claims to be the highest town in the Balkans, at about 1,450 metres, and it sits on a plateau among the peaks of Durmitor, a national park and UNESCO World Heritage site in northern Montenegro. The town is small, a place of low houses and mountain lodges, and it exists mostly for hikers, skiers and the occasional motorcyclist who has made the effort to come this far. Pines and spruce close in around it, the nights are cool even in summer, and the snow comes early.

  2. 2. Sedlo Pass

    Sedlo means saddle, and this one, at 1,907 metres, is the high point of the road that circles the Durmitor massif in the north of Montenegro. The road climbs through forest and then through bare limestone country, where the trees thin out and the ridges seem to catch the sky, and at the top, a pass opens to a view of Bobotov Kuk, the highest peak of the range at 2,523 metres, with its grey cliffs streaked with snow. The air is thin, clean and cold, and the silence is broken only by wind and the bells of sheep.

  3. 3. Trsa

    Trsa is a name on a map more than a place with a centre, a scatter of houses above the long green reservoir that drowned the Piva canyon. Plužine is the nearest village of any size, a few kilometres up the water, and Žabljak sits on the Durmitor plateau beyond that. What you are really riding here is the shoreline road, and it is one of the odd ones in the Balkans: it clings to the canyon wall, ducks through short rough tunnels that look as if they were cut by people in a hurry, and opens out every few hundred metres onto water the colour of bottle glass.

  4. 4. Tara Canyon

    Everyone who talks about the Tara canyon quotes the same number, and it holds up: up to about 1,300 metres from rim to river, which makes it among the deepest gorges in Europe and the deepest in this part of the world. The Tara runs for roughly eighty kilometres, cuts through the north of Montenegro on its way to join the Piva and become the Drina, and the whole thing sits inside a protected biosphere reserve. From the road you see the green end of it, black pine forest dropping into a thin turquoise line a very long way down.

Recorded leg-by-leg notes

These source notes may contain earlier estimates. They are not turn-by-turn directions or current measured distances.

Zabljak to Sedlo Pass

Zabljak → Sedlo Pass climbs about 1907 m in 13 km. This is the part worth slowing down for - look through the bends and use the pull-offs.

Sedlo Pass to Trsa

Sedlo Pass → Trsa drops about 1907 m over 14 km. Brakes and eyes forward; the views are behind the corners, not in them.

Trsa to Tara Canyon

Trsa → Tara Canyon is 36 km of rhythm rather than effort. Let it flow and let the view breathe.

Tara Canyon to Zabljak

Tara Canyon → Zabljak is 18 km of rhythm rather than effort. Let it flow and let the view breathe.
