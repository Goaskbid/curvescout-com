Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Near Pokhara

    Pokhara is the point where Nepal's mountain drama starts to feel close. The city lies at about eight hundred metres, beside the long blue Phewa Lake, and from the lakeside you can see the Annapurna range lined up along the northern horizon, including the sharp summit of Machapuchare, the Fishtail, which has never been climbed because it is held sacred. On a clear morning the effect is almost unfair; on a hazy afternoon the peaks vanish altogether.
