  2. 2. Scenic stretch in Waller

    Waller is an unincorporated community on the eastern side of Tacoma, in Pierce County, a semi-rural area of small farms, woodlots and scattered homes between the city and the Puyallup River valley. It is not a famous place, but it is a good example of the green, rolling country that surrounds the Puget Sound cities.

  3. 3. High point near Prairie Ridge

    Prairie Ridge is a residential community on a plateau above the Puyallup and White River valleys, south of Bonney Lake in Pierce County. The ridge rises above the surrounding lowlands, and on clear days the view of Mount Rainier is spectacular, the great volcano filling the south-eastern sky with its glaciers and snowfields.
