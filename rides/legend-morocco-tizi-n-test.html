Why this ride

curated scenic road. High Atlas, hairpins, valley views. A one-way ride of 164 km, Marrakesh to Taroudant - roughly 2 h 59 in the saddle. It goes by way of Moulay Brahim and Morocco Tizi n Test. The highest point is about 2,100 m. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Marrakesh

    Red walls, palm trees and the white peaks of the High Atlas on the horizon: that is the first impression of Marrakesh, and on a clear winter morning, with snow on the mountains to the south, it is unforgettable. The city stands on the Haouz plain in central Morocco, a sprawling, noisy, beautiful place of nearly a million people, built around a walled medina that has been a centre of trade and power for close to a thousand years.

  3. 3. Morocco Tizi n Test point 3

    This is the top. The Tizi n Test pass stands at about 2,092 metres, and the saddle feels like a threshold in the most literal sense: on the north side the road has climbed in long, looping stretches through the High Atlas, and on the south the land opens toward the Souss valley, drier, hotter and wider.

  4. 4. Taroudant

    Taroudant is a walled town in the Souss valley of southern Morocco, between the High Atlas and the Anti-Atlas, and its red-ochre ramparts, about seven kilometres around, are the first thing you notice. They are made of rammed earth and glow orange in the late afternoon, enclosing a town of seventy thousand people, a maze of lanes, a pair of busy souks, orchards and market gardens. The town is sometimes called the little Marrakesh, and for a time in the sixteenth century it was the Saadian dynasty's capital, before they moved north. The calm, unhurried pace is the contrast with the larger city.
