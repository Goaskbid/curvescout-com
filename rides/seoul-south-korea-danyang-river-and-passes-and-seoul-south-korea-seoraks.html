Why this ride

From Anyang-si it runs 555 km to Yangp'yŏng; allow about 9 h 45 of riding. The climbs: near Ansan-si, Scenic stretch near Goyang-si, in Beobwon and near Osan. Much of it runs through woodland.

The stops

  1. 1. Fuel stop near Anyang-si

    Start here.

    Gwanaksan rises over the southern edge of Seoul, a ridge of bare granite with a small hermitage perched on a cliff near the summit, and its rocky profile is one of the landmarks of the city. Seoul National University, the country's top university, sits at its northern foot, and the trails up the mountain are crowded with hikers in bright outdoor gear on weekends.

    Read the complete Fuel stop near Anyang-si write-up.

  2. 2. High point near Ansan-si

    15 km in. You top out here; the road stops climbing.

  3. 3. Near Bucheon-si

    38 km in.

    Seoul does not really end; it merges into one city after another, and Bucheon is one of them, a dense, busy satellite city of around eight hundred and fifty thousand people squeezed between the capital and the port of Incheon. Nearby Gwangmyeong and the southwestern districts of Seoul blend into it, and the whole area is a sea of apartment towers, shopping streets, factories and elevated roads. It is not a beautiful place to finish, but it is a practical one.

    Read the complete Near Bucheon-si write-up.

  6. 6. Near Yangp'yŏng, on the way back

    169 km in.

    Yangpyeong is where Seoul riders go to breathe. An hour east of the capital, the Bukhan and Namhan rivers come together at a place called Dumulmeori, literally "where two waters meet", and around them spread wooded mountains, farmland, small towns and riverside cafés. The county has around eighty thousand people, and on weekends the roads fill with motorcycles heading out from the city.

    Read the complete Near Yangp'yŏng, on the way back write-up.

  7. 7. Food stop near Yangp'yŏng

    192 km in.

    Two rivers meet at Yangpyeong. The North Han and the South Han join at a place called Dumulmeori, literally the meeting of two waters, east of Seoul, and the spot, with its ancient zelkova tree, misty mornings and old ferry landing, has become one of the most photographed in Korea. At dawn, mist rises off the water and fishermen's boats sit motionless, a scene that seems far removed from the megacity an hour away.

    Read the complete Fuel stop near Yangp'yŏng write-up.

  8. 8. Back to Anyang-si

    257 km in.

    It does not look like a place for a story, but it has a few. The name means something like peaceful and safe, and was given in the tenth century after a Buddhist temple, Anyangsa, that once stood here. Today the city has a long stream, the Anyangcheon, with a green path along its banks, and, on the hillside near Mount Samseong, the Anyang Art Park, where sculptures and installations by artists from around the world are scattered through the woods.

    Read the complete Anyang-si write-up.

  10. 10. High point near Osan

    392 km in. High point near Osan tops the climb.

    Osan is a city in Gyeonggi province, south of Seoul and Suwon, best known abroad for the US air base that bears its name, though the base actually lies a little further south, in Pyeongtaek. The city itself is modern and busy, with apartment blocks, markets and a lively centre, and it makes a practical stop on the way south.

  12. 12. Incheon

    468 km in.

    On 15 September 1950 American and South Korean marines stormed ashore at Wolmido, a small island in Incheon harbour, in one of the boldest amphibious operations of modern war. The landing turned the tide of the Korean War, and a statue of General MacArthur, who planned it, still stands in Jayu Park above the old port. Incheon today is a huge, modern port city of nearly three million people, home to Korea's main international airport, but the old harbour district keeps traces of its past.

    Read the complete Incheon write-up.

  13. 13. Kwangmyŏng

    489 km in.

    Gwangmyeong is a satellite city on the south-western edge of Seoul, a place of high-rise apartment blocks, shopping centres and a high-speed railway station, and it is the finish point for rides that have spent the day in the mountains of Gangwon Province or the lakes and forests north of the capital. Arriving here after Seoraksan, with its granite peaks and Buddhist temples, can feel like a sudden return to the modern world.

    Read the complete Kwangmyŏng write-up.

  14. 14. Back to Anyang-si

    The end: 555 km.

    Yangpyeong is about ten kilometres to the north-east, and the city of Gwangju, about thirteen kilometres south-west, has about eighty thousand people. The valleys here are green, with small farms, orchards and pine woods on the slopes.

    Read the complete Anyang-si write-up.
