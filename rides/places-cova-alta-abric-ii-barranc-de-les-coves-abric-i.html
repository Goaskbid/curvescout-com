Why this ride

From Castell de Castells it runs 158 km to Castell de Castells; allow about 2 h 43 of riding. It goes by way of Port de Penaguila- Abric II, Coveta del mig- Abric I, Barranc de les Coves- Abric I and back through Coveta del mig- Abric I. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Cova Alta- Abric II

    On a clear day in this corner of Alicante you can see the long pale ridge of Aitana, the highest mountain in the province at about 1,558 metres, and the sea beyond it, which is a surprising combination for a country of rock shelters and mule tracks. Cova Alta II lies three and a half kilometres from Castell de Castells, in the high limestone country of the Serrella and Aitana massifs, and the name means high cave, which tells you what to expect: a climb. It is the second of the Cova Alta shelters, with Abric III and others nearby, and it forms part of the Mediterranean rock art listed by UNESCO in 1998.

  2. 2. Port de Penaguila- Abric II

    The Port de Penàguila is a mountain pass in the interior of Alicante province, on the road between Alcoy and the Vall de Guadalest, where the hills of the Comtat rise toward the Serra d'Aitana, the highest range in the province. The village of Penàguila, below the pass, is a small, ancient place huddled beneath a castle on a rocky crest, and Benilloba, Cocentaina and Alcoy are all close. The second shelter of the Port de Penàguila is one of several painted sites in the area.

  3. 3. Coveta del mig- Abric I

    Otos is a tiny village in the Vall d'Albaida, in the south of Valencia province, and it has an unusual claim to fame: its walls are decorated with dozens of sundials, designed by artists and scattered through the streets, making the village a kind of open-air museum of time. Nearby, in the hills around Otos, Bèlgida and Ráfol de Salem, prehistoric people left paintings in rock shelters, and the Coveta del Mig is one of the sites included in the World Heritage site of the rock art of the Mediterranean Basin. The art here includes schematic figures, simplified human and animal forms, and the area is part of a rich zone of rock art that extends into the mountains of the Alicante province to the south.

  4. 4. Barranc de les Coves- Abric I

    Ibi is the toy capital of Spain, a fact that surprises everyone who rides through this mountain town in the hills of Alicante. Since the early twentieth century, workshops here have produced dolls, tin toys and plastic figures, and the Valencian Toy Museum tells the story. The rock shelter named Barranc de les Coves Abric I lies in the hills between Ibi and Alcoy, in a ravine whose name simply means the gully of the caves.
