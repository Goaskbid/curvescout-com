Plan a motorcycle trip in Germany

Treat the motorway transfer and the scenic road as separate planning questions. Choose the actual roadbook first, then check the authority responsible for each part of the journey.

Motorway reports describe the transfer

Autobahn GmbH publishes motorway traffic and works reports. These can affect the journey to the riding area; they do not establish access to every state, district or local road.

Do not infer local motorcycle permission

For a scenic road, identify the relevant local authority and inspect the actual dated notice and road signs. Absence from the motorway list is not evidence of unrestricted motorcycle access.

Budget a complete day from your start

Add the transfer, stops and return journey to the riding section. The day-budget tool makes those assumptions explicit instead of comparing only scenic-road kilometres.
