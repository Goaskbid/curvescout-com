Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Ballymena

    Ballymena sits in the middle of County Antrim on the River Braid, a brisk market town of nearly thirty thousand people that most riders meet as a roundabout on the way to somewhere prettier. That undersells it a little. The town grew rich on linen in the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, and the Scots-Irish settlers who came here left a dialect and a stubborn Presbyterian streak that you can still hear in the shops. Buses are the modern trade: the big coachbuilder on the edge of town ships its vehicles round the world.
