Why this ride

A 587 km round trip from Rotorua - roughly 11 h in the saddle. The stops in between: Second component, First component, Tongariro National Park and back through First component. It tops out around 1,050 m. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

The roads

Desert Road

69 km from Second component to First component, mostly on State Highway 1. Up about 1230 m to 1,639 m, then 330 m back down the far side. Take a break before the climb, not halfway up.

The stops

  1. 1. Rotorua

    Start here.

    Rotorua announces itself with a smell. The sulphur drifting from the geothermal fields has an eggy tang that takes a few minutes to get used to, and then you stop noticing. The town of about sixty-five thousand people sits on the shore of Lake Rotorua, inside an ancient volcanic caldera, and the ground beneath it steams, bubbles and spurts in a way that makes the place unlike anywhere else in the country.

    Read the complete Rotorua write-up.

  2. 2. Second component

    140 km in.

    The park was the first in New Zealand, created in 1887, when the paramount chief Te Heuheu Tūkino IV gifted the sacred summits to the Crown, in order to protect them from being carved up and sold. It is also a dual World Heritage Site, listed for both its natural and its cultural value, a rare distinction.

    Read the complete Second component write-up.

  3. 3. First component

    208 km in.

    Whakapapa is where Tongariro National Park lives most of the time. In winter the village is a ski resort, and in summer it is a trailhead for walkers.
