Why this ride

264 km from Schleswig to Schleswig, about 4 h 48 of riding. It goes by way of National Park Wadden Sea Niedersachsen and Kovirke Area 6. The coast is the point of it.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Kovirke Area 6

    The Danevirke is the longest ancient earthwork in northern Europe, a system of ramparts, ditches and walls that crossed the narrow neck of Jutland at the base of the peninsula. Over several centuries, from the early Middle Ages into the twelfth, the Danes built and rebuilt it, closing the land route between the Baltic and the North Sea. Kovirke is one of its branches, and the section here, near Fahrdorf, preserves a piece of the bank that you can still see rising in the grass and the trees.

  2. 2. National Park Wadden Sea Niedersachsen

    Here the Wadden Sea comes right up against the dyke. This is the coast of the Wurster Nordseeküste, between the Weser and Elbe estuaries, a country of low sea walls, drained farmland and wide skies, and the mudflats outside the dyke are part of the Lower Saxony Wadden Sea National Park. Together with the Dutch and Danish parts of the Wadden Sea, the area is a UNESCO World Heritage site, valued above all for the sheer scale of the tidal flats and the millions of birds that pass through on migration. When the tide goes out, it goes a very long way.
