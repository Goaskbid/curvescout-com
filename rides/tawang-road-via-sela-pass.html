Why this ride

From Tezpur it runs 326 km to Tawang; allow about 4 h 23 of riding. The stops in between: Bomdila, Sela Pass and Jaswant Garh. The highest point is about 4,400 m. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

Scenic road chapters

Bomdila → Sela Pass

The valley floor is behind you and the road starts working for its height at about 8.1%. Corners stack above one another where the ground allows nothing straighter. First and second gear more often than not. Look through each corner to the next one and let the engine do the holding.

Tawang Road via Sela Pass

Cold comes early up here and stays late. Ride it with something in reserve: the summit is not the end of the work, only the middle of it. The last of the trees go and the road runs out onto open ground. The summit sits at 4170 m, and what surrounds it is rock, weather and distance.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Tezpur

    Tezpur lies on the north bank of the Brahmaputra in Assam, and it is where a ride to Tawang really starts. The town has about fifty-eight thousand people, a river that is wide enough to look like a sea, and a gentle, slightly sleepy air. Its name means city of blood in Sanskrit legend, from the story of a battle between Krishna and the demon king Banasura, who is said to have ruled here. The ruins of old temples sit at Da Parbatia, and the Agnigarh hill park looks over the river.

  2. 2. Bomdila

    Bomdila is a hill town strung along a ridge in the far west of Arunachal Pradesh, at about 2,400 metres, and it is the traditional overnight stop on the long road to Tawang. The town is home to the Monpa people, who follow Tibetan Buddhism and have lived in these valleys for centuries, and you see it in the prayer flags, the prayer wheels and the monastery that sits above the main bazaar. The hills around are covered in orchards, and apples and kiwis are among the local products, sold at roadside stalls in the right season.

  3. 3. Sela Pass

    Sela Pass is the high point on the road to Tawang, at 4,170 metres, and it is the sort of place where the weather is more important than the map. The pass sits on a ridge in the eastern Himalaya in the Indian state of Arunachal Pradesh, between the West Kameng and Tawang districts, and it is the gateway to a country of high lakes, monasteries and army posts. In winter it is snowed under for weeks and the road can be closed, and even in summer it can be hit by snow, fog and sudden hail.

  4. 4. Jaswant Garh

    Jaswant Garh is a war memorial, but it is also a place with a legend, and the combination makes it one of the most talked-about stops on the road to Tawang. It stands in the high country just beyond the Sela Pass, near the Nuranang river, and commemorates Rifleman Jaswant Singh Rawat of the Garhwal Rifles, who is remembered for his part in the 1962 fighting with Chinese forces in this area. The battle at Nuranang is one of the best-known episodes of that war, and the Indian Army has kept the memory alive here in an unusual way.

  5. 5. Tawang

    Tawang is a town at 3,000 metres in the far west of Arunachal Pradesh, wedged between Bhutan and Tibet, and it is the destination that makes the long journey from Tezpur worthwhile. Above it, on a ridge, stands Tawang Monastery, the largest in India and one of the largest in the world, founded in the seventeenth century by Merak Lama Lodre Gyatso. It belongs to the Gelug school of Tibetan Buddhism, and the sixth Dalai Lama, Tsangyang Gyatso, is said to have been born near here. The Monpa people who live in the district are Buddhist, and the town feels more Tibetan than Indian in its prayer wheels and chanting.

Recorded leg-by-leg notes

These source notes may contain earlier estimates. They are not turn-by-turn directions or current measured distances.

Tezpur to Bomdila

Tezpur → Bomdila is 96 km of getting across. Ride it tidily and keep the enthusiasm for what comes after.

Bomdila to Sela Pass

Bomdila → Sela Pass climbs about 4170 m in 52 km. This is the part worth slowing down for - look through the bends and use the pull-offs.

Sela Pass to Jaswant Garh

Sela Pass → Jaswant Garh drops about 4170 m over 13 km. Brakes and eyes forward; the views are behind the corners, not in them.

Jaswant Garh to Tawang

Jaswant Garh → Tawang is 20 km of rhythm rather than effort. Let it flow and let the view breathe.
