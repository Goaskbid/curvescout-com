Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Drogheda

    Drogheda stands on the River Boyne, a short way from where the river meets the Irish Sea, and its position made it a frontier town for centuries. The old walled town, with its steep streets and the St Laurence's Gate, a medieval barbican, was one of the main ports of the Pale, the English-controlled district around Dublin. In 1649, Oliver Cromwell's army stormed the town, and a massacre of the garrison and civilians followed, a memory that still carries bitterness.
