  2. 2. Near Ambleside

    At 454 metres, the Kirkstone Pass is the highest pass in the Lake District that you can ride over, and the inn at the top, a squat stone building that has been serving travellers for centuries, claims to be one of the highest pubs in England. The pass gets its name from a large rock near the summit that looks a little like a church steeple, the kirk stone, and it is a natural stopping place where riders gather on summer weekends to admire the views and each other's bikes.

  3. 3. Near Penrith

    The northern shore of Ullswater, between Pooley Bridge and Glenridding, is one of the gentlest and most beautiful roads in the Lake District. The A592 runs along the water's edge, under old trees and past grassy shores, with the lake on one side and the wooded slopes of Gowbarrow Fell on the other, and at every bend there is a new view of the water and the fells at its southern end. Ullswater is the second-largest lake in the Lake District, long and winding, often called the most beautiful of them all.
