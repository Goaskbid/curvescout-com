Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Milan

    Milan does not try to charm you, and that is part of its charm. It is Italy's business capital, a city of fashion, finance and design that works hard and dresses well. At the centre stands the Duomo, a Gothic cathedral begun in 1386 and not finished for about five centuries, a forest of white marble spires and statues, and you can climb onto the roof among them. Beside it, the glass-roofed Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II is a nineteenth-century shopping arcade, and beyond it La Scala opera house.

    Read the complete Milan write-up.

  2. 2. Garda

    Garda is the town that gave the lake its name, and it sits on the eastern shore, on a gentle bay sheltered by the headland of Punta San Vigilio and the hill of the Rocca, where a castle once stood. It is worth saying straight away that if your ride is a western shore ride, you are on the opposite side here; the lake is long and narrow, and the car ferry between Torri del Benaco, just north of Garda, and Toscolano Maderno on the western shore is the quickest way to get across. The crossing itself, with the mountains rising on both sides, is a lovely break.
