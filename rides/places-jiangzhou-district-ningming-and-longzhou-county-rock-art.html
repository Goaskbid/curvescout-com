Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Jiangzhou District

    Jiangzhou is the district that holds the city of Chongzuo, in the far southwest of Guangxi, close to the Vietnamese border. The landscape is classic Guangxi karst, with steep, isolated limestone hills that jut from flat green plains like the backs of sleeping animals. Between them the Zuo River winds slowly through sugarcane fields, and the whole region is one of the largest sugar-producing areas in China. The climate is subtropical, warm and wet in summer, mild in winter, and often hazy with mist in the mornings.

  2. 2. Longhzou County rock art

    The great cliff painting here is known as Huashan, which means Flower Mountain, and it is part of the Zuojiang Huashan Rock Art Cultural Landscape, inscribed by UNESCO in 2016. It lies along the Ming River, a tributary of the Zuo, in the karst country of southwest Guangxi near Ningming. On a limestone cliff about two hundred meters wide and rising high above the water, thousands of red figures are painted in iron-rich pigment, showing people with raised arms, bent knees, swords and drums, and dogs among them. The shapes have lasted for roughly two thousand years, protected by an overhang and the dryness of the rock.
