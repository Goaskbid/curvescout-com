Why this ride

A 604 km one-way ride from near Ponderosa Park, about 10 h 46 of riding. The stops in between: Scenic stretch near Evergreen, near Fraser and near Kersey. near Perry Park is the high point. It tops out at about 3,250 m.

Scenic road chapters

Mount Blue Sky Scenic Byway

Expect company on a summer weekend. Early is better than fast. For about 130 km this is Mount Blue Sky Scenic Byway, a road with a name of its own and a reason for it.

    The ride's name points to Pikes Peak, which is far to the south; the country here is Weld County, a vast plain of wheat, cattle and oil derricks stretching away north-east of Denver. Weld County is among the largest agricultural counties in the United States, and the roads here carry grain trucks, tanker lorries and the occasional horse trailer.
