Why this ride

A 514 km one-way ride from near Ponderosa Park, about 9 h 24 of riding. The stops in between: Scenic stretch near Evergreen, near Fraser and near Kersey. near Perry Park is the high point. It tops out at about 3,250 m.

Mount Blue Sky Scenic Byway

Expect company on a summer weekend. Early is better than fast. For 112 km this is Mount Blue Sky Scenic Byway, a road with a name of its own and a reason for it.
