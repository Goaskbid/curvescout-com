Why this ride

curated scenic road. volcano, caldera, high altitude. 33 km from La Orotava to Vilaflor, about 36 min of riding. The stops in between: Realejo Alto, La Guancha and Guía de Isora. The highest point is about 1,150 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. La Orotava

    La Orotava is a town of balconies. The old streets of this hillside town on the north side of Tenerife are lined with seventeenth- and eighteenth-century houses, their wooden balconies carved from the dark local pine and often draped in flowers, and the best known, the Casa de los Balcones, shows a courtyard full of carved timber and plants. The cobbled streets are steep, so wear shoes you can walk in, and the church of La Concepción, with its twin baroque towers, dominates the skyline. Every June, for Corpus Christi, the streets are covered with intricate carpets made of coloured sand and flower petals, and the whole town turns out to see them.

  5. 5. Vilaflor

    Vilaflor lies on the southern slope of Tenerife at about 1,400 metres, and it is generally described as the highest village in Spain. The air up here is cool and thin, and the landscape is a mix of terraced fields, almond and pear trees, and the dark, scented forest of Canary pine that cloaks the mountain above. Below, the land falls away in long folds to a sea that is often hidden by a layer of cloud. It is a small, whitewashed place of just under two thousand people, with a church, a plaza and a long reputation for garlic.
