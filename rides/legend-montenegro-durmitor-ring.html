Why this ride

curated scenic road. limestone peaks, canyons, high plateau. 65 km from Žabljak to Žabljak, about 1 h 11 of riding. The highest point is about 1,900 m. Sedlo Pass is subject to seasonal closure - check it is open before you set off. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Žabljak

    Žabljak claims to be the highest town in the Balkans, at about 1,450 metres, and it sits on a plateau among the peaks of Durmitor, a national park and UNESCO World Heritage site in northern Montenegro. The town is small, a place of low houses and mountain lodges, and it exists mostly for hikers, skiers and the occasional motorcyclist who has made the effort to come this far. Pines and spruce close in around it, the nights are cool even in summer, and the snow comes early.

  3. 3. Near Plužine

    Plužine is the small hub of the Piva region in north-western Montenegro, and its setting is the reason anyone talks about it. The town stands above a huge artificial lake, Pivsko Jezero, created in the 1970s when the Mratinje dam was built across the Piva canyon. The dam is a concrete arch more than 200 metres high, among the tallest in Europe when it was finished, and the water behind it filled one of the deepest, narrowest gorges in the Balkans. Old Plužine and the monastery of Piva were moved higher up before the flooding, and the monastery, with its painted interior, is still the area's main cultural treasure.
