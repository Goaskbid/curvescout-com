Why this ride

curated scenic road. Greater Caucasus, canyon, high mountain village. 162 km from Quba to the finish, about 5 h of riding. It tops out around 2,600 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The roads

Azerbaijan Khinaliq Road

Azerbaijan Khinaliq Road point 3 is 106 km from Azerbaijan Khinaliq Road point 2 on Fətəli Xan Xoyski küç. Over the top at 2,121 m - roughly 2060 m up and 1840 m down. Above 2,000 m the weather turns fast; have a layer and a plan B.
