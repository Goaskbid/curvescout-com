Why this ride

Rhode Island's strongest uninterrupted coastal run. The road: US-1A and RI-108. Distance and riding time are estimates from the checkpoints until the road is measured on this page.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Narragansett

    Narragansett is where Rhode Island's south coast stops being a drive and starts being a habit. The town sits on a knuckle of land between Narragansett Bay and the open Atlantic, and the ocean here is the real thing: long gray rollers that arrive from Spain with nothing in between. Ocean Road, Route 1A, hugs the shoreline past the seawall, Scarborough Beach and a string of big shingled summer houses, and on a calm morning it is as pleasant a start to a coast ride as New England offers.

  3. 3. Westerly

    Westerly is the last town in Rhode Island, and it knows it. The Pawcatuck River forms the state line here, and the neighboring village of Pawcatuck, Connecticut, sits across a bridge so close that you can cross the border for a coffee and be back before it cools. For anyone arriving along the coast from Narragansett, this is a natural place to end a ride: a proper small town with a working center, not a resort.
