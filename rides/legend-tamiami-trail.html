Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Miami

    The Tamiami Trail was named for what it joined, Tampa and Miami, and it took thirteen years and an astonishing amount of dynamite to build. Begun in 1915 and opened in 1928, US 41 cuts straight west across the Everglades, one of the largest wetlands in North America, and it changed Florida by linking the Atlantic and Gulf coasts, though it also interrupted the flow of water across the Everglades in a way that scientists are still trying to repair. The road starts in downtown Miami as Southwest 8th Street, known to locals as Calle Ocho, the heart of Little Havana.

  4. 4. Naples

    Naples is the end of the Tamiami Trail on the Gulf of Mexico, and after a long ride across the wet flat heart of southern Florida, it can feel almost unreal. The town is a polished, prosperous resort of about twenty-one thousand people with white sand beaches, a long wooden pier, palm-lined avenues, golf courses and some of the most expensive real estate in the state. Retirees, snowbirds and yachts are everywhere, and the air smells of salt and suntan lotion.
