Why this ride

The stops in between: Segment Río de La Sal - Pampa del Inca, Segment Pampa del Inca-Finca de Chañaral and back through Segment Río de La Sal - Pampa del Inca. The highest point is about 3,200 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Segment Portal del Inca - Río de La Sal

    The name Portal del Inca suggests a gate, and in a way it is one: a threshold on the road that the Incas drove south across the desert from the Andean heartland. Qhapaq Ñan is the name of the whole system, a network of tracks that stretched for thousands of kilometres and carried armies, tax goods and runners with messages. In the Atacama it was built simply, since the ground was hard and dry. Stones were cleared and lined up to mark the path, and the tambos, or roadside stations, stood at intervals with stores of food and water for officials.

  2. 2. Segment Río de La Sal - Pampa del Inca

    Out here the Incas walked a road through country that most of us would call empty, and they did it without wheels, horses or a single petrol station. The stretch between the Río de la Sal and the Pampa del Inca is one of the Chilean pieces of the Qhapaq Ñan, the great Andean road network that tied Cusco to the far corners of the empire and was listed by UNESCO across six countries in 2014. In the Atacama region it shows up as a faint, dead-straight trace across gravel plains, sometimes edged with stones, sometimes marked by the low ruins of a tambo where runners and caravans once rested.

  3. 3. Segment Pampa del Inca-Finca de Chañaral

    The Atacama desert is the driest place on earth outside the poles, and the Inca crossed it on foot. The Qhapaq Ñan, the great Inca road system, included a southern branch that ran from Cusco down into what is now northern Chile, and this segment between Pampa del Inca and the farm of Chañaral lies in the region of Atacama, close to the town of Diego de Almagro. It is part of the UNESCO listing of the Qhapaq Ñan in 2014.
