Why this ride

From Shushary it runs 132 km to Shushary; allow about 2 h 28 of riding. It goes by way of P.K.Alexandrov's Datcha, The Maximov's Datcha, Zelenogorsky and back through The Maximov's Datcha. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The roads

Saint Petersburg Imperial Shore

P.K.Alexandrov's Datcha to The Maximov's Datcha: 19 km on Санкт-Петербургское шоссе. An easy profile, never above 26 m. Watch for sand and spray near the water.

The stops

  1. 1. Pulkovskaya Observatory

    Start here.

    On the Pulkovo heights, the low ridge south of St Petersburg, an observatory has been watching the sky since 1839. It was founded by Friedrich Georg Wilhelm von Struve, the German-born astronomer who became its first director, and it quickly became one of the leading observatories in the world, with the largest refracting telescope of its day. The Pulkovo meridian, running through the observatory's main building, served as the reference for Russian maps for many years. Struve also led the measurement of the Struve Geodetic Arc, a chain of survey triangulations stretching from the Arctic Ocean to the Black Sea, which helped determine the size and shape of the earth and is now a UNESCO site.

    Read the complete Pulkovskaya Observatory write-up.

  2. 2. P.K.Alexandrov's Datcha

    25 km in.

    Strelna lies at the beginning of the road to Peterhof, on the southern shore of the Gulf of Finland, and its main feature is the Konstantinovsky Palace, begun in the reign of Peter the Great and named for a later grand duke, Konstantin. Like Peterhof, it was heavily damaged in the Second World War and later rebuilt, and in the early 2000s it was restored for the three-hundredth anniversary of Saint Petersburg. The park, with its canals and ponds, runs down toward the water, and the view across the gulf is wide and grey.

    Read the complete P.K.Alexandrov's Datcha write-up.

  3. 3. The Maximov's Datcha

    45 km in.

    The south shore of the Gulf of Finland west of St Petersburg is a strip of dachas, palaces and old military ground, and the place marked here as Maximov's Datcha sits within it, a couple of kilometres from the town of Lomonosov.

    Read the complete The Maximov's Datcha write-up.

  4. 4. Zelenogorsky

    65 km in. The ride passes about 6 km from it.

    The northern shore of the Gulf of Finland, west of Saint Petersburg, was for centuries the summer playground of the capital, and Zelenogorsk, which the Finns called Terijoki, was the best known of its resort towns.

    Read the complete Zelenogorsky write-up.
