Why this ride

568 km round from Berlin - plan on about 10 h 19 of riding. It goes by way of Burg bei Magdeburg and Elbe. Long stretches are under trees.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Berlin

    The tower, finished in 1969 and 368 metres high, is the tallest structure in Germany and can be seen from nearly everywhere in the city. It was built by the East German government as a showpiece of socialist engineering, and when the sun hits its steel sphere, it throws a cross of light that Berliners cheekily called the Pope's revenge.

    Read the complete Berlin write-up.

  2. 2. Burg bei Magdeburg

    Burg likes to call itself the town of towers, and as you approach across the flat Elbe plain, you can see why: medieval church spires and the remnants of old fortifications rise above the rooftops. It is a market town of some twenty-five thousand people, east of Magdeburg, and for most riders on the Prignitz ride it is simply a convenient place to refuel. But it has a few surprises.

    Read the complete Burg bei Magdeburg write-up.

  3. 3. Elbe

    Despite the name, there is no great river here. This Elbe is a village in the rolling farmland of southeastern Lower Saxony, between Salzgitter and Hildesheim, and the river that shares its name flows more than a hundred kilometres to the east. It is a quiet place of brick farmhouses, a church, fields of sugar beet, wheat and rapeseed, and gentle hills, close to the small towns of Baddeckenstedt and Holle in the valley of the Innerste.

    Read the complete Elbe write-up.
