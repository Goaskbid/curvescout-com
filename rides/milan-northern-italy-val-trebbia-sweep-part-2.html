Why this ride

A 223 km one-way ride from near Giubiasco, about 4 h 07 of riding. The highest point is about 1,300 m. It crosses from Switzerland into Italy - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Food stop near Giubiasco

    Giubiasco lies at the southern edge of Bellinzona, the capital of the Swiss canton of Ticino, where the valley of the Ticino river opens towards the Magadino plain and Lake Maggiore. For centuries, this was the gateway to the Alpine passes, the Gotthard, San Bernardino and Lukmanier, and whoever controlled Bellinzona controlled the trade routes between Italy and northern Europe.

  3. 3. Near Binasco

    Leave Milan to the south and within twenty minutes the city gives way to rice. The Parco Agricolo Sud Milano protects a wide belt of farmland around the city, and between Binasco, Zibido San Giacomo and Noviglio the fields are flooded each spring, turning the plain into a vast mirror of sky, laced with canals and lines of poplars. The locals call it the chequered sea.
