Why this ride

Out of Fort Collins, one way: 130 km, roughly 2 h 06 in the saddle. On the way it passes Poudre Canyon and Cameron Pass. The highest point is about 3,150 m. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

Scenic road chapters

Cache la Poudre North Park Scenic Byway

The valley floor is behind you and the road starts working for its height at about 9.5%. Corners stack above one another where the ground allows nothing straighter — first and second gear more often than not. Look through each corner to the next one and let the engine do the holding.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Fort Collins

    Fort Collins is a university town of about a hundred and sixty thousand people at the foot of the Rockies in northern Colorado, with Colorado State University at its heart and a well-preserved Old Town of brick buildings, trees and fountains. It is a famously bicycle-friendly place, and also a famous place for beer: New Belgium, Odell and dozens of smaller breweries started here, and the craft beer scene is serious. It has a relaxed, outdoorsy feel, and a motorcycle is very much at home.

  2. 2. Poudre Canyon

    The Cache la Poudre is the only river in Colorado with National Wild and Scenic status, and Highway 14 spends a good thirty miles of its length following the water up into the mountains. The name is French, from the early trappers who, according to the story, buried a stock of gunpowder somewhere along its banks in the 1820s after a blizzard forced them to lighten their loads, and called the place the hiding place of the powder.

  3. 3. Cameron Pass

    The top of Cameron Pass is a quiet, broad saddle at 3,132 metres between the Never Summer Mountains to the south and the Medicine Bow Mountains to the north, with spruce and fir all around and snow lingering in the shaded hollows well into summer. It is a gentle sort of summit, and the sign that marks it seems almost shy. A few hundred metres away, the land begins to fall westward into North Park, a vast open basin that feels as though it belongs to another state.

  4. 4. Walden

    Walden sits in the middle of North Park, an enormous high basin in northern Colorado, ringed by mountains, and it is one of the most remote little county seats in the state. About six hundred people live here, at around eight thousand one hundred feet, in a village of low buildings and wide streets, with sagebrush and hay meadows stretching away on every side. The Medicine Bow Mountains to the north and the Never Summer range to the south frame the view, and the wind is a constant companion.

Recorded leg-by-leg notes

These source notes may contain earlier estimates. They are not turn-by-turn directions or current measured distances.

Fort Collins to Poudre Canyon

Fort Collins → Poudre Canyon is 61 km of getting across. Ride it tidily and keep the enthusiasm for what comes after.

Poudre Canyon to Cameron Pass

Poudre Canyon → Cameron Pass climbs about 3132 m in 33 km. This is the part worth slowing down for - look through the bends and use the pull-offs.

Cameron Pass to Walden

Cameron Pass → Walden drops about 3132 m over 51 km. Brakes and eyes forward; the views are behind the corners, not in them.
