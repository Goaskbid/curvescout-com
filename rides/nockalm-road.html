Why this ride

Out of Thomatal, one way: 41 km, roughly 42 min in the saddle. It goes by way of Eisentalhohe and Schiestelscharte. The highest point is about 2,050 m. Eisentalhohe and Schiestelscharte are open in summer only - check it is open before you set off. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

Scenic road chapters

Nockalm Road

Cold comes early up here and stays late. Ride it with something in reserve: the summit is not the end of the work, only the middle of it. The last of the trees go and the road runs out onto open ground. The summit sits at 2042 m, and what surrounds it is rock, weather and distance.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Innerkrems

    Innerkrems is the top end of the Krems valley, a scatter of farms, hotels and ski lifts at around fifteen hundred metres in the Nockberge, the rounded green mountains of western Carinthia. Unlike the sharp limestone of the Dolomites or the glaciated peaks of the Hohe Tauern, the Nocks are soft, grassy domes, shaped by ice and worn smooth, and they roll away in every direction like waves frozen in place. They are protected as a biosphere reserve, a landscape of alpine pasture, dwarf pine, lakes and stone huts that is among the gentlest in the Alps.

  2. 2. Eisentalhohe

    At 2,042 metres, the Eisentalhöhe is the highest point on the Nockalm Road, and it earns the title in a gentle way. There are no needle peaks here, no glaciers hanging on a cliff. The Nockberge are rounded, grassy mountains, the sort that look like sleeping animals under a blanket, and the road crosses them in a succession of curves that feels almost private, as if the mountains had been designed for riding.

  3. 3. Schiestelscharte

    A Scharte is a notch in a ridge, and the Schiestelscharte, at about 2,024 metres, is one of the notches the Nockalm Road threads through on its way across the Nockberge. It sits only a little lower than the Eisentalhöhe, a few kilometres away, and the two are often grouped together, but the character is different: the Schiestelscharte feels more exposed, with the hillside opening out suddenly and the road swinging round the shoulder of a ridge.

  4. 4. Ebene Reichenau

    Ebene Reichenau lies in the Gurk valley of Carinthia, at the lower gate of the Nockalm Road, and it is where the toll booth sits across the road, with a car park and a cafe beside it. The village is small, with wooden farmhouses and a church spire, and the fresh air, the sound of a stream and the smell of mown hay make it feel far from anywhere. It lies at about eleven hundred metres, so the climb to the top of the Nocks, which begins right behind the village, has about a thousand metres of height to gain.

Recorded leg-by-leg notes

These source notes may contain earlier estimates. They are not turn-by-turn directions or current measured distances.

Innerkrems to Eisentalhohe

Innerkrems → Eisentalhohe climbs about 2042 m in 7 km. This is the part worth slowing down for - look through the bends and use the pull-offs.

Eisentalhohe to Schiestelscharte

Eisentalhohe → Schiestelscharte is 3 km of rhythm rather than effort. Let it flow and let the view breathe.

Schiestelscharte to Ebene Reichenau

Schiestelscharte → Ebene Reichenau drops about 2024 m over 14 km. Brakes and eyes forward; the views are behind the corners, not in them.
