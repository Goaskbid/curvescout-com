Why this ride

From Hanau am Main it runs 245 km to Marburg an der Lahn; allow about 3 h 29 of riding. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Hanau

    Hanau sits where the Kinzig flows into the Main, east of Frankfurt, and it is the starting point of the German Fairy Tale Route because it is the birthplace of the Brothers Grimm. Jacob was born here in 1785 and Wilhelm a year later, and a large bronze monument of the two seated figures stands in front of the town hall on the market square. Hanau has about eighty-nine thousand people and was heavily damaged in the Second World War, so it is not a pretty old town, but it has a pleasant riverside and a long tradition of goldsmithing and jewellery.

  2. 2. Steinau an der Strasse

    The Brothers Grimm spent part of their childhood here, and the town has not forgotten it. Jacob and Wilhelm lived in Steinau from 1791 to 1796, when their father was the district magistrate, and they played in the courtyard of the Amtshaus, a handsome half-timbered building that is now a museum. The Fairy Tale Route runs through the town, and the old market square has a fountain and a plaque dedicated to them.

  3. 3. Marburg

    Marburg climbs a steep hillside above the Lahn river, with a castle at the top, a Gothic church on the slope and a student at nearly every corner. It is a university town in the real sense: the university was founded in 1527 as the first Protestant university in the world, and roughly a quarter of the population is made up of students. That gives the narrow lanes of the old town an energy that few places in Hesse can match, with bookshops, cafes and bars crowded into every nook.
