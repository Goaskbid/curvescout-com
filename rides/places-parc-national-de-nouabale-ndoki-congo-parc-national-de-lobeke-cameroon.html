Why this ride

It goes by way of Parc National de Dzanga-Ndoki - CAR, Parc National de Lobeké - Cameroon and back through Parc National de Dzanga-Ndoki - CAR. It crosses from Central African Republic into Cameroon - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Parc National de Nouabalé-Ndoki - Congo

    In the far north of the Republic of Congo, the forest is so big and so unbroken that it can feel like another planet. Nouabalé-Ndoki National Park covers more than four thousand square kilometers of lowland rainforest, swamp and river, and it is part of a conservation landscape that continues into Cameroon and the Central African Republic. Together with Lobéké and Dzanga-Sangha, it forms the Sangha Trinational, which UNESCO listed in 2012. This is one of the last large tracts of intact forest in the Congo Basin, and its remoteness has kept it largely free of the pressures found elsewhere.

  2. 2. Parc National de Dzanga-Ndoki - CAR

    In the extreme south-west corner of the Central African Republic, where the forest runs on into Cameroon and Congo, the Sangha river sweeps past a national park that protects one of the last great blocks of Congo Basin rainforest. Dzanga-Ndoki is part of the Sangha Trinational, a World Heritage area shared by three countries, and it is one of the few places on Earth where you can sit in the forest and watch elephants and gorillas in the open.
