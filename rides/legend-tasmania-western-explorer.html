Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Smithton

    Smithton is the main town of Tasmania's far northwest, a farming and timber service town at the mouth of the Duck River, in a corner of the island known as Circular Head. With about four thousand people it is the last place of any size before the wild country west and south, and the land around it is gently rolling dairy and beef pasture, very green and often very wet. The air that blows in here has crossed the Southern Ocean with nothing in its way, and the Cape Grim station, not far off, measures it as some of the cleanest on the planet.
