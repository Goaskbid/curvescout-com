  1. 1. Gdańsk

    Gdańsk is a port that has been rich, ruined and rebuilt more than once, and the city wears all three eras at once. The old Hanseatic merchants of the Middle Ages grew fat on grain, timber and amber, and their tall, narrow merchant houses still line the Long Market, where the Neptune fountain gazes at the Gothic town hall. Almost everything in the old centre was flattened in 1945 and rebuilt with such care, from old paintings and plans, that the facades look centuries old. Along the Motława river the great wooden medieval crane still straddles the quay.
