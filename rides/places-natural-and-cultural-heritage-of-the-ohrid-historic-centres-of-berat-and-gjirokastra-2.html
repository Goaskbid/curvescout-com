Why this ride

A 377 km round trip from Udënisht - roughly 6 h 30 in the saddle. It goes by way of The Historic Center of Berat, Historic Centres of Berat and Gjirokastra and back through The Historic Center of Berat. The highest point is about 1,100 m. It links historic places along the way.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Natural and Cultural Heritage of the Ohrid

    Lake Ohrid is one of the oldest lakes in Europe, perhaps a million years old or more, a deep, clear basin shared between Albania and North Macedonia, and this point lies on the Albanian side, close to Pogradec. The lake is so old and so isolated that it has developed its own species, with dozens of fish, snails and crustaceans found nowhere else. The most famous is the Ohrid trout, locally known as koran, which is protected and appears on menus with some controversy. UNESCO listed the lake and the town of Ohrid together for their natural and cultural heritage, and the Albanian shore is a part of the same landscape.

  2. 2. The Historic Center of Berat

    Berat is called the town of a thousand windows, and one look across the Osum river explains why. The Ottoman-era houses of the Mangalem quarter stack up the slope in white tiers, their upper floors set back so that each window seems to look over the next roof. On the opposite bank the Gorica quarter faces them, linked by a graceful stone bridge, and above them both the castle walls crown a rock, with the long, bare ridge of Mount Tomorr beyond.

  3. 3. Historic Centres of Berat and Gjirokastra

    Gjirokastër is built of stone, and it means that literally. The old houses climbing the slopes below the castle are made of grey limestone, roofed with heavy slabs of the same stone, and tall and narrow like small fortresses, with the lower floors used for storage and cisterns and the living quarters above. The town grew under Ottoman rule, and its architecture, preserved as a museum town in the communist era, earned it a place on the UNESCO list in 2005, with Berat added in 2008. The castle, one of the largest in the Balkans, holds a collection of artillery, a captured American aircraft from the Cold War, and a prison with a grim history.
