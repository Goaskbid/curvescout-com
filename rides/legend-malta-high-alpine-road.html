Why this ride

official scenic toll road. waterfalls, dam, high alpine. From Gmünd it runs 43 km to Muhr; allow about 1 h of riding. It tops out around 1,850 m. The line favours views over speed.

The roads

Malta High Alpine Road

It is 13 km from Gmünd to Near Gmünd. You climb most of the way: 810 m, up to 1,597 m. Top up fuel at the bottom - pumps thin out on the way up.
