Why this ride

Out of Los Angeles and back again: 265 km, roughly 4 h 49 in the saddle. The highest point is about 1,200 m.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Los Angeles

    Route 66 does not end in a blaze of glory. After more than three thousand kilometres from Chicago, the old highway frays into the boulevards of Los Angeles, and its last stretch is a patchwork of Foothill Boulevard, Colorado Boulevard and Santa Monica Boulevard, ending officially at the pier in Santa Monica, with the Pacific beyond. The point on the map here is the old centre, where the city began in 1781 as a Spanish pueblo, and a few blocks of adobe buildings and a Mexican market street, Olvera Street, are still standing, near the grand Spanish-style Union Station, which opened in 1939.

    Read the complete Los Angeles write-up.

  2. 2. Ojai

    At sunset, the Topatopa Mountains above the Ojai Valley glow pink for a few minutes, a phenomenon locals call the pink moment, and people pause in the streets to watch. It is that kind of town: small, artsy, a little spiritual and very proud of its valley, tucked into the mountains about an hour and a half north-west of Los Angeles. The philosopher Jiddu Krishnamurti lived and taught here for decades, and the town still attracts seekers, artists and people looking for a slower pace of life.
