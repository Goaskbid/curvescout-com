Why this ride

Scenery first, traffic last, with checkpoints that make sense on a bike. Out of Saint-Jean-de-Bournay and back again: 600 km, roughly 12 h 30 in the saddle. The stops in between: near Cognin, Scenic stretch in Buellas and Saint-Yan. near Saint-Martin-du-Frêne is the high point. It tops out at about 1,300 m. Long stretches are under trees.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Fuel stop near Saint-Jean-de-Bournay

    There is a particular kind of French country town that exists mainly to serve the farms around it, and Saint-Jean-de-Bournay is a good example: a square with plane trees, a church, a café where the same men have been drinking their morning coffee for decades, a pharmacy, a bakery and a weekly market. It sits in a shallow valley in the hills of the Bas-Dauphiné, about twenty-five kilometres east of Vienne, and it does not try to be anything else.

  4. 4. Scenic stretch in Buellas

    Buellas is a small village in the Bresse, just west of Bourg-en-Bresse in the Ain department. The Bresse is a flat, fertile region of farms, hedgerows, ponds and woods, known above all for its chickens: the Bresse chicken, with its blue feet, white plumage and red comb, matching the colours of the French flag, is the only poultry in France with its own protected designation of origin. Farmers here raise them in open pastures, and they are prized by chefs across the country.

  5. 5. Near Saint-Yan, on the way back

    White cattle, hedges and Romanesque bell towers: that is the Brionnais, the corner of southern Burgundy where the Loire bends towards Digoin. This stop lies on the plain near Saint-Yan, a village best known in France for its airfield, where the national civil aviation school trains its pilots. Small aircraft circle overhead most days, and the open grassland around the runway gives the place an unusually wide sky.

  6. 6. Photo stop near Noirétable

    Noirétable is a small town in the Monts du Forez, close to the border between the Loire and Puy-de-Dôme departments, on the old route between Lyon and Clermont-Ferrand. Its name comes from the dark forests of the Bois Noirs that cover the surrounding hills, and the town has the feel of a mountain market town, with granite houses, a church and a cool, fresh climate even in summer.

  7. 7. Near Miribel

    East of Lyon, the Rhône splits into channels and lakes on its way into the city, and the vast Grand Parc de Miribel-Jonage has been made out of that watery landscape. This finish lies just above it, on the edge of the Côtière, the steep slope that separates the river plain from the Dombes plateau, between Thil, Beynost and Saint-Maurice-de-Beynost.

  8. 8. Back to Loire Gorges

    This corner of the Isère département lies between Lyon and Grenoble, in a broad, gentle valley called the Bièvre, the work of glaciers that once poured out of the Alps and left a plain of gravel and clay. Saint-Jean-de-Bournay, Beaurepaire and Eyzin-Pinet are market villages of two to four thousand people, with stone churches, farmhouses and fields of maize, orchards and pasture. It is a rural, unhurried landscape, rarely visited by outsiders, and the roads are quiet.

Recorded leg-by-leg notes

These source notes may contain earlier estimates. They are not turn-by-turn directions or current measured distances.

Food stop near Cognin to High point near Saint-Martin-du-Frêne

Food stop near Cognin → High point near Saint-Martin-du-Frêne is the logistics leg. Watch junctions, villages and speed changes, then let the roadbook do its job: move you out of the clutter without spending your best attention too early.

High point near Saint-Martin-du-Frêne to Scenic stretch in Buellas

Treat High point near Saint-Martin-du-Frêne → Scenic stretch in Buellas as bend craft. Brake early, look high through the turn, and keep throttle changes gentle. It is more satisfying to link ten clean bends than rescue one silly one.

Scenic stretch in Buellas to Near Saint-Yan, on the way back

Scenic stretch in Buellas → Near Saint-Yan, on the way back is the reward section in Lyon. Look through the bends, use legal pull-offs for photos and keep a little margin for tractors, cyclists and damp shade. The best ride here is smooth, not noisy.

Photo stop near Noirétable to Near Miribel

Photo stop near Noirétable → Near Miribel is shade riding: beautiful, cooler and sometimes slick. Expect damp patches, leaves, gravel and wildlife where the trees meet the road.
