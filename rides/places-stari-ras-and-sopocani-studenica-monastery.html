Why this ride

From Novi Pazar it runs 303 km to Novi Pazar; allow about 5 h 13 of riding. The stops in between: Studenica Monastery, Monastery of Djurdjevi Stupovi, back through Monastery of Djurdjevi Stupovi and back through Studenica Monastery. The highest point is about 1,150 m. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Stari Ras and Sopoćani

    In the hills of southwestern Serbia, near the town of Novi Pazar, lie the ruins of Ras, the first capital of the medieval Serbian state, and the monastery of Sopoćani, which holds some of the finest Byzantine painting in Europe. Stefan Nemanja, founder of the dynasty that ruled for two centuries, was born in this region, and the monuments here were the heart of his realm. Ras was a fortress on a hill above the river, and a short distance away the little church of St Peter, one of the oldest in Serbia, stands on a mound of an earlier cemetery with a round, early plan that suggests Byzantine and local roots.

  2. 2. Studenica Monastery

    The monastery of Studenica lies in a green gorge of the Golija mountains in central Serbia, where a clear river runs down through beech and oak woods toward the Ibar. Stefan Nemanja, the founder of the medieval Serbian state, began building it in the 1190s, and it became the model for all the monasteries that followed. Nemanja himself abdicated in 1196 and later became a monk, dying on Mount Athos in 1199. His son, Saint Sava, brought his father's remains back here, and they rest in the church today.

  3. 3. Monastery of Djurdjevi Stupovi

    Đurđevi stupovi, the Pillars of Saint George, crowns a hill above Novi Pazar, a small church of Saint George with a dome and towers that give the monastery its name. Stefan Nemanja, the founder of the Serbian dynasty that dominated the Balkans for two centuries, built it in the later twelfth century, and it was one of the first great foundations of his rule. The painting inside is Byzantine in style, and the cluster of dome and towers on the hilltop makes a strong silhouette.
