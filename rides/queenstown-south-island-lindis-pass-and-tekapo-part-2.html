Why this ride

On the way it passes Queenstown / South Island, near Wanaka and Queenstown. The highest point is about 1,050 m. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Lindis Pass and Tekapo photo pull-off

    The nearest way in is a walking track, the Hollyford, which runs for days through the valley to the sea at Martins Bay, and the nearest roads stop well short of it. The ride is named for the Lindis Pass and Tekapo, which lie a long way east, so take this stop as a nod to the wilderness behind the road.

  2. 2. Queenstown / South Island return

    Omarama sits in the Waitaki basin, a wide, high, dry valley in the lee of the Southern Alps, and the land around it has the stripped look of the South Island's high country, with tawny tussock slopes, braided rivers and a huge sky. Omarama is best known to pilots, since the wind that comes over the mountains makes it one of the best gliding sites in the world.

  4. 4. Near Queenstown

    South of Queenstown, the road to Kingston follows the eastern shore of Lake Wakatipu, squeezed between deep, cold water and the jagged wall of the Remarkables. In winter they are white to their bases; in summer the bare rock glows gold and grey in the evening light.
