Why this ride

From Castell de Castells it runs 69 km to Castell de Castells; allow about 1 h 11 of riding. On the way it passes Port de Confrides- Abric I, Coves de la Vila and back through Port de Confrides- Abric I. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Pla de Petracos- Abric VII

    The first impression of Petracos is of tilted rock. Limestone strata lie across the plateau like a book dropped on its side, with caves and ledges under the edges, and the shelters tucked beneath them hold paintings that have lasted thousands of years because the overhang keeps the rain off. The plateau is a short distance above Castell de Castells, which gives you a village for coffee and a starting point for exploring the valley.

  2. 2. Port de Confrides- Abric I

    Inland from Benidorm, barely half an hour from the high-rise towers and the beach crowds, the mountains of Alicante rise steeply and the whole mood changes. The Port de Confrides is the saddle on the CV-70 between the Guadalest valley and the upland basin of Confrides and Benasau, with the long limestone wall of the Sierra de Aitana to the south. Aitana, at 1,558 metres, is the highest peak in the province, and on a clear winter morning you can see the sea glittering behind you as you climb.

  3. 3. Coves de la Vila

    Planes sits in a cleft of the green hills above the Serpis river, in the Comtat, and the village of Beniarrés a few kilometres away is known for its reservoir, which fills the valley floor with a long, quiet sheet of water. The Coves de la Vila are a set of rock shelters in the cliffs above the village, and they belong to the Mediterranean Basin rock art that UNESCO listed in 1998. The paintings are in the Levantine style, red figures of hunters, goats and deer, and the shelters look out over the valley, giving you the same view as the people who painted them.
