Why this ride

Out of London and back again: 598 km, roughly 10 h 52 in the saddle. It goes by way of Fairford and Brecon. Long stretches are under trees.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. London

    London is the one place on the map where the best advice is to leave the bike at the edge. The centre around Charing Cross and Trafalgar Square is a maze of one-way streets, bus lanes, taxis and tourists, and parking is limited and expensive. The city also has a congestion charge and a low-emission zone, whose rules for motorcycles have changed over the years, so check the current conditions for your own machine before you ride in. For a start and finish, a quiet side street or a secure garage on the outskirts is a better plan.

    Read the complete London write-up.

  2. 2. Fairford

    Fairford is a small, quiet town on the southern edge of the Cotswolds, built of honey-coloured stone along the River Coln, and it hides one of the great treasures of English medieval art. The parish church of St Mary has the only complete set of medieval stained glass windows to survive in any parish church in England: twenty-eight windows, made around 1500, telling the story of the Christian faith from the Creation to the Last Judgement. How they survived the iconoclasm of the Reformation and the Civil War is a mystery, but they did, and they are well worth twenty minutes of your time.

    Read the complete Fairford write-up.

  3. 3. Brecon

    Brecon is a small market town at the meeting of the Usk and the Honddu, in the shadow of the highest mountains in southern Britain. To the south rise the Brecon Beacons, now officially known by their Welsh name, Bannau Brycheiniog: a range of grassy, flat-topped sandstone summits with steep north-facing scarps, culminating in Pen y Fan, 886 metres high. On a clear day, the view of the mountains from the town is magnificent; on a wet day, which is often, they disappear into the clouds.

    Read the complete Brecon write-up.

    Return visit. Read the place description above.
