Why this ride

A 323 km round trip from Shetpe - roughly 5 h 34 in the saddle. On the way it passes Karaman-Ata, Shakpak-Ata and back through Karaman-Ata. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Rock Mosques and Associated Sacred Sites o

    The Mangystau peninsula in western Kazakhstan looks like the surface of another planet: chalk cliffs, red canyons, salt flats, and a plateau, the Ustyurt, that stretches away to a horizon without a tree. In this empty land, where the Caspian Sea lies far to the west and the nearest big town is Aktau on the coast, a number of holy sites are cut into the soft limestone, the so-called rock mosques. These are underground or cave-like sanctuaries, some hollowed out centuries ago, associated with Sufi teachers and with the ancestors of the local Kazakh clans.

  2. 2. Karaman-Ata

    Mangystau is the bleak, brilliant desert peninsula on the eastern shore of the Caspian Sea, in the far west of Kazakhstan. It looks like the surface of another planet, with chalk cliffs, canyons, salt flats, strange rock formations and fossil seabeds from the days when this was an ocean. For centuries it has also been a land of Sufi pilgrimage, and its network of underground mosques, cut into the soft limestone, is the reason for the UNESCO listing that includes this site. Karaman-Ata is one of the sacred sites that mark the memory of a holy man, in a quiet spot among low cliffs, reached by a path and accompanied by a small cemetery and shrine.

  3. 3. Shakpak-Ata

    Shakpak-Ata is another of the holy places of the Mangystau region, a desert shrine in the northern part of the peninsula, where the rocky Ustyurt plateau drops into canyons and dry valleys of white and orange stone. The name refers to a revered figure, Shakpak, who is said to have been a Sufi saint of the area, and the complex is built into the slope of a ravine, with prayer rooms, an underground mosque and a necropolis of carved gravestones, some of them marked with symbols of horses and weapons that tell of the clan's history.
