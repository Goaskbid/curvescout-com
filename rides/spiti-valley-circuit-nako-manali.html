Why this ride

On the way it passes Kaza and Kunzum Pass. The highest point is about 5,050 m. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

Scenic road chapters

Kaza → Kunzum Pass

The valley floor is behind you and the road starts working for its height at about 8.1%. Corners stack above one another where the ground allows nothing straighter — first and second gear more often than not. Look through each corner to the next one and let the engine do the holding.

Kunzum Pass → Manali

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Nako

    Nako is a village you reach after a day of mountain road, and you feel the effort as soon as you arrive. It lies at about 3,600 metres in the Hangrang valley of Kinnaur, in Himachal Pradesh, close to the border with Tibet. The village is built of stone and timber and clings to a hillside above a small lake, with willow and poplar trees around its edge and the great peak of Reo Purgyil standing in the distance, more than 6,700 metres high.

  2. 2. Kaza

    Spiti means the middle land, the place between India and Tibet, and Kaza is its capital in the way that a cluster of low whitewashed buildings in a very large landscape can be a capital. It lies at about thirty-eight hundred metres on the bank of the Spiti river, in a wide valley of grey and ochre mountains with almost no trees, where the sky is a hard dark blue and the air is thin and dry. The climate is cold desert, with snow in winter and little rain in summer, and the people live by barley, peas and yak herding, and by the monasteries that have shaped life here for a thousand years.

  3. 3. Kunzum Pass

    At 4,551 metres, Kunzum Pass is the gate between two worlds. On one side lies Lahaul, with the Chandra river and the green fringes of the Himalayan monsoon. On the other side lies Spiti, a high, dry cold desert where the mountains are bare and brown, the sky is absurdly blue, and villages sit in oases on the valley floor. The pass is the only road link between the two valleys, and it is closed by snow for much of the year, usually opening around June and shutting again by October or earlier.

  4. 4. Manali

    Manali is where the plains give up and the Himalaya begins in earnest. The town sits in the Kullu valley in Himachal Pradesh, on the banks of the Beas, among deodar forests and apple orchards, with snow peaks rising behind. Old Manali, across the river, is the quieter half, a tangle of lanes, wooden houses and cafes, and in the cedar woods above the town stands the Hadimba temple, a pagoda-like wooden shrine built in the sixteenth century and dedicated to a figure from the Mahabharata. The valley has long been a stopping place for traders, travellers and, more recently, backpackers and motorcyclists.

Recorded leg-by-leg notes

These source notes may contain earlier estimates. They are not turn-by-turn directions or current measured distances.

Nako to Kaza

Nako → Kaza is 81 km of getting across. Ride it tidily and keep the enthusiasm for what comes after.

Kaza to Kunzum Pass

Kaza → Kunzum Pass climbs about 4551 m in 56 km. This is the part worth slowing down for - look through the bends and use the pull-offs.

Kunzum Pass to Manali

Kunzum Pass → Manali drops about 4551 m over 56 km. Brakes and eyes forward; the views are behind the corners, not in them.
