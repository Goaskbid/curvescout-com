  1. 1. Nelson

    Nelson claims to be one of the sunniest places in New Zealand, and the locals say it with the smugness of people who have earned it. The city sits at the top of the South Island on Tasman Bay, with a wide sheltered beach, a backdrop of hills and the mountains of Kahurangi and Abel Tasman beyond. About fifty-nine thousand people live here, and it has a reputation for craft beer, pottery, galleries and a general willingness to take life slowly.
