  1. 1. Forgotten World Highway point 1

    Taumarunui is where the Forgotten World Highway starts, and the name of the road is not a marketing slogan so much as a fair description. The town stands where the Ongarue and Whanganui rivers meet, in the heart of the North Island, and it was once a bustling railway and river port, with paddle steamers carrying passengers and goods down the Whanganui toward the coast. The railway still passes through, but the trains are fewer, and the town has the quiet, slightly faded look of a place that has seen busier days.

  2. 2. Forgotten World Highway point 2

    Whangamomona is a village with a population that can be counted on one's fingers and a political history that is considerably larger. In 1988, when regional boundaries were redrawn and the village found itself moved from Taranaki to Manawatu-Wanganui against its wishes, locals shrugged, declared independence and proclaimed the Republic of Whangamomona the following year. Presidents have been elected since then, and they have included a goat and a poodle, with the position taken more seriously by its supporters than its critics might suppose.
