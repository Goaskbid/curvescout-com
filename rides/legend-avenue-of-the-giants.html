Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Near Redway

    The Avenue of the Giants begins here, just north of Garberville and Redway, where the old route of US 101 peels off the modern freeway and plunges into the redwoods along the South Fork of the Eel River. For the next fifty kilometres or so the road winds through Humboldt Redwoods State Park, and for much of that distance you are riding through some of the tallest trees on Earth.

  2. 2. Avenue of the Giants point 2

    Rockefeller Forest is the largest remaining stand of old-growth coast redwoods on the planet, and it lies just west of the Avenue of the Giants along Bull Creek. The Mattole Road branches off the avenue near Dyerville and runs into it, and within a few hundred metres you are surrounded by trees so large and so close together that the forest feels like a vast, dim hall. The ground is covered with ferns and sorrel, and the silence is profound.

  4. 4. Near Rio Dell

    At its northern end the Avenue of the Giants loosens its grip. The redwoods give way to open river flats and dairy pasture around Pepperwood, the Eel River widens into a broad, gravelly channel, and the road rejoins US 101 near the little town of Rio Dell. It is a gentler landscape than the cathedral groves to the south, and a good place to pause before the last run north to Fortuna and the coast.
