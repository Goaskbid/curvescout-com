Why this ride

From Indé it runs 279 km to Indé; allow about 4 h 49 of riding. On the way it passes Chapel of San Mateo of the Former hacienda, Town of San Pedro del Gallo and back through Chapel of San Mateo of the Former hacienda. The highest point is about 2,000 m. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Town of Indé

    In the high desert of northern Durango, between mountain ranges and dry basins, the town of Inde lies along the old silver road that ran from Mexico City to Santa Fe in New Mexico. The Camino Real de Tierra Adentro, the Royal Road of the Interior Land, was in use from the sixteenth century to the nineteenth, carrying silver, mercury, supplies, settlers and missionaries over 2,500 kilometres, and the Mexican sections were inscribed on the World Heritage list in 2010. Inde was a stopping point on that road, with a church, a plaza and the haciendas that supplied the mining camps.

  2. 2. Chapel of San Mateo of the Former hacienda

    Indé is a small town in northern Durango, and the chapel of San Mateo belonged to a hacienda that supplied the silver road. The Camino Real de Tierra Adentro ran for about 2,600 kilometres from Mexico City to Santa Fe, and this stretch crossed some of the driest country on the way. Around here the landscape is bajada and basin, with mesquite, huisache and prickly pear on the plains and dark mountain ranges in the distance. Rainfall is low, and the wells and springs that haciendas depended on decided where people could live.

  3. 3. Town of San Pedro del Gallo

    San Pedro del Gallo is a very small town in a very big landscape, and that is precisely its appeal. Fewer than a thousand people live here, in the high desert of northern Durango, among dry plains, low mountains and endless sky, and the town grew up as a stop on the Camino Real de Tierra Adentro, the royal road that linked Mexico City with the northern frontier and Santa Fe. San Pedro del Gallo is one of the places included in the UNESCO listing of the Camino Real, with its church, simple adobe houses and the quiet atmosphere of a place where time moves slowly.
