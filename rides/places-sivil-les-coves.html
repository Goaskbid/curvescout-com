Why this ride

From Bárcabo it runs 168 km to Bárcabo; allow about 2 h 54 of riding. The stops in between: El Remosillo, Les Coves and back through El Remosillo. The highest point is about 1,000 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Sivil

    Sivil is one of those names you will not find on a signpost, and that is rather the point. It belongs to the dry, folded country of the Somontano in northern Aragon, where the last foothills of the Pyrenees run out into the Ebro basin. The labels attached to it speak of a Moorish cave and a tobacco cave, which tells you the real subject: shelters in the limestone where people painted animals and hunters thousands of years ago, part of the Mediterranean rock art that UNESCO lists as a single family of sites.

  2. 2. El Remosillo

    If you ride north from Barbastro toward Graus, the Ésera river eventually forces its way through a ridge of limestone, and the result is the Congosto de Olvena, a short, deep gorge with the road squeezed between rock and water. Up on the cliffs around here are rock shelters with prehistoric paintings, and El Remosillo is one of the best known in this corner of Aragón, between Estadilla, Graus and Fonz.

  3. 3. Les Coves

    The western edge of Catalonia, around Lleida, is fruit country. Irrigation canals bring water from the Pyrenean rivers to a broad, flat plain of orchards growing apples, pears and peaches, laid out in neat lines that stretch to the horizon. Les Coves lies in this area, close to the Aragonese border, between Alfarràs, Albelda and Algerri, where the valley of the Noguera Ribagorçana spills out of the hills and onto the plain.
