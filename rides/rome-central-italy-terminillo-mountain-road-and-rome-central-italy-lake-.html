Why this ride

From Casal Palocco it runs 696 km to Cave; allow about 12 h 40 of riding. The stops in between: La Massimina-Casal Lumbroso, Scenic stretch near Anguillara Sabazia and near Campagnano di Roma. The climb of the day is in Lido di Ostia. Long stretches are under trees.

Slow enough to look. The surface follows the shoreline, so the corners arrive where the water decided, not where an engineer did. Water arrives beside the road and stays for 59 km. The line follows the shore because there is nowhere else for it to go.

  3. 3. La Massimina-Casal Lumbroso

    La Massimina and Casal Lumbroso are not names you will find in a guidebook. They are western outskirts of Rome, just outside the great ring road, the Grande Raccordo Anulare, where the city spills into a landscape of housing estates, garden centres, warehouses and stubborn pockets of farmland. Around eleven thousand people live here, and it is a practical place to finish: close to the motorway, close to the city, with everything you need.

  6. 6. Near Gerano, on the way back

    East of Rome, past Tivoli, the land rises into the Monti Ruffi, a small range of limestone hills cloaked in chestnut and oak, with old villages perched on every ridge. Gerano, Rocca Santo Stefano and Pisoniano are among them, small places of a few hundred to a thousand people, built of grey stone around churches and the remains of medieval castles, looking out over the Aniene valley towards the higher Simbruini mountains.

    The suburbs here were built in the twentieth century, with gardens and villas in the shade of tall umbrella pines, and the atmosphere is leafy and a little dated, like a resort for families who had cars before most people did. This is the end of a ride from Terminillo, and it is an easy ride in, mostly straight and quick.

  14. 14. Marina di Ardea-Tor San Lorenzo

    Long before Rome mattered, Ardea was the capital of the Rutuli, the people of Turnus, Aeneas's rival in Virgil's Aeneid. The old town still sits on its tufa outcrop a few kilometres inland. The coast below, Marina di Ardea and Tor San Lorenzo, is something else entirely: a sprawling strip of holiday homes, campsites and beach clubs along a long, straight shore of dark volcanic sand, between Ostia and Anzio.

  16. 16. Back to Lake Bracciano

    Cave is a hill town in the countryside south-east of Rome, at the foot of the Prenestini mountains, and the ride that ends here has come a long way round from Lake Bracciano to the north-west of the capital. The town has about 8,000 people, and it is a working place of old stone houses, cafés, bakeries and shops, with a church and a small square at its centre. Fuel and supplies are easy to find, and the local bars serve strong coffee early in the morning.
