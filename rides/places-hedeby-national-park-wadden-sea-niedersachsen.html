Why this ride

A 268 km round trip from Schleswig - roughly 4 h 52 in the saddle. The stops in between: National Park Wadden Sea Niedersachsen and Hedeby. The coast is the point of it.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Hedeby

    Hedeby is the name the Danes used for a town that the Germans call Haithabu, and for roughly three centuries it was one of the great ports of the Viking world. It lay at the head of an inlet, the Haddeby Noor, on the narrow neck of the peninsula where goods could be carried overland from the North Sea to the Baltic, and its streets are said to have held perhaps a thousand people at its height, a huge number for the time. Traders arrived from Norway, Sweden, the Frankish lands, the Slavic coast and even the distant Islamic world, to sell furs, amber, iron, slaves, glass and silver.

  2. 2. National Park Wadden Sea Niedersachsen

    Here the Wadden Sea comes right up against the dyke. This is the coast of the Wurster Nordseeküste, between the Weser and Elbe estuaries, a country of low sea walls, drained farmland and wide skies, and the mudflats outside the dyke are part of the Lower Saxony Wadden Sea National Park. Together with the Dutch and Danish parts of the Wadden Sea, the area is a UNESCO World Heritage site, valued above all for the sheer scale of the tidal flats and the millions of birds that pass through on migration. When the tide goes out, it goes a very long way.
