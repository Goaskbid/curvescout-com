  2. 2. Al Hadā

    Al Hada sits right on the lip of the escarpment, a small town of a few thousand people perched at the top of the great drop to the Tihama plain, and it has the feel of a place that knows the view is its fortune. From the terraces and viewpoints the land falls away in rocky folds toward a hazy plain, and on a clear day the haze gives way to a long reach of the lowlands. The air is cooler than in the cities, and the sense of being above the rest of Arabia is real.
