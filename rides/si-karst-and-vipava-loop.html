  1. 1. Postojna

    Postojna is a small town on the Karst plateau of south-western Slovenia, and almost everyone who comes here has one purpose: the cave. The Postojna Cave system, carved by the Pivka river through limestone, runs for more than twenty kilometres, and part of it is visited by an electric train that carries you deep into the mountain before you walk through halls of stalactites and pale columns. It has been a tourist attraction since the early nineteenth century, one of the oldest in Europe. The temperature inside hovers around ten degrees all year, so bring a warm layer even if you arrive in a heat wave.
