Why this ride

254 km from Teulada to Teulada, about 4 h 23 of riding. The stops in between: Penya de Benicadell, Abric de Tollos II o de Cambriquia and back through Penya de Benicadell. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Cova de la Catxupa- Abric II

    Teulada is the town, and Moraira is its seaside annexe, and between them they hold most of what people know about this slice of the Costa Blanca. The Catxupa shelter lies just outside the town, which has some fourteen and a half thousand residents, many of them northern Europeans who came for the sun and stayed. The old centre of Teulada, though, remains a Valencian one, with a fortified parish church and narrow lanes that fill with the smell of coffee and baking at midday.

  2. 2. Penya de Benicadell

    From almost anywhere in the southern half of Valencia province you can see it: a long, jagged ridge of grey limestone with a sharp tooth at its eastern end, standing on the skyline like a broken saw. The Benicadell, at 1,104 metres, separates the Vall d'Albaida to the north from the Comtat to the south, and the sharp summit, the Penya, is the mountain's signature. At its foot lie the villages of Beniarrés, Ràfol de Salem and Otos.

  3. 3. Abric de Tollos II o de Cambriquia

    Bicorp is famous for a single painting. In the Cuevas de la Araña, a short walk from the village, a figure clings to ropes on a cliff, reaching into a hole surrounded by bees: one of the oldest known depictions of honey gathering in the world. The Abric de Tollos, also called Cambriquia, is another of the painted shelters around Bicorp, in the deep gorges of the Valencian interior, one of the World Heritage rock art sites of the Spanish east.
