Why this ride

415 km from Bárcabo to Bárcabo, about 7 h of riding. It goes by way of Pyrénées - Mont Perdu, Cueva de los moros. and back through Pyrénées - Mont Perdu. It tops out around 1,950 m. The line favours views over speed.

The roads

Pyrenees N260 Route

Pyrénées - Mont Perdu is 86 km from Cueva de Malifeto, mostly on A-2205. A proper up-and-over: 850 m of climbing, 1,347 m at the top, 520 m down. Take a break before the climb, not halfway up.

The stops

  1. 1. Cueva de Malifeto

    Start here.

    The Sierra de Guara is limestone country, and it behaves like it: the rivers have sliced through the rock in deep canyons, the villages sit on crags, and the sky is full of vultures. Cueva de Malifeto is in the foothills of the range, eleven kilometres from Alquézar, a village that grew up around a hilltop collegiate church above the gorge of the Río Vero. The name has a slightly sinister ring in Spanish, and shepherds in these hills have always had stories about odd and shadowy places.

    Read the complete Cueva de Malifeto write-up.

  2. 2. Pyrénées - Mont Perdu

    86 km in. The ride passes about 6 km from it.

    Monte Perdido, the lost mountain, at 3,355 metres, is the highest limestone massif in Europe and the centrepiece of a cross-border World Heritage site shared by Spain and France. On the Spanish side, it gives its name to the Ordesa y Monte Perdido National Park, with deep canyons radiating out from the summit; on the French side, it looks down on the great cirques of Gavarnie and Troumouse.

    Read the complete Pyrénées - Mont Perdu write-up.

  3. 3. Cueva de los moros.

    221 km in.

    There is a Cueva de los Moros in half the valleys of Spain. Every unexplained cave, ruin or rock shelter used to be credited to the Moors, and this one, in the hills of the western Aragonese Pyrenees between the valleys of Hecho and Ansó, is a rock shelter with ancient traces. You should approach it as an excuse to spend time in two of the most beautiful and least spoiled valleys of the Pyrenees, rather than as a monument.

    Read the complete Cueva de los moros. write-up.
