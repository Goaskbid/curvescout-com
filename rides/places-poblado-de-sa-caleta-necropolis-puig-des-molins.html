Why this ride

From Ibiza it runs 45 km to Ibiza; allow about 49 min of riding. It goes by way of Natural site, Necropolis Puig des Molins and Poblado de sa Caleta.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Poblado de sa Caleta

    On a low cliff above the sea on Ibiza's south-west coast stand the foundations of some of the oldest buildings on the island. The Phoenician settlement of sa Caleta was founded around the eighth or seventh century BC, and for about a century it was a small trading post, with rectangular stone houses laid out along narrow streets, a few metres from the water. The inhabitants worked iron, a sign of their sophistication, and traded with the mainland and North Africa. Around 600 to 550 BC the village was abandoned, and its people, it seems, moved to the harbour of Ebusus, which became the main town. UNESCO included the site in its Ibiza listing of 1999.

  3. 3. Necropolis Puig des Molins

    The hill of Puig des Molins, the hill of the windmills, rises on the edge of Ibiza Town, and under its slopes lies one of the biggest ancient cemeteries in the western Mediterranean. The Phoenicians and then the Carthaginians, who founded the city of Ebusus on this coast in the seventh century BC, buried their dead here for centuries, cutting underground chambers into the soft rock, and they are known as hypogea. Over three thousand of these tombs have been found, and the city's archaeological museum, set at the top of the hill, displays the grave goods that came out of them, including pottery, jewellery, glass beads, terracotta figurines and amulets that came from as far away as Egypt.
