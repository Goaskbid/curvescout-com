Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Saint John

    Saint John sits at the mouth of the Saint John River on the Bay of Fundy, a city of about eighty-eight thousand with the dock cranes of a working port, the red-brick faces of a Victorian downtown and a reputation for fog that is not undeserved. The bay beside it has some of the highest tides in the world, and the effect is on show at the Reversing Falls, where the river is squeezed through a narrow gorge. At low tide the river pours out to the sea in rapids; at high tide the sea pushes upstream; and for a short time in between the water is flat. It is an odd sight, and a good reminder that the real boss here is the moon.
