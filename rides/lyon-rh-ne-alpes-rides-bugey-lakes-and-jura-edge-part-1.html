Why this ride

A 248 km one-way ride from near Épinouze, about 4 h of riding. On the way it passes near Riotord and Scenic stretch near Boën-sur-Lignon. It tops out around 900 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The roads

Épinouze · Marcigny and Valleiry Loop fuel gate → Épinouze · Marcigny and Valleiry Loop high p

The 60 km between Fuel stop in Épinouze and High point near Riotord on Route d'Annonay. Mostly uphill - about 1030 m, topping out near 1,131 m. Take a break before the climb, not halfway up.

  4. 4. Photo stop near Marcigny

    The end: 248 km.

    Marcigny is a small market town on the right bank of the Loire in southern Burgundy, in the heart of the Brionnais, a rolling country of green pastures, hedgerows and white Charolais cattle. It is also a land of Romanesque churches: within a short ride you can visit Semur-en-Brionnais, Anzy-le-Duc, Iguerande and many others, each with carved portals and capitals from the eleventh and twelfth centuries. The great abbey of Cluny lies to the east, and its influence is everywhere.
