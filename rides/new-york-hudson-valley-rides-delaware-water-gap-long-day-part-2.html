Why this ride

A 211 km one-way ride from near Hackettstown, about 3 h of riding. The plan has 1 food stop marked.

The stops

  1. 1. Photo stop near Hackettstown

    Start here.

    Hackettstown smells faintly of chocolate on some days, thanks to the Mars factory in town, where candy has been made for decades. It is a pleasant small town in the valley of the Musconetcong River, in the hills of north-western New Jersey, with a main street, old churches and a college. For riders, it is a gateway to some of the best roads in the state.

    Read the complete Photo stop near Hackettstown write-up.

  2. 2. Food stop near Danbury

    174 km in. Food stop near Danbury is where this day stops to eat.

  3. 3. New York / Hudson Valley rides

    The end: 211 km.

    This corner of the state is a landscape of reservoirs, small rivers and stone walls, with the Housatonic River held in two long lakes behind dams at Lake Zoar and Lake Lillinonah. The ride that uses this point is billed as a Delaware Water Gap long day, and that lies a long way west, so treat this as a starting or finishing area for a much bigger loop through New York and New Jersey.

    Read the complete New York / Hudson Valley rides write-up.
