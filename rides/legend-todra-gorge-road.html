Why this ride

A canyon mountain road from Tinghir, Morocco to Agoudal, Morocco, via Todra Gorge. Key character: narrow gorge; high plateau; villages. Surface: paved and mixed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Tinghir

    Tinghir lies on the southern side of the High Atlas in Morocco, where the river Todra runs out of the mountains and across a hard, red desert, and the contrast is startling. A long ribbon of palm trees follows the river for kilometres, with small terraced fields of barley, alfalfa, almonds and vegetables underneath, all watered by channels that have been in use for centuries. From a viewpoint on the edge of town, the green oasis stretches away between bare ochre hills, a strip of life in a landscape of stone.

  3. 3. Todra Gorge Road point 3

    The Todra Gorge is the moment the whole region has been building to. The river, which has been a lazy ribbon in the palm grove, enters a narrow gap in the High Atlas, and the walls rise on both sides to well over a hundred metres, almost sheer, in rust and orange limestone. At the narrowest point the gap is only a few metres wide, and the road, the river and a handful of hotels and stalls are all squeezed between the rocks.
