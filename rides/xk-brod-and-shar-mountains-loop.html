  1. 1. Prizren

    Prizren lies where the Lumbardhi river pours out of the Šar Mountains, with the old town packed on both banks and a hilltop fortress, the Kalaja, keeping watch above. It is the most attractive city in Kosovo, and also one of the most layered. Ottoman mosques, an Orthodox church, a Catholic cathedral and a stone bridge crowd close together around the central square, and on summer evenings the whole place is out walking by the river. The population is large, well over a hundred thousand in the municipality, but the old centre feels almost village-sized.
