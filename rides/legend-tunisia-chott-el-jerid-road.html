Why this ride

curated scenic road. salt lake, desert mirages, oases. From Tozeur it runs 82 km to Kebili; allow about 1 h 29 of riding. The line favours views over speed.

    Out here the land seems to run out of ideas. The Chott el Jerid is a vast salt lake, one of the largest in the Sahara, and the road across it runs on a raised causeway through a landscape that is flat, white and shimmering.
