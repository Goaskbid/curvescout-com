Why this ride

curated scenic road. marble gorge, high mountains, cloud forest. A one-way ride of 84 km, Hualien City to Puli - roughly 1 h 32 in the saddle. The highest point is about 3,300 m. The line favours views over speed.

  1. 1. Hualien City

    Mountains and sea crowd against each other at Hualien. The city sits on a narrow coastal plain on Taiwan's east coast, with the Pacific Ocean on one side and the Central Mountain Range rising steeply to more than three thousand metres on the other, and the view from the waterfront, with the peaks looming over the town, is one of the most striking in Taiwan.

  2. 2. Taiwan Central Cross Island Highway point 2

    Taroko Gorge is a slot cut through marble. The Liwu river has sawn down through a block of metamorphic rock that was lifted from the sea floor as the Philippine Sea plate pushed into Asia, and the result is a canyon so narrow in places that the cliffs nearly meet overhead, their faces streaked white, grey and pale green. The road, Provincial Highway 8, follows the river through tunnels and under overhangs, and on a quiet morning, with the river roaring below and swifts darting along the walls, it is one of the great rides in Asia.

  3. 3. Taiwan Central Cross Island Highway point 3

    Tianxiang is the place where Taroko Gorge opens out for a moment, and where most people stop. Two rivers meet here at the upper end of the gorge, and on a small terrace above them stand a hotel, a few shops, a bus stop and a temple, with a white pagoda on the hill above, reached by a suspension bridge across the river. After the dim tunnels and narrows below, it feels like coming up for air: the cliffs step back, the sky widens, and you can hear yourself think.

  4. 4. Taiwan Central Cross Island Highway point 4

    At 3,275 metres, Wuling Pass is the highest point on Taiwan's road network, and getting there is a story in itself. From the lowland heat of Hualien or Puli, the road climbs through subtropical forest, then cool broadleaf woods, then forests of hemlock and fir, until at the top you are in a world of dwarf bamboo grassland, rocky peaks and thin, cold air. It is a remarkable change for an island that sits on the Tropic of Cancer, and it means that riders who left the coast in a T-shirt need every layer they packed.

  5. 5. Puli

    Puli sits almost in the middle of Taiwan, in a basin ringed by the mountains of Nantou County, and people often call it the island's geographic heart. At the end of a long climb out of the high mountains, it is a relief: a town of about eighty-six thousand people with flat streets, warm air, and shops that stay open late. The elevation here is modest, so after the cold of the high passes the temperature feels like a gift.
