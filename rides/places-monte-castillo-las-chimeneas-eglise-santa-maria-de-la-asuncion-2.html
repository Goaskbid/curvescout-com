Why this ride

From Puente Viesgo it runs 415 km to Puente Viesgo; allow about 7 h 09 of riding. The stops in between: El Pindal, Eglise Santa Maria de la Asuncion and back through El Pindal. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Monte Castillo - Las Chimeneas

    Everyone talks about Altamira, but Monte Castillo has a better claim to the title of Europe's oldest gallery. El Castillo, the cave that gives the hill its name, contains a red disc that was dated in 2012 to more than forty thousand years ago, which makes it among the oldest known cave paintings anywhere. Las Chimeneas, the cave marked here, is one of its neighbours inside the same hill, and the whole mountain is a kind of honeycomb of Ice Age art.

  2. 2. El Pindal

    On the coast of eastern Asturias, near the village of Pimiango, a cave opens in the cliffs above the sea, and inside, Palaeolithic artists painted and engraved animals on the walls: a mammoth, horses, a bison, a fish, and many red dots and signs. El Pindal is one of the caves included in the World Heritage listing of the Cave of Altamira and Paleolithic Cave Art of Northern Spain, and its location, with the sea pounding the rocks below and the Picos de Europa rising inland, is spectacular. Visits are limited and guided, to protect the paintings.

  3. 3. Eglise Santa Maria de la Asuncion

    Castro-Urdiales has one of the best harbour fronts on the whole Cantabrian coast, and it knows it. The Gothic church of Santa María de la Asunción stands on a rocky headland above the old port, its buttresses and pinnacles silhouetted against the sea, flanked by the remains of a castle that now holds a lighthouse and by a medieval bridge leading to a little chapel on a rock. Built from the thirteenth century onward, the church is one of the finest Gothic buildings in northern Spain, and it lies on the coastal route of the Way of Saint James, which is part of a UNESCO listing that covers the pilgrimage roads of the north.
