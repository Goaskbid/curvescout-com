Why this ride

A proper escape from Melbourne with roads worth waking up for. An 870 km loop that starts and ends near Mount Martha, about 15 h 19 of riding. It goes by way of Scenic stretch near Bacchus Marsh, near Trentham and Melbourne / Victoria. Sorrento is the high point. The line favours views over speed.

The stops

  1. 1. Fuel stop near Mount Martha

    Start here.

  2. 2. Sorrento

    38 km in. You top out here; the road stops climbing.

    This is where Victoria began, briefly. In 1803 a British expedition under David Collins landed near Sorrento to found a settlement, but the site lacked water and the soil was poor, and after a few months they gave up and sailed to Tasmania. A small reserve marks the spot, with the graves of some of the settlers.

    Read the complete Sorrento write-up.

  3. 3. Scenic stretch near Bacchus Marsh

    253 km in.

    Coming into Bacchus Marsh from the east, you ride through an arch of tall elms: the Avenue of Honour, planted after the First World War with a tree for each local man who enlisted. It is a moving entrance to a town that sits in a deep, fertile bowl where the Werribee and Lerderderg rivers meet, ringed by dry hills. The flats along the rivers have been orchards and market gardens since the nineteenth century, and roadside stalls still sell apples, pears and vegetables straight from the farms.

    Read the complete Scenic stretch near Bacchus Marsh write-up.

  4. 4. Photo stop near Trentham

    329 km in.

    Trentham is a small town on the Great Dividing Range, set high and cool among tall forest and potato paddocks between Daylesford and Woodend. Its main street has a handful of heritage buildings, a historic bakery and pub, and a quiet, unhurried charm that makes it a favourite stop for riders and weekenders alike.

    Read the complete Photo stop near Trentham write-up.

  5. 5. Melbourne / Victoria return

    596 km in.

    The rivers here, the Goulburn and its tributaries, the Howqua and the Jamieson, run clear and cold from the mountains, and the pools along them are good for a dip on a hot day.

    Read the complete Melbourne / Victoria return write-up.

  6. 6. Food stop near Alexandra

    631 km in. Food stop near Alexandra is where this day stops to eat.

  7. 7. Altona

    785 km in.

  8. 8. Back to Mornington

    Back where you started, 870 km.

    Mount Martha is a bayside town on the Mornington Peninsula, a leafy cluster of beaches, coastal scrub and big houses that rises on the slopes of a low hill between Mornington and Dromana. For a ride described as easy and scenic, it is a fitting place to end: the road runs close to the water, and the sea is rarely out of view.

    Read the complete Mornington write-up.

Leg by leg

Fuel stop near Mount Martha to Sorrento

39 km, mostly on Mornington Peninsula Freeway and Point Nepean Road.

Sorrento to Scenic stretch near Bacchus Marsh

215 km, mostly on Western Freeway Onramp.

Scenic stretch near Bacchus Marsh to Photo stop near Trentham

76 km, mostly on Ballan-Daylesford Road and Geelong-Ballan Road.

Photo stop near Trentham to Melbourne / Victoria return

266 km, mostly on Calder Freeway.

Melbourne / Victoria return to Food stop near Alexandra

35 km, mostly on Eildon - Warburton Road and Eildon - Jamieson Road.

Food stop near Alexandra to Altona

154 km, mostly on Maroondah Highway.

Altona to Back to Mornington

85 km.
