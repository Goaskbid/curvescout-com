Why this ride

A 251 km round trip from Nerpio - roughly 4 h 20 in the saddle. The stops in between: Cañada de la Cruz, Cueva de la Hiedra and back through Cañada de la Cruz. The highest point is about 1,400 m. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Abrigo del Sabinar

    The Abrigo del Sabinar lies in the remote mountains of north-western Murcia, near Moratalla, where the Sierra de Segura and the Sierra del Taibilla meet, and its paintings of human figures and animals, including deer and goats, are among the finest examples of Levantine rock art in the region. The shelter is part of the Rock Art of the Mediterranean Basin listing, and the area around Moratalla and Nerpio is particularly rich in painted shelters. The landscape is wild and rugged, with pine forests, limestone cliffs and deep river valleys, and the villages are few and far between.

  2. 2. Cañada de la Cruz

    The Cañada de la Cruz is a high valley or drovers' pasture in the Segura mountains; cañada is the word for the traditional livestock routes and grazing grounds used by shepherds moving their flocks between summer and winter pastures, and this part of Spain has a long history of transhumance.

  3. 3. Cueva de la Hiedra

    Ivy is a modest plant to give a cave its name, but the Cueva de la Hiedra, the Cave of the Ivy, tells you something about the place. On a dry limestone hillside above Huesa, any cave that holds damp enough to grow ivy stands out, and the plant may mark the mouth of a shelter. The cave lies about four kilometres from Huesa and a little more from Quesada, in the folded limestone country between the olive plain and the wooded ridges of the Cazorla natural park.
