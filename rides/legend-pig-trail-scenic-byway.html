Why this ride

A forest byway from Ozark, Arkansas to Brashears, Arkansas, via Boston Mountains. Key character: tight curves; Ozark forest; river valleys. Surface: paved.

The roads

Pig Trail Scenic Byway

It is 33 km from Ozark to Pig Trail Scenic Byway point 2, mostly on AR 23. You climb most of the way: 350 m, up to 352 m.

The stops

  1. 1. Ozark

    Start here.

    Ozark, Arkansas, sits on the south side of the Arkansas River valley, where the flat bottomlands end and the Ozark foothills begin. It is a quiet county seat of about thirty-five hundred people, with an old courthouse on a square, a few brick storefronts and a couple of cafes, and it serves as the starting point for the Pig Trail, the road that heads north through the Ozark National Forest toward Eureka Springs and Fayetteville. The name itself may come from the French aux Arcs, to the Arkansas, though the exact origin is debated.

    Read the complete Ozark write-up.

  2. 2. Pig Trail Scenic Byway point 2

    33 km in.

    Past Cass the road finally starts to do what its reputation promises. The forest closes in, the hills steepen, and Highway 23 begins to wind through a run of tight, banked hairpins that climb and drop through deep hardwood forest, with the Mulberry River tucked away on the left in a rocky valley. This is the Boston Mountains, the highest and roughest part of the Ozarks, a plateau cut by streams into a maze of steep ridges and hollows, and the forest of oak, hickory and maple is among the most extensive in the central United States.

    Read the complete Pig Trail Scenic Byway point 2 write-up.

  3. 3. Pig Trail Scenic Byway point 3

    The end: 56 km.

    Further north, the Boston Mountains begin to relax. The ridges flatten into a broad plateau, the hairpins ease into longer, smoother bends, and the road settles into a gentle rhythm through deep hardwood forest with occasional clearings for farms, hay meadows and small chapels. The nearest towns, Huntsville and Elkins, lie off to the north and west, and between them is some of the quietest country in Arkansas.
