Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Daxi

    Daxi is a small town in the hills of Taoyuan, on the Dahan river, and it marks the start of one of Taiwan's most popular mountain roads. The old street, with arched facades in baroque style built during the Japanese colonial period, runs down toward the river, lined with shops selling dried tofu, a local speciality that Taiwanese people travel for. The tofu is dense, savoury and a little chewy, and you can buy it in many flavours. In the evening the street fills with families and the smell of grilled food.
