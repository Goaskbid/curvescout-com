Why this ride

A 351 km round trip from Ntsoudjini - roughly 67 h 25 in the saddle. On the way it passes Mutsamudu and Ntsudjini. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The stops

  1. 1. Ntsudjini

    Start here.

    Ntsoudjini lies on the western coast of Grande Comore, a short ride from Moroni, and it is one of those places where the island's volcanic past is easy to see. The village sits among black lava rock that spilled from Karthala, and the coast beside it is a jagged line of dark stone, with natural pools and small coves where the sea comes in with a hiss. Behind the village the land rises gently to fields of coconut palms, mango trees and banana plants.

    Read the complete Ntsudjini write-up.

  2. 2. Mutsamudu

    175 km in.

    Mutsamudu, or Moutsamoudou, is the capital of Anjouan, called Ndzuwani by its people, one of the three islands of the Comoros, and it sits tucked against the north-west coast under steep green hills. The old town climbs the slope in a tangle of narrow alleys, with a medina of coral-stone houses, carved doors and whitewashed mosques, while the harbour below is busy with dhows, small cargo boats and fishermen. A fortress above the town recalls the days when Anjouan's sultans controlled a prosperous trade, and when the island had a reputation as a stopping place for ships on the route to India.
