Why this ride

A 544 km round trip from Alquézar - roughly 8 h in the saddle. On the way it passes Pyrénées - Mont Perdu, Cueva de los moros. and back through Pyrénées - Mont Perdu. It tops out around 1,950 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The roads

Pyrenees N260 Route

Pyrénées - Mont Perdu is 148 km from Covacho de Labarta on Autovía Mudéjar. A proper up-and-over: 1450 m of climbing, 1,310 m at the top, 1270 m down. Take a break before the climb, not halfway up.

The stops

  1. 1. Covacho de Labarta

    Start here.

    Somontano means under the mountain, and that is just what this district is: the rolling foothills of the Pyrenees below the Sierra de Guara, where the land opens from the stony gorges into a country of vineyards, almond groves and golden fields. The wine region of that name, around Barbastro, is one of the quiet success stories of Spanish viticulture, and its reds, whites and rosés are well worth carrying home. The Covacho de Labarta is six kilometres from Alquézar, in the hills that look down on the gorge of the Río Vero.

    Read the complete Covacho de Labarta write-up.

  2. 2. Pyrénées - Mont Perdu

    148 km in. The ride passes about 6 km from it.

    Monte Perdido, the lost mountain, at 3,355 metres, is the highest limestone massif in Europe and the centrepiece of a cross-border World Heritage site shared by Spain and France. On the Spanish side, it gives its name to the Ordesa y Monte Perdido National Park, with deep canyons radiating out from the summit; on the French side, it looks down on the great cirques of Gavarnie and Troumouse.

    Read the complete Pyrénées - Mont Perdu write-up.

  3. 3. Cueva de los moros.

    282 km in.

    There is a Cueva de los Moros in half the valleys of Spain. Every unexplained cave, ruin or rock shelter used to be credited to the Moors, and this one, in the hills of the western Aragonese Pyrenees between the valleys of Hecho and Ansó, is a rock shelter with ancient traces. You should approach it as an excuse to spend time in two of the most beautiful and least spoiled valleys of the Pyrenees, rather than as a monument.

    Read the complete Cueva de los moros. write-up.
