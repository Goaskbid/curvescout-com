Why this ride

curated scenic road. Julian Alps, exposed hairpins, panoramas. From Bovec it runs 15 km to Tarvisio; allow about 16 min of riding. The highest point is about 2,050 m. Passo del Predil and Mangart Saddle are open in summer only - check it is open before you set off. The line favours views over speed.

Slovenia Vrsic Pass Road

Expect company on a summer weekend. Early is better than fast. For 10 km this is Slovenia Vrsic Pass Road, a road with a name of its own and a reason for it.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Bovec

    Bovec sits at the head of the Soča valley, in a wide bowl of green meadows ringed by limestone walls that look as if they were stacked there deliberately. Kanin rises to the south, the high Julian Alps close in to the east, and the river that gave the valley its name runs cold and unbelievably turquoise just down the road. It is a small town of around sixteen hundred people, but in summer its population of kayakers, rafters, paragliders and riders makes it feel much larger.
