Why this ride

A 57 km one-way ride from near Formazza, about 58 min of riding. The stops in between: Nufenen Pass and All Acqua. The highest point is about 2,500 m. Nufenen Pass is subject to seasonal closure - check it is open before you set off. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

Scenic road chapters

Furka Pass

The last of the trees go and the road runs out onto open ground. The summit sits at 2478 m, and what surrounds it is rock, weather and distance — cold comes early up here and stays late. Ride it with something in reserve: the summit is not the end of the work, only the middle of it.

Rider note

Check the pass is open before you commit to it.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Ulrichen

    Ulrichen is a small Valais village in the Goms, the upper valley of the Rhône, where the river is still a shallow, quick stream running between wide meadows. The valley is long, high and quiet, about 1,350 metres up, and its villages are clusters of dark wooden houses raised on stone stilts, burned black by the sun over centuries. This is a Walser landscape, a place settled by mountain people who lived at the limit of the habitable, and even now there is a sense of space and slow time. Summer visitors come for cycling and walking, winter ones for the long cross-country ski trails that run the length of the valley floor.

  2. 2. Nufenen Pass

    The Nufenen is the highest road pass that lies entirely inside Switzerland, at 2,478 metres, and it feels it. The road climbs out of the Goms valley in the canton of Valais and crosses a bleak, rocky crest before dropping into the Ticino, and nothing grows at the top except a few tough mosses and lichens. The wind blows hard across the saddle, the air is thin and cold, and on a clear day the view of the Bernese Alps to the north and the Ticino peaks to the south is vast and sharp.

  3. 3. All Acqua

    All'Acqua is the last place before the Nufenen on the Ticino side, and the sort of place that makes you want to stop even though there is hardly anything there. A few stone buildings, an inn with a restaurant and a small chapel stand at the head of the Bedretto valley, at about 1,600 metres, with the long slope of the pass rising behind. The air is cold and clear, and the light has a hard brilliance that makes the grey stone look almost white.

  4. 4. Airolo

    Airolo sits at the southern mouth of the Gotthard, the point where northern and southern Europe seem to hand over to each other in a single valley. At 1,175 metres it is a small town of about fifteen hundred people, and the first sign of Italian Switzerland if you come from Andermatt: the roofs turn to stone, the stucco gets warmer, and the language changes to Italian. It has fuel, cafes, a couple of bakeries and a railway station, and it is a natural place to start or finish a day among the passes.

Recorded leg-by-leg notes

These source notes may contain earlier estimates. They are not turn-by-turn directions or current measured distances.

Ulrichen to Nufenen Pass

Ulrichen → Nufenen Pass climbs about 2478 m in 14 km. This is the part worth slowing down for - look through the bends and use the pull-offs.

Nufenen Pass to All Acqua

Nufenen Pass → All Acqua drops about 2478 m over 3 km. Brakes and eyes forward; the views are behind the corners, not in them.

All Acqua to Airolo

All Acqua → Airolo is 15 km of rhythm rather than effort. Let it flow and let the view breathe.
