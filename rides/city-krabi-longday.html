Why this ride

From Krabi it runs 328 km to Krabi; allow about 5 h 39 of riding. It goes by way of Wat Phra Mahathat Woramahawihan and back through Wat Phra Mahathat Woramahawihan. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Krabi

    Krabi is the sort of place that makes visitors reach for superlatives and then settle for pointing. Limestone karst rises straight out of the mangrove and the sea, with sheer grey cliffs wrapped in jungle and topped with pinnacles, and the town sits on the Krabi river as it widens into a muddy estuary. Out in the bay, long-tail boats run to the islands and the beaches around Ao Nang, which has turned into a lively resort strip about twelve kilometres west. The town itself, with about thirty thousand people, has a riverfront promenade and a busy night market where the day's catch ends up on the grill.

  2. 2. Wat Phra Mahathat Woramahawihan

    Nakhon Si Thammarat is not on the usual list of Thai holiday places, and that is part of its charm. It is an old city on the Gulf coast of the southern peninsula, with a walled centre, a lot of history and a big temple at its heart. Wat Phra Mahathat is the biggest and most revered in the south, with a bell-shaped chedi in the Sri Lankan style that rises about thirty-seven metres, topped by a gilded spire that you can see across the rooftops. According to legend, the relics of the Buddha were brought here many centuries ago, and pilgrims have been making offerings since the city was part of the maritime trading empire of Srivijaya.
