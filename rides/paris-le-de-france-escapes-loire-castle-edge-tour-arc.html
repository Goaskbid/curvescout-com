  3. 3. Food stop near Évreux

    Évreux is the capital of the Eure department in Normandy, a medium-sized city on the river Iton about an hour and a half west of Paris. It has had a turbulent history, sacked by Vikings, burned during the Hundred Years' War and heavily bombed in 1940 and 1944, but it has kept a fine Gothic cathedral, Notre-Dame d'Évreux, whose stained glass is among the best in Normandy. The former bishop's palace beside it now houses the city museum, with Gallo-Roman finds from the area.

  4. 4. Near Ingré, on the way back

    North of Orléans the Beauce begins, the great wheat plain of France, flat as a table and sown to the horizon. This stop lies on its southern edge, between Ormes, Bricy and Ingré, where the plain meets the suburbs of Orléans and the military air base at Bricy, home to the French air force's transport aircraft. You may see the big grey A400Ms lumbering overhead.

  6. 6. Photo stop near Sergines

    Sergines is a quiet village in the north of the Yonne department, on the chalk plateau between the Seine and the Yonne, roughly where Burgundy shades into Champagne. The landscape is wide and open, with huge fields of wheat, barley, sugar beet and rapeseed stretching to the horizon, broken only by clumps of trees, church spires and the occasional water tower.
