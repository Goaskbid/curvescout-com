Why this ride

A 347 km one-way ride from near Ponderosa Park, about 6 h 48 of riding. near Perry Park is the high point. It tops out at about 3,368 m.

The roads

Mount Blue Sky Scenic Byway

The 130 km between Scenic stretch near Evergreen and Photo stop near Fraser. It climbs 1290 m to the top at 3,368 m and drops 1130 m again. Cold up there even in July - pack a warm layer and check it is open.

Rider note

Check the pass is open before you commit to it.
