Why this ride

A 258 km round trip from Cizer - roughly 4 h 27 in the saddle. The stops in between: Gilău - Dealul Cetății, Roșia Montană Mining Landscape and back through Gilău - Dealul Cetății. The highest point is about 1,150 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Hurez - La Frasin 2

    Frasin is the Romanian word for ash tree, and a place called La Frasin simply means by the ash. Toponyms like this are the real map of rural Romania. Hurez, a commune in the county of Sălaj, and the hills around it are scattered with names that describe a landscape in a few words: a spring, a clearing, a saddle, a lonely tree that once marked a boundary or a meeting place. You can read the countryside by them.

  2. 2. Gilău - Dealul Cetății

    Gilau sits where the flat plain of the Somesul Mic river meets the first hills of the Apuseni mountains, about fifteen kilometres west of Cluj-Napoca, in Transylvania, and the hill called Dealul Cetatii, which simply means Fortress Hill, rises almost on the edge of the town, about a kilometre away. The town has nearly eight thousand people, and from the road you can see the high, forested ridges of the Apuseni to the west.

  3. 3. Roșia Montană Mining Landscape

    Gold has been mined at Roșia Montană, in the Apuseni mountains of western Romania, for over two thousand years. The Romans, who called the place Alburnus Maior, dug an extensive network of galleries into the mountains to extract the gold, and some of these galleries survive, along with wax tablets found in the mines, recording contracts and daily life. In 2021, after years of controversy, the mining landscape of Roșia Montană was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
