Why this ride

A 144 km round trip from Madīnat Ḩamad - roughly 2 h 37 in the saddle. On the way it passes Al Zubarah Archaeological Site and back through Al Zubarah Archaeological Site. It crosses from Bahrain into Qatar - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. A’ali West Burial Mound Field

    The west field at A'ali tells a more awkward story than its neighbour to the east, because here you can see exactly where the island's past and present collide. The mounds sit in clusters between roads and housing developments, and in places a modern wall or a road cuts straight through what was once a continuous cemetery. A mound has an electricity pylon beside it, another a rubbish bin. Bahrain is a small, crowded country with a fast-growing population, and its heritage is under constant pressure.

  2. 2. Al Zubarah Archaeological Site

    Out on the north-west coast of Qatar, where the desert slides into a shallow turquoise sea, stand the low ruins of Al Zubarah, once one of the busiest pearling and trading towns in the Gulf. In the late eighteenth and nineteenth centuries the town thrived on pearls dived from the oyster beds offshore, traded on to India, Persia and beyond. Then it declined, was abandoned in the early twentieth century, and the sand covered it. Archaeologists have since uncovered streets, houses, a souk, mosques and a long town wall running in an arc around the old harbour. UNESCO inscribed the site in 2013.
