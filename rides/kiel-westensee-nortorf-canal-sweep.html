Why this ride

The best part sits around Westensee shore, Rendsburg canal view, Bredenbek forest road, while Nortorf coffee stop keeps the day practical instead of turning it into a fuel or food hunt. Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Westensee shore

    The Westensee lies a short way west of Kiel, a long, branched lake set in rolling country of fields, woods and low glacial hills. It is the biggest lake in the area, and around it lies a nature park that is roughly shaped like a lung between the city, the Kiel Canal and the Eider lowlands. The shore is a mixture of reed beds, small beaches, boat jetties and wooded banks, and the sense of calm after the city traffic comes quickly.

  2. 2. Nortorf coffee stop

    Nortorf will not make anyone's list of great European destinations, and it does not pretend to. It is a farming town of about six thousand people in the middle of Holstein, set on the edge of the Geest, the sandy, gently rolling ridge of central Schleswig-Holstein, halfway between Neumünster and Rendsburg. Its job, in a riding day, is a useful one: a place to fill the tank, get a decent breakfast, and set off along quiet back roads.

  3. 3. Rendsburg canal view

    The Kiel Canal is the busiest artificial waterway in the world, measured by the number of ships, and in Rendsburg you can see why. The town sits right on its bank, and ships glide silently through the middle of the landscape, appearing over the fields like something out of a dream. Opened in 1895 as the Kaiser Wilhelm Canal, it cuts across the neck of the Jutland peninsula, linking the North Sea and the Baltic and saving vessels a long and stormy voyage around Denmark.

  4. 4. Bredenbek forest road

    Between Kiel and Rendsburg the land has a gentle rumple, and the woods near Bredenbek are among its better parts. The forest is mixed, beech and oak with patches of spruce, and it sits in the middle of an agricultural area where large fields are divided by Knicks and farm tracks. Here the roads are narrow, quiet and well shaded, a world away from the roar of the nearby motorway.
