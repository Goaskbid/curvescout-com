Why this ride

National Scenic Byway. Cascade volcanoes, lakes, forest. 97 km from Bend to Bend, about 1 h 46 of riding. The highest point is about 1,650 m. The line favours views over speed.

Scenic road chapters

Cascade Lakes Scenic Byway

Slow enough to look. The surface follows the shoreline, so the corners arrive where the water decided, not where an engineer did. Water arrives beside the road and stays for 9 km. The line follows the shore because there is nowhere else for it to go.
