Why this ride

From Ibi it runs 71 km to Ibi; allow about 1 h 13 of riding. On the way it passes Abric de les finestres IV, Abric de la Penya and back through Abric de les finestres IV. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Barranc de les Coves- Abric III

    Most places on a route have one personality. This one has two, because the ride runs between the toy town of Ibi on one side and Alcoy on the other, and the stop itself sits between them in a dry barranc of pine and rock. It is the third of a series of painted shelters here, small overhangs with faded red figures that belong to the Levantine tradition of eastern Spain.

  2. 2. Abric de les finestres IV

    Bocairent is a village built on a rock, its old houses clinging to a limestone spur above a ravine in the Serra de Mariola, in the south of the province of Valencia. Across the ravine, in the cliff face, a row of small square openings looks out like windows: these are the Covetes dels Moros, a series of artificial caves cut into the rock, connected by internal passages, whose purpose is still debated. They may have been granaries, refuges or even a monastery, and their name means the little caves of the Moors.

  3. 3. Abric de la Penya

    Moixent, or Mogente in Castilian, stands at the head of a corridor that has carried traffic between the Valencian coast and the interior of Castile for as long as anyone has been moving goods. The old road, the railway and now the motorway all funnel through here, between low limestone ridges with vineyards and olive groves in the valley floor. The rock shelter known as the Abric de la Penya sits in this landscape a few kilometres from the town, close also to Vallada.
