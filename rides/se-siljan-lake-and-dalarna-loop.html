  1. 1. Mora

    Mora sits at the northern end of Lake Siljan, in the heart of Dalarna, the province that Swedes think of as the soul of the country. The lake, formed by a meteorite impact some three hundred and sixty million years ago, is nearly round, a huge ring of water set among forested hills, and the villages around its shore have kept their folk traditions: red-painted wooden houses, midsummer poles hung with flowers, and fiddlers in regional costume.
