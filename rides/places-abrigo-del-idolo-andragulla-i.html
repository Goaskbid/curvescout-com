Why this ride

90 km from Nerpio to Nerpio, about 1 h 42 of riding. On the way it passes Cañaica del Calar l, Andragulla I and back through Cañaica del Calar l. The stored terrain estimate reaches about 1,400 m; a measured road-elevation profile is not available. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Abrigo del Ídolo

    Shelters collect names the way villages collect nicknames. The Abrigo del Ídolo lies barely a kilometre and a half from Nerpio, and it carries the name of a figure that someone once thought looked like an idol, which tells you less about the figure than about the person who named it. Across the district you will find shelters called after mills, springs, farms and the families who worked them, and the whole collection reads like a gazetteer of the people who walked these hills before the archaeologists arrived.

  2. 2. Cañaica del Calar l

    Very few people get here. The Cañaica del Calar lies in the far north-west of the region of Murcia, near the border with Albacete and Andalusia, in a wild landscape of limestone plateaus, pine forests and deep valleys around Moratalla and Nerpio. The calar is the local word for a limestone plateau, and the rock shelters on its edges hold prehistoric paintings in the Levantine style, part of the World Heritage rock art of the Mediterranean. The area around Nerpio is one of the richest in Spain for rock art.

  3. 3. Andragulla I

    In the far north-west corner of the Region of Murcia, where the land climbs towards the mountains of Albacete and Jaén, lies Moratalla, a town of whitewashed houses piled up a hillside beneath a castle tower. Its countryside is surprisingly wild for Murcia: pine forests, rivers, gorges and limestone ridges, some of the greenest land in a famously dry region. The rock shelter of Andragulla is one of many painted overhangs scattered through these hills, part of the Levantine rock art sites inscribed together by UNESCO. Many are protected behind fences and need a guide; the tourist office in Moratalla can advise.
