Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Clarksville Arkansas

    Clarksville sits in the Arkansas River Valley, in the low country between the Boston Mountains to the north and the long, flat-topped ridge of Mount Magazine to the south. It is a proper small city of nine thousand or so, with a courthouse square, a college, a ring of motels along the interstate, and a reputation that rests on one fruit: Johnson County peaches. In summer the roadside stands sell them by the bushel, and the local festival has been running for decades. If you pass through in July, eat one and judge for yourself.

  2. 2. Oark

    Oark is a place that exists mostly because motorcyclists love it. A handful of buildings stand at a crossroads in the Ozark National Forest, and the general store is the centre of everything. On a good weekend there are dozens of bikes parked outside, and riders from three or four states are comparing notes on the roads they have just ridden. Highways 21, 215, and 103 meet here, and all three are good, which is why people come.

  3. 3. Boxley Valley

    A wide, quiet valley opens along Arkansas Highway 21 where the Buffalo National River comes down out of the Ozarks, and for a few miles the road runs through farmland that feels almost European in its calm. Boxley Valley is a historic district, with old farmsteads, a mill, a church, and fields bordered by split-rail fences, all protected as part of the national river. The mountains stand back on either side, and the light in the early morning can be remarkable.

  4. 4. Jasper Arkansas

    Jasper is a courthouse square with a handful of shops and cafés in the middle of the Boston Mountains, and it is the finish line for a ride that has earned its sit-down. Newton County has fewer people than almost anywhere in Arkansas, and the village, a few hundred strong, is the county seat in the way small places often are, with a stone-fronted square, a pharmacy and a long front porch of a restaurant that has fed travellers since 1909. Harrison, twenty-six kilometres to the north, is the nearest town of any size.
