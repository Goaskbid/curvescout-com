Why this ride

curated scenic road. remote Alps, forest, hairpins. A one-way ride of 26 km, Borgo Valsugana to Castello Molina di Fiemme - roughly 28 min in the saddle. The highest point is about 2,050 m. Passo Manghen is open in summer only - check it is open before you set off. The line favours views over speed.

Scenic road chapters

Passo Manghen Road

Expect company on a summer weekend. Early is better than fast. For 14 km this is Passo Manghen Road, a road with a name of its own and a reason for it.
