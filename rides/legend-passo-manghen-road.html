Why this ride

curated scenic road. remote Alps, forest, hairpins. A one-way ride of 43 km, Borgo Valsugana to Castello Molina di Fiemme - roughly 49 min in the saddle. The highest point is about 2,047 m. Passo Manghen is open in summer only - check it is open before you set off. The line favours views over speed.

The roads

Passo Manghen Road

From Borgo Valsugana to Near Palù del Fersina is 25 km. You climb most of the way: 1630 m, up to 2,033 m. Snow can close it outside summer, so check before you go.

Rider note

Check the pass is open before you commit to it.
