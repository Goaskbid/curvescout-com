Why this ride

curated scenic road. remote Alps, forest, hairpins. A one-way ride of 43 km, Borgo Valsugana to Castello Molina di Fiemme - roughly 49 min in the saddle. The highest point is about 2,050 m. Passo Manghen is open in summer only - check it is open before you set off. The line favours views over speed.
