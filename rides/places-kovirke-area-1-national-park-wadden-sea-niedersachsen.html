Why this ride

329 km from Kropp to Kropp, about 6 h of riding. The stops in between: National Park Wadden Sea Niedersachsen and Kovirke Area 1. The coast is the point of it.

The stops

  1. 1. Kovirke Area 1

    Start here.

    Kropp is the town that this stretch of the Danevirke leans on. It is a modest place of about six thousand people, set just off the motorway that runs from Hamburg to Denmark, and in practical terms it is the closest place with proper services. The wall itself lies a few kilometres to the north, in fields and hedges near the villages of Dannewerk and Groß Rheide, and at this point it runs westward towards the marshy valley of the Treene, where the old defence simply ended in wet ground that no army could cross.

    Read the complete Kovirke Area 1 write-up.

  2. 2. National Park Wadden Sea Niedersachsen

    164 km in. The ride passes about 15 km from it.

    Here the Wadden Sea comes right up against the dyke. This is the coast of the Wurster Nordseeküste, between the Weser and Elbe estuaries, a country of low sea walls, drained farmland and wide skies, and the mudflats outside the dyke are part of the Lower Saxony Wadden Sea National Park. Together with the Dutch and Danish parts of the Wadden Sea, the area is a UNESCO World Heritage site, valued above all for the sheer scale of the tidal flats and the millions of birds that pass through on migration. When the tide goes out, it goes a very long way.
