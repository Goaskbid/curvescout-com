Why this ride

From Javea it runs 345 km to Javea; allow about 5 h 44 of riding. On the way it passes Morro Carrascal, Cueva de las Arañas del Carabassi and back through Morro Carrascal. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The stops

  1. 1. Coves Santes de Baix

    Start here.

    The Montgó is the mountain that defines this coast, a great limestone whale of a massif, 753 metres high, standing between Dénia and Xàbia and visible from far out at sea. It is a natural park, with rare plants, Mediterranean scrub and cliffs where birds of prey nest, and its profile is the one that sailors used for centuries as a landmark. Xàbia, known to English speakers as Jávea, lies at its foot, a town of about 30,000 people in three parts: a walled old town on a hill, a harbour district, and a long arc of beach, the Arenal.

    Read the complete Coves Santes de Baix write-up.

  2. 2. Morro Carrascal

    79 km in.

    Inland from Benidorm, barely half an hour from the tower blocks and beaches, the mountains of the Alicante interior rise steep and empty. Morro Carrascal is a rocky height in this country, near the villages of Benilloba, Sella and Benimantell, where the sierras of Aitana, Serrella and Mariola come together. Shelters in these mountains hold prehistoric paintings in both the Levantine and the older Macroschematic styles, and they belong to the World Heritage listing of Iberian rock art.

    Read the complete Morro Carrascal write-up.

  3. 3. Cueva de las Arañas del Carabassi

    174 km in.

    The Carabassí is one of the last stretches of wild-ish coast between Alicante and Santa Pola: a line of dunes held together by pine and marram grass, a long beach, and the sea glittering under the flight path of Alicante airport. In the low rocky ground behind it lies a small cave known for painted marks, the Cueva de las Arañas del Carabassí, close to the village of El Altet and a short hop from Santa Pola.

    Read the complete Cueva de las Arañas del Carabassi write-up.
