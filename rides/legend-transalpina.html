Why this ride

DN67C, the highest road in Romania at 2,145 m. A one-way ride of 40 km, Novaci to Sălişte - roughly 44 min in the saddle. The highest point is about 1,750 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Transalpina point 1

    Up here the Parâng Mountains show their true shape, a broad, grassy massif that rolls away to the horizon more like a Scottish moor than an Alpine peak. The Transalpina, Romania's DN67C, climbs onto this high plateau above the treeline, where spruce forest gives way to open pasture, juniper and heather, and the road becomes a thin ribbon across a vast, empty landscape. Its high point, the Urdele Pass, reaches 2,145 metres, which makes it the highest paved road in the country, and the surrounding country has the lonely, windswept feel of the roof of the Carpathians.

  2. 2. Transalpina point 2

    Here the road leaves the bare plateau and drops into the valley of the Lotru, a long, wooded corridor threaded by one of the main rivers of the Southern Carpathians. The river has been tamed for hydroelectric power, with large dams and reservoirs, notably Lake Vidra, and from the road you catch glimpses of still, deep blue water between steep slopes of spruce. After the exposure of the high pass, the forest comes as a relief, with the smell of resin and damp earth.
