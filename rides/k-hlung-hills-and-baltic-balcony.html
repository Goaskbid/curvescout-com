Why this ride

The Rostock escape that actually feels like a ride. Out of Bad Doberan and back again: 102 km, roughly 1 h 46 in the saddle. It goes by way of Kühlungsborn sea road, Rerik coast view and Satow inland bends. It keeps close to the shore for long stretches.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Bad Doberan minster

    Bad Doberan has one of the finest brick Gothic churches in Europe, and it was built by monks. The minster, the Doberaner Münster, was the church of a Cistercian abbey founded in the twelfth century, and the present building, finished in the fourteenth century, is a tall, plain, pale-red brick basilica whose clear lines and soaring interior are the essence of the Baltic Gothic style. The abbey was dissolved at the Reformation, but the church survived, and the dukes of Mecklenburg were buried inside. Its stained glass, carved altars and a huge cross from the Middle Ages are still there.

  2. 2. Kühlungsborn sea road

    Kühlungsborn is the largest seaside resort on the German Baltic coast, and from the top of the sea road you see why. A promenade runs for several kilometres along the beach, backed by rows of white villas in the old seaside style, with turrets, balconies and carved woodwork, many of them built in the decades before the First World War, when this was a fashionable resort for the Mecklenburg court and for visitors from Berlin. The beach is wide, pale and fine-grained, and the sea beyond is calm and shallow.

  3. 3. Rerik coast view

    Rerik lies on the Salzhaff, a sheltered lagoon on the Mecklenburg Baltic coast, separated from the open sea by a long narrow peninsula called Wustrow, and the viewpoint looks across a landscape of water, reeds and low hills. It is a place of birds: in spring and autumn migrating geese, cranes and waders fill the lagoon, and the quiet shore draws birdwatchers with binoculars. The air smells of salt, seaweed and grass, and the light is wide and pale.

  4. 4. Satow inland bends

    Satow is a village on the long, low plateau behind the Mecklenburg Baltic coast, a few kilometres inland from the sea and well off the usual tourist track. The town of Satow-Oberhagen has about six thousand people, and the countryside around it is a mix of cornfields, small woods and the avenues of old lime and chestnut trees that line so many roads in Mecklenburg. Following them is half the pleasure of riding here.

Kühlungsborn sea road to Rerik coast view

Kühlungsborn sea road → Rerik coast view is the reward section in Rostock. Look through the bends, use legal pull-offs for photos and keep a little margin for tractors, cyclists and damp shade. The best ride here is smooth, not noisy.

Rerik coast view to Satow inland bends

This Rerik coast view → Satow inland bends stretch is for rhythm and scenery. Keep your head up, let the view breathe, and resist turning every open bend into a target. Stop once properly rather than half-stopping everywhere.
