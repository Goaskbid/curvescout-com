Plan a motorcycle trip in Austria

Separate a scenic toll-road visit from an unrestricted all-day journey. On an Austrian mountain-road plan, the admission time and vehicle conditions can matter as much as the length of the route.

Treat the gate as a timed stop

Grossglockner publishes seasonal opening hours and separate last-admission times. Plan arrival at the relevant gate, not only departure from your hotel.

Do not reuse one road's rules elsewhere

Nockalm publishes its own motorcycle hours. A permitted visit to one Alpine road does not establish access to another. Use the operator source beside the relevant road, with the correct vehicle and visit date.

Recheck conditions on departure day

The Grossglockner operator notes possible short motorcycle closures caused by freezing meltwater. A published operating season is therefore only part of the access check.
