Why this ride

From Huachis it runs 91 km to Huachis; allow about 1 h 34 of riding. The stops in between: Segment Inka Hamash - Ayash, Segment Tambo Chico - Taparaku and back through Segment Inka Hamash - Ayash. The highest point is about 4,300 m. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Segment Taulli - Tambillo

    In the high valleys of Huánuco, north of Lima and east of the great white wall of the Cordillera Blanca, an Inca road crosses a landscape of stone-walled fields and thatched farms. The Qhapaq Ñan ran north from Cusco to Quito by way of an inland route, and one of its major administrative centres was Huánuco Pampa, a vast planned town on a plateau at about 3,700 metres, with a great plaza, storehouses and barracks, where the emperor's officials gathered tribute and fed travelling armies. Segments of the road around Llata, in the Huamalíes province, connect with that old network.

  2. 2. Segment Inka Hamash - Ayash

    The high Andes of central Peru are less visited than the Cusco region, but the Incas were here too, and the old royal road, the Qhapaq Nan, runs through the mountains of Huanuco Region, with segments surviving in the hills above the towns of Llata and La Union.

  3. 3. Segment Tambo Chico - Taparaku

    The Qhapaq Ñan, the royal road of the Incas, linked an empire that stretched from what is now Colombia to Chile and Argentina, and in the highlands of Huánuco in central Peru one of its best-preserved stretches runs across the high puna between Tambo Chico and Taparaku.
