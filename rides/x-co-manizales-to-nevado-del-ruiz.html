  1. 1. Manizales

    Manizales is a city built on a ridge, and it takes some getting used to. The streets climb and fall at gradients that make a bicycle useless and a motorcycle a sensible choice, and at about 2,150 metres the air is cool and often misty. The city is the capital of Caldas, a coffee department, and it was founded in the middle of the nineteenth century by settlers from Antioquia who cut their way into the forest to plant coffee.

  2. 2. Nevado del Ruiz gate

    A volcano that has killed on a scale most people never forget makes for a sobering ride. Nevado del Ruiz rises to about 5,320 metres in the Central Cordillera, in the Los Nevados National Natural Park above Manizales, and in November 1985 an eruption melted part of its ice cap and sent mudflows down the river valleys, burying the town of Armero and killing more than twenty thousand people. It remains an active volcano under close watch, and the park authorities close access when activity rises, so check before you set out.
