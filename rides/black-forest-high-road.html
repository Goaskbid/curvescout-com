Why this ride

A 89 km one-way ride from near Baden-Baden, about 1 h 32 of riding. The stops in between: Mummelsee, Ruhestein and Kniebis. The highest point is about 900 m. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

Scenic road chapters

Schwarzwaldhochstrasse

Water arrives beside the road and stays for 38 km. The line follows the shore because there is nowhere else for it to go. Slow enough to look. The surface follows the shoreline, so the corners arrive where the water decided, not where an engineer did.

Schwarzwaldhochstrasse

First and second gear more often than not. Look through each corner to the next one and let the engine do the holding. The valley floor is behind you and the road starts working for its height at about 16.9%. Corners stack above one another where the ground allows nothing straighter.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Baden Baden

    Baden-Baden is so nice they named it twice, as the old joke goes, though the doubling actually comes from distinguishing it from other places called Baden. Romans soaked in its hot springs, and the remains of their baths lie under the old town, but the town's golden age came in the nineteenth century, when European royalty, aristocrats, writers and gamblers flocked here to take the waters, stroll the avenues and lose fortunes at the casino. Dostoevsky, Turgenev and Brahms all spent time here. Baden-Baden is one of the eleven Great Spa Towns of Europe, listed together by UNESCO in 2021.

  2. 2. Mummelsee

    Mummelsee is a small, round, black lake in a hollow beneath the Hornisgrinde, the highest peak in the northern Black Forest at about 1,160 metres, and nearly every German has heard of it because of what lives there. According to the legend, the lake is home to the Mummel, water nymphs who dance on the surface at night, and to a lake king who holds court in a crystal palace on the bottom. Anyone who throws a stone in will cause a storm, and a few unwary visitors are said to have been pulled down. The old stories have given the place a quiet reputation for trouble, and the dark water does nothing to discourage it.

  3. 3. Ruhestein

    Ruhestein has no village, no church and no permanent population, and yet it is one of the more important places in the northern Black Forest. This is a pass on the B500, at 915 metres, where the High Road meets the road that drops west toward Allerheiligen and Oppenau, and where a junction has drawn a few inns and a car park. Today it is the gateway to the Black Forest National Park, which was founded in 2014, and where the forest is being allowed to grow wild, with fallen trunks left lying and storm damage left to regenerate on its own.

  4. 4. Kniebis

    Kniebis is a high, straggling village on the ridge at the southern end of the Black Forest High Road, a place where the forest opens out into heath and old fields. At around nine hundred metres, with open views to the south and the dark ridges of the Black Forest in every other direction, it has a bracing, slightly lonely feel. It belongs administratively to Freudenstadt, and it is best known as a spot for walking and cross-country skiing, with a network of trails across the moorland.

  5. 5. Freudenstadt

    Freudenstadt was built to a plan. In 1599, Duke Frederick I of Württemberg laid out the town as a refuge for Protestants fleeing persecution in Austria, and the heart of it is a vast market square, one of the largest in Germany, an almost perfect square about two hundred metres on each side, with covered arcades along its edges. There was meant to be a castle in the middle, but it was never built, and the open space became the town's living room instead. The town, with about twenty-four thousand people, was almost destroyed at the end of the Second World War and rebuilt in a similar style.

Recorded leg-by-leg notes

These source notes may contain earlier estimates. They are not turn-by-turn directions or current measured distances.

Baden Baden to Mummelsee

Baden Baden → Mummelsee is 23 km of rhythm rather than effort. Let it flow and let the view breathe.

Mummelsee to Ruhestein

Mummelsee → Ruhestein climbs about 915 m in 5 km. This is the part worth slowing down for - look through the bends and use the pull-offs.

Ruhestein to Kniebis

Ruhestein → Kniebis drops about 915 m over 15 km. Brakes and eyes forward; the views are behind the corners, not in them.

Kniebis to Freudenstadt

Kniebis → Freudenstadt is 10 km of rhythm rather than effort. Let it flow and let the view breathe.
