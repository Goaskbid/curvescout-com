Why this ride

A 90 km round trip from Ares del Maestre - roughly 1 h 33 in the saddle. The stops in between: Coveta de montegordo, Abric del mas dels Ous and back through Coveta de montegordo. The highest point is about 1,100 m. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Cingle de la mola Remigia- Abric III

    A farmhouse in the Maestrat is called a mas, and the plateau around the Gasulla used to be dotted with them: low, thick-walled stone buildings with a threshing floor, a sheepfold, a cistern and a few fields of cereal, each one a small self-sufficient world. Many are now roofless, with trees growing through the floors, and the shell of an abandoned mas at the end of a farm track is a typical sight here. Depopulation has been the story of the interior of eastern Spain for a century, and the Cingle de la Mola Remigia lies in the middle of its effects.

  2. 2. Coveta de montegordo

    Up in the dry limestone uplands of inland Castellón, the Valltorta and Gasulla ravines hold one of the densest concentrations of Levantine rock art in Spain, and the Coveta de Montegordo is one of its many small shelters. Painted under rock overhangs some seven or eight thousand years ago, perhaps more, the figures here are tiny, often no taller than a finger, yet astonishingly alive: archers running with bows drawn, goats and deer leaping, hunters following wounded animals. The whole region is part of the Rock Art of the Mediterranean Basin listing inscribed by UNESCO in 1998, and the famous Cova dels Cavalls in the Valltorta shows a deer hunt with a line of archers waiting in ambush.

  3. 3. Abric del mas dels Ous

    Xert is the kind of village you pass through on the way to somewhere else and then wonder why you did not stop. It lies in the Baix Maestrat in northern Castellón, below a ridge of bare limestone, with the old village climbing the slope and a newer part down by the road. The shelter called the Abric del Mas dels Ous, the shelter of the farmhouse of the eggs, is out in the dry hills nearby, between Xert, Canet lo Roig and Rosell.
