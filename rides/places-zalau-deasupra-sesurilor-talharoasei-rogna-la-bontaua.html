Why this ride

282 km from Zalău to Zalău, about 6 h of riding. It goes by way of Hurez - Între Dealul Mare și Arsură, Tihău - Grădiște, Rogna - La Bontauă and back through Tihău - Grădiște. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The stops

  1. 1. Zalău - Deasupra Șesurilor Tâlhăroasei

    Start here.

    The Meseș mountains rise to the west of Zalău like a long green wall, and they formed the natural western boundary of Roman Dacia in this region. Along their crest and on the slopes above the plains, the Romans built a line of watchtowers, and the site above the fields with the evocative name of Șesurile Tâlhăroasei, roughly the robbers' flats, near the villages of Treznea and Aghireș, is one of them. It is part of the Dacian frontier on the World Heritage list. Up in the forest the remains are modest, but the position, high on a slope with a view over the valley, shows how the frontier used every advantage of the terrain.

    Read the complete Zalău - Deasupra Șesurilor Tâlhăroasei write-up.

  2. 2. Hurez - Între Dealul Mare și Arsură

    28 km in.

    Somewhere between Dealul Mare and Arsură, two wooded hills above the village of Hurez in Sălaj county, the Roman frontier of Dacia ran along the heights, marked by watchtowers and small earthworks that are now almost hidden by forest. Together with the towers further north on the Meseș ridge, they formed a line that looked west towards the lands beyond the empire, and they were included in the World Heritage listing of the Dacian frontier in 2024. Finding the remains requires a map and some determination; the reward is a sense of how thinly Rome stretched its soldiers across these hills.

    Read the complete Hurez - Între Dealul Mare și Arsură write-up.

  3. 3. Tihău - Grădiște

    107 km in.

    The Someș river makes its way through the hills of north-western Romania between Cluj and Baia Mare, and near the small town of Jibou, in Sălaj county, it passes through a narrow valley guarded on its slopes by the remains of a Roman fort. The site, known locally as Grădiște, which simply means old fort, lies above the village of Tihău, close to Surduc and Gâlgău Almașului.

    Read the complete Tihău - Grădiște write-up.

  4. 4. Rogna - La Bontauă

    144 km in.

    Rogna is a scatter of farms in the hills of northern Sălaj, close to the Someș valley and the border with Maramureș, and La Bontauă is the name the locals give to a piece of high ground where a Roman frontier site has been recorded. The northern edge of Roman Dacia ran through these hills, and the Romans placed watchtowers and small forts on high points to keep an eye on the river valleys and the free Dacians to the north. Today there is little to see apart from the shape of the land, but the view over the rolling hills makes it easy to imagine a sentry looking out across the valley.

    Read the complete Rogna - La Bontauă write-up.
