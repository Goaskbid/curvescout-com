Why this ride

Norwegian Scenic Route. waterfalls, fjord, hairpins. From Balestrand it runs 45 km to Førde; allow about 49 min of riding. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Norwegian Scenic Route Gaularfjellet

Expect company on a summer weekend. Early is better than fast. For 13 km this is Norwegian Scenic Route Gaularfjellet, a road with a name of its own and a reason for it.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Balestrand

    Balestrand is a village on the north shore of the Sognefjord, and it has one of the best settings in Norway: a wide, calm stretch of the fjord with mountains reaching up on all sides, orchards at the water's edge and a quiet, old-fashioned atmosphere. It has fewer than eight hundred inhabitants, and in the nineteenth century it became a destination for British and German tourists, who came by steamer to stay in the large wooden hotels. The Kviknes Hotel, a grand dragon-style building from 1877, is still in operation, and the English church of St Olaf, built by an Englishwoman who married a local painter, stands nearby. Vik and Hermansverk are the nearest towns of any size.

  4. 4. Near Førde

    Moskog is not much more than a junction in the woods south of Førde, but it marks the western end of the Gaularfjellet route, where the E39 meets the road that climbs east over the mountains to Balestrand on the Sognefjord. Around it the country is green and rolling, with forests of pine and birch, lakes, small farms on cleared ground and steep hills rising behind. The river Gaula runs through the area and gives the route its name.
