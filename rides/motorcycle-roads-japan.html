Plan a motorcycle trip in Japan

Use regional seasonal context and road-specific access information. A single best month for all Japan hides differences between the intended regions and high roads.

Start with the climate region

The Japan Meteorological Agency provides regional climate information. Use the region relevant to the journey instead of applying one month label to the whole country.

Keep climate and access separate

Climate context helps choose a period. It does not establish that an individual mountain road, toll section or stop is open on the date of travel.

Inspect the route variants

Open the full roadbook to compare named stops and the return leg. Different variants can share a landmark while offering different complete journeys.
