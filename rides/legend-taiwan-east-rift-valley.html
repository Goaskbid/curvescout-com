Why this ride

curated scenic road. rice fields, mountains, hot springs. From Hualien City it runs 146 km to Taitung City; allow about 2 h 39 of riding. The highest point is about 1,550 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

  1. 1. Hualien City

    Mountains and sea crowd against each other at Hualien. The city sits on a narrow coastal plain on Taiwan's east coast, with the Pacific Ocean on one side and the Central Mountain Range rising steeply to more than three thousand metres on the other, and the view from the waterfront, with the peaks looming over the town, is one of the most striking in Taiwan.

  2. 2. Taiwan East Rift Valley point 2

    Ruisui sits in the middle of the East Rift Valley, a long, narrow trough between the Central Mountain Range to the west and the Coastal Mountain Range to the east, and it is also right on the Tropic of Cancer. A monument by the highway marks the line, and it is a popular photo stop. The valley here is flat and fertile, with rice paddies, pineapple and pomelo orchards, and tea gardens on the terraces above, and the mountains on either side rise steeply into cloud.

  3. 3. Taiwan East Rift Valley point 3

    Rice is the reason anyone in Taiwan knows the name Chishang. This quiet farming town in the southern East Rift Valley grows some of the island's most prized rice, watered by clean streams from the Central Mountain Range, and a lunchbox of Chishang rice with a few side dishes has become a nationwide institution, sold at railway stations and convenience stores everywhere. Here, at the source, the lunchbox shops near the station make a cheap and very satisfying meal, and you can taste why people care.
