Why this ride

From Faro it runs 377 km to Faro; allow about 6 h 30 of riding. It goes by way of Doñana National Park and back through Doñana National Park. It crosses from Portugal into Spain - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Faro

    Faro is the capital of the Algarve and the southern end of Portugal's long road, though the people who land here usually head straight for the beaches. The town sits on a lagoon, the Ria Formosa, a protected area of sandbanks, salt marshes and channels that shelters flamingos, spoonbills and a very rich shoreline. Boats run out to the barrier islands, where long empty beaches face the open Atlantic. The old town, inside a medieval wall, has narrow cobbled streets, a cathedral built partly on the site of a mosque, and a pleasant marina.

  2. 2. Doñana National Park

    Where the Guadalquivir finally reaches the Atlantic, the river loses its way, spreading into marshes, dunes and pine woods that make up Doñana, one of the great wetlands of Europe. The park is a refuge for creatures you do not expect to see in southern Spain: the Iberian lynx, the world's most endangered cat, the Spanish imperial eagle, and in winter, hundreds of thousands of migrating geese and ducks. Flamingos feed in the shallows, and wild boar and red deer move through the cork oak woods.
