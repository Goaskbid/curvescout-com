Why this ride

349 km from Moroni to Moroni, about 67 h 24 of riding. On the way it passes Mutsamudu and Ikoni. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The stops

  1. 1. Ikoni

    Start here.

    Ikoni, often spelled Iconi, lies on the southwest coast of Grande Comore, a few kilometers from Moroni, and in the history of the island it is a place of some weight. It was once an important seat of power, associated with the old sultanate of Bambao, the most powerful of the island's traditional states, and its streets still hold traces of old houses, mosques and tombs. The coast here is rugged, with cliffs of black volcanic rock dropping to the sea, and a small harbor tucked into a cove.

    Read the complete Ikoni write-up.

  2. 2. Mutsamudu

    174 km in.

    Mutsamudu, or Moutsamoudou, is the capital of Anjouan, called Ndzuwani by its people, one of the three islands of the Comoros, and it sits tucked against the north-west coast under steep green hills. The old town climbs the slope in a tangle of narrow alleys, with a medina of coral-stone houses, carved doors and whitewashed mosques, while the harbour below is busy with dhows, small cargo boats and fishermen. A fortress above the town recalls the days when Anjouan's sultans controlled a prosperous trade, and when the island had a reputation as a stopping place for ships on the route to India.
