Why this ride

A 205 km round trip from Mombasa - roughly 3 h 35 in the saddle. The stops in between: The Historic Town and Archaeological Site and back through The Historic Town and Archaeological Site.

The stops

  1. 1. Mombasa

    Start here.

    Mombasa is an island, and that single fact explains its history. A small coral island lies between two inlets on Kenya's coast, joined to the mainland by causeways and a bridge, with a deep natural harbour that made it one of the great ports of the Indian Ocean. Arab, Persian, Indian and Portuguese traders came here for centuries, bringing cloth, spices and slaves and leaving behind a coastal Swahili culture that mixes African and Arab elements.

    Read the complete Mombasa write-up.

  2. 2. The Historic Town and Archaeological Site

    102 km in.

    Gedi does not announce itself. You drive along the Kenyan coast through palms, cashew trees and roadside stalls selling mangoes, turn in under the shade of the Arabuko-Sokoke forest, and suddenly there are coral-stone walls in the trees, a mosque with an arched doorway, a palace courtyard, houses with wells and sleeping niches, all roofed with branches and leaves instead of stone. This was a Swahili town, founded around the twelfth century and flourishing in the fifteenth, when it traded with the Indian Ocean world, with Chinese porcelain and Venetian glass turning up in the digging. By the early seventeenth century the people had gone. Nobody knows exactly why, and the theories, a shift of the coast, raids, or the failure of the water, have never been settled.
