Why this ride

A coastal mountain pass from Da Nang, Vietnam to Hue, Vietnam, via Hai Van summit. Key character: sea views; jungle; historic pass. Surface: paved.

The roads

Hai Van Pass

The 27 km between Near Da Nang and Near Liên Chiểu, mostly on Đèo Hải Vân. Mostly uphill - about 420 m, topping out near 432 m.

Rider note

Check the pass is open before you commit to it.

The stops

  1. 1. Near Da Nang

    Start here.

    Da Nang is a port city on Vietnam's central coast, with a long beach, a wide river and a skyline that has changed dramatically in a generation. Where fishing boats and a French colonial quarter once stood, towers and bridges now line the Han River, and the Dragon Bridge, opened in 2013, spans the water in the shape of a golden dragon that breathes fire and water on weekend evenings. Behind the city, the green mass of the Son Tra peninsula rises above the sea.

  3. 3. Hai Van Pass point 3

    40 km in.

    Lang Co is a thin spit of sand between the South China Sea and a wide, still lagoon, tucked on the northern side of the Hai Van mountains, and for many riders it is the reward at the bottom of the pass. The beach runs for miles, pale and almost empty outside the summer weekends, and behind it the lagoon is dotted with fish farms, oyster stakes and stilt houses. Behind, the green mountains rise steeply, usually with a cloud lying on the ridge. The village is small, but there is fuel, and a line of seafood restaurants along the road serves crab, clams and grilled fish.
