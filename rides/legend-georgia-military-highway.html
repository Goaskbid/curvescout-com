Why this ride

curated scenic road. Greater Caucasus, gorges, historic monuments. A one-way ride of 158 km, Tbilisi to Vladikavkaz - roughly 2 h 52 in the saddle. It goes by way of Zhinvali, Gudauri and St’epants’minda. The highest point is about 2,750 m. It crosses from Georgia into Russia - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. The line favours views over speed.

Scenic road chapters

Georgian Military Highway

Expect company on a summer weekend. Early is better than fast. For 4 km this is Georgian Military Highway, a road with a name of its own and a reason for it.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Near Tbilisi

    Tbilisi spreads along the Mtkvari river in a bowl of dry hills, and its first impression is of balconies. Old houses lean over narrow streets with carved wooden verandas, sulphur bathhouses sit under domes of brick in the Abanotubani district, and on the ridge above, the ruined Narikala fortress watches the city. The name Tbilisi comes from a Georgian word for warm, and the story says that a hunting king found hot springs here and decided to found a city.

  3. 3. Gudauri

    Gudauri is a ski resort first and a village by accident, strung along the road a little below the Jvari Pass in the High Caucasus, at roughly two thousand metres. In winter it is full of Georgians, Russians and Gulf visitors with skis, and the lifts rise from just behind the hotels. In summer it empties out, the slopes turn into flower meadows, and paragliders drift above the grass. Either way the main road, the old Georgian Military Highway, runs straight through the middle, and everything on it, from lorries to cows, goes by at its own speed.

  6. 6. Vladikavkaz

    Vladikavkaz means rule the Caucasus, which tells you what the Russians thought of it when they built a fortress here in 1784. It stands on the Terek river where the mountains meet the plain, capital of North Ossetia, and from the broad avenues you can see the snow-covered chain of the central Caucasus on a clear day. It is a calm, orderly city with a colonial look, with tree-lined streets, a long riverside promenade and a scattering of old merchant houses.
