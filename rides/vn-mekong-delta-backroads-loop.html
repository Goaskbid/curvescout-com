  1. 1. Cần Thơ

    Can Tho is the capital of the Mekong Delta, a flat green country of rivers, canals and orchards that is built by water and travels by water. The city sits on the Hau River, one of the big distributaries of the Mekong, and its riverfront at Ninh Kieu is the place where people stroll in the evening, eat, and watch the boats. The real show is at dawn, when the floating market at Cai Rang gathers boats loaded with pineapples, watermelons and sweet potatoes, each with a sample of its cargo hung on a pole at the bow so that buyers can see what is for sale from a distance.
