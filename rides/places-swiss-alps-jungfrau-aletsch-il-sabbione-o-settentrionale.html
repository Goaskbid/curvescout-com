Why this ride

From Fiesch it runs 450 km to Fiesch; allow about 8 h 45 of riding. On the way it passes Sacro Monte Calvario, Sacro Monte della SS.Trinità, Sacro Monte del Rosario di Varese and Il Sabbione o settentrionale. It tops out around 2,350 m. It crosses from Switzerland into Italy - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

The roads

Swiss Alps Jungfrau-Aletsch to Sacro Monte Calvario

It is 64 km from Swiss Alps Jungfrau-Aletsch to Sacro Monte Calvario on BLS Autoverlad Brig-Iselle. Over the top at 2,516 m - roughly 1820 m up and 2670 m down. Cold up there even in July - pack a warm layer and check it is open.

The stops

  1. 1. Swiss Alps Jungfrau-Aletsch

    Start here.

    No road comes anywhere near. This is a landscape of ice, rock and sky, the first Alpine area to be listed as a World Heritage site, and the riding is all around it rather than through it.

    Read the complete Swiss Alps Jungfrau-Aletsch write-up.

  2. 2. Sacro Monte Calvario

    64 km in.

    Domodossola's holy mountain rises immediately above the town, on the wooded Mattarella hill, and the walk or ride up gives you the whole Ossola valley at your feet. The Sacro Monte Calvario was begun in the seventeenth century as a devotional path of chapels depicting the Passion of Christ, each holding painted and sculpted scenes, leading up to a sanctuary church at the top. It is one of the nine Sacri Monti of Piedmont and Lombardy inscribed together on the UNESCO list, and it has a particular link with Antonio Rosmini, the philosopher-priest who founded his religious institute here in the nineteenth century.

    Read the complete Sacro Monte Calvario write-up.

  3. 3. Sacro Monte della SS.Trinità

    115 km in.

    High above the western shore of Lake Maggiore, in a forest of chestnut and beech above the village of Ghiffa, a small sanctuary dedicated to the Holy Trinity looks out over the water to the hills of Lombardy. The Sacro Monte of Ghiffa began in the seventeenth century as a series of chapels along a path, each meant to illustrate a scene from sacred history for pilgrims, but it was never completed as planned. It is the smallest of the nine Sacri Monti of Piedmont and Lombardy listed by UNESCO in 2003, and its charm lies in its intimacy and its setting: a few chapels, a porticoed walkway and a church in the woods, with the lake shining below.

    Read the complete Sacro Monte della SS.Trinità write-up.

  4. 4. Sacro Monte del Rosario di Varese

    203 km in.

    Above Varese, on the flank of the Campo dei Fiori, a cobbled path climbs two kilometres or so through woods and past fourteen chapels to a medieval sanctuary and the tiny village of Santa Maria del Monte. Each chapel contains a life-size scene of the mysteries of the Rosary, with painted terracotta figures and frescoed backgrounds, and the whole ensemble, built in the seventeenth century under the architect Giuseppe Bernascone, is one of the nine Sacri Monti of Piedmont and Lombardy inscribed as a World Heritage Site in 2003.

    Read the complete Sacro Monte del Rosario di Varese write-up.

  5. 5. Il Sabbione o settentrionale

    225 km in.

    Lake Monate is small, round and remarkably clean, set in the gentle hills of the Varese province between Lake Maggiore and Lake Varese. It has no river flowing into it, being fed by springs, and its water is among the clearest of any lake in Lombardy, which makes it a favourite with swimmers on hot summer days. On its northern shore, near Cadrezzate, the site known as Il Sabbione preserves the remains of a prehistoric village built on piles at the water's edge, part of the UNESCO listing of prehistoric pile dwellings around the Alps. The Varese area is rich in such sites, and the finds from them, including pottery, tools and the remains of wooden canoes, are displayed in local museums.

    Read the complete Il Sabbione o settentrionale write-up.
