Why this ride

103 km from Jibou to Jibou, about 2 h of riding. On the way it passes Moigrad - La Poiana de Sus, Agrij - Coasta Lată and back through Moigrad - La Poiana de Sus. The coast is the point of it.

The roads

Moigrad - La Poiana de Sus → Agrij - Coasta Lată

Moigrad - La Poiana de Sus to Agrij - Coasta Lată: 28 km on DJ108R. It climbs 500 m to the top at 597 m and drops 330 m again. Water alongside for much of it - pick one good pull-out and stop.

The stops

  1. 1. Var - Dealul Tărăvăilor

    Start here.

    Dealul Tărăvăilor is a hill above the village of Var, near Creaca and Jibou, in Sălaj county, north-western Romania, and like many hilltops in this part of the country, it held a Roman watchtower on the frontier of the province of Dacia. The Limes Porolissensis, as the northern frontier is known, was a system of towers, forts and earthworks guarding the roads leading to the great fortress of Porolissum, and the towers were placed on hills with views over the valleys, so that signals could pass quickly along the line.

  2. 2. Moigrad - La Poiana de Sus

    17 km in.

    The Romans did not build their frontier in Dacia as a single wall. They built it as a chain of forts, watchtowers and signal posts along the crest of the Meseș mountains in what is now north-western Romania, and Moigrad is where the chain had its main fortress. The ancient town was called Porolissum, and it controlled the Meseș Gate, a natural pass through the ridge, where merchants paid customs and soldiers watched for raiders from the north. Walk the site today and you find an amphitheatre, a forum, a temple, stretches of the fort wall and long grassy slopes, all overlooking the green valley below.

    Read the complete Moigrad - La Poiana de Sus write-up.

  3. 3. Agrij - Coasta Lată

    45 km in.

    The Meseș mountains are a modest ridge by Carpathian standards, but for the Romans they were a natural wall, and they built their frontier along it. The north-western edge of Dacia ran along these hills, with forts, watchtowers and walls guarding the passes from the plains of the Barbaricum beyond. Agrij - Coasta Lată, near the villages of Stârciu, Buciumi and Meseșenii de Jos, is one of the watchtower sites named in the World Heritage inscription of 2024. Nearby, at Buciumi, are the remains of a fort, and a little further north, at Moigrad, lies Porolissum, the largest Roman site in the region, with a reconstructed gate.
