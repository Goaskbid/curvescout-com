  4. 4. London motorcycle escapes

    Cranbrook stands in the High Weald of Kent, a hilly, wooded country of sandstone ridges and winding lanes, and in the Middle Ages it was one of the richest towns in the county, thanks to the cloth trade. Edward III encouraged Flemish weavers to settle here in the fourteenth century, and the broadcloth they made brought wealth to the town, visible today in the big timber-framed houses and the huge church known as the Cathedral of the Weald. On the edge of the town stands Union Mill, an eighteenth-century smock mill that is the tallest working windmill of its kind in Britain.
