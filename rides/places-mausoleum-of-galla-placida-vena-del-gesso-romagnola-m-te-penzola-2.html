Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Mausoleum of Galla Placida

    It is a surprise how small Galla Placidia's mausoleum is. A cross-shaped brick chamber no bigger than a modest chapel, it stands hidden behind the church of San Vitale in a quiet corner, with plain walls that give no hint of what is inside. Step in and the dark opens into a deep blue night. The vault is covered in tiny gold stars on a field of lapis-coloured glass, and a gold cross shines in the dome above, with figures of the apostles and doves drinking from a fountain in the lunettes. The only light comes through thin alabaster windows, which turn the daylight amber.

  2. 2. Vena del Gesso Romagnola - M.te Mauro

    Driving through the hills of Romagna, south of Faenza, you notice a long, pale ridge cutting across the valleys like a wall. This is the Vena del Gesso Romagnola, a band of gypsum rock about twenty-five kilometres long, and Monte Mauro is its highest point, rising above the villages of Riolo Terme, Brisighella and Fognano. The ridge is now a regional park and part of the UNESCO site for the evaporitic karst of the northern Apennines.

  3. 3. Gessi Bolognesi

    Ride south out of Bologna and within ten minutes the city's suburbs give way to something surprising: a range of low hills made of gypsum, a soft, crystalline rock that dissolves in water and forms caves, sinkholes and strange, shiny outcrops. These are the Gessi Bolognesi, a protected regional park between the Savena and Idice valleys, and in 2023 they became part of the UNESCO site for the evaporitic karst and caves of the northern Apennines.

  4. 4. Vena del Gesso Romagnola - M.te Penzola

    The Santerno valley climbs south from Imola into the Apennines toward Tuscany, and about fifteen kilometres up, the river cuts through a ridge of shining grey-white rock. This is the western end of the Vena del Gesso Romagnola, the gypsum vein that runs across this part of Romagna, and Monte Penzola rises above the village of Borgo Tossignano, with Fontanelice and Casalfiumanese nearby.
