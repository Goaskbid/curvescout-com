  1. 1. Medan

    Medan is the biggest city on Sumatra, a hot, humid, entirely unpretentious place that has been a boom town since the late nineteenth century, when the surrounding highlands and plains were turned into plantations for tobacco, rubber and, later, palm oil. The money of those years is visible in the buildings. The Maimun Palace, built in 1888 for the Sultan of Deli, is a yellow-and-gold mixture of Malay, Mughal and Italian styles, and the nearby Great Mosque, with its black domes, is one of the most elaborate in the country. The mansion of Tjong A Fie, a Chinese merchant who became the richest man in the region, is a fascinating glimpse into the city's multi-ethnic past.
