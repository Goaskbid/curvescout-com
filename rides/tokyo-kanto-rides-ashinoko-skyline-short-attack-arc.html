Why this ride

410 km one way from Sakado - plan on about 7 h 27 of riding. It goes by way of Tanashichō and Tateyama. The climbs: near Narutō and Scenic stretch near Mobara. It tops out at about 850 m. The line favours views over speed.

  2. 2. Near Tanashichō

    The western suburbs of Tokyo go on and on, and Nishitokyo, Kiyose and the old town of Tanashi sit in the middle of them: a quiet, flat, residential belt of low houses, apartment blocks, train stations and shopping streets, about twenty kilometres from the centre. Nishitokyo was formed in 2001 when Tanashi and Hoya merged, and its tallest landmark is a slim tower that carries broadcasting and observation equipment, visible from far away.

  4. 4. Scenic stretch near Mobara

    Mobara is a quiet city in central Chiba, on the eastern side of the Boso Peninsula, where the low hills of the interior meet the long, straight coast of Kujūkuri Beach. The landscape is a gentle mix of rice paddies, woods and small towns, with the hills rising gradually to the south and west.

  5. 5. Tateyama

    Tateyama faces west across the mouth of Tokyo Bay, and its bay is so calm that it has long been called Kagami-ga-ura, the mirror bay. On a still evening, Mount Fuji, across the water, is reflected in the sea, and the sunset turns the whole bay gold. Hōjō beach, in front of the town, is one of the best swimming beaches on the Boso peninsula, with calm water, fine sand and lifeguards in summer.

  6. 6. Food stop near Fujiyoshida

    Fujiyoshida sits on the northern foot of Mount Fuji, and the mountain fills the sky above the town so completely that it can feel almost unreal. For centuries, this was the starting point for pilgrims climbing Fuji, and the great shrine of Kitaguchi Hongū Fuji Sengen Jinja, set in a forest of ancient cedars, marks the traditional beginning of the Yoshida trail. Walking through its huge wooden torii into the dark, quiet forest is a moving experience.
