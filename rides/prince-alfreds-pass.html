Why this ride

Out of Knysna, one way: 75 km, roughly 1 h 15 in the saddle. It goes by way of Avontuur and Prince Alfreds Pass. The highest point is about 1,050 m. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

Scenic road chapters

Prince Alfreds Pass

The valley floor is behind you and the road starts working for its height at about 16.3%. Corners stack above one another where the ground allows nothing straighter — first and second gear more often than not. Look through each corner to the next one and let the engine do the holding.

Rider note

Check the pass is open before you commit to it.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Knysna

    Knysna lies around a tidal lagoon on the Garden Route, with a pair of sandstone cliffs known as the Heads guarding the narrow channel to the sea. Forest runs down to the water, and the whole town has a green, relaxed look, with a population around sixty-nine thousand and a waterfront full of small shops and restaurants. The lagoon is an estuary, home to rare seahorses and a rich oyster industry, and every year there is an oyster festival in the winter that fills the town.

  2. 2. Avontuur

    Prince Alfred's Pass runs north from the forests of Knysna over the Outeniqua range to the dry valley called the Langkloof, and Avontuur sits at its far end, a small farming outpost where the green of the coast gives way to orchards and rock. The name means adventure in Afrikaans, and the pass, opened in 1867 and designed by the road engineer Thomas Bain, is among the finest of his works, climbing in great bends through indigenous forest, across a high stretch of mountain fynbos, and down the northern slope.

  3. 3. Prince Alfreds Pass

    Prince Alfred's Pass is one of the great gravel roads of the Cape, and it is wonderfully unhurried. It runs for around seventy kilometres from the forests of Knysna north to Avontuur, climbing through the Outeniqua Mountains to just over a thousand metres, and it is the sort of road where the journey is more important than the destination. The pass was designed by Thomas Bain and built in the 1860s, and it is named for Queen Victoria's son Prince Alfred, who visited the Cape in 1860.

  4. 4. Uniondale

    Uniondale is a small Karoo town on the northern side of the Outeniqua mountains, a place of whitewashed houses, tin roofs and a long, wide main street that has seen little change in decades. It lies in a dry valley at the foot of the pass, on the road between the coast and the Little Karoo, and it serves as the gateway to Prince Alfred's Pass, one of the most beautiful gravel roads in South Africa. The surrounding country is open and rolling, with sheep and goats grazing among rocky hills.

Recorded leg-by-leg notes

These source notes may contain earlier estimates. They are not turn-by-turn directions or current measured distances.

Knysna to Avontuur

Knysna → Avontuur is 45 km of rhythm rather than effort. Let it flow and let the view breathe.

Avontuur to Prince Alfreds Pass

Avontuur → Prince Alfreds Pass climbs about 1040 m in 6 km. This is the part worth slowing down for - look through the bends and use the pull-offs.

Prince Alfreds Pass to Uniondale

Prince Alfreds Pass → Uniondale drops about 1040 m over 5 km. Brakes and eyes forward; the views are behind the corners, not in them.
