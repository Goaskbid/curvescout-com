Why this ride

From Ligourión it runs 287 km to Ligourión; allow about 4 h 57 of riding. The stops in between: Archaeological Sites of Mycenae and Tiryns, Monasteries of Daphni and back through Archaeological Sites of Mycenae and Tiryns. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Sanctuary of Asklepios at Epidaurus

    Drop a coin in the centre of the orchestra at Epidaurus and people sitting in the top row, fifty-odd rows above, will hear it. The theatre of the sanctuary of Asklepios, built in the fourth century BC, is famous for its acoustics and its almost perfect state of preservation, and it still hosts performances of ancient drama during the summer festival. But the theatre was only part of the sanctuary. Epidaurus was the most famous healing centre of the ancient Greek world, where the sick came to sleep in the sacred buildings and hope that the god Asklepios would visit them in a dream with a cure.

  2. 2. Archaeological Sites of Mycenae and Tiryns

    The plain of Argos is a wide, flat basin of orange groves and olive trees tucked into the Peloponnese, and on its edge, a few kilometres apart, stand the two strongholds that gave the Greek Bronze Age its name. Mycenae sits on a rocky spur between two ravines, with Argos eleven kilometres to the south-west and the sea beyond, and Tiryns lies lower down near the shore, on a low rocky mound that once stood by the water.

  3. 3. Monasteries of Daphni

    The real Daphni monastery is a Byzantine church of the eleventh century on the western edge of Athens, along the old Sacred Way to Eleusis, and it is a UNESCO site together with Hosios Loukas, which lies only a few kilometres from this point.
