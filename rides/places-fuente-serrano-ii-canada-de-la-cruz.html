Why this ride

From Nerpio it runs 177 km to Nerpio; allow about 3 h 03 of riding. The stops in between: Abrigo de las Cañadas I, Letreros de los mártires, Cañada de la Cruz and back through Letreros de los mártires. The highest point is about 1,450 m. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Fuente Serrano II

    Most of the water that Cartagena drinks was once rain on the mountains of the Sierra del Segura. The Taibilla river rises in the high country near Nerpio, and a long chain of canals, tunnels and aqueducts carries it, through a public body created in the 1920s, to taps across Murcia and parts of Alicante and Albacete. It is a good thing to know when you stand beside the dry streambeds of Fuente Serrano, seven kilometres from Nerpio, where the springs seem to have a purpose beyond the paintings in the rock.

  2. 2. Abrigo de las Cañadas I

    High up in the Sierra del Segura, where the province of Albacete meets Granada and Jaén, the village of Nerpio sits at around eleven hundred metres in a country of oak, pine and bare limestone. The Abrigo de las Cañadas, a rock shelter, lies about twelve kilometres from the village among its dry gullies, in one of the most thinly settled corners of Castilla-La Mancha.

  3. 3. Letreros de los mártires

    Out in the high north-east of Granada province, Huéscar feels a long way from the Alhambra and the beaches of the Costa Tropical. This is the altiplano, a broad upland of wheat fields, almond trees and pale badlands at around a thousand metres, ringed by mountains, with the great limestone hump of La Sagra rising to the north. The rock shelter called Letreros de los Mártires lies in this country, a dozen or so kilometres from the town.

  4. 4. Cañada de la Cruz

    The Cañada de la Cruz is a high valley or drovers' pasture in the Segura mountains; cañada is the word for the traditional livestock routes and grazing grounds used by shepherds moving their flocks between summer and winter pastures, and this part of Spain has a long history of transhumance.
