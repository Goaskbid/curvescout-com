Why this ride

From Bayramaly it runs 474 km to Bayramaly; allow about 9 h of riding. It goes by way of Togoluk, Akja Gala Caravanserai and back through Togoluk. The line favours views over speed.

The stops

  1. 1. State Historical and Cultural Park “Ancien

    Start here.

    South of the Karakum and just outside the modern city of Mary, the ground is littered with the ghosts of cities. Nagym Kala, the Greater and Lesser forts, are two of the walled settlements that make up the old Merv oasis, one of the great cities of the Silk Road. The Murgab river spreads out here into a delta that has fed farmers for thousands of years, and Merv grew up where the water met the desert.

    Read the complete State Historical and Cultural Park “Ancien write-up.

  2. 2. Togoluk

    77 km in. The ride passes about 5 km from it.

    What I can say is that the surrounding land belongs to the Murghab oasis, where a river born in the Afghan mountains fans out into irrigated fields, then dies in the sand. This is one of the oldest farming and trading landscapes in Central Asia, and everything around here is shaped by water and by the lack of it.

    Read the complete Togoluk write-up.

  3. 3. Akja Gala Caravanserai

    227 km in. The ride passes about 13 km from it.

    The Silk Road across the Karakum was never a single road. It was a series of wells, forts and caravanserais, a day's travel apart, where camels could drink and merchants could sleep behind walls. Akja Gala is one of these shelters, a square brick building with a central courtyard and rooms around it, set in sand and saxaul scrub between Merv and the Amu Darya, the river called the Oxus by the Greeks.

    Read the complete Akja Gala Caravanserai write-up.
