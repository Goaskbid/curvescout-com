Why this ride

From Ogasawara it runs 117 km to Ogasawara; allow about 2 h 08 of riding. On the way it passes Some parts of Hahajima and peripheral reef and back through Some parts of Hahajima and peripheral reef. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The stops

  1. 1. Some parts of Chichijima and peripheral re

    Start here.

    Chichijima and Hahajima are the two inhabited islands of the Ogasawara chain, and the only ones where a visitor can really spend time. Chichijima, the father island, has the port village of Futami, a handful of beaches, quiet roads and a small museum that tells the settlers' story. Hahajima, the mother island, lies about fifty kilometres further south, a long green ridge with a tiny village and trails that climb through forest.

    Read the complete Some parts of Chichijima and peripheral re write-up.

  2. 2. Some parts of Hahajima and peripheral reef

    About 59 km in.

    The Ogasawara Islands lie about a thousand kilometres south of Tokyo, in the Pacific, and getting there takes a full day on a ferry, which is a good start to understanding why they have remained so special. The Bonin Islands, as they are also known, have never been connected to a continent, and as a result the plants and animals that reached them evolved in isolation, giving rise to a wealth of species found nowhere else. UNESCO listed the islands in 2011, and they are often called the Galapagos of the East.
