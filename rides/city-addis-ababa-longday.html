Why this ride

From Addis Ababa it runs 183 km to Addis Ababa; allow about 3 h 09 of riding. The stops in between: Tiya and back through Tiya. The highest point is about 2,700 m.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Addis Ababa

    At about 2,350 metres, Addis Ababa is one of the highest capitals on Earth, and you feel it in the first hour: the sun is strong, the air is thin and cool, and a flight of stairs is a small event. The city sits in a bowl of eucalyptus-covered hills, founded in the 1880s by Emperor Menelik II and his wife Taytu, whose name for it, the new flower, stuck. It is a sprawling, restless, cheerful place, and the pace of building is astonishing.

  2. 2. Tiya

    Tiya lies some ninety kilometres south of Addis Ababa, on the high plateau of the Great Rift's northern edge, and it holds one of the strangest open-air museums in Africa. In a field beside a village, dozens of weathered stone pillars stand in clusters, some leaning, some upright, each carved with swords, symbols and abstract figures. They are stelae, grave markers raised several centuries ago, probably between the twelfth and fourteenth, by a culture whose name has been lost. UNESCO listed the site in 1980, and a custodian will usually walk you around and explain which carvings are thought to show warriors and which show something nobody has managed to read.
