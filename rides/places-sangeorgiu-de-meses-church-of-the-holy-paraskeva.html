Why this ride

A 300 km round trip from Cizer - roughly 5 h 10 in the saddle. It goes by way of Cliț - Fața Chicerii, Church of the Holy Paraskeva and back through Cliț - Fața Chicerii. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Sângeorgiu de Meseș

    The village of Sangeorgiu de Meses lies on the southern flank of the Meses mountains, in the old Hungarian-Romanian borderland of Salaj, and its name carries the saint of horsemen, George, and the mountain that gives the area its character. It is a place that rides begin and end, which suits it well: a quiet village with a church, a shop, a couple of bars, and an easy exit onto roads that climb in several directions. A few kilometres away is Cizer, and a little further, the small settlements of Starciu and Mal, each with its own cluster of orchards and farm tracks.

  2. 2. Cliț - Fața Chicerii

    Northern Sălaj is quiet country: low wooded hills, hay meadows, plum orchards and villages strung along the Someș river and its side valleys, with wooden churches and haystacks built around poles in the fields. Cliț is a small village near Băbeni, and the point named Fața Chicerii lies in the hills above it. Names in this list follow the pattern of the Romanian archaeological record, a village and then the local name of a hillside, and in this part of Sălaj many such points belong to the line of watchtowers and small forts of the Roman frontier of Dacia, which UNESCO recognised in 2024.

  3. 3. Church of the Holy Paraskeva

    In 1780 a painter named Radu Munteanu covered the inside of the wooden church at Desești with scenes that still shock and delight: a Last Judgment in which sinners are marched off to a hell full of grinning devils, alongside saints, prophets and lively village figures. The church, dedicated to the Holy Paraskeva and built in 1770 from oak logs, rises above the village with a tall, slender tower and a steep double roof of shingles. It is one of the eight wooden churches of Maramureș inscribed by UNESCO in 1999, and its paintings are among the best preserved of them all.
