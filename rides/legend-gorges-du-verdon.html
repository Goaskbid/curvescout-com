Why this ride

Europe's deepest canyon, with the Corniche Sublime on the south rim. 48 km from Aups to Riez, about 58 min of riding. It tops out around 1,150 m. The line favours views over speed.

The roads

Gorges du Verdon

It is 14 km from Gorges du Verdon point 1 to Near Castellane. Downhill for the most part, 350 m in all, never above 926 m. Over quickly - ride it slowly.

The stops

  1. 1. Gorges du Verdon point 1

    Start here.

    Where the Verdon canyon is at its deepest, the walls fall away on the south rim in cliffs of pale limestone that plunge several hundred metres to a river the colour of a green bottle. La Palud-sur-Verdon, on the northern edge, is the little hub for the Route des Crêtes, the one-way loop that runs along the rim of the gorge, and this stretch of road is its starting ground. Castellane is to the east and Aups to the west, each seventeen kilometres or so by road.

    Read the complete Gorges du Verdon point 1 write-up.

  2. 2. Near Castellane

    14 km in.

    West of Castellane, the Verdon river disappears into the earth. Or that is how it looks from the road: the D952 climbs away from the town along the river, through a valley of pale limestone, and then the river plunges into the Grand Canyon du Verdon, a gorge up to seven hundred metres deep, while the road clings to the northern rim above. This spot lies on that rim, near Point Sublime and the village of Rougon, where the view down into the gorge is one of the most famous in France.

    Read the complete Near Castellane write-up.

  3. 3. Near Riez

    The end: 48 km.

    On the eastern side of Riez, the Valensole plateau starts to tip down toward the Verdon, and this point lies in that open country between Riez, Moustiers-Sainte-Marie and the great turquoise reservoir of the Lac de Sainte-Croix. It is a land of wide fields and big skies, where in June and July the lavender fields bloom in long purple rows, filling the air with a scent so strong you can smell it through your helmet.

    Read the complete Near Riez write-up.
