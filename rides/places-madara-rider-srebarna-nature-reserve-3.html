Why this ride

From Kaspichan it runs 290 km to Kaspichan; allow about 5 h of riding. On the way it passes Thracian Tomb of Sveshtari, Srebarna Nature Reserve and back through Thracian Tomb of Sveshtari. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Madara Rider

    Above the village of Madara, on a vertical cliff about a hundred metres up, a horseman is carved into the rock, thrusting a spear at a lion that lies under his horse, while a dog runs behind. The Madara Rider is the only relief of its kind in Europe, made in the early eighth century by the first Bulgarian state, probably to celebrate a victory by Khan Tervel, and it is a rare relic of the pre-Christian era of the country. UNESCO listed it in 1979. Beside it are Greek inscriptions recording the deeds of Bulgarian khans, among the oldest written documents in the country.

  2. 2. Thracian Tomb of Sveshtari

    In a quiet corner of the Ludogorie, the wooded plateau of north-eastern Bulgaria, a grassy mound hides one of the most remarkable tombs in the Balkans. Sveshtari is a Thracian tomb of the Getae, a tribe that lived on both sides of the lower Danube, and it dates from the third century BC. Over the burial chamber, the builders raised a mound, and inside they built a stone vestibule, a corridor and three chambers, decorated with carved and painted figures, the likes of which are not found anywhere else in the Thracian world. UNESCO listed it in 1985.

  3. 3. Srebarna Nature Reserve

    Srebarna is a freshwater lake beside the Danube in the far northeast of Bulgaria, a few kilometres from the Romanian border, and it is one of the most important wetlands in Europe. The lake and its reed beds are separated from the river by a dyke, and in spring the water level is topped up by the Danube floods. UNESCO listed the reserve in 1983, and it is also a Ramsar wetland and a biosphere reserve.
