Why this ride

From Kronstadt it runs 200 km to Kronstadt; allow about 4 h of riding. The stops in between: The Park «Alexandria», Krasnoe Selo, The Lugovoy and back through Krasnoe Selo.

The roads

Saint Petersburg Imperial Shore

The 36 km between Forts of the Island Kotlin and The Park «Alexandria», mostly on КАД. An easy profile, never above 60 m. Water alongside for much of it - pick one good pull-out and stop.

The stops

  1. 1. Forts of the Island Kotlin

    Start here.

    Kotlin island carries so many forts that the usual way to describe it is as a single stone ship moored in the middle of the gulf. The whole island is fortified, and you can see the evidence from the water, from the road and from the air: low batteries, round towers, harbour walls and the great forts built on artificial islands in the shallows. Many carry the names of tsars and grand dukes, and some, like the fort known as Alexander I, have a more sinister reputation, since it was used as a research station for plague bacteria in the early twentieth century and is still called the Plague Fort.

    Read the complete Forts of the Island Kotlin write-up.

  2. 2. The Park «Alexandria»

    36 km in.

    Alexandria Park is one part of the great Peterhof estate that most visitors skip, which is a good reason to visit it. Peterhof is known for its Grand Cascade and golden statues, the fountains that Peter the Great designed to rival Versailles, and for the crowds that pack the Lower Park in summer. Alexandria, on the other side of the road, is quieter, with winding paths, ponds, old oaks and a collection of small romantic buildings set among the trees.

    Read the complete The Park «Alexandria» write-up.

  3. 3. Krasnoe Selo

    64 km in.

    Readers of Anna Karenina will know Krasnoye Selo, even if they have never been within a thousand kilometres of it. It was here, at the summer camp of the imperial guards, that Tolstoy set the officers' steeplechase in which Vronsky rides his mare Frou-Frou to disaster in front of the court. For more than a century, from the early nineteenth century until the revolution, the guards regiments of the Russian army gathered each summer in this area south-west of Saint Petersburg for manoeuvres, parades and horse races, and the tsar and the court came to watch. It is listed as one of the monuments associated with the historic centre of Saint Petersburg.

    Read the complete Krasnoe Selo write-up.

  4. 4. The Lugovoy

    99 km in.

    Peterhof is the work of a tsar who wanted his own Versailles on the Gulf of Finland. Peter the Great began the palace and its gardens in the early eighteenth century, and by the time of his daughter Elizabeth and later of Catherine the Great, the Great Cascade, with its gilded statues and its fountains, was one of the most extravagant waterworks in Europe. Behind the formal gardens, the Lugovoy, meaning the meadow park, is a larger, quieter landscape of open lawns, ponds, tree-lined paths and old oaks, a place where Petersburgers come to walk dogs and push prams on weekends.

    Read the complete The Lugovoy write-up.
