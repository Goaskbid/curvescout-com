Why this ride

From Ulldecona it runs 92 km to Ulldecona; allow about 1 h 35 of riding. On the way it passes Cova dels Rossegadors o del Polvorin, La fenellosa and back through Cova dels Rossegadors o del Polvorin. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Abric d'Ermites IIIb

    The ridge behind Ulldecona has a name for each of its overhangs. Abric d'Ermites IIIb is a neighbour of the IIIa shelter, a twin in the numbering and a few paces away in the rock, and its paintings tell the same story in a slightly different voice. The red figures belong to the Levantine tradition, and the group in this part of Catalonia is part of the UNESCO listing of Mediterranean basin art.

  2. 2. Cova dels Rossegadors o del Polvorin

    The Tinença de Benifassà is one of the emptiest corners of the Valencian Community, a group of tiny stone villages scattered across rugged limestone mountains in the far north of Castellón, right where Valencia, Catalonia and Aragón meet. The Cova dels Rossegadors, also called the Cova del Polvorí, lies on the edge of this country near Rossell, with Mas de Barberans and Canet lo Roig the other nearby villages.

  3. 3. La fenellosa

    People in Aragón like to call the Matarraña their Tuscany, and for once the comparison is not entirely marketing. It is a comarca of honey-coloured stone villages on hilltops, olive groves on terraces, almond trees and vineyards, with the limestone wall of the Ports de Beseit rising behind. La Fenellosa lies in this country, between Valderrobres, Fuentespalda and Mas de Barberans across the Catalan line, in the rough hills where the farmland gives way to the mountains.
