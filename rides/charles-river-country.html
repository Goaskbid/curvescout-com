Why this ride

From Needham it runs 46 km to Medfield; allow about 53 min of riding. It goes by way of Dover and Sherborn. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

  2. 2. Dover

    Dover has a reputation for money, and a first look at its roads confirms it: long stone walls, tall oak trees and big, discreet houses set well back from the road. It is one of the more rural towns in the Boston area, with no real town centre, no streetlights to speak of and a strong tradition of preserving open land. It is also one of the best places to ride a motorcycle within half an hour of the city, because it has the kind of roads that developers have not yet got around to improving.

  3. 3. Sherborn

    Sherborn is a very small town with a very old pedigree. It was settled in the 1650s, incorporated in 1674, and it was only a few years old when it was caught up in King Philip's War, the vicious conflict between New England colonists and the Native peoples that devastated frontier towns in 1675 and 1676. The scars of that time are gone from the landscape, but the town still has the compact, thoughtful air of a place that has been looking after itself for a long time, with a green, a library and a few white houses around the meeting place.
