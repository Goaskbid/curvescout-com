Why this ride

curated scenic road. High Atlas, kasbahs, hairpins. 131 km from Marrakesh to Ouarzazat, about 2 h 23 of riding. On the way it passes Zerkten, Telouet and Amerzgane. The highest point is about 2,250 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Scenic road chapters

Tizi n'Tichka Pass

Expect company on a summer weekend. Early is better than fast. For 9 km this is Tizi n'Tichka Pass, a road with a name of its own and a reason for it.

Rider note

Check the pass is open before you commit to it.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Marrakesh

    Red walls, palm trees and the white peaks of the High Atlas on the horizon: that is the first impression of Marrakesh, and on a clear winter morning, with snow on the mountains to the south, it is unforgettable. The city stands on the Haouz plain in central Morocco, a sprawling, noisy, beautiful place of nearly a million people, built around a walled medina that has been a centre of trade and power for close to a thousand years.
