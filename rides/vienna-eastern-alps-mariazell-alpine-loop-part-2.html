Why this ride

Out of Vienna and back again: 240 km, roughly 4 h 22 in the saddle. The highest point is about 1,050 m.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Vienna

    Vienna rewards the rider who parks the bike and walks. The Innere Stadt, the old centre, sits inside the line of the Ringstrasse, the great boulevard laid out in the nineteenth century where the medieval walls once stood, and it is lined with the opera house, the parliament, the university and a procession of grand museums. At the heart is Stephansdom, the cathedral with a roof of coloured tiles and a tall south tower that has been a landmark for centuries. The Hofburg, once the imperial palace, takes up a sizable chunk of the neighbourhood.

    Read the complete Vienna write-up.

  2. 2. Mariazell

    According to legend, in 1157 a Benedictine monk named Magnus was travelling through these forests with a small wooden statue of the Virgin when a rock blocked his path; he prayed, the rock split, and he settled on the spot, placing the statue on a tree stump and building a cell around it. That cell became Mariazell, Austria's most important place of pilgrimage. The great basilica that now dominates the little town, with its Gothic tower flanked by two Baroque ones, still houses the statue, and every year hundreds of thousands of pilgrims come, many on foot from Vienna.
