Why this ride

From Negrilești it runs 76 km to Negrilești; allow about 1 h 19 of riding. It goes by way of Perișor - Vârful Zgăului, Șintereag - Dealul Oului and back through Perișor - Vârful Zgăului. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Ciceu-Poieni - Strunga Găvojdenilor

    The hills east of Bistrita are the northern edge of the Transylvanian plateau, and Ciceu-Poieni lies in their folds. The label, Strunga Gavojdenilor, points to a saddle or gap on the ridge, and the word strunga means a narrow passage where sheep were once driven between pastures. Bistrita, the nearest large town, was a Saxon city, founded by German settlers invited by the Hungarian kings in the Middle Ages, and its medieval centre still shows the trace of that past.

  2. 2. Perișor - Vârful Zgăului

    Perișor lies in the hills north of Năsăud, in the county of Bistrița-Năsăud, and Vârful Zgăului is the hilltop to which the label points. Zagra and Spermezeu are the nearest villages, while the great wall of the Rodna Mountains stands to the east, the highest part of the northern Eastern Carpathians, with peaks over two thousand metres and glacial lakes among the crags. The Rodna range is a national park and a biosphere reserve, home to chamois, marmots, brown bears and a rich alpine flora. On a clear day the sharp, pale line of the ridge can be seen from many of the hills around.

  3. 3. Șintereag - Dealul Oului

    Egg Hill, Dealul Oului, is a gentle rise near the village of Șintereag in Bistrița-Năsăud, and the Romans used it as a watch post on the north-eastern frontier of Dacia. Like the other sites in this area, it is part of the frontier listed by UNESCO in 2024, and like many of them, it is more a place to imagine than to see: a low hill, a few traces in the ground, a wide view over the valley of the Șieu and the hills toward Bistrița. Șintereag, a village of a few thousand, lies close by, with Șieu-Odorhei and Cociu as neighbours.
