Why this ride

National Scenic Byway. river canyon, Rockies, high pass. A one-way ride of 116 km, Fort Collins to Walden - roughly 2 h 07 in the saddle. The highest point is about 3,150 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Fort Collins

    Fort Collins is a university town of about a hundred and sixty thousand people at the foot of the Rockies in northern Colorado, with Colorado State University at its heart and a well-preserved Old Town of brick buildings, trees and fountains. It is a famously bicycle-friendly place, and also a famous place for beer: New Belgium, Odell and dozens of smaller breweries started here, and the craft beer scene is serious. It has a relaxed, outdoorsy feel, and a motorcycle is very much at home.

  4. 4. Walden

    Walden sits in the middle of North Park, an enormous high basin in northern Colorado, ringed by mountains, and it is one of the most remote little county seats in the state. About six hundred people live here, at around eight thousand one hundred feet, in a village of low buildings and wide streets, with sagebrush and hay meadows stretching away on every side. The Medicine Bow Mountains to the north and the Never Summer range to the south frame the view, and the wind is a constant companion.
