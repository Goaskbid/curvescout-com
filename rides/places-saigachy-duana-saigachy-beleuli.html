Why this ride

It goes by way of Saigachy, Saigachy-Beleuli and back through Saigachy. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Saigachy-Duana

    There is nothing here with a name on a signpost, and that is the point. The label comes from the saiga, the strange, bulbous-nosed antelope that has lived on these steppes since the days of mammoths, and the plateau's rim breaks away in cliffs, called chinks, that glow white, pink and ochre when the light is low.

  2. 2. Saigachy

    Out on the plateau east of the Caspian, there are places where you can drive for a full day and meet nothing but sky. The Ustyurt, a vast, flat tableland of clay and limestone between the Caspian Sea and the Aral Sea basin, was the route of caravans for centuries, travelling between the oases of Khiva and the Mangyshlak coast, and old wells and graveyards still mark the way.

  3. 3. Saigachy-Beleuli

    This is a place where the map offers few names and even fewer roads. What I can describe is the landscape, and it is one of the most austere in the world: pale, stony ground, scattered tufts of saltwort and wormwood, and a horizon as flat as a table, broken only by the edge of the plateau where it drops in chalk cliffs known locally as chinks.
