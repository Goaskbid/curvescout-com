Why this ride

From Firminy it runs 404 km to Firminy; allow about 6 h 58 of riding. On the way it passes Couvent Sainte-Marie-de-la-Tourette, Hautecombe and back through Couvent Sainte-Marie-de-la-Tourette. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Maison de la Culture de Firminy

    Firminy is a coal-and-steel town in the Loire valley that happens to hold the largest group of Le Corbusier buildings in Europe. The architect was invited here in the 1950s to plan a new neighbourhood, Firminy-Vert, for workers whose old houses were crumbling, and over the next decades several of his designs went up: a housing block, a stadium, a youth and culture centre and, finished long after his death, a great concrete church. The Maison de la Culture is the long, bold building with a sagging roof that looks as if it had been poured from a jug, and it is part of the UNESCO listing given to his work in 2016.

  2. 2. Couvent Sainte-Marie-de-la-Tourette

    A concrete box on stilts, set on a slope among trees, is not what you expect from a Dominican priory. Le Corbusier designed the convent of La Tourette in the late 1950s for the friars of the order, and it was completed in 1960, with the young composer and architect Iannis Xenakis working on its rhythmic window walls. The church is a bare cube of rough concrete, lit by a few carefully placed openings, and the cells for the friars are small and very quiet, each with a balcony. It is a place where the building is the sermon, and it was added to the UNESCO list in 2016.

  3. 3. Hautecombe

    Hautecombe Abbey stands on the western shore of Lac du Bourget, in Savoy, and it is almost impossible to reach without seeing the lake first. The Lac du Bourget is the largest natural lake entirely in France, a deep blue sheet under limestone cliffs, and the abbey sits on a small rocky spur above the water, with a boat landing and a church whose tower is visible across the bay. The poet Lamartine, who fell in love here in 1816, wrote a famous poem called Le Lac, about a lost love at this water.
