Why this ride

From Beniatjar it runs 264 km to Beniatjar; allow about 4 h 14 of riding. On the way it passes Cova de la Catxupa- Abric I, Garganta de la Hoz I and back through Cova de la Catxupa- Abric I. The line favours views over speed.

The stops

  1. 1. Penya de Benicadell

    Start here.

    From almost anywhere in the southern half of Valencia province you can see it: a long, jagged ridge of grey limestone with a sharp tooth at its eastern end, standing on the skyline like a broken saw. The Benicadell, at 1,104 metres, separates the Vall d'Albaida to the north from the Comtat to the south, and the sharp summit, the Penya, is the mountain's signature. At its foot lie the villages of Beniarrés, Ràfol de Salem and Otos.

    Read the complete Penya de Benicadell write-up.

  2. 2. Cova de la Catxupa- Abric I

    75 km in.

    The Marina Alta is the northern Costa Blanca at its most mixed: villas on every slope, a ribbon of coast running from Moraira to Jávea, and just behind it, farm terraces, dry stone walls and small rock shelters where prehistoric people left their mark. The Cova de la Catxupa, near Teulada and Benitachell, is one of them, a shelter in the hills a few kilometres from the sea, listed with the other Mediterranean rock art of the Iberian Peninsula.

    Read the complete Cova de la Catxupa- Abric I write-up.

  3. 3. Garganta de la Hoz I

    132 km in. The ride passes about 11 km from it.

    Behind the beaches and high-rises of the Costa Blanca, a few kilometres inland from Alicante and El Campello, the land rises quickly into dry limestone hills, scrub and almond terraces. This is the beginning of the Betic ranges, and the area is cut by ravines, barrancos, which carry water only after heavy storms and have carved gorges in the soft rock. Garganta de la Hoz is the name of such a gorge, a narrow cut in the hills, and the I suggests that more than one point on the ride lies along it.
