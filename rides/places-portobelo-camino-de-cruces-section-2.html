Why this ride

A 272 km round trip from Portobelo - roughly 4 h 39 in the saddle. On the way it passes Colón and San Vicente. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The stops

  1. 1. Portobelo

    Start here.

    Columbus anchored in this bay in 1502 and called it the beautiful port, which was generous of him, because the bay is lovely and the climate is a wet, steamy furnace. Portobelo is now a sleepy village of fewer than fifteen hundred people on Panama's Caribbean coast, with the jungle pressing down from the hills and a string of grey stone forts along the shore. It is hard to believe that for a couple of centuries this was one of the richest places on earth.

    Read the complete Portobelo write-up.

  2. 2. Near Colón

    65 km in.

    West of the Panama Canal's Caribbean end, the land goes quiet surprisingly fast. You cross from the Colón side on the Atlantic Bridge, the long cable-stayed span that finally replaced the old swing bridge and the ferry over the canal entrance, and within minutes the container cranes give way to rainforest, old American base buildings and a narrow road heading north toward the mouth of the Chagres River.

    Read the complete Near Colón write-up.

    128 km in.

    North of Panama City, the Transístmica highway climbs out of the sprawl toward the Caribbean, following the old route across the isthmus.
