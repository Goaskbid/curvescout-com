  1. 1. Kajaani

    Kajaani is a quiet town in central Finland, in the region of Kainuu, but its quiet is deceptive, because in this modest place a good part of Finnish culture was written down. In the 1830s the physician Elias Lönnrot worked here as a district doctor and traveled through the surrounding villages, collecting oral poetry from singers, and from those verses he compiled the Kalevala, the national epic of Finland, first published in 1835. A statue and a small museum in town recall his work.
