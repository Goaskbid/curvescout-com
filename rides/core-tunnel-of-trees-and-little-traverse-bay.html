Why this ride

A narrow lakeside road under forest canopy, paired with Lake Michigan towns. The road: M-119 and US-31. Distance and riding time are estimates from the checkpoints until the road is measured on this page.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Harbor Springs

    Harbor Springs is a small resort town on the north shore of Little Traverse Bay, in the top left corner of Michigan's mitten, and it has one of the nicest natural harbors on the Great Lakes. The bay is long and deep, sheltered from the big waves of Lake Michigan, and the town curves along its edge, with sailboats at anchor in the harbor and handsome summer houses on the hills. The area's Native name, Waganakising, meaning crooked tree, is still used by the Odawa people, whose community remains strong here.
