Why this ride

208 km from Yarmolyntsi to Yarmolyntsi, about 4 h of riding. The stops in between: FELSCHTIN Felschtin, KATERINOWKA Katerinowka and back through FELSCHTIN Felschtin.

The stops

  1. 1. BARANOWKA Baranowka

    Start here.

    Podolia, the rolling country of western Ukraine between the Dniester and the Southern Bug, is a land of black soil, limestone canyons and old towns. It was a frontier between Poland, Lithuania, the Ottomans and Russia for centuries, and many of its villages carry the layers of that history, with Catholic and Orthodox churches, ruined castles and Jewish cemeteries. The label here, Baranowka, with its Polish-style spelling, gives a clue to the mix, and the ride pairing with Satanivska Dacha, a forested nature area in the region, hints at the walk and the ride on offer.

    Read the complete BARANOWKA Baranowka write-up.

  2. 2. FELSCHTIN Felschtin

    68 km in.

    Felshtin is a name that has almost disappeared from maps. The Yiddish name belongs to a small town in Podolia, in western Ukraine, now called Hvardiiske, where for generations Jews and Ukrainians lived side by side among the orchards and rolling fields. The Jewish community lived by trade and craft, with a synagogue, a market square and a cemetery on the edge of town. In February 1919, during the chaos of the civil war, the town was attacked, and hundreds of its Jewish residents were murdered in one of the worst pogroms of that period. Later the community was destroyed completely in the Second World War.

    103 km in.

    Katerinowka is a village in the Khmelnytskyi region of western Ukraine, in the rolling farmland of Podolia, and on the map it is mainly a point on a quiet back road between larger settlements.
