  1. 1. Norwegian Scenic Route Havoysund point 1

    Out here the road to Havøysund begins to feel like the edge of the map. This is Finnmark, the northernmost county of Norway, and the land is a rolling plateau of bare rock, bog, low birch scrub and small lakes, with no tall trees to break the wind. The road leaves the main highway near Skaidi and heads north across the tundra, and the sense of space is powerful: low hills fade into each other, the sky is huge and the light has a clear, hard quality. In summer the sun does not set for weeks, and in winter it vanishes entirely.

  2. 2. Norwegian Scenic Route Havoysund point 2

    Halfway along the Havøysund road the land begins to tilt toward the sea. The plateau is lower here, the lakes are bigger and the first fjords start to cut into the coast, with long inlets of cold, dark water between rounded hills. The vegetation is thin, a few birches in sheltered hollows and otherwise heather, lichen and dwarf shrubs, and in autumn the whole landscape turns red and gold with astonishing intensity. In summer the cotton grass makes white patches in the bogs.

  3. 3. Near Ávanuorri

    The last kilometres of the road to Havøysund run across one of the barest landscapes in Norway, a coast of grey rock, heather and lichen, with the Barents Sea on one side and nothing taller than a person for miles. Ávanuorri is the Sami name for Havøysund, the fishing village at the end of the road, and this point lies just before you reach it, on the open stretch where the road makes its final run toward the village.

  4. 4. Ávanuorri

    Havøysund, Ávanuorri in Sami, is a fishing village at the very end of a road at the top of Norway, in Måsøy municipality, where the land runs out into the Barents Sea. It is a working place, not a pretty one: a harbour with fishing boats, a fish processing plant, rows of houses painted in bright colours against the grey rock, and a sense that the people here have chosen to live with the weather rather than fight it. The Hurtigruten coastal ships call here on their way between Hammerfest and Honningsvåg.
