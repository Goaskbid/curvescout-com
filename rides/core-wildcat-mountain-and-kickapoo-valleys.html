Why this ride

Tight coulee roads and wooded ridges in Wisconsin's strongest riding area. The road: WI-33, WI-131 and WI-82. Distance and riding time are estimates from the checkpoints until the road is measured on this page.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Baraboo

    Baraboo is a small city in a very old landscape. The ridges that curl around it, the Baraboo Range, are among the most ancient rock formations in the Midwest, made of tough pink quartzite laid down well over a billion years ago and later pushed up into a crescent of bluffs that glaciers then flowed around, not over. The most famous piece of this is Devil's Lake, a deep, clear lake held in a gorge, with sheer purple cliffs above and a state park that is one of the most visited in Wisconsin.

  4. 4. Viroqua

    Viroqua sits on a ridge in the Driftless country of southwestern Wisconsin, and the rolling, many-folded land around it is the point. The glaciers that flattened most of the Midwest flowed around this region, and the result is a maze of steep hills, narrow valleys and winding creeks that feels more like Appalachia than Wisconsin. Farms cling to the ridgetops, and the valleys below hold cool springs and trout streams, so every road seems to climb a crest or dive into a hollow.
