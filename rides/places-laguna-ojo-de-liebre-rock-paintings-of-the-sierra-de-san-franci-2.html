Why this ride

A 347 km round trip from Guerrero Negro - roughly 5 h 59 in the saddle. On the way it passes El Vizcaíno, Rock Paintings of the Sierra de San Franci and back through El Vizcaíno. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Laguna Ojo de Liebre

    Ojo de Liebre, which means hare's eye, is a huge shallow lagoon on the Pacific side of Baja California, and every winter it receives the largest gathering of grey whales on earth. The animals travel about ten thousand kilometres from the Bering and Chukchi seas, one of the longest migrations of any mammal, to give birth in warm, sheltered water. Calves are born between January and March, and in the lagoon, mothers teach them to swim and surface.

  2. 2. El Vizcaíno

    Baja California is a long, thin peninsula, and El Vizcaino is where it gets properly wide. The biosphere reserve covers a huge, dry belt of the central peninsula, from the Pacific lagoons on one side to the Sierra de San Francisco on the other, and it is named after Sebastian Vizcaino, the Spanish captain who sailed these coasts in 1602. Scattered over the desert are the strange plants that make Baja famous, the towering cardon cactus, the squat elephant tree, and the cirio or boojum, a tall tapering spike that looks like an upside-down carrot with a few frizzy branches. After a rare rain the ground is briefly green.

  3. 3. Rock Paintings of the Sierra de San Franci

    Far down the Baja California peninsula, in the mountains above the Pacific coast, are some of the finest rock paintings in the Americas. The Sierra de San Francisco is a rugged range of volcanic mesas and deep canyons, and in the shelters and overhangs along the canyon walls the Cochimí people and their ancestors painted enormous figures: humans, deer, rabbits, birds, snakes and whales, in red, black, white and yellow. Many of the human figures are life-size or bigger, with arms raised, some pierced by arrows, and the older panels are thought to be a few hundred to several thousand years old. UNESCO listed the site in 1993.
