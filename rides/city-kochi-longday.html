Why this ride

From Cochin it runs 419 km to Cochin; allow about 9 h 16 of riding. On the way it passes Eravikulam National Park, Mankulam Range, Mannavan Shola and Grass Hills National Park. It tops out around 1,600 m. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

The roads

Munnar Tea Country Loop

From Kochi to Eravikulam National Park is 172 km. Over the top at 1,769 m - roughly 2680 m up and 1210 m down. Fill the tank before the climb.

The stops

  1. 1. Kochi

    Start here.

    Kochi is the old spice port of Kerala, and it still has the look of a place where traders from many countries have come and gone. Arab, Chinese, Portuguese, Dutch and British merchants all passed through, and the old quarter around Fort Kochi keeps traces of them: Chinese fishing nets on the waterfront, a Portuguese-era church, a synagogue in Jew Town and colonial houses facing the water. The city itself is spread across islands and the mainland, tied together by bridges and ferries, with a lot of water around.

    Read the complete Kochi write-up.

  2. 2. Eravikulam National Park

    172 km in.

    Anamudi is the highest mountain in India south of the Himalaya, and it rises from the Eravikulam plateau like a dark tooth. The national park, in the high Western Ghats near Munnar in Kerala, protects a landscape of rolling grassland, thick shola forest in the folds of the hills, and open skies, at heights above two thousand metres. It forms part of the Western Ghats World Heritage Site, inscribed by UNESCO in 2012, and it is the best place in the world to see the Nilgiri tahr, a stocky wild goat that lives on steep, grassy slopes.

    Read the complete Eravikulam National Park write-up.

  3. 3. Mankulam Range

    209 km in.

    The Mankulam range lies in the high country of Idukki district in Kerala, in the Munnar uplands that form the highest part of the Western Ghats south of the Nilgiris. The mountains here are enormous, rolling green downs of grass dotted with dark patches of shola, the stunted evergreen forest that fills the folds between the ridges, and the entire landscape is part of a protected block that includes Anamudi, at 2,695 metres the highest peak in India south of the Himalaya. The Grass Hills National Park sits here, with its sweeping slopes and strange, windswept look.

    Read the complete Mankulam Range write-up.

  4. 4. Mannavan Shola

    Same place as the previous stop.

    Mannavan Shola is a patch of forest in the high hills of Idukki, in the Mankulam forest range, and the word shola is the key to understanding the whole place. In these mountains, the ridges are covered in open grassland, while the folds between them hold dense, dark, evergreen forest, the sholas. They are very old, a relic of colder times, and they trap mist and rainwater, releasing it slowly into the streams. The grassy hillsides and the sholas work as a single system, and it is the reason this part of the Western Ghats is so important as a source of water for southern India.

    Read the complete Mannavan Shola write-up.
