Why this ride

A 687 km one-way ride from near Kingston, about 10 h of riding. The climbs: near Kingston, Scenic stretch near Te Anau and Kingston · Wanaka and Queenstown Loop. It tops out at about 1,100 m. The line favours views over speed.

The roads

Milford Road (SH94)

It is 67 km from Scenic stretch near Te Anau to Queenstown. Over the top at 714 m - roughly 410 m up and 470 m down. This is the bit the ride is built around.

Rider note

Check the pass is open before you commit to it.

  4. 4. Queenstown

    241 km in. The ride passes about 21 km from it. The high point of this section.

    The nearest way in is a walking track, the Hollyford, which runs for days through the valley to the sea at Martins Bay, and the nearest roads stop well short of it. The ride is named for the Kingston · Wanaka and Queenstown Loop, which lie a long way east, so take this stop as a nod to the wilderness behind the road.

    Read the complete Queenstown write-up.

  5. 5. Wanaka

    687 km in.

    Omarama sits in the Waitaki basin, a wide, high, dry valley in the lee of the Southern Alps, and the land around it has the stripped look of the South Island's high country, with tawny tussock slopes, braided rivers and a huge sky. Omarama is best known to pilots, since the wind that comes over the mountains makes it one of the best gliding sites in the world.
