Why this ride

372 km from Canneto to Canneto, about 6 h 25 of riding. It goes by way of Mount Etna, Militello Val di Catania and back through Mount Etna. The stored terrain estimate reaches about 1,200 m; a measured road-elevation profile is not available. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Isole Eolie

    The Aeolian Islands rise from the Tyrrhenian Sea north of Sicily like the tops of a drowned mountain range, which is more or less what they are. Seven main islands, Lipari, Vulcano, Salina, Stromboli, Panarea, Filicudi and Alicudi, are the summits of volcanoes, and two of them are still active: Stromboli, which has thrown out glowing fragments every few minutes for centuries and is known as the lighthouse of the Mediterranean, and Vulcano, whose steaming crater gave every volcano its name. UNESCO listed the archipelago in 2000 for its value in the study of volcanism.

  2. 2. Mount Etna

    Etna is not so much a mountain as a living presence that dominates eastern Sicily. Europe's highest active volcano rises over 3,300 metres above Catania, its height changing as eruptions add and remove material, and it smokes, rumbles and occasionally spits fire with a regularity that the locals regard with a mix of reverence and resignation.

  3. 3. Militello Val di Catania

    Militello in Val di Catania is a small town in the hills south-west of Catania, in eastern Sicily, surrounded by orange groves and olive trees, with Mount Etna visible on the horizon. Like many towns in this part of Sicily, it was devastated by the earthquake of 1693, which destroyed much of the south-east of the island, and was rebuilt in the Baroque style. Its churches and palaces are part of the UNESCO-listed Late Baroque Towns of the Val di Noto, inscribed in 2002, together with Noto, Ragusa, Modica, Caltagirone and others.
