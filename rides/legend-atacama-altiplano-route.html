Why this ride

curated scenic road. salt flats, volcanoes, high desert. A one-way ride of 183 km, San Pedro de Atacama to the finish - roughly 3 h 20 in the saddle. The highest point is about 4,600 m. It crosses from Chile into Argentina - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. The line favours views over speed.

Scenic road chapters

Paso de Jama

Expect company on a summer weekend. Early is better than fast. For 10 km this is Paso de Jama, a road with a name of its own and a reason for it.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. San Pedro de Atacama

    San Pedro de Atacama is a small adobe town in the driest desert on Earth, and the contradiction is the attraction. It lies at 2,400 metres on the edge of the Salar de Atacama, a vast salt flat, in an oasis fed by streams from the Andes. The streets are dusty and low, the walls are the colour of dry mud, and behind the town the Licancabur volcano, nearly 5,920 metres, stands as a perfect cone on the border with Bolivia. Everything here seems to lean towards the sky.
