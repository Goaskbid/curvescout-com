Why this ride

From Socovos it runs 75 km to Socovos; allow about 1 h 18 of riding. It goes by way of Las Cazuelas, Abrigo de la Ventana I and back through Las Cazuelas. The highest point is about 1,300 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Benizar II

    If you are in Moratalla in Holy Week, you will hear it from far off: the drums. The town's tamborada, in which hundreds of people in tunics beat their drums in unison through the streets, is one of the most distinctive Easter traditions in Murcia, and it gives this stony, high country a pulse that outsiders do not expect. Benizar is a small hamlet in the municipality, eleven or twelve kilometres from the town, and the shelter lies in the broken hills near Socovos, where the border between Murcia and Albacete wanders across ridges and dry ravines.

  2. 2. Las Cazuelas

    Moratalla is the kind of hill town that looks as though it was poured down a slope: white houses tumbling below a castle tower, narrow lanes too steep for comfort, church bells echoing off the hills. Ten kilometres out, in the rugged country of north-west Murcia, the shelter of Las Cazuelas holds prehistoric paintings, part of the region's contribution to the UNESCO-listed Rock Art of the Mediterranean Basin. The paintings are fragile and protected, and access is usually by guided visit, arranged in Moratalla or through the regional authorities.

  3. 3. Abrigo de la Ventana I

    High in the Sierra de Segura, where Albacete, Murcia and Jaén meet, the Taibilla river cuts a deep valley through limestone mountains, and the cliffs and shelters along its banks hide some of the finest prehistoric paintings in Spain. The Abrigo de la Ventana, the window shelter, is one of them, near the village of Nerpio, and it is part of the World Heritage site of Levantine rock art.
