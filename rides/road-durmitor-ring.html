Why this ride

119 km round from Žabljak - plan on about 1 h 59 of riding. On the way it passes Trsa, Sedlo Pass and Tara Canyon. The highest point is about 1,550 m. Sedlo Pass is subject to seasonal closure - check it is open before you set off.

Road summary

A mountain loop based around Zabljak, Montenegro, via Sedlo Pass. Key character: karst peaks; canyons; high pasture. Surface: paved and mixed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Zabljak

    Žabljak claims to be the highest town in the Balkans, at about 1,450 metres, and it sits on a plateau among the peaks of Durmitor, a national park and UNESCO World Heritage site in northern Montenegro. The town is small, a place of low houses and mountain lodges, and it exists mostly for hikers, skiers and the occasional motorcyclist who has made the effort to come this far. Pines and spruce close in around it, the nights are cool even in summer, and the snow comes early.

  2. 2. Trsa

    Trsa lies in the high, rolling country of north-western Montenegro, close to the border with Bosnia and Herzegovina and not far from the long, narrow reservoir of Lake Piva. It is not a village that appears in many guidebooks, which is why it is pleasant to find: an upland hamlet of stone houses, hay meadows and pine woods, with the limestone summits of the Durmitor massif and the Volujak range somewhere beyond the horizon. The nearest town in Montenegro is Plužine, about ten kilometres away, and Foča in Bosnia is the closest sizeable place across the line.

  3. 3. Sedlo Pass

    Sedlo means saddle, and this one, at 1,907 metres, is the high point of the road that circles the Durmitor massif in the north of Montenegro. The road climbs through forest and then through bare limestone country, where the trees thin out and the ridges seem to catch the sky, and at the top, a pass opens to a view of Bobotov Kuk, the highest peak of the range at 2,523 metres, with its grey cliffs streaked with snow. The air is thin, clean and cold, and the silence is broken only by wind and the bells of sheep.

  4. 4. Tara Canyon viewpoint

    The Tara river has cut one of the deepest canyons in Europe, a gorge that plunges as much as thirteen hundred metres below the rim at its greatest. It runs through the heart of Durmitor National Park in northern Montenegro, a UNESCO World Heritage area, with the clear green water of the Tara, known as the Tear of Europe, tumbling through rapids at the bottom. Standing at a viewpoint on the rim, you see the walls drop away through layers of pine and rock, with the river a bright thread far below and the sound arriving late.
