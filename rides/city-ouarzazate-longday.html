Why this ride

A 360 km round trip from Ouarzazat - roughly 6 h 12 in the saddle. On the way it passes Medina of Marrakesh, Médina et le jardin d'Agdal, Jardin de la Menara and back through Médina et le jardin d'Agdal. The highest point is about 1,500 m. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Ouarzazate

    Ouarzazate is a town built where the High Atlas gives way to the desert, and the light there is so strong and clear that the film industry came to rely on it. It sits on a high plateau at the southern foot of the mountains, with the pale red earth and flat-topped hills of southern Morocco spreading out in front, and it has been the location for films such as Lawrence of Arabia, Gladiator and numerous others. Huge studio complexes on the edge of town offer visits, with sets of ancient Egypt and Tibet standing in the dust, but the real attraction lies in the kasbahs.

  2. 2. Medina of Marrakesh

    Marrakesh began as a camp. In about 1070, the Almoravids, a Berber dynasty from the Sahara, pitched their tents on this plain at the foot of the High Atlas and began to build a city, and within a century it was the capital of an empire that stretched from Spain to Senegal. The name Morocco comes from it. The medina, a walled maze of lanes with the red ochre colour that gives the city its nickname, is still the heart of the place, and the whole thing is a UNESCO site.

  3. 3. Médina et le jardin d'Agdal

    The Agdal Gardens are a vast orchard on the southern edge of Marrakesh, a rectangle of olive, orange, pomegranate and apricot trees that stretches for about three kilometres behind high red walls. They were laid out in the twelfth century by the Almohad sultans, and later rulers extended them, and the great reservoirs at their heart, one of which is large enough to have been used for swimming lessons and boating parties by the sultans, are still filled with water brought from the Atlas by underground channels. The scale of the whole thing is hard to grasp from outside, but from the top of the walls you see a green plain stretching away toward the mountains.
