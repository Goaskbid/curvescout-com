Plan a motorcycle trip in France

Use the French roadbook to identify the exact department and road sections before checking a pass. A national traffic overview and a local high-pass notice answer different questions.

Separate the approach from the pass

Bison Fute provides national traffic context. A clear approach does not establish that a departmental mountain road is open. Identify the department and inspect its road information for the actual section.

Use the event time, not only its icon

Hautes-Alpes publishes current events, planned works and high-pass categories. Compare the event dates with your arrival; a planned reopening is not a current-open observation.

Budget the return and any diversion

A closure can change the return journey as well as the outward leg. Recalculate the complete itinerary after a changed crossing, then compare the riding, stop and delay budget.

Road and weather reference points

  • Bison Fute: current traffic
    National traffic context. Check the road owner and exact section rather than treating the national display as every local pass.
  • Hautes-Alpes: Info Route 05
    Departmental road events, planned works and named high-pass categories in Hautes-Alpes. Not a France-wide opening guarantee.
