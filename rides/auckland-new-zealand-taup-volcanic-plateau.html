Taupō Volcanic Plateau

Why this ride

A proper escape from Auckland with roads worth waking up for. Out of Auckland and back again: 612 km, roughly 11 h 07 in the saddle. It goes by way of Hamilton and Taupo. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Auckland

    Auckland is built on a volcanic field. More than fifty cones and craters, some now covered in grass and sheep, others in houses, stand across the narrow isthmus between two harbours, the Waitematā to the north and the Manukau to the south. The Māori name for the city, Tāmaki Makaurau, is often translated as Tāmaki desired by many, a nod to the fertile soil and the sea on either side that made it a prized place to live long before Europeans arrived. The largest cones, such as Maungawhau, Mount Eden, and Maungakiekie, One Tree Hill, still carry the terraces of old fortified villages.

    Read the complete Auckland write-up.

  2. 2. Hamilton

    Hamilton straddles the Waikato River, New Zealand's longest, in the middle of some of the richest dairy country on earth. It is the country's fourth-largest city, a university and agricultural research town, and it is often treated by passing travellers as somewhere to get through on the way to somewhere else. That is a little unfair, but on a ride to Taupō it is mostly what it is: a place to fill the tank, eat something and get back on the road.

    Read the complete Hamilton write-up.

  3. 3. Taupo

    The lake at Taupō is so big and so calm on a still morning that it is hard to believe what it actually is: the flooded crater of one of the most violent volcanoes on the planet. Around 25,500 years ago, the Oruanui eruption blew out an enormous volume of rock and ash, and the collapse formed much of the basin the lake now fills. Then, roughly 1,800 years ago, the Hatepe eruption sent ash and pumice across the North Island and, according to some accounts, coloured sunsets as far away as Rome and China. Lake Taupō, the largest lake in New Zealand, sits in that scar.

    Read the complete Taupo write-up.
