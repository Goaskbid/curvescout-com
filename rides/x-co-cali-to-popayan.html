  1. 1. Cali

    Cali is the capital of salsa and the city does not let you forget it. Bars and dance schools fill the evenings with brass and congas, and by weekend nights even the taxi radios seem to be playing the same tune. It is a large city of over two million people in the Cauca valley, about 1,000 metres above sea level, with sugar-cane fields spreading out in every direction and the Western Cordillera standing as a blue wall to the west.

  2. 2. Popayan

    Popayan is known as the White City, because the colonial buildings in its centre are whitewashed, with tiled roofs, arched cloisters, balconies and quiet courtyards, and it feels like a different century. About 258,000 people live here, at around 1,700 metres in the Cauca valley, and the city is one of the best-preserved colonial towns in Colombia. Cajibio is a small town nearby, and the road to the south climbs toward the high mountains of the south.
