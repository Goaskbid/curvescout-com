Why this ride

curated scenic road. fjords, sea cliffs, Hajar Mountains. From Dibba Al-Hisn it runs 158 km to Khasab; allow about 2 h 18 of riding. It crosses from United Arab Emirates into Oman - carry ID and check the toll rules. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.
