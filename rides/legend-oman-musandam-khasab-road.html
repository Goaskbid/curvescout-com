Why this ride

curated scenic road. fjords, sea cliffs, Hajar Mountains. From Dibba Al-Hisn it runs 70 km to Khasab; allow about 1 h 16 of riding. It crosses from United Arab Emirates into Oman - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.
