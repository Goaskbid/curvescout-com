Why this ride

From Kilmia it runs 227 km to Kilmia; allow about 4 h 08 of riding. On the way it passes Samha and back through Samha. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Abd Alkuri

    The people of Abd al Kuri are fishermen, and their lives follow the sea more than the land. The island is thinly populated, with a handful of small settlements, including Kilmia, a village on the coast, and the houses are low and simple, made of stone and cement, with boats drawn up on the beach. Fish, dried and salted, is the staple, along with goat meat, dates and rice brought from the mainland. The islanders speak a language related to the Socotri language of the main island, which is a South Arabian tongue that is quite different from Arabic and has no written tradition.

  2. 2. Samha

    Samha is a small, barren island in the Indian Ocean, one of the two islands known as the Brothers that lie between the main island of Socotra and the Horn of Africa. Together with Socotra and the other islands of the archipelago, it belongs to Yemen and is part of the Socotra Archipelago World Heritage Site, inscribed in 2008 for its extraordinary biodiversity. A few hundred people live on Samha, mostly fishermen, in a village by the shore, and the island has its own endemic plants, adapted to the harsh, dry climate.
