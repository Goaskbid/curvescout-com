Why this ride

From Dubai it runs 147 km to Dubai; allow about 2 h of riding. It goes by way of Faya Palaeolandscape and back through Faya Palaeolandscape.

The stops

  1. 1. Dubai

    Start here.

    Dubai is a city that grew from a creek. For most of its history, it was a small trading and pearl-diving town on a tidal inlet, with merchants' houses behind wind towers, and the old quarters of Al Fahidi and Deira still show the low coral-and-gypsum buildings, with narrow lanes, where abras, the little wooden ferries, carry people across the water for a dirham or so. Then oil money and an extraordinary appetite for building transformed it, and today the skyline includes the Burj Khalifa, which at over 800 metres is the tallest structure ever built.

  2. 2. Faya Palaeolandscape

    77 km in.

    Sharjah's interior is a world of red dunes, gravel plains and jagged limestone ridges, and in the middle of it, near the town of Mleiha, the hill of Jebel Faya holds one of the most important records of early human presence outside Africa. Excavations in rock shelters at the foot of the mountain have revealed stone tools spanning many tens of thousands of years, suggesting that early modern humans were present in Arabia far earlier than once thought, during periods when the climate was wetter and the desert greener. The Faya Palaeolandscape was inscribed on the UNESCO list in 2025, recognising its significance for understanding human dispersal out of Africa.
