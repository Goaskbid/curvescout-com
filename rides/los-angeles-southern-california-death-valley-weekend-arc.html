  2. 2. Las Flores

    Las Flores Canyon Road climbs straight up from the Pacific Coast Highway into the Santa Monica Mountains, so steep in places that it feels like riding up a wall, with tight hairpins, broken edges and views back over the ocean that get better with every metre. It connects to Rambla Pacifico and the ridge roads above Malibu, and it is a favourite of local riders who know it, and a nasty surprise for those who do not.

  3. 3. Near Beverly Hills

    Beverly Hills is a city within a city, an enclave of palm-lined streets, mansions and luxury shops surrounded by Los Angeles, and this finish lies just south of it, between Beverly Hills, Century City and Culver City. The towers of Century City, built on the old backlot of 20th Century Fox, rise to the north, and the studios of Culver City, where MGM once made its musicals, are a short ride south.

  6. 6. Near Los Serranos, on the way back

    The hot, dry winds that blow down from the deserts in autumn and winter, fanning wildfires across Southern California, are called the Santa Anas, and they are often said to take their name from the Santa Ana Canyon, where they funnel through the hills towards the coast. This stop lies right at the mouth of that canyon, near Yorba Linda and Corona, where the Santa Ana River cuts through the hills between Orange County and the Inland Empire.
