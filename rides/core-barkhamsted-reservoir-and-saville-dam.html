Why this ride

Water, forest and rolling roads on Hartford's quieter northwest side. The road: CT-181, CT-20 and CT-219. Distance and riding time are estimates from the checkpoints until the road is measured on this page.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Winsted

    Ask anyone in Winsted about the summer of 1955 and you will get a story. In August that year two hurricanes in quick succession dumped rain on northwest Connecticut, the Mad River overflowed its banks, and the flood tore through the center of town, taking out much of Main Street. The town rebuilt, and today the downtown is a plain, practical place with a few handsome brick blocks, a long lake called Highland Lake on its northern edge, and a settled, hill-country feel that has little to do with tourism.
