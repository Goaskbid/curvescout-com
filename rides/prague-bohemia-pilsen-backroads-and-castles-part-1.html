Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Prague

    Prague does not need introduction, but it does need a plan if you are leaving it by motorcycle. The city lies on the Vltava river in the heart of Bohemia, with a skyline of spires and towers climbing the hill to the castle, and a medieval centre that survived the twentieth century better than most. The Charles Bridge, begun in 1357 under Emperor Charles IV, is lined with baroque statues and almost always full of people. The Old Town Square has its astronomical clock, dating back to the fifteenth century, and Vyšehrad, the rock above the river, holds an old church and a quiet cemetery where Dvořák and Smetana are buried.

  2. 2. Pilsen

    In 1842, a Bavarian brewer named Josef Groll brewed a new kind of beer in the town brewery of Plzeň: a clear, golden lager, crisp and hoppy, made with the soft local water, Saaz hops and pale malt. It was an instant success, and the style, named after the town, became the most imitated beer in the world. The original is still brewed here as Pilsner Urquell, and a tour of the brewery, with its historic cellars and a glass of unfiltered beer straight from the oak barrel, is the obvious thing to do. Save it for the end of the day, though; the Czech blood alcohol limit for riders is zero.
