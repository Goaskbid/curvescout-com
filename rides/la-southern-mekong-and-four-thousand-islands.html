Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Pakse

    Pakse sits where the Xe Don river meets the Mekong in the far south of Laos, a lazy, low-rise town of fewer than a hundred thousand people that works as the gateway to the Bolaven Plateau. The Mekong here is wide and brown, with sandbanks in the dry season, and a long bridge opened in 2000 with Japanese help carries the road over to the far bank. Streets are quiet, shuttered shops sell motorbike spares and coffee, and the pace is slow in a way that grows on you.
