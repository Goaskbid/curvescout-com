Why this ride

67 km from Albocàsser to Albocàsser, about 1 h 09 of riding. On the way it passes Barranc del Puig- Abric I, mas d'en Badenes and back through Barranc del Puig- Abric I. The highest point is about 1,250 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. La Cova dels Tolls Alts

    The high, folded country behind the Valencian coast, the Maestrat, has long been a land of isolated farms, stone walls and windy ridges, and it is one of the least known parts of eastern Spain. La Cova dels Tolls Alts lies five kilometres from Albocàsser, in limestone hills dotted with holm oak, pine and almond, where the ravines run down toward the lowlands and the sea. The name means cave of the high pools, a reminder that water, even in this dry region, is what makes a place habitable.

  2. 2. Barranc del Puig- Abric I

    The Maestrat is the high, rugged interior of the province of Castellón, a land of limestone plateaus, deep ravines and stone villages that seem to grow out of the rock. Near Benassal, the Barranc del Puig cuts into the hills, and its rock shelters contain prehistoric paintings, part of the UNESCO serial site of the rock art of the Mediterranean Basin. This area is one of the richest in Spain for Levantine rock art, and the nearby Valltorta gorge, between Albocàsser and Tírig, has some of the most famous paintings, including scenes of hunters chasing deer and wild goats. The Valltorta museum in Tírig explains the art and organises visits to the shelters.
