Why this ride

A 351 km round trip from Hollingstedt - roughly 6 h 10 in the saddle. On the way it passes National Park Wadden Sea Niedersachsen and Crooked Wall Areas 3 to 4. It keeps close to the shore for long stretches.

The stops

  1. 1. Crooked Wall Areas 3 to 4

    Start here.

    Hollingstedt is a village of only a thousand people, but a thousand years ago it was a harbour that connected two seas. Ships came up the Treene, a slow, winding river that runs from the North Sea marshes, as far as Hollingstedt, and from there goods were carried by wagon the last few kilometres across the neck of the peninsula to Hedeby on the Baltic side. It was the western half of a transport system that let Viking traders avoid the dangerous voyage around the tip of Denmark, and the Danevirke was built to guard the land bridge between them.

  2. 2. National Park Wadden Sea Niedersachsen

    175 km in. The ride passes about 15 km from it.

    Here the Wadden Sea comes right up against the dyke. This is the coast of the Wurster Nordseeküste, between the Weser and Elbe estuaries, a country of low sea walls, drained farmland and wide skies, and the mudflats outside the dyke are part of the Lower Saxony Wadden Sea National Park. Together with the Dutch and Danish parts of the Wadden Sea, the area is a UNESCO World Heritage site, valued above all for the sheer scale of the tidal flats and the millions of birds that pass through on migration. When the tide goes out, it goes a very long way.
