Why this ride

curated scenic road. alpine lake, granite shore, mountains. From South Lake Tahoe it runs 35 km to Incline Village; allow about 38 min of riding. The highest point is about 2,250 m. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. South Lake Tahoe

    Lake Tahoe sits at 6,225 feet, which makes it one of the highest large lakes in the country, and the water is so clear that on a calm day you can see objects more than sixty feet down. It is also very deep, about 1,645 feet at its deepest, making it the second deepest lake in the United States after Crater Lake. The basin formed as the Sierra Nevada rose along fault lines and glaciers later carved the valleys, and the Washoe people spent their summers on its shores for thousands of years, long before Europeans arrived. Their name for the lake, Da ow a ga, means edge of the lake.

  2. 2. Near Kingsbury

    US 50 runs along the Nevada shore above coves of white granite boulders and water so clear and blue it looks unreal, with the snow-capped peaks of the Desolation Wilderness rising across the lake to the west. Just south is Kingsbury, a residential community on the slopes above Stateline, and the casinos of the state line strip are only a few minutes away.

  3. 3. Near Incline Village

    Sand Harbor is the postcard of Lake Tahoe's Nevada shore: smooth granite boulders scattered into turquoise water, a sandy beach and a forest of Jeffrey pine behind. It sits just south of Incline Village, a well-heeled resort town on the northeast shore, and the stretch of Nevada State Route 28 that runs past it is one of the most beautiful lakeside roads in the West.

  4. 4. Incline Village

    Incline Village sits on the north-east shore of Lake Tahoe, on the Nevada side, a community of about 8,800 people with some of the most expensive lake-view houses in the country, though from the road the mood is quiet pine woods and low buildings. It is the sort of place where a casual cafe has a view of blue water, and where the boulders on the nearby beaches are so large and smooth that they seem to have been placed by a sculptor.
