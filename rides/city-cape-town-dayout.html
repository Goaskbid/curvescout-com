Why this ride

140 km round trip from Cape Town, about 2 h 51 of riding. It runs out through Cape Floral Region Protected Areas and comes back by another road.

The roads

Chapmans Peak Drive

The 64 km between Cape Town and Cape Floral Region Protected Areas, mostly on Main Road. An easy profile, never above 164 m. This is the bit the ride is built around.

The stops

  1. 1. Cape Town

    Start here.

    Cape Town sits at the foot of Table Mountain, a flat-topped sandstone wall about a kilometre high, and on a day when the tablecloth of cloud spills over the edge it is one of the great sights of the world. The city lies between the mountain and the Atlantic, with a harbour that was founded in 1652 by the Dutch East India Company as a supply station for ships on the route to the East. It grew into a port of many cultures, and the mix of Dutch, Malay, African and British influences is still visible in its food, language and architecture.

    Read the complete Cape Town write-up.

  2. 2. Cape Floral Region Protected Areas

    62 km in.

    The Cape Peninsula ends in a long, wind-battered headland, and at its tip, Cape Point, the Atlantic and Indian oceans seem to argue. Here the rock plunges to the sea in great cliffs, the sky is huge, and on most days a gale tries to remove your hat. This is the southern part of Table Mountain National Park, set aside to protect the Cape Floral Region, the smallest and richest of the world's six plant kingdoms.
