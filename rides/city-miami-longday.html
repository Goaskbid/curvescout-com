Why this ride

A 227 km round trip from Miami - roughly 3 h 55 in the saddle. On the way it passes Everglades National Park and back through Everglades National Park.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Miami

    The Tamiami Trail was named for what it joined, Tampa and Miami, and it took thirteen years and an astonishing amount of dynamite to build. Begun in 1915 and opened in 1928, US 41 cuts straight west across the Everglades, one of the largest wetlands in North America, and it changed Florida by linking the Atlantic and Gulf coasts, though it also interrupted the flow of water across the Everglades in a way that scientists are still trying to repair. The road starts in downtown Miami as Southwest 8th Street, known to locals as Calle Ocho, the heart of Little Havana.

  2. 2. Everglades National Park

    You will not see much on a map that tells you where you are, because the Everglades are a river wider than most counties and only a few inches deep. Marjory Stoneman Douglas called it the River of Grass in 1947, the year the park was dedicated, and the description holds: water flows slowly south from Lake Okeechobee across a sea of sawgrass toward Florida Bay, with islands of hardwood and pine rising a few feet above the flood.
