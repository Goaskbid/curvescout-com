Why this ride

From Gâlgău it runs 166 km to Gâlgău; allow about 2 h 52 of riding. It goes by way of Dumbrăveni - Dealul Podului 1, Perișor - Păltiniș, Groșii Țibleșului - Preluci and back through Perișor - Păltiniș. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Gâlgău - Șaua Dealul Arsurei

    A șa, in the language of the Carpathians, is a saddle, a low point between two summits that allows a road or a path to cross a ridge. Șaua Dealul Arsurei, the saddle of the burnt hill, lies near Gâlgău, a village of fewer than nine hundred people in the hills of Sălaj, and the name is a good example of how such crossings are remembered. Arsură means a burn or a scorched place. It might refer to a fire in the forest, or to the old practice of clearing woodland for pasture by burning it, which left a hillside bare and blackened.

  2. 2. Dumbrăveni - Dealul Podului 1

    The northern edge of Roman Dacia was a long line of watchtowers, small forts and earthworks drawn across the hills of what is now northern Transylvania, and Dealul Podului, the bridge hill, near Dumbrăveni, was one of the points along it. Watchtowers here kept an eye on the valleys that led north from the province, toward the lands of the free Dacians and other peoples beyond the frontier, and their remains, now mostly low earthworks in the fields and forest, are part of the Frontiers of the Roman Empire listing for Dacia, added by UNESCO in 2024.

  3. 3. Perișor - Păltiniș

    The hills of northern Transylvania, in Bistrita-Nasaud county, are quieter than the better-known Saxon towns further south, a country of wooded ridges, narrow valleys and farming villages where hay is still cut by hand and stacked on wooden poles. The Perisor to Paltinis label sits in this landscape, about four kilometres from the village of Spermezeu and not far from Zagra, in the foothills below the Rodna mountains.

  4. 4. Groșii Țibleșului - Preluci

    The Țibleș massif rises like a dark green wall between Maramureș and the Bistrița country of northern Romania, an old volcanic range topped by the Țibleș peak at around eighteen hundred metres. On its southern flanks, above the village of Groșii Țibleșului, lie the forests of Preluci, part of the Ancient and Primeval Beech Forests of the Carpathians and Other Regions of Europe that UNESCO has protected as one sprawling, multi-country site. These are woods left largely to themselves, where old beeches grow huge, fall, rot and feed the next generation without a forester in sight.
