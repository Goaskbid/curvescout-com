The stops

  1. 1. Puerto Río Tranquilo

    Start here.

    Puerto Río Tranquilo is a small settlement on the western shore of Lago General Carrera, the vast lake that Chile shares with Argentina, where it is known as Lago Buenos Aires. The water here has a startling colour, a milky turquoise caused by glacial flour, and the lake is so big that the far shore is lost in haze. The village itself is a handful of streets with wooden houses, guesthouses and boat operators, and a population of only a few hundred.

    Read the complete Puerto Rio Tranquilo write-up.

  2. 2. Cochrane Chile

    The end: 114 km.

    Cochrane is the last proper town on the southern Carretera Austral before the road runs out into the far south, and it feels like it. About three thousand people live here, in low houses around a spacious plaza, with a river valley and glacial lakes nearby and the high Patagonian steppe to the east. It was founded in the 1950s and named after Thomas Cochrane, the Scottish admiral who commanded the Chilean navy in the independence war against Spain, so there is an odd flavour of Scottish seafaring in the middle of the mountains.
