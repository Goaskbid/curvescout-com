Why this ride

curated scenic road. Wakhan valley, Pamir peaks, fortresses. A one-way ride of 354 km, Khorugh to Murghob - roughly 6 h 26 in the saddle. On the way it passes Ishqoshim, Khandūd and Wakhan Corridor Road. The highest point is about 4,500 m. The line favours views over speed.
