Why this ride

A 320 km round trip from Kolkata - roughly 5 h 31 in the saddle. The stops in between: Sundarbans National Park, The Sundarbans and back through Sundarbans National Park. It crosses from India into Bangladesh - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Kolkata

    Kolkata is the city that was once the capital of British India, and it still carries itself with the air of a place that remembers. The great colonial buildings, the Victoria Memorial of white marble, the Writers' Building and the High Court, stand beside the Hooghly river, and trams, the only ones still running in India, clatter along the streets. The Howrah Bridge, a huge cantilever of steel opened in 1943, carries a river of people and vehicles across the water every day, and it is held together with rivets rather than nuts and bolts.

  2. 2. Sundarbans National Park

    Let's be straight about it from the start: nobody rides a motorcycle into the Sundarbans. The national park is a maze of tidal channels, mudbanks and mangrove islands at the bottom of the Ganges–Brahmaputra delta, and the last solid tarmac gives out at the jetties around Gosaba and the villages beyond Canning. The ride is about getting to the edge of the land, parking the bike in a village yard, and stepping onto a wooden boat for the part that matters.
