Why this ride

A 66 km round trip from Nerpio - roughly 1 h 08 in the saddle. On the way it passes Abrigo de las Cañadas II, Río frío and back through Abrigo de las Cañadas II. The highest point is about 1,450 m. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. mingarnao II

    Nerpio is a high village in the far southwest of Albacete province, about eleven hundred metres above sea level, and Mingarnao is one of the names scattered across the countryside around it. The shelter, Mingarnao II, is one of dozens in the Nerpio area that make up a notable concentration of rock paintings. Several have been found here, and some show Levantine figures and others show later schematic signs, a mixture that makes the area unusual.

  2. 2. Abrigo de las Cañadas II

    Nerpio is about as remote as mainland Spain gets. The village sits in the far southwest corner of Albacete province, in the Sierra de Segura, where the mountains of Andalusia, Murcia and Castilla-La Mancha meet, and the rock shelters scattered around it make up one of the richest concentrations of prehistoric art in the region. The Abrigo de las Cañadas II belongs to that group, an overhang in the hills between Nerpio and the high plateaus toward Huéscar and Yeste.

  3. 3. Río frío

    Cold water is a rare luxury in southern Spain, and a stream called Río Frío promises exactly that. It is a land of pine forests, limestone crags, deep river gorges and tiny villages, and the painted rock shelters scattered through these hills are part of the UNESCO listing of rock art of the Mediterranean Basin on the Iberian Peninsula.
