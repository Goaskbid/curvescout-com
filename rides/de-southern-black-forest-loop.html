Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Freiburg

    Freiburg is where the Rhine plain meets the western edge of the Black Forest, and the transition is abrupt: one minute you are on a flat road past vineyards, the next the hills climb into dark spruce and the road begins to wind. The city of just over two hundred thousand has a long reputation for sunshine, one of the sunniest big cities in Germany, and its students, cyclists and solar panels give it an easy, green character.

  3. 3. Schluchsee

    The biggest lake in the Black Forest is not natural. The Schluchsee was created in the 1930s, when the valley was dammed to supply a hydroelectric scheme, flooding farms and hamlets and lifting the water level to form a long, slim lake in the high country at about 930 metres. It looks as though it has always been there, with forested hills on every side, and the older people in the villages can still remember the old landscape.
