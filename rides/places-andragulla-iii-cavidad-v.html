Why this ride

A 67 km round trip from Moratalla - roughly 1 h 09 in the saddle. The stops in between: fuente del Sauco, Cavidad V and back through fuente del Sauco. The highest point is about 1,350 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Andragulla III

    Bats live in cavities, and so do eagles. The rocky country around Andragulla, in the municipality of Moratalla, is full of holes: overhangs, clefts, small caves that the survey of rock art calls cavidades, and each of them is a home for something. Bats roost in the dark ones, swifts and choughs nest in the cracks, and on the highest cliffs a golden eagle or a Bonelli's eagle may build a nest of sticks. The shelters with painted figures are only the human part of a much larger story of life in the rock.

  2. 2. fuente del Sauco

    The Fuente del Sauco is the elder-tree fountain, a small spring that gave its name to a shelter in the hills between Letur and Socovos in the south of Albacete. Water is the story of this country. The Mundo river rises not far to the west, at a spectacular spring in the Calar del Mundo near Riópar, where it bursts out of a cave in a series of waterfalls, and it flows through the gorges below Letur before joining the Segura. Along its banks, and in the cliffs above, ancient people sheltered and painted, and the Andragulla and other shelters here belong to the same tradition, part of the Mediterranean Basin rock art that UNESCO protects.

  3. 3. Cavidad V

    Nerpio is about as far from anywhere as you can get in mainland Spain. This village of around sixteen hundred people sits in the Sierra del Segura, in the far south-west of Albacete province, close to the borders of Murcia and Andalusia, and the surrounding hills are dotted with shelters containing prehistoric paintings. The numbered sites around here, Cavidad V among them, are part of the UNESCO-listed Rock Art of the Mediterranean Basin, and the Nerpio area is one of the richest concentrations of Levantine art in Spain, with hunting scenes, archers and animals painted thousands of years ago.
