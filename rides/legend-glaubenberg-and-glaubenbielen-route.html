Why this ride

curated scenic road. moorland, alpine views, forest. A one-way ride of 44 km, Luzern to Giswil - roughly 48 min in the saddle. On the way it passes Sarnen and Flühli. The highest point is about 1,850 m. Glaubenbuelen Pass is subject to seasonal closure - check it is open before you set off. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Emmental Panorama Road

Expect company on a summer weekend. Early is better than fast. For 22 km this is Emmental Panorama Road, a road with a name of its own and a reason for it.

  2. 2. Near Sarnen

    Sarnen is the capital of Obwalden, and it wears the title lightly. The town sits at the northern end of Lake Sarnen, a long blue lake in the middle of Switzerland, with the Pilatus above and a line of wooded hills on the other side. About nine thousand people live here, and the main street has the usual arrangement of bakeries, banks and cafes, with a large parish church that stands on a rise above the old centre.

  4. 4. Near Giswil

    Giswil sits at the bottom of the Brünig road, and so it is a place where riders gather. The village, with about three and a half thousand people, lies in the valley of the Sarner Aa, at 485 metres, south of Lake Sarnen, between wooded hills and steep slopes. It is a quiet farming community in the canton of Obwalden, the heart of central Switzerland, and the main road runs through it on the way up to Lungern and the Brünig pass.
