Why this ride

curated scenic road. rock formations, monastery, Catalan landscape. From Eixample it runs 61 km to Monistrol de Montserrat; allow about 1 h of riding. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The roads

Monserrat Mountain Road

Monistrol de Montserrat to Near Monistrol de Montserrat: 9 km on Carretera de Montserrat. You climb most of the way: 550 m, up to 741 m. Short, so take your time with it.
