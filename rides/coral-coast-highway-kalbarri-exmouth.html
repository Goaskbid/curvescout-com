Why this ride

1653 km from Kalbarri to Exmouth, about 22 h 42 of riding. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Kalbarri

    Kalbarri is a small town on the mouth of the Murchison River, where the river meets the Indian Ocean, and it is the gateway to a national park of the same name. The park covers a huge area of sandstone plateau, cut by deep gorges that expose layers of red and white rock laid down hundreds of millions of years ago. Lookouts such as Nature's Window, a natural arch framing the river below, and the Z Bend, where the gorge folds back on itself, are the main attractions, and the Skywalk platforms stick out into the void for those with a head for heights.

  2. 2. Shark Bay

    The bay is a huge, shallow, salty inlet of the Indian Ocean, and it holds the largest seagrass meadows in the world, which feed a population of dugongs, turtles and dolphins. The water is clear, the sand is white and the cliffs are red.

  3. 3. Exmouth

    Exmouth is a long way from anywhere, and that is the whole point. The town sits near the tip of the North West Cape in Western Australia, where the Coral Coast Highway runs out of continent, with the Indian Ocean on one side and the shallow Exmouth Gulf on the other. From Carnarvon it is a full day of red dirt, scrub and sky, and when the first sign of the Cape Range appears on the horizon you feel genuinely rewarded.
