Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Cape Town

    Cape Town sits at the foot of Table Mountain, a flat-topped sandstone wall about a kilometre high, and on a day when the tablecloth of cloud spills over the edge it is one of the great sights of the world. The city lies between the mountain and the Atlantic, with a harbour that was founded in 1652 by the Dutch East India Company as a supply station for ships on the route to the East. It grew into a port of many cultures, and the mix of Dutch, Malay, African and British influences is still visible in its food, language and architecture.

    Read the complete Cape Town write-up.

  2. 2. Hermanus

    In Hermanus, one of the town's municipal employees blows a horn made from kelp to announce the whales. The whale crier is a local tradition, and it makes sense, because between roughly June and November southern right whales come into Walker Bay to calve and play, and from the cliff path that runs along the shoreline you can watch them from a few metres above the surf. Mothers and calves roll in the shallows, males breach and slap the water with their tails, and you hear their breath carried across the bay. It is among the best land-based whale watching in the world.
