Why this ride

Leave the city behind and spend the day on roads with texture. Out of Mornant and back again: 236 km, roughly 4 h 22 in the saddle. On the way it passes in Saint-Clair-du-Rhône, Scenic stretch in Satolas-et-Bonce and Miribel. in Tignieu is the high point.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Fuel stop near Mornant

    Two thousand years ago, engineers from Lugdunum ran an aqueduct through these hills to carry water from the Gier valley to the Roman city, a channel some eighty kilometres long that bridged valleys on stone arches. Around Mornant, sections of those arches still stand in the fields, weathered and patched with ivy, and stumbling upon them on a back road is one of the quiet pleasures of riding in the Monts du Lyonnais.

  8. 8. Back to Romans and

    The land here belongs to the Monts du Lyonnais, a range of rounded hills south-west of Lyon that rise to about nine hundred metres at their highest and are made of old crystalline rock. Mornant, Chassagny and Taluyers sit on the eastern flank, where the slope falls toward the Rhône and the soil supports orchards, pasture and, in places, vineyards. The wines of the Coteaux du Lyonnais, mostly light, fruity Gamay reds, come from these hills, and there are small producers who will sell you a bottle in a cellar with a hand-painted sign.

Recorded leg-by-leg notes

These source notes may contain earlier estimates. They are not turn-by-turn directions or current measured distances.

Food stop in Saint-Clair-du-Rhône to Scenic stretch in Satolas-et-Bonce

Food stop in Saint-Clair-du-Rhône → Scenic stretch in Satolas-et-Bonce is the logistics leg. Watch junctions, villages and speed changes, then let the roadbook do its job: move you out of the clutter without spending your best attention too early.

Scenic stretch in Satolas-et-Bonce to High point in Tignieu

Scenic stretch in Satolas-et-Bonce → High point in Tignieu is the reward section in Lyon. Look through the bends, use legal pull-offs for photos and keep a little margin for tractors, cyclists and damp shade. The best ride here is smooth, not noisy.

High point in Tignieu to Miribel

High point in Tignieu → Miribel rides best with patience. Coastal roads change character with wind and visitors; keep overtakes boring and take the photo from a proper stop.

Photo stop in Blacé to Back to Romans and

Photo stop in Blacé → Back to Romans and can flow quickly, but only if you keep village limits and junction sightlines sacred. Fast connectors are where rides get untidy when fatigue creeps in.
