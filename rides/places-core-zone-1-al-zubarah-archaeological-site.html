Why this ride

A 152 km round trip from Jidd Ḩafş - roughly 2 h 46 in the saddle. It goes by way of Al Zubarah Archaeological Site and Core zone 1. It crosses from Bahrain into Qatar - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Core zone 1

    Core zone is bureaucratic language, and the thing it describes is simply the heart of the hill: the central area of the Qal'at al-Bahrain archaeological site, where the oldest and most important remains lie. It sits on the north coast of Bahrain, between the shore and the palm gardens, and a short distance from the suburbs of Jidd Hafs and Madinat Isa. The idea of a core zone, with a buffer around it, is how UNESCO protects its sites from being swallowed by the surrounding city, and this one was marked out when the site was inscribed in 2005.

  2. 2. Al Zubarah Archaeological Site

    Out on the north-west coast of Qatar, where the desert slides into a shallow turquoise sea, stand the low ruins of Al Zubarah, once one of the busiest pearling and trading towns in the Gulf. In the late eighteenth and nineteenth centuries the town thrived on pearls dived from the oyster beds offshore, traded on to India, Persia and beyond. Then it declined, was abandoned in the early twentieth century, and the sand covered it. Archaeologists have since uncovered streets, houses, a souk, mosques and a long town wall running in an arc around the old harbour. UNESCO inscribed the site in 2013.
