Why this ride

From Hellín it runs 368 km to Hellín; allow about 6 h 38 of riding. The stops in between: Cueva del Esquilo, Cueva Ambrosio and back through Cueva del Esquilo. It tops out around 1,200 m.

The stops

  1. 1. Abrigo Grande de minateda

    Start here.

    The Abrigo Grande de Minateda is the big one, the name says so, and for once the name is accurate. It is among the most important rock art shelters in the south-east of Spain, a wide overhang in the soft rock outside Hellín in Albacete, where layers of painting from different periods sit one on top of the other. Hunters, deer, goats and strange, elongated human figures were drawn here over thousands of years, and the site is covered by World Heritage protection. The country around it is dry, broken and beautiful, a land of badlands, gullies and flat-topped hills that looks almost like another continent.

    Read the complete Abrigo Grande de minateda write-up.

  2. 2. Cueva del Esquilo

    111 km in.

    The border between Albacete and Murcia runs through a tangle of mountains here, and the villages of Socovos, Férez and Letur sit among them like small islands. The Cueva del Esquilo is one of the painted rock shelters in these hills, a reminder that people lived and hunted in the Segura mountains thousands of years before any border existed. Like most of the rock art in the region, it is protected and best visited with local guidance; the town halls and tourist offices of the surrounding villages are the place to ask.

    Read the complete Cueva del Esquilo write-up.

  3. 3. Cueva Ambrosio

    190 km in. The ride passes about 8 km from it.

    Cueva Ambrosio belongs to the high, empty north of Almería province, on the dry plateau where Andalusia runs into Murcia, fifteen or so kilometres out of Vélez-Blanco. This is a landscape of broad, pale fields, almond and cereal farms, low limestone ridges and long skylines, with the pines of the Sierra María to the south and almost nothing built on the horizon.
