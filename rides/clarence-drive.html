Why this ride

A 37 km one-way ride from near Grabouw, about 42 min of riding. On the way it passes Kogel Bay and Rooi Els. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

The roads

Clarence Drive

The 14 km between Gordons Bay and Kogel Bay. An easy profile, never above 98 m. Short, so take your time with it.

The stops

  1. 1. Gordons Bay

    Start here.

    Gordon's Bay is a small harbor town at the northeastern corner of False Bay, with the Hottentots Holland mountains rising abruptly behind it and the Atlantic-facing bulk of the Cape Peninsula far away across the water. It is the start of Clarence Drive, the coastal road that clings to the cliffs along the eastern shore of False Bay, and for many riders it is the gate to one of the best short rides in the Western Cape. The town itself is a laid-back place with a marina, a few restaurants and a beach that fills up in summer.

    Read the complete Gordons Bay write-up.

  2. 2. Kogel Bay

    14 km in.

    Clarence Drive is the coast road that South Africans point to when they want to impress visitors, and Kogel Bay is one of its quieter pleasures. The road squeezes between steep mountain slopes and the cold Atlantic, hugging the cliffs for miles, and then dips to a long, pale beach where the dunes roll up against the fynbos. The sea here is rough and the undertow dangerous, so it is a place for walking and watching rather than swimming.

    Read the complete Kogel Bay write-up.

  3. 3. Rooi Els

    24 km in.

    Rooi-Els is a handful of houses and a fierce mountain, and the combination is spectacular. The village sits on the shore of False Bay, squeezed between the Atlantic and the cliffs of the Hottentots-Holland and Kogelberg ranges, and the name means red alder, after a tree that grows along the stream. The steep slopes behind the village are covered with fynbos and cut by dark gorges, and the whole place feels like the end of the world.

    Read the complete Rooi Els write-up.

  4. 4. Bettys Bay

    The end: 37 km.

    Betty's Bay lies about midway along Clarence Drive, a scattering of low houses between the mountains and the sea, and it is the part of the coast where nature takes over. It lies within the Kogelberg Biosphere Reserve, a UNESCO-recognized area that holds one of the highest concentrations of plant species anywhere on earth, with fynbos shrubs such as proteas, ericas and restios covering the slopes in a bewildering variety. In spring, the colors are wonderful, and in winter the mountains are sharp with cloud and rain.

    Read the complete Bettys Bay write-up.
