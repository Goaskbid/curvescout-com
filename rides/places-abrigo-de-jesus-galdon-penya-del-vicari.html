Why this ride

A 318 km round trip from Bicorp - roughly 5 h 29 in the saddle. It goes by way of Barranc de la falaguera- Abric I, Cova de la Petxina, Barranc de la Penya Blanca and Penya del Vicari. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Abrigo de Jesús Galdón

    Bicorp is a village of about six hundred people in the interior of Valencia province, and it is famous among archaeologists for the Cueva de la Araña, whose painting of a person climbing a rope to reach a wild bees' nest is one of the best-known images of Levantine art. The shelter marked Abrigo de Jesús Galdón is a few kilometres from Bicorp, in the same dry, rugged country of limestone ridges and pine, and it belongs to the same tradition. The honey-hunting scene is also part of the reason that this rock art is protected by UNESCO, which listed it in 1998.

  2. 2. Barranc de la falaguera- Abric I

    Alginet sits in the Ribera Alta, the flat, fertile plain south of Valencia where the Júcar river spreads out among orange groves, rice fields and small towns. To the west, the plain meets the first hills of the interior, low ridges of limestone covered in pine and scrub. The Barranc de la Falaguera, the ravine of the fern, cuts into these hills near Alginet, Alfarp and Benifaió.

  3. 3. Cova de la Petxina

    Bellús is a small village in the valley of the Albaida river in southern Valencia, just upstream from where the river flows into a reservoir and then joins the Júcar. The old village was partly flooded by the reservoir, and the new one stands on higher ground. The Cova de la Petxina, the cave of the shell, is in the hills near the village, close to Guadasequies and Genovés.

  4. 4. Barranc de la Penya Blanca

    Planes is a small village in the hills of the Comtat in northern Alicante province, crowned by a castle and looking out over a valley of olive and cherry orchards. The Barranc de la Penya Blanca, the ravine of the white rock, lies close by, among the folded limestone ridges between Planes, Benimarfull and the reservoir at Beniarrés.

  5. 5. Penya del Vicari

    On a map of the Costa Blanca, the Serra de Bernia looks like a wall dropped between two very different worlds. On one side, high-rise Benidorm and the busy coast; on the other, the quiet valleys of Jalón and Guadalest. The Penya del Vicari is one of the rocky outcrops in these hills above Altea and Calp, a place where the limestone is cut by caves and shelters, some of which carry traces of prehistoric painting. It is a small, quiet site in an area otherwise famous for sun loungers.
