Why this ride

182 km from Benasau to Benasau, about 3 h 08 of riding. On the way it passes Barranc de les Coves- Abric I, Abric de la fos, Abric del Garrofero and back through Abric de la fos. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Barranc del Salt- Abric V

    A good day in this part of the Serra de Mariola might go like this. You leave early, when the air is cool and the light is low, and ride the lane from Benilloba up into the hills. The shelter at Barranc del Salt, Abric V, lies about four kilometres from the village, and carries painting in the Levantine tradition, protected by UNESCO as part of the rock art of the Mediterranean basin in Spain. The ride links it with Benirrama, in the Vall de Gallinera, a quiet valley to the east. You walk to the wall, look, and sit for a while with the view.

  2. 2. Barranc de les Coves- Abric I

    Ibi is the toy capital of Spain, a fact that surprises everyone who rides through this mountain town in the hills of Alicante. Since the early twentieth century, workshops here have produced dolls, tin toys and plastic figures, and the Valencian Toy Museum tells the story. The rock shelter named Barranc de les Coves Abric I lies in the hills between Ibi and Alcoy, in a ravine whose name simply means the gully of the caves.

  3. 3. Abric de la fos

    The Vall d'Albaida is a broad valley in the south of the province of Valencia, between the Serra Grossa to the north and the Serra de Mariola to the south, and its towns grew wealthy on textiles. Aielo de Malferit, a small town on the edge of the valley, has a rock shelter called the Abric de la Fos in the hills nearby, with prehistoric paintings in the Levantine style. The shelter is protected and access is limited, so check with the local tourist office or interpretation centres before visiting.

  4. 4. Abric del Garrofero

    Bicorp is a small village in the Canal de Navarrés, a valley in the interior of Valencia province walled in by rugged mountains and deep ravines. The surrounding Macizo del Caroig is one of the largest wild areas in the Valencian Community, a vast tableland of pine, scrub and gorges cut by the Júcar's tributaries. The Abric del Garrofero, the shelter of the carob tree, lies in the hills near Bicorp, Quesa and Navarrés.
