Why this ride

National Scenic Byway. barrier islands, lighthouses, ferries. From Nags Head it runs 149 km to the finish; allow about 2 h 43 of riding. The stops in between: Buxton and Outer Banks Scenic Byway. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Scenic road chapters

Outer Banks Scenic Byway

Expect company on a summer weekend. Early is better than fast. For 15 km this is Outer Banks Scenic Byway, a road with a name of its own and a reason for it.
