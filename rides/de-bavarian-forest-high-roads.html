Why this ride

Shaded two-lane riding with rolling corners and little urban exposure. It also links naturally to Bavarian Forest National Park.

The roads

Regensburg → Bodenmais

From Regensburg to Bodenmais is 98 km, mostly on A 3. It climbs 730 m to the top at 845 m and drops 410 m again. Top up fuel at the bottom - pumps thin out on the way up.
