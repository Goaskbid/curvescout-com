Why this ride

426 km one way from Baruth - plan on about 7 h 45 of riding. The stops in between: Scenic stretch near Premnitz and near Wittstock. near Niedergörsdorf is the high point. It crosses from Germany into Poland - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Fuel stop near Baruth

    Glassblowers once worked in the forest just outside Baruth. In the eighteenth century, the counts of Solms-Baruth founded a glassworks here, using the local sand and the wood of the surrounding forests, and a village grew up around it. Glashütte, as it is still called, is now a museum village, with workshops where you can watch glass being blown, old workers' houses, a café and craft shops, a charming stop deep among pine trees.

  4. 4. Photo stop near Wittstock

    In October 1636, on the hills south of Wittstock, a Swedish army crushed the combined forces of the Holy Roman Emperor and Saxony in one of the bloodiest battles of the Thirty Years' War. Archaeologists later found a mass grave of soldiers on the battlefield, and a museum in the tower of the old bishop's castle in Wittstock tells the story of the war and its brutal impact on ordinary people. The town itself is enclosed by one of the most complete medieval brick walls in Germany, with towers and a gate.
