Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Photo stop near Blewbury

    Blewbury is a village of thatched cottages, chalk streams and old cob walls at the foot of the Berkshire Downs, south of Didcot. It has the slightly enchanted feel of a place that has been left alone, and it is no surprise that Kenneth Grahame chose to live here in the years after he wrote The Wind in the Willows. Wander the lanes and you could easily imagine Ratty and Mole somewhere among the willows by the stream.

  2. 2. Near Woodford Green

    On fine weekends half the bikers of north-east London seem to end up at the same tea hut in the middle of Epping Forest, at High Beach, drinking from paper cups among the hornbeams. This finish lies just south of the forest's tip, between Walthamstow, Highams Park and Woodford Green, and the forest is the last green thing you see before the city closes around you again.

  3. 3. Back to Hampshire Test

    You are near Speldhurst, a quiet Kent village a couple of kilometres north-west of Tunbridge Wells, with Rusthall and its sandstone outcrops just to the south. The oast houses with their white cowls, built to dry the hops, are still scattered across the fields, though most are now houses.
