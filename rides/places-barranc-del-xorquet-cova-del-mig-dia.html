Why this ride

140 km round trip from Barranc del Xorquet, about 3 h 06 of riding. It runs out through Barranc dels Garrofers, Barraac de la Palla and Cova del mig dia and comes back by another road.

The stops

  1. 1. Barranc del Xorquet

    Start here.

    Ask a cyclist on the Costa Blanca about Coll de Rates and watch their face change. The pass road climbing from Parcent toward Tàrbena is a pilgrimage for professional teams in winter training, and it lies only a few kilometres from the Barranc del Xorquet, one of the ravines in the tangled limestone hills behind the coast. This is an area rich in prehistoric rock art: the shelters at Pla de Petracos near Castell de Castells hold some of the best examples of the so-called macro-schematic style, big abstract human figures painted in red thousands of years ago, and the ravines around here hide further sites.

    Read the complete Barranc del Xorquet write-up.

  2. 2. Barranc dels Garrofers

    38 km in.

    Between the towns of Alcoy and Gandia, the mountains of the northern Alicante province fold into a tangle of narrow valleys, and the barranc, the ravine, of the carob trees is a dry gully among them, a few kilometres from the villages of Planes and Beniarrés. The carob, or garrofer in Valencian, is a dark, leathery tree that has grown here for centuries, and its long brown pods were once used as animal feed and sweeteners. The land is rugged limestone, laced with terraces now mostly abandoned and covered in pines and rosemary.

    Read the complete Barranc dels Garrofers write-up.

  3. 3. Barraac de la Palla

    73 km in.

    Tormos and Sagra are tiny villages at the foot of the mountains in the north of Alicante province, at the point where the plain of the Marina Alta meets the hills. They are surrounded by orange groves and orchards, with the mountains of the Vall de Laguar and the Serra del Migdia rising steeply behind. The Barranc de la Palla, the ravine of the straw, cuts into the hills near Tormos, close also to Orba and the town of Pego.

    Read the complete Barraac de la Palla write-up.

  4. 4. Cova del mig dia

    98 km in.

    The Montgó dominates the coast between Dénia and Xàbia, a massive limestone mountain rising to 753 metres straight from the sea, its profile said to resemble a sleeping elephant. The Cova del Mig Dia, the cave of midday, is in the flanks of the Montgó, one of several caves and shelters on the mountain, some with traces of prehistoric occupation and rock art. The Montgó is a natural park, with paths leading through scrub and pine to caves, viewpoints and the summit.

Leg by leg

34 km, mostly on Carrer Carretera and CV-700.

Barraac de la Palla to Cova del mig dia

25 km, mostly on carretera de Pedreguer and Carrer de la Terrasseria.

Cova del mig dia to Barranc del Xorquet

42 km, mostly on carretera d’Oliva a Polop and avinguda de Benidorm.
