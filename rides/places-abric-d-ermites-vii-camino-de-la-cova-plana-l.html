Why this ride

From Ulldecona it runs 245 km to Ulldecona; allow about 4 h 13 of riding. The stops in between: Cueva de mas del Abogat, Camino de la Cova Plana l and back through Cueva de mas del Abogat. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Abric d'Ermites VII

    Ulldecona is a country town of about six thousand people in the far south of Catalonia, close to the border with Valencia, with a long reputation for olive oil, and the rock shelters in the hills above it, known as the Abrics de l'Ermita after the hermitage of La Pietat on the slopes, are among the most rewarding painted sites in the region. Abric d'Ermites VII is one of the series, and carries Levantine painting like its neighbours, part of the UNESCO-listed rock art of the Iberian Mediterranean basin. The other shelters in the group are more famous, but this one rewards a patient eye.

  2. 2. Cueva de mas del Abogat

    Calaceite is the kind of village that makes you slow down without being asked: honey-coloured stone, arcaded squares, a big baroque church and grand old houses with carved doorways, all perched on a ridge in the Matarraña, the corner of Teruel where Aragón meets Catalonia and Valencia. A few kilometres from it lies the Cueva de Mas del Abogat, one of the painted rock shelters that belong to the UNESCO listing of prehistoric art in the Mediterranean Basin of the Iberian Peninsula. As with its sisters across eastern Spain, the paintings are small and often hard to read; the setting is what you take away.

  3. 3. Camino de la Cova Plana l

    Where the Segre and the Cinca rivers meet the Ebro, the land opens out into a dry, tawny basin that feels far from the sea. Mequinenza sits at the heart of it, a small town of about 2,500 people that has a story unlike any other on the Ebro. The old town was flooded in the 1960s when the Ribarroja dam created the Mar de Aragón, a huge reservoir, and the inhabitants were moved to a new town built higher up. The writer Jesús Moncada, born here, wrote about the vanished town in his novel Camino de sirga, and the loss still colours the way people talk about the place.
