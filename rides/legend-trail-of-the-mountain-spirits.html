Why this ride

National Scenic Byway. Gila Mountains, archaeology, forest. A one-way ride of 108 km, Silver City to Silver City - roughly 1 h 58 in the saddle. The highest point is about 2,350 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Scenic road chapters

Trail of the Mountain Spirits

Expect company on a summer weekend. Early is better than fast. For 12 km this is Trail of the Mountain Spirits, a road with a name of its own and a reason for it.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Silver City

    Silver City is the kind of place that makes people wonder why they did not find it sooner. It sits at about 5,900 feet on the edge of the Gila country in southwest New Mexico, a few miles from the Continental Divide, and its brick downtown still carries the mark of the mining boom that created it in the 1870s. A flood in the 1890s carved out the main street into a ravine, and the Big Ditch park now runs where Main Street once was, which is one of those local facts that stops visitors in the middle of the pavement.
