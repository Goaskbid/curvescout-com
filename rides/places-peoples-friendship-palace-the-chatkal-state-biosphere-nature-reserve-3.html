Why this ride

From Tashkent it runs 166 km to Tashkent; allow about 2 h 52 of riding. It goes by way of Big Solar Furnace, The Chatkal State Biosphere Nature Reserve and back through Big Solar Furnace. The highest point is about 1,950 m. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Peoples’ Friendship Palace

    The Palace of Peoples' Friendship is a Soviet-era building of Tashkent that rewards a slow look, and the name itself is a period piece. Friendship of the peoples was a standard phrase of Soviet ideology, a promise that the many nationalities of the union lived in harmony, and Tashkent, with its mix of Uzbeks, Russians, Tatars, Koreans and others, was held up as the model of it. The palace, a large concert and congress hall in the modernist style, carries the ambitions of the 1960s and 1970s, when the earthquake rebuilding turned the city into a laboratory of Soviet design, and the structure stands out among the plane trees with its confident, geometric form.

  2. 2. Big Solar Furnace

    In the foothills of the Tian Shan, about an hour north-east of Tashkent, there is a building that looks as if it belongs in a science fiction film: a huge curved mirror, made of thousands of smaller mirrors, facing a field of tracking mirrors that follow the sun and focus its light onto a single point. This is the Big Solar Furnace near Parkent, built in the Soviet era in the 1980s, and still used for research into materials that can withstand extremely high temperatures.

  3. 3. The Chatkal State Biosphere Nature Reserve

    East of Tashkent, the land rises steeply into the western ranges of the Tian Shan, and the Chatkal range forms the border with Kyrgyzstan. The Chatkal State Biosphere Reserve, established in the Soviet era, protects a stretch of these mountains, with juniper forests, walnut and wild fruit trees, alpine meadows and rocky peaks.
