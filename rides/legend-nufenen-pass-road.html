Why this ride

curated scenic road. high alpine, hairpins. From Formazza it runs 26 km to Airolo; allow about 28 min of riding. The highest point is about 2,500 m. Nufenen Pass is subject to seasonal closure - check it is open before you set off. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Furka Pass

Expect company on a summer weekend. Early is better than fast. For 12 km this is Furka Pass, a road with a name of its own and a reason for it.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Nufenen Pass Road point 1

    Ulrichen is the first village you meet when the Nufenen Pass road leaves the Rhône valley, and the valley it sits in is one of the quietest and most characterful in Switzerland. This is the Goms, the upper reach of the Valais, a long, flat-bottomed trough just a kilometre or so wide, with the young Rhône winding through it and steep forested slopes on both sides. The villages are clusters of dark larch houses, burnt almost black by the sun, perched on stone stilts to keep the mice from the grain, and the church towers are slender and white.

    The nearest one climbs up from the Maggia side to Fusio, a stone village at the head of Val Lavizzara, and continues to the Sambuco dam, a curved concrete wall holding back a long reservoir. It is about as far from the bustle of the Gotthard as you can get while staying within a few kilometres of it.

  4. 4. Airolo

    Airolo sits at the southern mouth of the Gotthard, the point where northern and southern Europe seem to hand over to each other in a single valley. At 1,175 metres it is a small town of about fifteen hundred people, and the first sign of Italian Switzerland if you come from Andermatt: the roofs turn to stone, the stucco gets warmer, and the language changes to Italian. It has fuel, cafes, a couple of bakeries and a railway station, and it is a natural place to start or finish a day among the passes.
