Why this ride

curated scenic road. high alpine, historic route, gorges. A one-way ride of 41 km, Brig to Domodossola - roughly 45 min in the saddle. The highest point is about 2,500 m. Simplon Pass is subject to seasonal closure - check it is open before you set off. It crosses from Switzerland into Italy - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Scenic road chapters

Simplon Pass Road

Expect company on a summer weekend. Early is better than fast. For 16 km this is Simplon Pass Road, a road with a name of its own and a reason for it.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Brig

    Brig is a small town with a big palace, and its position at the foot of the Simplon Pass gives it a significance out of proportion to its size. It sits in the upper Rhône valley in the canton of Valais, where the river runs west to Lake Geneva, and a route over the mountains to Italy has existed here since Roman times. The Stockalperschloss, the biggest private residence in Switzerland when it was completed in the late seventeenth century, was built by Kaspar Jodok von Stockalper, a merchant who controlled the Simplon trade and became the richest man in the country. With its three onion-domed towers, named for the Three Kings, it dominates the old town and is open to visitors.

  4. 4. Domodossola

    Domodossola is the Italian doorstep of the Simplon, a town of about eighteen thousand that sits at the junction of several Alpine valleys in the northern Piedmont, at the point where the Ossola valley opens toward Lake Maggiore. For centuries it has been a place of passage, with merchants, pilgrims and armies crossing the mountains by the Simplon road, and its old centre, built around the Piazza Mercato, still has the arcades, wooden balconies and frescoed houses of a market town. The weekly market is lively, and local cheeses, cured meats and the sweet wines of the valley are for sale.
