Why this ride

A coastal highway from Hualien, Taiwan to Taitung, Taiwan, via Shitiping. Key character: Pacific cliffs; terraces; coastal villages. Surface: paved.

East Coast Highway 11

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Hualien City

    Mountains and sea crowd against each other at Hualien. The city sits on a narrow coastal plain on Taiwan's east coast, with the Pacific Ocean on one side and the Central Mountain Range rising steeply to more than three thousand metres on the other, and the view from the waterfront, with the peaks looming over the town, is one of the most striking in Taiwan.

  2. 2. East Coast Highway 11 point 2

    South of Hualien, the Rift Valley opens up on the left, and on the coast the road follows a long, low shelf of land with the Pacific on one side. The rocks are volcanic and sedimentary, folded and eroded into odd shapes, and on a clear day the light on them is strange and beautiful. The coast road here is known for its quiet, with fewer cars than the main valley road.

  3. 3. East Coast Highway 11 point 3

    Chenggong is a small fishing port halfway down the east coast, and the nearby Sanxiantai is one of the great sights of Highway 11. A small island of rock lying just off the coast, it is connected to the shore by a long, curving footbridge with eight arches, like a dragon undulating across the sea. According to legend, three immortals once rested on the island, and the name means Three Immortals Platform. The reefs around it are rich with coral, and snorkelling and diving are possible when the sea is calm.
