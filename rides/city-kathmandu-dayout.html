Why this ride

374 km from Kathmandu to Kathmandu, about 6 h 25 of riding. It goes by way of Chitwan National Park and back through Chitwan National Park. It tops out around 2,050 m. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

The stops

  1. 1. Kathmandu

    Start here.

    It is a place that overwhelms at first, with narrow lanes, shrines on every corner, motorbikes and tuk-tuks, and the smell of incense, diesel and frying dough. On a clear morning, snow peaks appear to the north, a surprise after the haze.

    Read the complete Kathmandu write-up.

  2. 2. Chitwan National Park

    187 km in.

    Chitwan is the lowland end of Nepal, a flat, hot and green country of river and forest that feels like a different nation from the mountains. The park, set up in 1973 as Nepal's first, lies along the Rapti and Narayani rivers in the Terai, and UNESCO listed it in 1984. It is a home for the greater one-horned rhinoceros, once close to extinction here, whose numbers have recovered thanks to strict protection, as well as Bengal tigers, gharial crocodiles with their long narrow snouts, sloth bears and hundreds of bird species.
