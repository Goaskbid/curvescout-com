Why this ride

Austria's alpine toll road to 2,504 m. Closed in winter, and worth the toll. From Kaprun it runs 25 km to Heiligenblut; allow about 27 min of riding. The highest point is about 2,504 m. Fuscher Torl and Hochtor are open in summer only - check it is open before you set off. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The roads

Grossglockner Hochalpenstrasse

From Near Kaprun to Near Heiligenblut is 6 km. Over quickly - ride it slowly.

  2. 2. Near Heiligenblut

    About 6 km in.

    At 2,428 metres, Fuscher Törl is the high balcony of the Grossglockner High Alpine Road, a saddle on the northern side where the road loops around a ridge and the whole of the Fusch valley drops away below. A stone monument stands here in memory of the men who built the road, designed by the architect Clemens Holzmeister, and from the terrace you look across a sea of peaks to the summit of the Grossglockner itself, at 3,798 metres the highest mountain in Austria.

  5. 5. Heiligenblut

    The end: about 25 km.

    Heiligenblut is the village that every picture of the Grossglockner seems to include. The Gothic church of St Vincent, with its slender spire, stands on a knoll at the head of the Mölltal, and behind it the great snow cone of the mountain rises above forested slopes, the whole composition so tidy that it looks like a painting. At about 1,300 metres, the village is the last settlement on the Carinthian side before the high road climbs toward the glacier, and it has been a pilgrimage stop for centuries.
