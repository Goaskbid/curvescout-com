Why this ride

From Bellinzona it runs 303 km to Bellinzona; allow about 6 h 14 of riding. The stops in between: Sacri Monti of Piedmont and Lombardy, Monte San Giorgio, Isolino Virginia-Camilla-Isola di San Biag and back through Monte San Giorgio. It tops out around 1,550 m. It crosses from Switzerland into Italy - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules.

The roads

Grand Tour of Switzerland

Sacri Monti of Piedmont and Lombardy is 70 km from Chateau de Montebello, mostly on A2. A proper up-and-over: 790 m of climbing, 799 m at the top, 870 m down. Fill the tank before the climb.

The stops

  1. 1. Chateau de Montebello

    Start here.

    Bellinzona has three castles and a wall, and together they once decided who got through the Alps. The town sits at the head of the Ticino valley where several routes meet, including the roads to the San Bernardino and Gotthard passes, and whoever held it controlled the north-south traffic between Milan and the Swiss lands. Montebello is the middle one of the three, perched on a small hill on the east side of town, and the smallest of the trio. It was built in stages from the thirteenth century onward and expanded by the Visconti and Sforza dukes of Milan before the Swiss cantons of Uri, Schwyz and Nidwalden took over around 1500.

    Read the complete Chateau de Montebello write-up.

  2. 2. Sacri Monti of Piedmont and Lombardy

    70 km in.

    On the western shore of Lake Como, above the village of Ossuccio and facing the little Isola Comacina, a cobbled path climbs the hillside through olive groves and woods, past fourteen small chapels, to a sanctuary at the top. This is the Sacro Monte of Ossuccio, one of nine sacred mountains in Piedmont and Lombardy listed together by UNESCO. Built in the seventeenth century, the chapels illustrate the Mysteries of the Rosary with life-size terracotta and plaster figures arranged in dramatic scenes, each one a small piece of theatre.

    Read the complete Sacri Monti of Piedmont and Lombardy write-up.

  3. 3. Monte San Giorgio

    118 km in.

    Monte San Giorgio is a wooded, pyramid-shaped mountain on the shores of Lake Lugano, straddling the border between Switzerland and Italy. To the eye, it is a pleasant green hill with villages on its slopes. To palaeontologists, it is one of the most important fossil sites in the world. About two hundred and forty million years ago, during the Middle Triassic, this area was a tropical lagoon, and its sediments preserved an extraordinary range of marine reptiles, fish, invertebrates and plants. The mountain was added to the UNESCO list in 2003 on the Swiss side, and in 2010 on the Italian side.

    Read the complete Monte San Giorgio write-up.

  4. 4. Isolino Virginia-Camilla-Isola di San Biag

    151 km in.

    Lago di Varese is the quiet one. The big lakes, Maggiore and Lugano, grab the postcards and the coach tours, while this shallow, reedy sheet of water just west of Varese gets on with being pretty in a modest way. Off its western shore, a few hundred metres out from Biandronno, floats a tiny wooded island called Isolino Virginia, and that speck of land is one of the most important prehistoric sites in the Alpine world.
