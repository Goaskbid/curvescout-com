Why this ride

A 216 km round trip from Asheville - roughly 3 h 43 in the saddle. On the way it passes Great Smoky Mountains National Park and back through Great Smoky Mountains National Park. The highest point is about 950 m.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Asheville

    Asheville lies in a bowl of the Blue Ridge, where the French Broad River slides past old brick warehouses and the mountains stand on every side in layers of blue. At about six hundred and fifty metres it is cooler than the Carolina lowlands in summer, and the air smells of woodsmoke and wet leaves in autumn. The town has long attracted people who like to be away from the crowds: artists, craftspeople, musicians, brewers, and, a century ago, invalids seeking mountain air.

  2. 2. Great Smoky Mountains National Park

    The Smokies get their name from the haze. Water vapour and natural compounds released by the forest rise from the ridges and form a blue-grey mist that settles in the valleys, so that the mountains look like layers of smoke. They are among the oldest mountains on Earth, worn down for hundreds of millions of years, and rounded and green. This is the most visited national park in the country, and strangely, it charges no entry fee.
