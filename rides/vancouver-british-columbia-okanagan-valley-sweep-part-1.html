Why this ride

776 km round from Vancouver - plan on about 14 h 06 of riding. On the way it passes Merritt and Okanagan. The highest point is about 1,250 m. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Vancouver

    Vancouver is one of those cities where the mountains feel close enough to touch from downtown. The North Shore peaks rise straight out of Burrard Inlet, the Lions Gate Bridge leaps across to them from Stanley Park, and on a clear day you can watch floatplanes take off over the harbour while snow still sits on the summits. It is a big, busy, expensive city, but for a rider it is above all a gateway: to the Sea-to-Sky Highway north, the Fraser Valley east, and, for these rides, the ferries across the Strait of Georgia to Vancouver Island.

    Read the complete Vancouver write-up.

  2. 2. Merritt

    Merritt likes to call itself the country music capital of Canada, and for years it hosted a big country music festival; the town is decorated with murals of country stars, and a walk of stars on the main street honours musicians who have played here. It is that kind of place: a ranching town in the Nicola Valley, in the dry interior of British Columbia, surrounded by grasslands, pine forests and low, rolling hills.

    Read the complete Merritt write-up.

  3. 3. Okanagan

    Ask anyone in the Okanagan about the lake and sooner or later they will mention Ogopogo, the serpent said to live in the deep waters of Okanagan Lake, a legend rooted in the Syilx stories of a lake spirit and kept alive with cheerful enthusiasm by the tourist trade. You are unlikely to see it, but the lake itself, long, narrow and deep blue between dry, pine-covered hills, is worth the trip anyway.

    Read the complete Okanagan write-up.
