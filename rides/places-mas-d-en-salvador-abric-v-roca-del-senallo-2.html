Why this ride

From Albocàsser it runs 66 km to Albocàsser; allow about 1 h 08 of riding. On the way it passes Abric del.mas Blanc, Roca del Senallo and back through Abric del.mas Blanc. The highest point is about 1,250 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Mas d'en Salvador- Abric V

    In the Maestrat, shelters are named after whoever owned the nearest farm, and Mas d'en Salvador is an example: a mas is a farmstead, and Salvador was presumably the man who worked this patch of land at some point. Abric V is one in a numbered series of painted rock shelters in the ravine near the farm, about five kilometres from Albocàsser, carrying the small red figures of the Levantine tradition that UNESCO lists among the rock art of the Mediterranean basin in Spain. Such shelters have no grand frontage, just a lip of rock, a dry floor and a good view down the ravine.

  2. 2. Abric del.mas Blanc

    Benassal is a stone village on a high plateau in the Alt Maestrat, in the interior of Castellón, at around eight hundred metres above sea level, and it is known throughout the region for its spring water, the Font d'en Segures, which is bottled and sold across Spain. The Abric del Mas Blanc, the shelter of the white farmhouse, is one of the many rock-art sites in the area, part of the UNESCO listing of Mediterranean prehistoric art. The nearby Valltorta gorge, about twenty kilometres to the east, has some of the most famous Levantine paintings, including dramatic hunting scenes, and the Valltorta museum in Tírig is the best place to learn about them.

  3. 3. Roca del Senallo

    The Maestrat of inland Castellón is a highland of stone: stone villages on hilltops, stone walls running for kilometres across the slopes, stone farmhouses called masos scattered over the plateau. Roca del Senallo is a rock shelter near Culla, Vilafranca and Benassal, one of many painted shelters in this region, and it belongs to the World Heritage Site of Rock Art of the Mediterranean Basin. The Maestrat is one of the richest areas for Levantine art, with famous sites in the Valltorta gorge and around Ares and Albocàsser.
