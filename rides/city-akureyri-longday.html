Why this ride

From Akureyri it runs 529 km to Akureyri; allow about 18 h of riding. The stops in between: Vatnajökull National Park - Dynamic Nature and back through Vatnajökull National Park - Dynamic Nature. It tops out around 1,600 m. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

The stops

  1. 1. Akureyri

    Start here.

    Akureyri calls itself the capital of the north, and with a bit under eighteen thousand people it is the second city of Iceland by some margin. It lies at the head of Eyjafjörður, the longest fjord in the country, with steep snow-streaked mountains rising straight from the water on both sides. The air is sharper than in Reykjavik and the winters are colder and drier, and in summer the town has a gentler, greener feel than most of the island. The church, a tall concrete building designed by the architect Guðjón Samúelsson and completed in 1940, stands above the town at the top of a flight of steps, and its stained glass includes a window rescued from a church in Coventry after the war.

  2. 2. Vatnajökull National Park - Dynamic Nature

    265 km in. The ride passes about 20 km from it.

    Vatnajökull is the biggest ice cap in Europe by volume, a white plateau covering about eight percent of Iceland, with outlet glaciers that spill into valleys and lagoons. Beneath the ice lie volcanoes, including Grímsvötn and Bárðarbunga, that erupt from time to time, melting the ice from below and sending floods of water down the rivers. The national park that surrounds it, one of the largest in Europe, is a place where fire and ice are visibly at work on the same ground.
