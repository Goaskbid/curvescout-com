Why this ride

Norwegian Scenic Route. Arctic tundra, birdlife, Barents Sea. From Vuonnabahta it runs 110 km to Vardø; allow about 2 h of riding. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Vuonnabahta

    Vuonnabahta, Varangerbotn in Norwegian, is the small settlement at the head of the Varangerfjord, where the main roads of East Finnmark meet. The E6 comes in from the west and the south, the E75 heads east along the fjord toward Vadsø and Vardø, and it is a natural place to pause before tackling the long Varanger route along the northern shore. Around it the landscape is low and open, with birch scrub, bogs and gentle hills, and the fjord stretches away to the east, wide and grey.

  2. 2. Vadsø

    Vadsø is the administrative centre of Finnmark, a small town on the north shore of the Varangerfjord with a distinctive history and a population of mixed origin. In the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, large numbers of Finnish-speaking settlers, known as Kvens, came here from northern Finland and Sweden, fleeing famine and looking for work in fishing, and for a time they were the majority of the town's population. Their heritage is still visible in the old farmhouses, the family names and the culture.

  3. 3. Near Vadsø

    Ekkerøy is not quite an island: a low ridge of rock connected to the mainland by a narrow sandy isthmus, about fifteen kilometres east of Vadsø, with a small village on the sheltered side and a sheer bird cliff on the seaward side. In summer that cliff holds a colony of kittiwakes in the thousands, and the noise and smell of them reach you long before you see the nests, packed on every ledge above the waves.

  4. 4. Vardø

    Vardø is the easternmost town in Norway, further east than Istanbul, sitting on a small island off the tip of the Varanger peninsula and connected to the mainland by an undersea tunnel, the first of its kind in the country when it opened in 1982. The town is small and weathered, with a harbour full of fishing boats, colourful houses, abandoned buildings and an air of having seen better days, but it has a powerful history and a few sights that are worth the long ride.
