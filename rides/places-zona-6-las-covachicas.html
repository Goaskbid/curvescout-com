Why this ride

From Nerpio it runs 73 km to Nerpio; allow about 1 h 16 of riding. The stops in between: Las Casas de las Ingenieros Il, Las Covachicas and back through Las Casas de las Ingenieros Il. The highest point is about 1,400 m. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Zona 6

    Zona 6 sits seven kilometres from Nerpio, in a stretch of country where the numbers of the rock art catalogue replace the poetry of place names. It is part of the group of shelters in the Nerpio basin, the territory of the upper Taibilla river, and the whole group falls inside the UNESCO listing of the Mediterranean rock art of the Iberian Peninsula. The routes that visit it also take in the town of Úbeda, which is a long way off in the province of Jaén and a very different sort of heritage site.

  2. 2. Las Casas de las Ingenieros Il

    Water from these mountains ends up in the taps of Cartagena. The Taibilla river, which rises in the hills above Nerpio, feeds the canals of the Mancomunidad de los Canales del Taibilla, built in the twentieth century to supply towns across Murcia and beyond, and a name like the engineers' houses hints at how much that project marked the valley. Las Casas de los Ingenieros II is a rock shelter near Nerpio, one of the many painted sites in this remote corner of the Sierra de Segura.

  3. 3. Las Covachicas

    The Sierra del Segura, in the far south of Albacete province, is a world of its own: deep valleys, rivers lined with poplars, terraces of olive and almond trees, and white villages that look more Andalusian than Castilian. Letur is one of the most beautiful, a village of Moorish origin built on a rock above a stream, with narrow alleys, arches and gardens watered by old irrigation channels. Las Covachicas, a group of shelters, lies in the hills nearby, between Letur, Socovos and Férez.
