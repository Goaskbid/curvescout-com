Why this ride

From Petrila it runs 259 km to Petrila; allow about 4 h 28 of riding. On the way it passes Sarmizegetusa - Colonia Ulpia Traiana Augu, Iaz Tibiscum / Satu Bătrân and back through Sarmizegetusa - Colonia Ulpia Traiana Augu. The highest point is about 1,450 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Comărnicel III

    Not every ruin in the Orastie Mountains is a famous one. Comarnicel III sits on the same forested massif as the great Dacian capital of Sarmizegetusa Regia, a few kilometres east of it as the crow flies, and the number in its name tells you that archaeologists have found several sites by that name, each a patch of terrace, wall or platform on a ridge. What I can say is that the Dacians, before the Roman conquest in the early second century, shaped these mountains with terraces cut into the slopes, with houses, workshops, shrines and defensive walls, and that the remains are scattered across many hillsides.

  2. 2. Sarmizegetusa - Colonia Ulpia Traiana Augu

    After conquering Dacia in AD 106, the emperor Trajan founded a new capital for the province in the Hațeg basin, and named it Colonia Ulpia Traiana Augusta Dacica Sarmizegetusa, borrowing the name of the Dacian royal capital in the Orăștie mountains to the north-east. The city grew into a prosperous centre, with a forum, temples, baths, and an amphitheatre that could seat thousands, and its ruins, partly excavated, lie in fields beside the modern village of Sarmizegetusa. The site has a museum, and you can walk around the forum and the amphitheatre, imagining the gladiatorial games that once filled it.

  3. 3. Iaz Tibiscum / Satu Bătrân

    Where the Bistra and Timiș rivers meet, the Romans built one of the more important towns in their province of Dacia. Tibiscum, near the village of Jupa, grew from a fort into a sizeable settlement, and the low foundations, streets and bath remains can still be walked today with a small museum alongside. Iaz and Satu Bătrân are the neighbouring places on the map, and the whole district sits in the Caransebeș basin, hemmed in by the Țarcu and Semenic mountains.
