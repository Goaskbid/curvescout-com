Why this ride

Rolling fields, manor houses and lake-country calm. 150 km round from Schwaan - plan on about 2 h 35 of riding. It goes by way of Basedow manor roads, Malchin lake view and Teterow window. It keeps to quiet secondary roads.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Schwaan river road

    Schwaan lies on the Warnow river a short ride south of Rostock, and it is one of those small Mecklenburg towns that surprise you with a bit of art. In the late nineteenth century, painters were drawn to the Warnow valley for its meadows, light and quiet, and Schwaan became known as a colony of artists, a bit like Ahrenshoop on the coast. A small museum shows work from the period, and the old town still has a gentle, creative atmosphere. The Gothic brick church, built in the typical north German style of red brick, stands above the river and gives the place its character.

  3. 3. Malchin lake view

    Malchin is a lake town in the heart of what is called Mecklenburg Switzerland, a name that makes visitors from the Alps smile, because the highest hills here are not much more than a hundred metres. But the name has a point. After the flat plains of northern Germany, this area of rolling moraine, deep lakes and beech woods does feel like a small world of its own, shaped by the last ice sheet that left behind a landscape of ridges, hollows and scattered boulders.

  4. 4. Teterow food window

    Teterow is a small town beside a lake, and among motorcycle people it has a bigger reputation than its size suggests. The Bergring, a hilly circuit just outside the town, has hosted motorcycle racing since the 1920s and 1930s, and it was one of the best-known tracks in East Germany, drawing crowds to watch grass track and speedway on the steep slopes. It is a place where riders from across the region come to watch, and the local pride in it is real. If you ride through when a meeting is on, the roads fill with bikes and the air smells of two-stroke.
