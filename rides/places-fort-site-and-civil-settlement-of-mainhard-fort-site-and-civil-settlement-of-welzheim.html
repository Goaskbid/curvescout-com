Why this ride

114 km round trip from Fort site and civil settlement of Mainhard, about 2 h 12 of riding. It runs out through Welzheim-Ost civil settlement northern par, Fort site and civil settlement of Welzheim and Watchtowers WP 12/39 and comes back by another road.

The stops

  1. 1. Fort site and civil settlement of Mainhard

    Start here.

    Mainhardt sits on a plateau in the Swabian-Franconian Forest, a wooded upland east of Heilbronn, and in the second century, a Roman army unit was stationed here. The fort was built around the middle of the second century, as part of the forward movement of the frontier, to guard the Limes that ran just to the east. It held a unit of around five hundred soldiers, and a civil settlement grew up beside it, with houses, workshops, baths and inns, where families, craftsmen and traders lived.

    Read the complete Fort site and civil settlement of Mainhard write-up.

  2. 2. Welzheim-Ost civil settlement northern par

    36 km in.

    In the Swabian Forest east of Stuttgart, the Roman frontier ran as straight as a ruler for some eighty kilometres, ignoring hills and valleys in a way that still puzzles historians. Welzheim lies on this straight stretch of the Upper German-Raetian Limes, and the town had two Roman forts, the west and east forts, with a civil settlement around them. The northern part of the settlement near the east fort is one of the components of the Limes World Heritage Site, and the Ostkastell has been turned into an archaeological park with a reconstructed gate.

    Read the complete Welzheim-Ost civil settlement northern par write-up.

  3. 3. Fort site and civil settlement of Welzheim

    36 km in.

    Welzheim had not one but two Roman forts, and that alone tells you how seriously the frontier was taken in the Swabian Forest. The larger west fort held a cavalry unit, while the smaller east fort, now an archaeological park on the edge of town, housed an auxiliary infantry detachment. At the east fort the west gate has been rebuilt to its full height, and standing in front of it you get a rare sense of scale that low foundations never give. Around both forts lay the civil settlement, the vicus, with workshops, taverns and homes.

    Read the complete Fort site and civil settlement of Welzheim write-up.

  4. 4. Watchtowers WP 12/39

    66 km in.

    Iggingen is a small village on the plateau above the Rems valley, a few kilometres east of Schwäbisch Gmünd, and like many villages in this part of Württemberg it has a Roman frontier running through its fields. The site labelled Watchtower WP 12/39 sits on the Raetian Limes, the stone wall that marked the edge of Roman Raetia, and from here the line runs eastwards towards Aalen, the largest Roman cavalry fort north of the Alps, now home to an excellent Limes museum.

    Read the complete Watchtowers WP 12/39 write-up.
