Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. New York / Hudson Valley rides

    This is the valley of the South Branch of the Raritan River, a trout stream that runs clear enough over its stones to keep fly fishermen loyal for generations. Califon itself is a tiny place, barely a thousand people, with an old railroad right-of-way now used as a rail trail and a main street you will cross before you notice it. Long Valley sits a few kilometres to the north, and the hills on its far side belong to Schooley's Mountain, the first stretch of real ridge you meet when riding in from the east.

  3. 3. Ridgefield

    Before Hollywood, there was Fort Lee. In the early 1900s the cliffs and towns along this stretch of the Hudson were the centre of American film-making, with studios turning out westerns, serials and melodramas, and serial heroines were filmed dangling from the very cliffs you ride above. The industry moved west for the sunshine, but the story is a good one to carry into the last miles of a ride.

  4. 4. Back to Tarrytown Croton

    Garden City and Hempstead sit on Long Island's flat middle, far from the Croton reservoirs, in what was once a huge natural prairie called the Hempstead Plains. This was the only true prairie in the east, a grassland of tall grasses and wildflowers that stretched for miles, and nearly all of it has been covered by houses, shops and roads, with only small patches protected. It is a strange thing to ride across a landscape you know used to look like Kansas.
