  1. 1. Brisbane

    Brisbane winds along its river like a long, lazy snake, with the city centre in one loop and suburbs spreading over low hills on every side. It began in 1825 as a penal settlement, chosen for its position on the Brisbane River, and grew slowly into the capital of Queensland. A humid subtropical climate means jacaranda trees blaze purple in October and November, thunderstorms roll in on summer afternoons, and the winters are mild and sunny, ideal for riding. The Story Bridge, the riverside parks at South Bank and the ferries that glide up and down the water give the city a relaxed charm.
