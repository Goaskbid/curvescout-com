Why this ride

A 401 km round trip from Cerro - roughly 6 h 55 in the saddle. The stops in between: Viñales Valley and back through Viñales Valley. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Havana

    Havana comes at you all at once: sea wind, car horns, ocean spray over the Malecón, pastel colonnades in various stages of collapse, and the unmistakable smell of old engines and the sea. It is the capital of Cuba and home to roughly two million people, and the old centre, La Habana Vieja, was given UNESCO World Heritage status in 1982 for its Spanish colonial streets and fortifications.

  2. 2. Viñales Valley

    The Viñales Valley is one of those places where the landscape looks like a stage set. Flat red fields lie between steep, rounded limestone hills called mogotes, sheer-sided and covered in vegetation, standing like green loaves on a tablecloth. They are the remains of an ancient cave system whose roof collapsed over millions of years, and the surrounding karst is among the oldest in the Caribbean. The valley sits in the western province of Pinar del Río, and UNESCO recognised it as a cultural landscape in 1999.
