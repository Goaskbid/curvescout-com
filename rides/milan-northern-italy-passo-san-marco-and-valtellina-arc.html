Why this ride

384 km round from Milan - plan on about 6 h 59 of riding. On the way it passes Monticelli Brusati and Marco. The highest point is about 1,250 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Milan

    Milan does not try to charm you, and that is part of its charm. It is Italy's business capital, a city of fashion, finance and design that works hard and dresses well. At the centre stands the Duomo, a Gothic cathedral begun in 1386 and not finished for about five centuries, a forest of white marble spires and statues, and you can climb onto the roof among them. Beside it, the glass-roofed Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II is a nineteenth-century shopping arcade, and beyond it La Scala opera house.

    Read the complete Milan write-up.

  2. 2. Monticelli Brusati

    Franciacorta is Italy's answer to Champagne, and Monticelli Brusati sits in the middle of it. Sparkling wine made by the classic method, second fermentation in the bottle, has been the business of these low hills between Brescia and Lake Iseo since the 1960s, and the result is a landscape of tidy vineyards, cypress-lined drives and cellar doors, with old stone villages tucked into the folds of the land.

    Read the complete Monticelli Brusati write-up.

  3. 3. Marco

    Dante may have ridden past here. In the twelfth canto of the Inferno he compares a steep, broken descent in hell to a great landslide on the Adige south of Trento, and most scholars believe he meant the Lavini di Marco, the chaos of boulders and bare rock above this village. The slope has another secret: in the 1990s, scientists found hundreds of dinosaur footprints preserved in its limestone slabs, and a path now leads up to them.
