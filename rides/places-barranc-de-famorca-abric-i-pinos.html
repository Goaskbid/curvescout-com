Why this ride

From Castell de Castells it runs 105 km to Castell de Castells; allow about 2 h 19 of riding. On the way it passes Barraac de la Palla, Pinós and back through Barraac de la Palla. The line favours views over speed.

The stops

  1. 1. Barranc de famorca- Abric I

    Start here.

    Famorca clings to the side of a mountain in the Comtat, a district of Alicante province where the roads are as steep as the villages are small. Abric I in the Barranc de Famorca is the first of a series of painted shelters in the ravine, and like its neighbours it shows Levantine red paintings, part of the UNESCO list for the Mediterranean basin. The shelters lie in a deep gully below the village, and getting down to them means a walk on a stony path.

    Read the complete Barranc de famorca- Abric I write-up.

  2. 2. Barraac de la Palla

    26 km in.

    Tormos and Sagra are tiny villages at the foot of the mountains in the north of Alicante province, at the point where the plain of the Marina Alta meets the hills. They are surrounded by orange groves and orchards, with the mountains of the Vall de Laguar and the Serra del Migdia rising steeply behind. The Barranc de la Palla, the ravine of the straw, cuts into the hills near Tormos, close also to Orba and the town of Pego.

    Read the complete Barraac de la Palla write-up.

  3. 3. Pinós

    49 km in.

    Inland from the busy coast of the Marina Alta, behind Benissa and Calp, the land folds into a series of steep, terraced valleys where almond and olive trees cling to dry-stone walls and white farmhouses sit among the pines. Pinós is a rural area in the hills near Benissa, Senija and Jalón, and the rides link it to the rock-art shelters of the barrancs, the ravines that cut through the limestone of this region, part of the UNESCO listing of Mediterranean prehistoric art. The exact contents of the nearby shelters are for specialists, but the landscape itself has a quiet, timeless beauty, especially in the soft light of late afternoon.
