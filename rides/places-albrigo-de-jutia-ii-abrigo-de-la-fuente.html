Why this ride

A 69 km round trip from Nerpio - roughly 1 h 11 in the saddle. The stops in between: Abrigo del Collado de la Cruz, Abrigo de la fuente and back through Abrigo del Collado de la Cruz. The highest point is about 1,400 m. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Albrigo de Jutia II

    Far inland in the south-east of Spain, in the folded highlands of the Sierra de Segura, there is a small village called Nerpio, and around it cliffs hold some of the most remarkable prehistoric paintings in Europe. Jutia is one of several shelters here, the overhangs where hunter-gatherers painted red figures of animals and people thousands of years ago. This is Levantine rock art, a tradition that runs along the Mediterranean side of the Iberian peninsula and is protected as a World Heritage group. Many of the shelters are fenced and some are reached only on foot, so check access before you go.

  2. 2. Abrigo del Collado de la Cruz

    The Collado de la Cruz is a saddle in the wild Sierra del Segura, in the south-west corner of Albacete province, and the shelter here belongs to one of the richest regions of prehistoric painting in Spain. Nerpio, the village about three and a half kilometres away, sits at over a thousand metres and is surrounded by dozens of rock-art sites, in both the Levantine style and the later schematic style, which uses simple dots, lines and stick-like figures. The Levantine paintings are protected by UNESCO as part of the Rock Art of the Mediterranean Basin on the Iberian Peninsula.

  3. 3. Abrigo de la fuente

    Nerpio is about as remote as it gets in southern Spain: a village in the far south-west corner of Albacete province, deep in the Sierra del Segura, close to the borders of Andalucía and Murcia. It sits in the valley of the Taibilla river, surrounded by mountains, with terraced fields, walnut trees and pine forests on the slopes. The Abrigo de la Fuente, the shelter of the spring, lies in the hills about eleven kilometres from the village.
