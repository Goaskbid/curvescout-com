Why this ride

71 km from María to María, about 1 h 13 of riding. It goes by way of Abrigo de los Gavilanes, Tejera and back through Abrigo de los Gavilanes. The highest point is about 1,200 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Lavadero Tello IV

    Vélez-Blanco has a castle that has been in two countries. Built in the early sixteenth century by the first Marquis of los Vélez, it was a Renaissance palace of white marble inside a fortified shell, and in 1904 the owner sold the marble patio, which was shipped in crates to Paris and then to New York, where it is now in the Metropolitan Museum. The castle's empty courtyard remains in the village. Lavadero Tello is sixteen kilometres away, in the Sierra María-Los Vélez natural park, where red-and-gold hills, almond groves and pale limestone peaks rise above the plains of Almería and Granada.

  2. 2. Abrigo de los Gavilanes

    Gavilanes means sparrowhawks, and the name fits a shelter high on a limestone face above the dry country where Murcia meets Almería. The Abrigo de los Gavilanes lies in the hills west of Lorca, in an area scattered with painted shelters such as the Collado de la Cruz and Los Grajos, the jackdaws, and it is part of the World Heritage Site of Rock Art of the Mediterranean Basin on the Iberian Peninsula. The figures are small, red and schematic, painted by communities who farmed and herded here thousands of years ago.

  3. 3. Tejera

    A tejera is a tile works, a kiln where roof tiles were baked from local clay, and the name is common across the dry south-east of Spain. The Tejera on your map is near Vélez-Blanco, about two kilometres from the village, in the country that falls away from the Sierra María towards the plain, where red soil, almond terraces and clumps of pine and juniper make up the view.
