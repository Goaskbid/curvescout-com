Why this ride

253 km from Ljubljana to Ljubljana, about 4 h 22 of riding. It goes by way of Vojsko - Idrijca Water Barrier, City Fortress of Palmanova and back through Vojsko - Idrijca Water Barrier. The highest point is about 900 m. It crosses from Slovenia into Italy - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Trnovo Bridge

    Trnovo Bridge is a modest, lovely thing that sums up Plecnik's way of working. In the 1930s, he rebuilt a small bridge over the Gradaščica stream in the Trnovo district of Ljubljana, at the foot of the hill where the church of St Francis of Assisi stands, and he made it wide, wider than the street it joined, so that it became a small square as much as a crossing. The bridge is lined with pillars and balustrades of pale stone, and he planted two rows of birch trees along its edges, a gentle, living touch in the middle of the city. The pyramids at the corners are a nod to Egyptian forms, which he used with a sense of play.

  2. 2. Vojsko - Idrijca Water Barrier

    The water barriers of the Idrijca are among the strangest industrial monuments in Europe: massive stone and timber dams built across a narrow mountain river not to store water for drinking or power, but to float logs. Locals call them klavže, and the biggest have been nicknamed the Slovenian pyramids for their huge, carefully cut blocks. They lie in the deep, wooded gorge below the Vojsko plateau, a short ride from the town of Idrija.

  3. 3. City Fortress of Palmanova

    From the air Palmanova is a perfect nine-pointed star, and even from the saddle you can feel the geometry. The roads come in through monumental gates, cross the moats and ramparts, and run dead straight to a vast hexagonal piazza in the middle, where the whole town seems to stop and look at itself. It is one of the purest examples of an ideal Renaissance city ever built, and it sits on the flat Friulian plain between Udine and the Adriatic like a diagram that somebody forgot to take back.
