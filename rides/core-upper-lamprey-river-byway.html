Why this ride

An understated rural loop with rivers, farms and old roadside settlements. The road: NH-43 and US-4. Distance and riding time are estimates from the checkpoints until the road is measured on this page.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Candia

    Candia is the kind of New Hampshire town where the main attractions are a road, a church and a view of fields. It sits in Rockingham County, a short drive east of Manchester, on gently rolling land of old farms, orchards and woodlots, divided by stone walls laid by farmers who cleared the glacial rocks from their fields two centuries ago. There is no grand sight and no crowd, and that is why it makes a fine place to start a ride.
