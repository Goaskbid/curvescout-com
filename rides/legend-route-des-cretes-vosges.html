Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Sainte-Marie-aux-Mines

    Sainte-Marie-aux-Mines sits in a narrow valley on the Alsace side of the Vosges, with steep wooded slopes rising on both flanks and a small river running through the middle. The name gives away its story. This was silver country, and from the Middle Ages until the sixteenth century miners dug galleries into the hills here, making the valley, the Val d'Argent, one of the richest in the region. Later, textiles took over, and tall old mill buildings still line the town's main street. Visitors can still go underground in an old mine to see the tunnels.

  5. 5. Cernay

    Cernay marks the southern end of the Vosges ridge road, a town of around eleven thousand people on the Thur river, where the mountains drop to the Alsace plain. The old centre has a fortified gate, the Tour de l'Horloge, and a few half-timbered houses, and the town has the practical air of a place that serves a valley. It is also the gateway to a part of the Vosges with a serious past.
