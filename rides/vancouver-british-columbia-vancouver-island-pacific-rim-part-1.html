Vancouver Island Pacific Rim · part 1

Why this ride

A 530 km loop that starts and ends near West End, about 9 h 38 of riding. On the way it passes Nanaimo (ferry from Horseshoe Bay), Port Alberni and Pacific Rim, Tofino.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Vancouver

    Vancouver is a city that sits between mountains and sea, so closely that you can ski in the morning and sail in the afternoon, as the local line goes. Glass towers crowd a peninsula with the North Shore peaks rising directly behind, and Stanley Park, a huge, forested park on the tip of the peninsula, wraps around the downtown with a seawall path and old-growth cedar. Gastown, the historic quarter, takes its name from a talkative saloon keeper, Gassy Jack Deighton, whose statue stands in the square.

    Read the complete Vancouver write-up.

  2. 2. Nanaimo (ferry from Horseshoe Bay)

    The crossing from Horseshoe Bay to Departure Bay is one of the classic openings of a Vancouver Island trip. The big BC Ferries ships leave from the little cove at the start of the Sea-to-Sky Highway and steam across the Strait of Georgia for about an hour and a half, past the islands of Howe Sound and out into open water, with the snowy Coast Mountains behind you and the long blue shape of the island ahead. On a clear day, it is a cruise in itself; in a winter gale, it is an adventure.

    Read the complete Nanaimo (ferry from Horseshoe Bay) write-up.

  3. 3. Port Alberni

    Port Alberni sits at the head of the Alberni Inlet, a long, narrow fjord that reaches almost halfway across Vancouver Island from the west coast. This geography has shaped its history: it became a major centre for logging, sawmills and fishing, and the smell of the pulp mill has long been part of daily life. In 1964, the great Alaska earthquake sent a tsunami up the inlet, and the funnelling effect amplified the waves, which flooded parts of the town. Today, the waterfront has a harbour quay with shops, cafés and boat tours down the inlet.

    Read the complete Port Alberni write-up.

  4. 4. Pacific Rim, Tofino

    The road ends at Tofino, and it feels like the end of the world in the best possible way. This small town sits at the tip of a peninsula on the wild west coast of Vancouver Island, surrounded by the waters of Clayoquot Sound, with forested islands, mountains and the open Pacific. It has become famous for surfing, storm watching and its relaxed, slightly bohemian atmosphere.

    Read the complete Pacific Rim, Tofino write-up.
