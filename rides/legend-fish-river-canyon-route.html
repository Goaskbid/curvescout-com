Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Keetmanshoop

    Keetmanshoop is the capital of the south, which in Namibia means a flat, dry town of some fifteen thousand people where everything useful happens at once. It is the place you buy tyres, groceries and cash, and fill every container you own, before heading for the Fish River Canyon or turning west towards Aus and the coast. Treat it with that respect and it is a perfectly good place to be.

  2. 2. Fish River Canyon Route point 2

    The map puts this point on the road south of Keetmanshoop, close to Seeheim, a spot whose name appears on road signs long before anything else does. It is the junction where the road to the coast and Lüderitz leaves the main north-south highway, and it lies on the Fish River, the longest river in Namibia, which here is mostly a wide, sandy bed with pools after rain and a thin line of green trees along the banks.

  4. 4. Fish River Canyon Route point 4

    The main viewpoint at the Fish River Canyon is a slightly strange place to arrive at, because the ground simply stops. You walk or ride the last stretch across flat, stony plateau, a low rail or a few boulders mark an edge, and below you the land falls away in a great meandering gorge. The river you see is a thin green-brown thread at the bottom, wandering in horseshoe bends between buttresses of layered rock.
