  1. 1. Keetmanshoop

    Keetmanshoop is the capital of the south, which in Namibia means a flat, dry town of some fifteen thousand people where everything useful happens at once. It is the place you buy tyres, groceries and cash, and fill every container you own, before heading for the Fish River Canyon or turning west towards Aus and the coast. Treat it with that respect and it is a perfectly good place to be.

  2. 2. Fish River Canyon

    The Fish River Canyon is one of those places where the map and the eye disagree. On paper it is a crack in the far south of Namibia, in the Karas region, a long way from anything. In person the ground simply stops, and a gorge on the order of 160 kilometres long and well over 500 metres deep drops away in a series of giant hairpin bends carved by a river that only runs properly after the summer rains. It is regularly named as the second-largest canyon on earth, which is a claim worth a shrug, because standing at the rim you are not comparing it with anything.
