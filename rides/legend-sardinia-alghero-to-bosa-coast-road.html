Why this ride

curated scenic road. wild coast, sea cliffs, Mediterranean. A one-way ride of 45 km, Alghero to Bosa - roughly 50 min in the saddle. The line favours views over speed.

The roads

Sardinia Alghero to Bosa Coast Road

From Alghero to Near Bosa is 34 km. A proper up-and-over: 520 m of climbing, 385 m at the top, 380 m down. Best in the early light, before the traffic.
