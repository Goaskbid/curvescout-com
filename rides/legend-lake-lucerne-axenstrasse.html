Why this ride

curated scenic road. lake cliffs, tunnels, mountains. A one-way ride of 34 km, Luzern to Altdorf - roughly 37 min in the saddle. The highest point is about 1,800 m. The line favours views over speed.

Scenic road chapters

Klausen Pass

Slow enough to look. The surface follows the shoreline, so the corners arrive where the water decided, not where an engineer did. Water arrives beside the road and stays for 5 km. The line follows the shore because there is nowhere else for it to go.

  4. 4. Near Altdorf

    Altdorf is the capital of the canton of Uri, a small town at the head of Lake Lucerne's southern arm where the Reuss river leaves the mountains and the Gotthard route begins. It is a practical, low-key place, flat and sheltered, with a pretty centre and a roar of traffic nearby from the motorway, and it is the sort of town where riders refill the tank, buy a coffee and look up at the wall of peaks above.
