Why this ride

Out of Icheon-si, one way: 318 km, roughly 6 h in the saddle. On the way it passes near Seonghwan and Scenic stretch near Hwaseong-si. It crosses from South Korea into North Korea - carry ID and check the toll rules.

The roads

Icheon-si · Gyodong and Hwaseong-si Loop high point → Icheon-si · Gyodong and Hwaseong-si Loop scenic ro

It is 87 km from High point near Seonghwan to Scenic stretch near Hwaseong-si. Water alongside for much of it - pick one good pull-out and stop.
