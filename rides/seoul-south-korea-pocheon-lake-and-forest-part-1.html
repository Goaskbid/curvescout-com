Why this ride

Out of Icheon-si, one way: 318 km, roughly 5 h 53 in the saddle. On the way it passes near Seonghwan and Scenic stretch near Hwaseong-si. It crosses from South Korea into North Korea - carry ID and check the toll rules.

Scenic road chapters

Pocheon Lake and Forest high point → Pocheon Lake and Forest scenic ro

Slow enough to look. The surface follows the shoreline, so the corners arrive where the water decided, not where an engineer did. Water arrives beside the road and stays for 87 km. The line follows the shore because there is nowhere else for it to go.
