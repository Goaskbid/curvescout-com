Why this ride

National Scenic Byway. ponderosa forest, Grand Canyon, high plateau. It tops out around 2,400 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The roads

Kaibab Plateau North Rim Parkway

Kaibab Plateau North Rim Parkway point 1 to Kaibab Plateau North Rim Parkway point 2: 32 km on Grand Canyon Highway. It stays up high the whole way, between 2,425 and 2,700 m. Cold up there even in July - pack a warm layer and check it is open.
