Why this ride

348 km from Camargo to Camargo, about 5 h 22 of riding. It goes by way of Chemin de la Liébana, Monastère de Santo Toribio de Liébana, Covaciella and back through Monastère de Santo Toribio de Liébana. It tops out around 1,300 m. The line favours views over speed.

The roads

Picos de Europa Loop

65 km from Monastère de Santo Toribio de Liébana to Covaciella on Carretera Nacional de León a Santander por Potes. You lose around 440 m along the way; the top is 507 m. This is the bit the ride is built around.

The stops

  1. 1. El Pendo

    Start here.

    In 1997, in a cave that people had been visiting for over a century, a group of researchers noticed paintings that everyone had walked past: a frieze of red figures, deer, horses and goats, high on a wall in the main chamber. El Pendo, in the municipality of Camargo just south of Santander, had long been known for its deep layers of Stone Age occupation, but the frieze made it one of the important painted caves of Cantabria, and in 2008 it joined the World Heritage listing of the Paleolithic cave art of northern Spain alongside Altamira.

    Read the complete El Pendo write-up.

  2. 2. Chemin de la Liébana

    57 km in.

    San Vicente de la Barquera is where the Camino Lebaniego begins, the old pilgrim route that leaves the Cantabrian coast and climbs south into the mountains of Liébana to the monastery of Santo Toribio. The town sits on a pair of estuaries, with a long medieval bridge carrying the road across the water, a castle and the Gothic church of Santa María above the harbour, and on clear days the snowy peaks of the Picos de Europa rising behind the marshes like a stage set.

    Read the complete Chemin de la Liébana write-up.

  3. 3. Monastère de Santo Toribio de Liébana

    111 km in.

    Liébana is a green bowl surrounded by mountains, a valley system in south-west Cantabria with the town of Potes at its centre and the limestone towers of the Picos de Europa rising to the north and west. On a hillside above Potes, among oak and chestnut woods, stands the monastery of Santo Toribio, one of the holy places of Christian Spain. It keeps the Lignum Crucis, said to be the largest surviving fragment of the True Cross, and for that reason it holds a jubilee year whenever the feast of Saint Toribio, the sixteenth of April, falls on a Sunday.

    Read the complete Monastère de Santo Toribio de Liébana write-up.

  4. 4. Covaciella

    176 km in.

    Between the coastal town of Llanes and the high limestone walls of the Picos de Europa, a small cave near Carreña de Cabrales holds images that have been in the dark for something like fifteen thousand years. Covaciella was only found in the 1990s, and its paintings of bison, horses and deer, in black and red, are among the finest examples of Palaeolithic art in Asturias. It was added to the UNESCO listing of the Cave of Altamira and Paleolithic Cave Art of Northern Spain in 2008. The cave is closed to the public to protect the paintings, but the nearby interpretation centre explains what is inside and why it matters.

    Read the complete Covaciella write-up.
