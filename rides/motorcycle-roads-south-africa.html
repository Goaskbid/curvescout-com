Plan a motorcycle trip in South Africa

Begin with the road and province. For Western Cape rides, check the provincial closure information; do not carry its status into a different part of South Africa.

Read the restriction type

The Western Cape public road-closure layer distinguishes no access, stop-and-go, proceed-when-safe and local-access conditions. Those are different instructions, not interchangeable warning labels.

Keep the itinerary intact

A pass article does not validate every approach or return road. Check the complete sequence and any surface notes before selecting a variant.

Save a practical plan

Allow time for stops and delays and retain a separate alternative where needed. Use the calendar workspace for rechecks, not as a guarantee of access.
