Why this ride

A proper escape from Munich with roads worth waking up for. A 245 km loop that starts and ends near Neufahrn bei Freising, about 5 h 07 of riding. On the way it passes Scenic stretch near Moosinning, near Holzkirchen and Oberhaching. The climb of the day is near Grafing bei München. The line favours views over speed.

  2. 2. Scenic stretch near Moosinning

    North-east of Munich, the land flattens into a wide plain of fields, drainage ditches and scattered villages. This is the Erdinger Moos, once a vast marsh, drained over the past two centuries and now home to farms, gravel pits and, a few kilometres away, Munich's international airport. Moosinning is one of the villages on its edge, quiet and practical, with a church, a few farms and modern housing.

  6. 6. Food stop near Herrsching am Ammersee

    On a hill above the Ammersee, the onion-domed church of Andechs Monastery has drawn pilgrims for centuries, but many modern visitors come for something else: the beer. The Benedictine monks of Andechs brew some of the most celebrated beers in Bavaria, and the beer garden next to the monastery, with its view over the countryside, is famous. The climb from Herrsching through the Kiental, a narrow wooded valley, is a pleasant walk for those who want to earn their beer; riders, of course, take the road and leave the beer for later.
