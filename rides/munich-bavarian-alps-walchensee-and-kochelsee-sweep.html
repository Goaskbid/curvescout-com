Why this ride

A proper escape from Munich with roads worth waking up for. A 316 km loop that starts and ends near Neufahrn bei Freising, about 5 h 12 of riding. On the way it passes Scenic stretch near Moosinning, near Holzkirchen and Oberhaching. The climb of the day is near Grafing bei München. The line favours views over speed.

The roads

Neufahrn bei Freising · Herrsching am Ammersee and Holzkirchen Loop fuel gate → Walchensee and Kochelsee Sw

Scenic stretch near Moosinning is 29 km from Fuel stop in Neufahrn bei Freising, mostly on B 388. An easy profile, never above 483 m. This is the bit the ride is built around.

The stops

  2. 2. Scenic stretch near Moosinning

    29 km in.

    North-east of Munich, the land flattens into a wide plain of fields, drainage ditches and scattered villages. This is the Erdinger Moos, once a vast marsh, drained over the past two centuries and now home to farms, gravel pits and, a few kilometres away, Munich's international airport. Moosinning is one of the villages on its edge, quiet and practical, with a church, a few farms and modern housing.

  6. 6. Food stop near Herrsching am Ammersee

    182 km in. Food stop near Herrsching am Ammersee is where this day stops to eat.

    On a hill above the Ammersee, the onion-domed church of Andechs Monastery has drawn pilgrims for centuries, but many modern visitors come for something else: the beer. The Benedictine monks of Andechs brew some of the most celebrated beers in Bavaria, and the beer garden next to the monastery, with its view over the countryside, is famous. The climb from Herrsching through the Kiental, a narrow wooded valley, is a pleasant walk for those who want to earn their beer; riders, of course, take the road and leave the beer for later.

  8. 8. Back to Neufahrn bei Freising

    Back where you started, 316 km.

    The nearest bigger town is Freising, perched on the Domberg above the Isar, with a cathedral and a brewery at Weihenstephan that claims to be the oldest in the world, running since 1040. That claim is proudly defended, and the brewery's tavern is a pleasant stop for a Bavarian lunch.
