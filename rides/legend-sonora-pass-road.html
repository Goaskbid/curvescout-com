Why this ride

curated scenic road. Sierra Nevada, high pass, granite. From Sonora it runs 116 km to Bridgeport; allow about 2 h 07 of riding. The highest point is about 2,950 m. Sonora Pass is open in summer only - check it is open before you set off. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Sonora

    Sonora takes its name from the Mexican miners from the state of Sonora who struck gold here in 1848 and gave the camp its first character. Within a couple of years it was one of the richest and rowdiest towns of the southern mines, so rich that it was called the Queen of the Southern Mines, and in 1850 the new state of California's foreign miners' tax drove many of its founders out. Today it is the seat of Tuolumne County, a busy little hill town on the western foot of the Sierra, and the starting point for Highway 108 and the long climb to Sonora Pass.

  3. 3. Sonora Pass Road point 3

    Sonora Pass, at 2,933 metres, is the second-highest highway pass in the Sierra Nevada, beaten only by Tioga Pass in Yosemite, and it may well be the most demanding. On the approach from the west the road climbs steeply over open slopes of volcanic rock, with long views back down into the Stanislaus canyon, and the final kilometres are a series of tight, steep switchbacks with gradients that reach well into the double digits. On the eastern side the descent toward the Walker River is even steeper, with signs warning of grades of up to 26 per cent in places.

  4. 4. Bridgeport

    Bridgeport is a small, sun-baked county seat in the eastern Sierra, at the edge of a high valley of sagebrush and meadow, ringed by snow-capped peaks. Fewer than six hundred people live here, at an elevation of about sixty-five hundred feet, and the main street along Highway 395 is a short row of cafes, motels, a store and a stately white courthouse. That courthouse, built in 1880 in a Victorian style with a cupola, is one of the oldest in California still in use, and gives the town a quiet dignity.
