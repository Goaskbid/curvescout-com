Why this ride

262 km from Mendrisio to Mendrisio, about 4 h of riding. It goes by way of Church and Dominican Convent of Santa Mari, Venetian Works of Defence between the 16th and back through Church and Dominican Convent of Santa Mari. It tops out around 900 m. It crosses from Switzerland into Italy - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

The roads

Lake Como Scenic Circuit

It is 71 km from Monte San Giorgio to Church and Dominican Convent of Santa Mari, mostly on A8. You lose around 430 m along the way; the top is 553 m. Water alongside for much of it - pick one good pull-out and stop.

The stops

  1. 1. Monte San Giorgio

    Start here.

    Monte San Giorgio is a wooded, pyramid-shaped mountain on the shores of Lake Lugano, straddling the border between Switzerland and Italy. To the eye, it is a pleasant green hill with villages on its slopes. To palaeontologists, it is one of the most important fossil sites in the world. About two hundred and forty million years ago, during the Middle Triassic, this area was a tropical lagoon, and its sediments preserved an extraordinary range of marine reptiles, fish, invertebrates and plants. The mountain was added to the UNESCO list in 2003 on the Swiss side, and in 2010 on the Italian side.

    Read the complete Monte San Giorgio write-up.

  2. 2. Church and Dominican Convent of Santa Mari

    71 km in.

    Most people who come to Santa Maria delle Grazie in Milan are here for one painting, and they are right to be. On the wall of the old refectory of the Dominican convent, Leonardo da Vinci painted the Last Supper in the 1490s for Duke Ludovico Sforza, and despite his experimental technique, which began to fail almost at once, despite centuries of damage and despite a bomb that destroyed much of the building in 1943, the painting survives. The wall was protected with sandbags, and the long restoration completed in 1999 revealed colours and faces that had been hidden for centuries.

    Read the complete Church and Dominican Convent of Santa Mari write-up.

  3. 3. Venetian Works of Defence between the 16th

    130 km in.

    Bergamo is two cities: the busy modern town on the plain, and the Città Alta on the hill above, enclosed by massive walls built by the Republic of Venice between 1561 and 1588. Bergamo was then the westernmost outpost of Venice's mainland territory, facing the Spanish-ruled Duchy of Milan, and the walls, more than five kilometres long, were designed to withstand artillery, with bastions, platforms and gates. They were never seriously tested in battle, which is why they are so well preserved. In 2017 UNESCO listed them, together with other Venetian fortifications in Italy, Croatia and Montenegro, as the Venetian Works of Defence.
