Why this ride

curated scenic road. high alpine, glaciers, hairpins. From Andermatt it runs 76 km to Andermatt; allow about 1 h 23 of riding. The stops in between: Engelberg, Meiringen and Three Passes Loop: Susten Grimsel Furka. The highest point is about 2,450 m. Susten Pass, Grimsel Pass and Furka Pass are subject to seasonal closure - check it is open before you set off. The line favours views over speed.

Scenic road chapters

Furka Pass

Expect company on a summer weekend. Early is better than fast. For 13 km this is Furka Pass, a road with a name of its own and a reason for it.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Andermatt

    Andermatt sits at 1,444 metres in the Urseren valley, a high, wide, treeless hollow in the heart of the Swiss Alps, and it has been a crossroads for centuries. Three great passes meet here: the Gotthard, which runs south to Italy and Ticino at 2,106 metres, the Furka, which climbs west over 2,429 metres toward the Valais, and the Oberalp, which leads east at 2,044 metres into Graubünden. For a motorcyclist it is a kind of Mecca, since the three roads are among the most famous in the Alps, and the town fills with bikes on any fine summer weekend.

  2. 2. Near Andermatt

    The church of Wassen stands on a terrace above the Reuss, and passengers on the old Gotthard railway see it three times as the line spirals through tunnels to gain height, a famous trick of nineteenth-century engineering that makes travellers think they are going in circles. This stop lies in that village, at the foot of the Susten pass, where the road leaves the Reuss valley and climbs west into the Meiental towards the Bernese Oberland.

  3. 3. Near Engelberg

    At 2,224 metres, the summit of the Susten Pass sits on the border between the cantons of Bern and Uri, under the glaciated peaks of the Sustenhorn and the Gwächtenhorn. Engelberg, the name on the label, lies on the other side of the Titlis massif, ten kilometres away as the crow flies but a long way round by road, so think of this as the pass itself, the highest point of one of the most beautiful mountain roads in the Alps.

  4. 4. Near Meiringen

    Above Meiringen the Hasli valley opens out, and this spot lies on the slopes between the town and the Brünig, the low pass that carries the road over to Lungern and Obwalden. Below, the Aare runs flat and straight across the valley floor toward Lake Brienz, and above rise the steep walls of the Bernese Oberland, with the Wetterhorn and Engelhörner peering over the ridge to the south. It is a place where several of the great Swiss passes come together.

  5. 5. Three Passes Loop: Susten Grimsel Furka point 5

    The Grimsel Pass is one of the great engineering landscapes of the Alps, a high granite country where glaciers, dams and lakes have been fitted together into a kind of outdoor power station. The summit sits at 2,164 metres, and the road climbs to it from Meiringen through the narrow Haslital valley, passing the Handeck waterfall, where the Aare drops some distance in a single leap, and the cable-car and funicular stations that lead to remote reservoirs.

  6. 6. Three Passes Loop: Susten Grimsel Furka point 6

    Gletsch is hardly a village at all, but a crossroads in a bleak, windswept bowl where the Grimsel and Furka roads meet, and where, for a long time, the Rhône Glacier filled the valley above it. The glacier gives birth to the Rhône, one of the great rivers of Europe, which begins here as a milky meltwater stream before running through the Valais, Lake Geneva and France to the Mediterranean. The glacier was once so large that it reached almost to the hotel, and photographs from a century ago show a wall of ice where there is now a grey, stony slope.
