Why this ride

Norwegian Scenic Route. fjord, snow road, plateau. From Aurlandsvangen it runs 27 km to Lærdalsøyri; allow about 29 min of riding. The highest point is about 1,300 m. Aurlandsfjellet high point is open in summer only - check it is open before you set off. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

  3. 3. Near Aurlandsvangen

    At 1306 metres you are near the roof of Aurlandsfjellet, the high plateau that the Snow Road crosses between Aurland on the fjord and Lærdal to the north. It is a landscape of bare rock, small lakes, patches of old snow and wide open sky, scraped flat by the ice and left almost empty. Only a few hours before, if you came up from Aurland, you were among fruit trees and fjord water; up here the plants are lichen, moss and a few tough grasses, and the snowbanks along the road can still be taller than a person in early summer.

  4. 4. Lærdalsøyri

    Lærdalsøyri is one of the best-preserved wooden towns in western Norway, a village of white and painted timber houses at the mouth of the Lærdal river, where it meets the inner Sognefjord. Its old quarter, known as Gamle Lærdalsøyri, has more than a hundred and fifty old buildings, from the eighteenth and nineteenth centuries, with narrow lanes between them, and it has been protected for its value. On a quiet evening it is one of the loveliest small places in the fjord country.
