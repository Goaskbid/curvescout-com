Why this ride

A lake loop based around South Lake Tahoe, California, via Emerald Bay. Key character: alpine lake; forest; mountain views. Surface: paved.

Scenic road chapters

Lake Tahoe Loop

Slow enough to look. The surface follows the shoreline, so the corners arrive where the water decided, not where an engineer did. Water arrives beside the road and stays for 12 km. The line follows the shore because there is nowhere else for it to go.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. South Lake Tahoe

    Lake Tahoe sits at 6,225 feet, which makes it one of the highest large lakes in the country, and the water is so clear that on a calm day you can see objects more than sixty feet down. It is also very deep, about 1,645 feet at its deepest, making it the second deepest lake in the United States after Crater Lake. The basin formed as the Sierra Nevada rose along fault lines and glaciers later carved the valleys, and the Washoe people spent their summers on its shores for thousands of years, long before Europeans arrived. Their name for the lake, Da ow a ga, means edge of the lake.

  4. 4. Incline Village

    Incline Village sits on the north-east shore of Lake Tahoe, on the Nevada side, a community of about 8,800 people with some of the most expensive lake-view houses in the country, though from the road the mood is quiet pine woods and low buildings. It is the sort of place where a casual cafe has a view of blue water, and where the boulders on the nearby beaches are so large and smooth that they seem to have been placed by a sculptor.
