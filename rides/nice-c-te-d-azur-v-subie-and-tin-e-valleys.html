Why this ride

A proper escape from Nice with roads worth waking up for. Out of Antibes and back again: 575 km, roughly 10 h 27 in the saddle. The stops in between: Scenic stretch near Montferrat, near Seyne-les-Alpes and Finale Ligure. Ramatuelle is the high point. It tops out at about 2,000 m. It crosses from France into Italy - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Antibes

    Old ramparts rise straight from the sea at Antibes, and behind them a tight knot of lanes, markets and ochre houses climbs toward a castle on the shore. The town sits between Nice and Cannes on the bay of the same name, facing the snowy line of the Alps on clear winter days, with the long wooded peninsula of Cap d'Antibes to the south and the jazz town of Juan-les-Pins on its western flank.

  2. 2. Ramatuelle

    Above the beaches of Saint-Tropez, Ramatuelle coils around its hilltop like a snail shell, its houses forming a wall around the old village, with narrow lanes and arches leading up to the church. Below it, the long sweep of Pampelonne beach curves for kilometres along the bay, and the vineyards of the Côtes de Provence climb the slopes between.

  5. 5. Near Finale Ligure, on the way back

    Finale Ligure is a seaside town with two personalities. On the coast, Finale Marina is a classic Ligurian resort, with a long beach, a palm-lined promenade and colourful houses. A few hundred metres inland, Finalborgo is a medieval town enclosed by walls, with narrow streets, old churches and the ruins of Castel Gavone on the hill above. The two are linked by a short road, and it is worth visiting both.

  6. 6. Borgio

    In the grottoes of Borgio Verezzi, the limestone has been stained by iron, copper and manganese, and the stalactites glow in shades of red, yellow and green, some of the most colourful caves in Italy. They were found in the 1930s and are now open on guided tours. It is a cool, otherworldly escape on a hot day.

Scenic stretch near Montferrat to Photo stop near Seyne-les-Alpes

Scenic stretch near Montferrat → Photo stop near Seyne-les-Alpes is the technical section. Hairpins, gradients and mixed traffic can stack up quickly; use low drama inputs, stay wide of centre lines and expect buses or riders appearing exactly where optimism says they will not.

Photo stop near Seyne-les-Alpes to Near Finale Ligure, on the way back

This Photo stop near Seyne-les-Alpes → Near Finale Ligure, on the way back stretch is for rhythm and scenery. Keep your head up, let the view breathe, and resist turning every open bend into a target. Stop once properly rather than half-stopping everywhere.
