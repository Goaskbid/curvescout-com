Why this ride

From Al Muharraq it runs 934 km to Al Muharraq; allow about 17 h of riding. It crosses from Bahrain into Qatar - carry ID and check the toll rules. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The stops

  1. 1. Badr Ghulum House

    Start here.

    Badr Ghulum house is the last of the Muharraq pearl houses on this list, and it is a good place to say something about the island as a whole. Muharraq is small, flat and low, joined by causeways to Manama and, farther south, to the main island of Bahrain, and the whole country of Bahrain is a little archipelago in the Gulf,. From here a longer causeway runs west to Saudi Arabia, and it carries heavy traffic at weekends, which gives the island a feeling of being at a crossroads.

    Read the complete Badr Ghulum House write-up.

  2. 2. Al Zubarah Archaeological Site

    About 467 km in.

    Out on the north-west coast of Qatar, where the desert slides into a shallow turquoise sea, stand the low ruins of Al Zubarah, once one of the busiest pearling and trading towns in the Gulf. In the late eighteenth and nineteenth centuries the town thrived on pearls dived from the oyster beds offshore, traded on to India, Persia and beyond. Then it declined, was abandoned in the early twentieth century, and the sand covered it. Archaeologists have since uncovered streets, houses, a souk, mosques and a long town wall running in an arc around the old harbour. UNESCO inscribed the site in 2013.
