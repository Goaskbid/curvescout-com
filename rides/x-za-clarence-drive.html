Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Gordons Bay

    Gordon's Bay is a small harbor town at the northeastern corner of False Bay, with the Hottentots Holland mountains rising abruptly behind it and the Atlantic-facing bulk of the Cape Peninsula far away across the water. It is the start of Clarence Drive, the coastal road that clings to the cliffs along the eastern shore of False Bay, and for many riders it is the gate to one of the best short rides in the Western Cape. The town itself is a laid-back place with a marina, a few restaurants and a beach that fills up in summer.

  2. 2. Bettys Bay

    Betty's Bay lies about midway along Clarence Drive, a scattering of low houses between the mountains and the sea, and it is the part of the coast where nature takes over. It lies within the Kogelberg Biosphere Reserve, a UNESCO-recognized area that holds one of the highest concentrations of plant species anywhere on earth, with fynbos shrubs such as proteas, ericas and restios covering the slopes in a bewildering variety. In spring, the colors are wonderful, and in winter the mountains are sharp with cloud and rain.

  3. 3. Hermanus

    In Hermanus, one of the town's municipal employees blows a horn made from kelp to announce the whales. The whale crier is a local tradition, and it makes sense, because between roughly June and November southern right whales come into Walker Bay to calve and play, and from the cliff path that runs along the shoreline you can watch them from a few metres above the surf. Mothers and calves roll in the shallows, males breach and slap the water with their tails, and you hear their breath carried across the bay. It is among the best land-based whale watching in the world.
