Why this ride

Out of Loveland, one way: 223 km, roughly 4 h 07 in the saddle. The climb of the day is Guanella Pass and Georgetown. It tops out at about 3,400 m. Guanella Pass is subject to seasonal closure - check it is open before you set off.

  2. 2. Guanella Pass and Georgetown photo pull-off

    At 3,570 metres the pass is among the highest paved routes in the state, linking Georgetown and Grant through forest, willow flats and finally open tundra. The summit of Bierstadt, at about 4,285 metres, is named for the painter Albert Bierstadt, who visited in 1863 and made the mountains of the West famous with his enormous luminous canvases. A rider stopping at a pull-off near the top can see a good deal of what he saw.
