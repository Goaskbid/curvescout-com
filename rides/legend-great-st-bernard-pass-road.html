Why this ride

curated scenic road. historic pilgrimage route, high alpine. 47 km from Martigny-Ville to Aosta, about 51 min of riding. On the way it passes Orsières and Saint-Rhémy. The highest point is about 2,450 m. Great St Bernard Pass is subject to seasonal closure - check it is open before you set off. It crosses from Switzerland into Italy - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

  2. 2. Orsières

    Orsières is where the Valais valleys split apart. Here the road from Martigny forks: one branch climbs into the Val Ferret toward Champex and the Mont Blanc massif, the other follows the Val d'Entremont up toward the Great St Bernard. The town has a little under three thousand inhabitants, the Dranse river rushing through it, and a small narrow-gauge railway that comes up from Martigny and ends at the station. It is the last real place of any size before the pass, and so everyone who rides the road stops at some point.

  4. 4. Aosta

    Aosta is a Roman town with the Alps standing around it like a stage set. The Romans founded it in 25 BC as Augusta Praetoria, after subduing the local Salassi tribe, and they laid it out on a neat grid that still shapes the old centre. The Arch of Augustus, the Porta Praetoria gate, and the remains of a theatre with a surviving wall nearly twenty metres high stand among cafes and shops, and snow-capped peaks rise at the end of every street. It has been called the Rome of the Alps.
