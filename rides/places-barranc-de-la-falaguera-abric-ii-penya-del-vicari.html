Why this ride

A 304 km round trip from Alginet - roughly 5 h 14 in the saddle. The stops in between: Abrigo de las Cañas II, Cova de la Petxina, Barranc de la Penya Blanca and Penya del Vicari. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Barranc de la falaguera- Abric II

    South of Valencia the land flattens into the Ribera, a plain of orange and persimmon orchards cut by irrigation ditches, and then, quite suddenly, it crumples into low hills. Alginet and Alfarp sit on this edge, with the Albufera lagoon and its rice fields a little to the east. The Barranc de la Falaguera is a dry ravine in the dusty foothills, and Abric II is a small rock shelter recorded with a group of others nearby. These belong to the Mediterranean-arc rock art that UNESCO listed in 1998, and the Valencian interior holds a remarkable number of them.

  2. 2. Abrigo de las Cañas II

    The Macizo del Caroig is one of the emptiest corners of the Valencian region, a high, rugged limestone massif cut by deep gorges, covered with pine, scrub and rosemary, and dotted with a few small villages. Bicorp, Millares and Quesa lie on its edges, and in the ravines around them are dozens of rock shelters with prehistoric paintings, part of the UNESCO-listed rock art of the Mediterranean Basin. The Abrigo de las Cañas is one of them, and the area as a whole is among the richest in Spain for this kind of art.

  3. 3. Cova de la Petxina

    Bellús is a small village in the valley of the Albaida river in southern Valencia, just upstream from where the river flows into a reservoir and then joins the Júcar. The old village was partly flooded by the reservoir, and the new one stands on higher ground. The Cova de la Petxina, the cave of the shell, is in the hills near the village, close to Guadasequies and Genovés.

  4. 4. Barranc de la Penya Blanca

    Planes is a small village in the hills of the Comtat in northern Alicante province, crowned by a castle and looking out over a valley of olive and cherry orchards. The Barranc de la Penya Blanca, the ravine of the white rock, lies close by, among the folded limestone ridges between Planes, Benimarfull and the reservoir at Beniarrés.

  5. 5. Penya del Vicari

    On a map of the Costa Blanca, the Serra de Bernia looks like a wall dropped between two very different worlds. On one side, high-rise Benidorm and the busy coast; on the other, the quiet valleys of Jalón and Guadalest. The Penya del Vicari is one of the rocky outcrops in these hills above Altea and Calp, a place where the limestone is cut by caves and shelters, some of which carry traces of prehistoric painting. It is a small, quiet site in an area otherwise famous for sun loungers.
