Why this ride

curated scenic road. terraced hills, volcanoes, forest. From Kabale it runs 62 km to Kanungu; allow about 1 h 08 of riding. The highest point is about 2,200 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Uganda Kigezi Highlands Road

Expect company on a summer weekend. Early is better than fast. For 8 km this is Uganda Kigezi Highlands Road, a road with a name of its own and a reason for it.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Kabale

    Kabale is a hill town at about nineteen hundred metres in the far south-west of Uganda, and the first thing you notice is how cool it is. After the heat of Kampala and the lowlands, evenings here call for a jumper. The surrounding countryside, known as Kigezi, is a tight patchwork of terraces, so steep and so carefully cultivated that early colonial visitors compared it to Switzerland, and the nickname has stuck. Potatoes, sorghum, beans and cabbages are grown on slopes that look too steep to stand on.

  3. 3. Kisoro

    Kisoro sits in the far south-west corner of Uganda, in a pocket between Rwanda and the Democratic Republic of Congo, and the first thing you notice is the row of volcanoes. Muhabura, Gahinga and Sabinyo stand on the skyline like a set of upturned bowls, their slopes green and their summits often hidden in cloud. They are part of the Virunga range, a chain of volcanoes that straddles three countries, and the town sits at the foot of them, on a high plateau where the air is cool and the land is cultivated to the last metre.
