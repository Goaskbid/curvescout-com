Why this ride

curated scenic road. cliff villages, castles, river valley. 50 km from Sarlat-la-Canéda to Gramat, about 55 min of riding. It goes by way of Cénac and Domme. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Sarlat-la-Canéda

    Sarlat is built of honey-coloured limestone, and when the evening sun hits it, the whole medieval town glows as if lit from inside. It lies in the Périgord Noir, a region of walnut groves, oak woods and steep green valleys in the Dordogne, and its centre is one of the best-preserved in France, a maze of lanes, courtyards and stone staircases that escaped the demolition fever of the nineteenth century. Pointed turrets, carved doorways and tall slate roofs crowd every street, and filmmakers have used it so often that locals barely look up when a camera crew appears. The writer Étienne de La Boétie, the close friend of Montaigne, was born here in 1530.

  4. 4. Domme

    Domme is a fortified town on a cliff, laid out as a bastide in 1281 by Philip the Bold, the king of France, who needed a stronghold against the English in the Dordogne. The plan is as tidy as a chessboard: straight streets, a central square and three great gates in the walls. The bastide stands on a limestone spur some hundred and fifty metres above the river, and the walk to the edge, along the Belvédère de la Barre, gives one of the greatest panoramas of the valley, with the Dordogne winding below through fields and woods and the castles of Castelnaud and Beynac on the horizon.
