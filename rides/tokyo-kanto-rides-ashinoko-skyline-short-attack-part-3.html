Why this ride

145 km one way from Fujiyoshida - plan on about 2 h 59 of riding. The climb of the day is near Chichibu. It tops out at about 850 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Food stop near Fujiyoshida

    Fujiyoshida sits on the northern foot of Mount Fuji, and the mountain fills the sky above the town so completely that it can feel almost unreal. For centuries, this was the starting point for pilgrims climbing Fuji, and the great shrine of Kitaguchi Hongū Fuji Sengen Jinja, set in a forest of ancient cedars, marks the traditional beginning of the Yoshida trail. Walking through its huge wooden torii into the dark, quiet forest is a moving experience.

  2. 2. High point near Chichibu

    Every December, on the night of the third, giant wooden floats lit with lanterns are hauled through the streets of Chichibu to the beat of drums and the crackle of fireworks, in a festival that is among the most famous in Japan. The Chichibu Night Festival has been held for centuries, and it draws huge crowds. The rest of the year, Chichibu is a quiet mountain town in a basin west of Tokyo, ringed by forested peaks.

  3. 3. Back to Ashinoko Skyline

    The Kawagoe streets, with their dark warehouses of tiled roofs, a bell tower called Toki no Kane and rows of sweet shops selling sweet potato treats, are the best place to feel the atmosphere of old Tokyo, and it is worth the detour on any ride that ends in this area.
