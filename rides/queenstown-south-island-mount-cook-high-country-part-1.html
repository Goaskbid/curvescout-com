Why this ride

The stops in between: Queenstown and Scenic stretch near Arrowtown. The climb of the day is near Queenstown. It tops out at about 1,293 m.

The stops

  1. 1. Fuel stop near Queenstown

    Start here.

    South of Queenstown, the road to Kingston follows the eastern shore of Lake Wakatipu, squeezed between deep, cold water and the jagged wall of the Remarkables. In winter they are white to their bases; in summer the bare rock glows gold and grey in the evening light.

    Read the complete Near Queenstown write-up.

    4.8 km in. The ride passes about 7 km from it. You top out here; the road stops climbing.
