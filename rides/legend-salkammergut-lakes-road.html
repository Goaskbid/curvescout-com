Why this ride

curated scenic road. lakes, limestone Alps, historic villages. From Salzburg it runs 73 km to Gosau; allow about 1 h 20 of riding. On the way it passes Fuschl am See, Sankt Gilgen and Bad Ischl. The highest point is about 1,400 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Scenic road chapters

Salkammergut Lakes Road

Slow enough to look. The surface follows the shoreline, so the corners arrive where the water decided, not where an engineer did. Water arrives beside the road and stays for 12 km. The line follows the shore because there is nowhere else for it to go.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Salzburg

    Salzburg owes its name and its wealth to salt, the white gold that was mined in the nearby hills and floated down the Salzach river for centuries. The prince-archbishops who ruled the city used that income to turn it into a baroque stage set, with domes, fountains and palaces crowded below the mighty fortress of Hohensalzburg, which looks down from its rock over the old town. The city is also the birthplace of Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, born in 1756 in a narrow house on the Getreidegasse, and every summer the Salzburg Festival fills the squares and churches with music.

  4. 4. Bad Ischl

    Bad Ischl is the town where an Emperor went on holiday and accidentally changed European history. Franz Joseph spent summers here for decades, in a villa given to him and Sisi as a wedding present, and it was from here, in July 1914, that he signed the declaration of war on Serbia that began the First World War. Today the Kaiservilla is a museum with a park, filled with hunting trophies, and a visit gives you a glimpse of how a monarch spent a holiday: shooting chamois and grumbling over paperwork.
