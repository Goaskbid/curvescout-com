Why this ride

279 km from Susques to San Pedro de Atacama, about 3 h 34 of riding. The highest point is about 4,780 m. It crosses from Argentina into Chile - carry ID and check the toll rules. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

The roads

Susques → Paso de Jama

From Susques to Paso de Jama is 117 km on Ruta Nacional 52. It gains around 680 m and ends high, near 4,129 m. Above 2,000 m the weather turns fast; have a layer and a plan B.

The stops

  1. 1. Susques

    Start here.

    Susques is a small high-altitude town on the road to Chile, perched on the Argentine puna at about 3,600 metres, where the air is thin, the sky is deep blue and the horizon is made of bare rust-coloured hills. The Ruta 52 climbs here from Jujuy through the Quebrada de Purmamarca, over the Cuesta de Lipán, one of the highest and most dramatic mountain roads in Argentina, then crosses the great white Salinas Grandes, the salt flats, before arriving in Susques. The town marks the beginning of the real altiplano.

    Read the complete Susques write-up.

  2. 2. Paso de Jama

    117 km in. Nothing on this route is higher.

    Nothing prepares you for the quiet at Paso de Jama. At about 4,200 metres, the border between Argentina and Chile lies on the Altiplano, a huge, high plateau of tawny grass, salt flats and shallow lakes, ringed by volcanoes. The sky is a hard, deep blue, the air is thin and dry, and the only sounds are the wind and the occasional passing truck. Flamingos feed in the pale lagoons, and vicuñas, the delicate wild cousins of the llama, graze beside the road.

    Read the complete Paso de Jama write-up.

  3. 3. San Pedro de Atacama

    The end: 279 km.

    San Pedro de Atacama is a small adobe town in the driest desert on Earth, and the contradiction is the attraction. It lies at 2,400 metres on the edge of the Salar de Atacama, a vast salt flat, in an oasis fed by streams from the Andes. The streets are dusty and low, the walls are the colour of dry mud, and behind the town the Licancabur volcano, nearly 5,920 metres, stands as a perfect cone on the border with Bolivia. Everything here seems to lean towards the sky.

    Read the complete Near San Pedro de Atacama write-up.
