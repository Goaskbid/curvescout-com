Why this ride

251 km from Hejnice to Hejnice, about 4 h 20 of riding. On the way it passes Herrnhut, Muskauer Park / Park Mużakowski and back through Herrnhut. It crosses Czechia, Germany and Poland - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Jizera Mountains

    The Jizera Mountains sit in the far north of Bohemia, right where the Czech Republic meets Poland and Germany, and they have a stern, quiet character that sets them apart from the better-known Krkonoše to the east. Hejnice, at their foot, is a small town in the valley of the Smědá river, famous for a large Baroque pilgrimage church with a basilica and a Franciscan monastery. Above it, the ridges rise to forested plateaus and open peat bogs, with roads that thread between spruce and beech.

  2. 2. Herrnhut

    Herrnhut is a small town in Upper Lusatia, in the far east of Saxony, with a name that means roughly "under the Lord's watch". It began in 1722, when Moravian Protestant refugees were given land on the estate of Count Zinzendorf and built a settlement here. From that settlement grew the Moravian Church, which sent missionaries to the Caribbean, Greenland and Africa within a decade and still has communities across the world. The town today feels calm and slightly austere in a way that suits the story, with straight streets, plain Baroque houses and a church hall whose big white room is simply there to hold people.

  3. 3. Muskauer Park / Park Mużakowski

    Muskau Park straddles a river and a border. The Neisse flows through it, and since 1945 it has been the frontier between Germany and Poland, which means that half the park lies in Bad Muskau and the other half in Leknica. Visitors walk across a reconstructed bridge from one country to the other, with no checks, and pass from the German part of the park to the Polish part in a few minutes. UNESCO listed the park as a joint site in 2004.
