Why this ride

A 259 km round trip from Dannewerk - roughly 4 h 43 in the saddle. On the way it passes National Park Wadden Sea Niedersachsen and Main Wall Area 1. It keeps close to the shore for long stretches.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Main Wall Area 1

    The Danevirke is the longest and most important archaeological monument in northern Europe, a network of earthworks and walls that stretches across the neck of the Jutland peninsula, and the Main Wall is its backbone. At this spot, close to the village of Dannewerk near Schleswig, you are standing on a grassy ridge that looks like nothing much until someone tells you it was built to stop armies. It runs for kilometres through fields and woods, and over a stretch of centuries it was raised, repaired and extended by Danish kings, from the early Viking age into the twelfth century.

  2. 2. National Park Wadden Sea Niedersachsen

    Here the Wadden Sea comes right up against the dyke. This is the coast of the Wurster Nordseeküste, between the Weser and Elbe estuaries, a country of low sea walls, drained farmland and wide skies, and the mudflats outside the dyke are part of the Lower Saxony Wadden Sea National Park. Together with the Dutch and Danish parts of the Wadden Sea, the area is a UNESCO World Heritage site, valued above all for the sheer scale of the tidal flats and the millions of birds that pass through on migration. When the tide goes out, it goes a very long way.
