Why this ride

Heath roads, open sky and calm bend rhythm south of the city. 1152 km round from Hamburg - plan on about 20 h 56 of riding. The stops in between: Lüneburg, Groß Twülpstedt, Sangerhausen and Neustadt an der Orla. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Hamburg

    Hamburg opens up to the water. The Elbe runs through the city, the port covers a large chunk of it, and the harbour front is a long working landscape of cranes, container ships, tugs and ferries, with the sound of horns carrying across the water at night. The old warehouse district, the Speicherstadt, is a block of red-brick buildings on oak piles, built in the 1880s and now partly part of a UNESCO listing, with canals running between them and gabled facades reflecting in the water. Next to it, the HafenCity is a modern district whose centrepiece is the Elbphilharmonie, a concert hall in the shape of a glass wave on top of an old warehouse, opened in 2017 after many delays and a scandalous overrun in costs.

  2. 2. Lüneburg

    Salt made Lüneburg, and the town looks as though it knows it. For more than a thousand years the brine springs under the old town were boiled down into white gold, which was carted to Lübeck and shipped across the Baltic to preserve herring, and the profits built a city of tall brick gables, grand merchant houses and a town hall that grew by accretion across six centuries.
