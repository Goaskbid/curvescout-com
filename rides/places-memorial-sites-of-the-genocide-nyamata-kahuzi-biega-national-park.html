Why this ride

From Nzega it runs 418 km to Nzega; allow about 7 h 12 of riding. It goes by way of Gisakura Natural Forest, Kahuzi-Biega National Park and back through Gisakura Natural Forest. The highest point is about 2,150 m. It crosses from Rwanda into Congo - Kinshasa - carry ID and check vignette or toll rules. The line favours views over speed.

  1. 1. Memorial sites of the Genocide: Nyamata

    This is a place to arrive quietly. Both belong to the group of four genocide memorials, Nyamata, Murambi, Gisozi in Kigali and Bisesero, that UNESCO inscribed on the World Heritage list in 2023, recognising them as places of remembrance of the genocide against the Tutsi, in which some eight hundred thousand to a million people were murdered in about a hundred days.

  2. 2. Gisakura Natural Forest

    In the far southwest of Rwanda, a small patch of forest surrounded by tea plantations is home to one of the most charming wildlife encounters in Africa. Gisakura forest lies on the western edge of Nyungwe National Park, near the tea estate that shares its name, and a large troop of Ruwenzori colobus monkeys lives there, black and white with long tails and flowing capes of fur. Watching them leap through the canopy, sometimes in groups of dozens, is a joy. The forest is a remnant of the great montane rainforest that once covered these mountains, isolated now by the tea fields.

  3. 3. Kahuzi-Biega National Park

    Kahuzi-Biega is the place in the world to meet an eastern lowland gorilla, the largest of all the great apes. The park lies in the east of the Democratic Republic of the Congo, in South Kivu province, west of Bukavu and Lake Kivu, and it covers a mix of lowland rainforest and the high forested slopes of two extinct volcanoes, Kahuzi at 3,308 metres and Biega at 2,790. Bamboo forest grows on the upper slopes, and the whole mass rises abruptly from the Albertine Rift, one of the richest areas for wildlife in Africa.
