Why this ride

From Teulada it runs 71 km to Teulada; allow about 1 h 13 of riding. On the way it passes Abric de Seguili, Abric de les Torrudanes and back through Abric de Seguili.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Cova de la Catxupa- Abric III

    Behind Teulada the land rises sharply, and the third shelter in the Catxupa series sits where the first foothills begin. It is not an obvious place for an ancient painted site. The coast is only a few kilometres away, and the view toward the sea is partly built over. But the Levantine rock art tradition ran right down to this edge of the Mediterranean, and the overhangs of the hills here were used by people long before anyone built a villa.

  2. 2. Abric de Seguili

    The Vall de Pop, or Jalón valley as many foreign residents call it, is a sheltered valley in the north of Alicante province, a few kilometres inland from the coast at Dénia and Xàbia. It is a landscape of vineyards, almond and olive groves and orange trees, with white villages and the mountains rising on all sides. Alcalalí is one of these villages, with a medieval tower in its centre, and Parcent and Orba are nearby. The Abric de Seguilí lies in the hills just outside Alcalalí.

  3. 3. Abric de les Torrudanes

    In the Vall d'Ebo, a small valley in the mountains of northern Alicante, the Abric de les Torrudanes is a rock shelter with prehistoric paintings, one of many in the area that form part of the Rock Art of the Mediterranean Basin World Heritage listing. The paintings include human figures and animals in the Levantine style, and the shelter's location, on a cliff above the valley, offers views over the mountains. Nearby, the Cova del Rull is a show cave with stalactites and stalagmites.
