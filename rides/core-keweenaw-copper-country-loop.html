Why this ride

Lake Superior shoreline, mining heritage and the peninsula's best forest roads. The road: US-41 and M-26. Distance and riding time are estimates from the checkpoints until the road is measured on this page.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Houghton

    Houghton sits on the Keweenaw Peninsula, a thumb of land that pokes into Lake Superior so far north that it feels like another country. Here the Portage Canal slices the peninsula in two, and the town and its twin, Hancock, face each other across the water, joined by a double-decked lift bridge whose road deck rises to let boats pass beneath. The twin hillside towns, with their brick storefronts and steep streets, look like an overgrown mining camp, and that is what they were.
