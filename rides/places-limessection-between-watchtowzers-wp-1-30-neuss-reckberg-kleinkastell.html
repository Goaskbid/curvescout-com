Why this ride

From Neuwied it runs 261 km to Neuwied; allow about 4 h 30 of riding. It goes by way of Kottenforst Nord | Am Weißen Stein 2, Neuss-Reckberg | Kleinkastell and back through Kottenforst Nord | Am Weißen Stein 2. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Limessection between watchtowzers WP 1/30

    The Roman frontier in Germany starts at the Rhine and heads off into the woods, and the Westerwald is where it first leaves the river. Near Rheinbrohl the Upper German Limes begins, and from there it climbs the hills to the east, a line of earth banks, ditches and watchtowers that once ran for about five hundred and fifty kilometres to the Danube. Between the towers numbered 1 and 30 it crosses the high ground behind Neuwied, through the area of Melsbach and Rengsdorf, and what remains today is mostly a low bank and a shallow ditch in the forest.

  2. 2. Kottenforst Nord | Am Weißen Stein 2

    Bornheim is asparagus country, and if you ride past the Kottenforst in late spring you will see why. The sandy soil on the Rhine terraces just south of Cologne suits the white spears perfectly, and from roughly April to the end of June the farm stands along the roads sell them by the kilo, along with strawberries that follow soon afterwards. This is the Vorgebirge, a low ridge between the Rhine plain and the Eifel foothills, long used for market gardening and orchards, and the Weißer Stein stop sits in the woodland at its edge.
