Why this ride

A complete lake circuit with mountain views and three useful stop towns. The road: NH-11 and NH-103. Distance and riding time are estimates from the checkpoints until the road is measured on this page.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Newbury

    Lake Sunapee is the reason for Newbury, and it is reason enough. The lake lies in a basin of low granite hills in western New Hampshire, about nine miles long, ringed by pines and old summer cottages, with Mount Sunapee rising at its southern end. The name comes from an Abenaki word often translated as goose lake, and the water is clear and cold, a pleasure to ride beside on a still morning when mist lifts off the surface.
