Scenic stretch in Cottage Lake to High point near Issaquah

This Scenic stretch in Cottage Lake → High point near Issaquah stretch is for rhythm and scenery. Keep your head up, let the view breathe, and resist turning every open bend into a target. Stop once properly rather than half-stopping everywhere.

Photo stop near Lakeland North to Boulevard Park

Photo stop near Lakeland North → Boulevard Park is the technical section. Hairpins, gradients and mixed traffic can stack up quickly; use low drama inputs, stay wide of centre lines and expect buses or riders appearing exactly where optimism says they will not.

Food stop near Belfair to Near Lofall, on the way back

Food stop near Belfair → Near Lofall, on the way back can flow quickly, but only if you keep village limits and junction sightlines sacred. Fast connectors are where rides get untidy when fatigue creeps in.
