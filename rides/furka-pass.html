Why this ride

From Andermatt it runs 47 km to Andermatt; allow about 47 min of riding. The stops in between: Furka Pass and Hotel Belvedere. The highest point is about 2,450 m. Furka Pass is subject to seasonal closure - check it is open before you set off. It suits an unhurried touring pace.

Scenic road chapters

Furka Pass

The last of the trees go and the road runs out onto open ground. The summit sits at 2429 m, and what surrounds it is rock, weather and distance — cold comes early up here and stays late. Ride it with something in reserve: the summit is not the end of the work, only the middle of it.

Rider note

Check the pass is open before you commit to it.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Realp

    Realp is the last village on the Urseren valley road before the Furka, a hamlet at about fifteen hundred metres that most people ride through without stopping. That would be a mistake, because the railway station here is the starting point of the Furka cogwheel steam railway, a heritage line run by volunteers, which was rebuilt from abandoned track after the original was closed in 1981. Steam locomotives climb to Oberwald by way of the old mountain route in summer, and even if you do not ride the train, the sight and smell of the engines in the station yard make a decent coffee stop.

  2. 2. Furka Pass

    Furka is one of the Alps' great stage roads: 2,429 metres of switchbacks linking Uri and Valais, with the Rhône Glacier at the western foot. From Andermatt the road climbs through the high Urseren valley, past Realp, up a series of hairpins to a pass where the air is thin and cold, and the views unfold across rock, snow and sky. The mountains here are bare and grand, and the sense of altitude is real.

  3. 3. Hotel Belvedere

    There is a hotel on the Furka Pass that has seen more cameras than most film stars. The Hotel Belvédère clings to the hairpins on the Valais side, a few kilometres below the summit, and for generations it sat at the foot of the Rhône Glacier, whose tongue of ice reached almost to the terrace. The glacier has retreated dramatically since then, leaving bare rock and a grey lake, and in summer it is partly covered with white blankets to slow the melt. The scene is a visible record of a changing climate.

  4. 4. Gletsch

    Gletsch is where a Bond car drove around a hairpin. The 1964 film Goldfinger used the road near here for its famous chase, with Sean Connery's Aston Martin DB5 winding along the Furka pass, and the old Hotel Belvédère, a big, handsome building from the age of grand Alpine tourism, stands at the foot of the climb. It has the look of a place that has been waiting for travellers for more than a century.

Recorded leg-by-leg notes

These source notes may contain earlier estimates. They are not turn-by-turn directions or current measured distances.

Realp to Furka Pass

Realp → Furka Pass climbs about 2429 m in 8 km. This is the part worth slowing down for - look through the bends and use the pull-offs.

Furka Pass to Hotel Belvedere

Furka Pass → Hotel Belvedere drops about 2429 m over 3 km. Brakes and eyes forward; the views are behind the corners, not in them.

Hotel Belvedere to Gletsch

Hotel Belvedere → Gletsch is 3 km of rhythm rather than effort. Let it flow and let the view breathe.
