Why this ride

284 km from Queenstown to Queenstown, about 4 h 54 of riding. On the way it passes Te Wahipounamu - South West New Zealand and back through Te Wahipounamu - South West New Zealand. The highest point is about 950 m. A steady touring day rather than a sprint.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Queenstown

    The town sits on the shore of Lake Wakatipu, a long, dark, glacier-carved lake shaped like a lightning bolt, with the Remarkables mountain range rising straight from the far side in a wall of grey rock and snow. A Māori legend says the lake is the burnt outline of a giant, Matau, whose heart still beats under the water, and the lake level does rise and fall in a slow rhythm, a seiche caused by wind and pressure, which is the scientific explanation for the legend. The old coal-fired steamship TSS Earnslaw, launched in 1912, still crosses the lake to a high-country farm.

  2. 2. Te Wahipounamu - South West New Zealand

    South-west New Zealand is the wettest, wildest quarter of the country, and Te Wahipounamu, which in Māori means the place of greenstone, is its official name. The World Heritage area stretches over about two and a half million hectares and takes in four national parks: Fiordland, Mount Aspiring, Aoraki Mount Cook and Westland Tai Poutini.
