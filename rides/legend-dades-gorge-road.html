Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Dades Gorge Road point 1

    Boumalne Dades sits at the mouth of the Dades Gorge, a dusty crossroads town on the road between Ouarzazate and Tinghir, and it is the gateway to one of the most scenic valleys in southern Morocco. The town itself is plain, with a long main street, a market, cafes and shops, but its position, with the High Atlas on one side and the Jbel Saghro on the other, makes it a natural base for riders.

  2. 2. Dades Gorge Road point 2

    Above Boumalne, the Dades Gorge starts to show what it is made of. The road follows the river upstream, squeezed between towering cliffs of red and ochre rock that have been worn into odd shapes, and the valley floor is a ribbon of green, palms, fields and small villages, in contrast to the dry mountains around. Around here lie the rock formations that visitors call monkey fingers, a row of eroded pillars that stand above the road like giant knuckles.

  3. 3. Dades Gorge Road point 3

    Higher up the Dades, the gorge pinches in and the road gets its reputation. Here a series of tight hairpins climbs a steep wall of rock, the road folding back on itself in a stack of switchbacks, with the valley dropping away below and the red cliffs closing in above. Riders who have seen photographs of the Dades will recognise this section, and they will also notice that the photographs do not convey the steepness.
