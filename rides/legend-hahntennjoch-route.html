Why this ride

curated scenic road. narrow valley, hairpins, limestone Alps. From Imst it runs 30 km to Elmen; allow about 42 min of riding. The highest point is about 1,894 m. Hahntennjoch is subject to seasonal closure - check it is open before you set off. The line favours views over speed.

The roads

Hahntennjoch Route

12 km from Imst to Near Imst. Mostly uphill - about 870 m, topping out near 1,728 m. Top up fuel at the bottom - pumps thin out on the way up.

Rider note

Check the pass is open before you commit to it.
