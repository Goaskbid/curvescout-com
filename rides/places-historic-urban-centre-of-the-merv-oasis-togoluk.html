Why this ride

From Bayramaly it runs 271 km to Bayramaly; allow about 7 h of riding. On the way it passes Yaz and Gobekli Tepes, Besh Agyz koshk, Togoluk and back through Besh Agyz koshk. The line favours views over speed.

The stops

  1. 1. Historic urban centre of the Merv oasis

    Start here.

    Merv is what is left of a city that once argued with Baghdad about which of them was the greatest in the Muslim world. Today it lies as a wide spread of mud-brick walls, domes and mounds in the Karakum desert of Turkmenistan, a few kilometres from the town of Bayramaly and not far from the regional capital Mary. The Murgab river fans out here into an oasis, and for more than two thousand years that thin green strip made Merv the natural halt on the Silk Road between Persia and Central Asia.

    Read the complete Historic urban centre of the Merv oasis write-up.

  2. 2. Yaz and Gobekli Tepes

    29 km in.

    Out on the edge of the Murghab oasis in south-eastern Turkmenistan, where irrigated cotton fields meet the sand of the Karakum, low mounds rise from the flat land. Tepe means hill, and these hills are the eroded remains of ancient settlements: Yaz Depe, which gave its name to an Iron Age culture of the early first millennium BC, and Gobekli Depe nearby. To an untrained eye they are just humps of clay, but they belong to one of the longest continuous stories of settlement in Central Asia.

    Read the complete Yaz and Gobekli Tepes write-up.

  3. 3. Besh Agyz koshk

    74 km in.

    Out on the edge of the Merv oasis in southeastern Turkmenistan, where irrigated fields fade into the sand of the Karakum desert, stand the ruins of buildings known as koshks. These were fortified mansions of mud brick, with heavily ribbed walls that look like giant corrugated cardboard, built in the early medieval period by the landed gentry of Merv. Besh Agyz, which means five mouths, is one of them, and it is part of the UNESCO listing of the Silk Roads, alongside the vast ruined cities of Ancient Merv, a World Heritage Site since 1999. Merv was for centuries one of the great cities of the Islamic world and a key stop on the routes between China and the Mediterranean, until the Mongols destroyed it in the thirteenth century.

    Read the complete Besh Agyz koshk write-up.

  4. 4. Togoluk

    135 km in. The ride passes about 5 km from it.

    What I can say is that the surrounding land belongs to the Murghab oasis, where a river born in the Afghan mountains fans out into irrigated fields, then dies in the sand. This is one of the oldest farming and trading landscapes in Central Asia, and everything around here is shaped by water and by the lack of it.

    Read the complete Togoluk write-up.
