Why this ride

curated scenic road. escarpment, forest, mountain views. From Lydenburg it runs 58 km to the finish; allow about 52 min of riding. The highest point is about 2,150 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The roads

Long Tom Pass and Panorama Link point 2 to Long Tom Pass and Panorama Link point 3

Long Tom Pass and Panorama Link point 2 to Long Tom Pass and Panorama Link point 3: 34 km, mostly on Long Tom Pass. You lose around 1040 m along the way; the top is 2,072 m. Above 2,000 m the weather turns fast; have a layer and a plan B.

The stops

  1. 1. Lydenburg

    Start here.

    The name Lydenburg means town of suffering, and there is real history behind it. In the 1840s, Voortrekker families settled at Ohrigstad, in the lowveld, but malaria drove them out, and in 1849 they moved up to higher, healthier ground and founded a new town, which they named in memory of what they had endured. Today Lydenburg sits at around 1,400 metres in the Mpumalanga highveld, a quiet market town of about twenty thousand people, with a wide main street, tree-lined avenues and a feel of farming country, where cattle and timber matter more than tourists.
