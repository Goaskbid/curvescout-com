Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Cusco

    Cusco sits at about 3,400 metres in a high Andean valley, and the first thing it asks of you is patience. The air is thin, so even a short walk uphill can leave you breathless, and visitors from sea level often feel it as a headache or a strange tightness for a day or two. Locals will offer you coca tea, which helps a little; rest, water and a light dinner help more. On a motorcycle, thin air also saps engine power, a carburetted bike may run rich and a fuel-injected one will cope better, so do not be surprised if your machine feels a little tired.

  2. 2. Vinicunca gate

    Nobody paid much attention to this ridge until the snow started thinning a few years back and the stripes underneath showed up. Vinicunca, the so-called Rainbow Mountain, sits in the Vilcanota range south-east of Cusco, and the colours are plain geology: layers of sediment stained by different minerals, tilted and then exposed, rust-red where iron has oxidised, mustard and cream where other salts and sandstones sit. The summit viewpoint is a little over 5,000 metres, which puts it in the same air as the highest passes in the Alps with another kilometre on top.
