Why this ride

369 km from Pushkin to Pushkin, about 5 h 10 of riding. The stops in between: Northern battery No. 1, The Road of Life and back through Northern battery No. 1. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The roads

Saint Petersburg Imperial Shore

From Kievskoe Highway to Northern battery No. 1 is 68 km, mostly on КАД. An easy profile, never above 101 m. Water alongside for much of it - pick one good pull-out and stop.

The stops

  1. 1. Kievskoe Highway

    Start here.

    South of Saint Petersburg, the flat, damp plain of Ingria runs off towards Pskov and the old western frontier, and the Kievskoe Highway is the road that heads that way, the route to Kyiv that gave it its name.

    Read the complete Kievskoe Highway write-up.

  2. 2. Northern battery No. 1

    69 km in.

    Kotlin Island is a long, thin sliver of land in the Gulf of Finland, and Kronstadt, its fortress-town, was founded by Peter the Great in 1704 to guard the sea approaches to his new capital. It became the base of the Russian Baltic Fleet, and ring after ring of forts, batteries and ramparts was built on the island and on artificial islands around it. The Northern Battery No. 1 is one of those fortifications, a low military work on the north shore of Kotlin, part of a defensive system that went on being added to for more than two centuries.

    Read the complete Northern battery No. 1 write-up.

  3. 3. The Road of Life

    198 km in.

    On the south-eastern shore of Lake Ladoga, east of Saint Petersburg, a road ends at the water. This is the start of the Road of Life, the supply route across the ice that kept Leningrad alive during the siege of 1941 to 1944. The siege lasted 872 days, from September 1941 to January 1944, and it cut the city off from nearly everything except the lake. In the first winter, when Ladoga froze, trucks and horse-drawn sledges began to cross the ice with food and fuel, and on the return journey they carried out women, children and the sick. Lorries broke through the ice, and bombing and artillery attacked the route, but it kept going.

    Read the complete The Road of Life write-up.
