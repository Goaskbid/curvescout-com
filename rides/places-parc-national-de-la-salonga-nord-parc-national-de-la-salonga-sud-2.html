Why this ride

It goes by way of Salonga National Park, Parc national de la Salonga Sud and back through Salonga National Park. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Parc national de la Salonga Nord

    Salonga is the largest tropical rainforest reserve in Africa, covering about thirty-six thousand square kilometres of the Congo basin in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, in two big blocks separated by a corridor of villages and farmland. UNESCO listed the park in 1984, and it has spent years on the list of World Heritage in Danger.

  2. 2. Salonga National Park

    Salonga is a place you read about rather than ride to, so let us be honest from the start. It is the largest tropical rainforest national park in Africa, a block of lowland forest in the central basin of the Democratic Republic of the Congo, bigger than Belgium, split into a northern and a southern sector with a corridor of villages between them. UNESCO listed it in 1984, and since 1999 it has also been on the list of sites in danger, because of poaching, conflict and the sheer difficulty of guarding something so large.

  3. 3. Parc national de la Salonga Sud

    Where the northern block of Salonga is wet and tangled, the southern block feels like the farther side of the moon. It lies roughly a degree south of the equator in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, in the great bowl of the Congo basin, and it is part of the same World Heritage reserve, which at thirty-six thousand square kilometres is the biggest protected tropical rainforest in Africa. A corridor of land between the two halves is home to people who farm, fish and hunt, and their villages are the only human mark on the map.
