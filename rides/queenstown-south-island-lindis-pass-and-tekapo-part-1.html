Why this ride

Out of Kingston, one way: 285 km, roughly 5 h 47 in the saddle. The climbs: near Kingston and Scenic stretch near Te Anau. It tops out at about 900 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The roads

Milford Road (SH94)

The 97 km between Scenic stretch near Te Anau and Queenstown.

The stops

    Start here.

  2. 2. High point near Kingston

    About 70 km in. You top out here; the road stops climbing.

  3. 3. Scenic stretch near Te Anau

    About 188 km in. This is where the road stops climbing.

  4. 4. Queenstown

    The end: about 285 km.

    The nearest way in is a walking track, the Hollyford, which runs for days through the valley to the sea at Martins Bay, and the nearest roads stop well short of it. The ride is named for the Kingston · Wanaka and Queenstown Loop, which lie a long way east, so take this stop as a nod to the wilderness behind the road.
