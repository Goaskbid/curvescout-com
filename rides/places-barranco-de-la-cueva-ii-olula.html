Barranco de la Cueva II and Olula

Why this ride

From El Campello it runs 353 km to El Campello; allow about 6 h 05 of riding. The stops in between: Cueva de las Arañas del Carabassi, Barranc de les Coves- Abric I, Abric de Gontrán and Olula. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Barranco de la Cueva II

    This is the dry hinterland of the Costa Blanca, in the stretch between El Campello and Villajoyosa, where the sea is never far but you can easily forget it. A few kilometres inland the hills turn to scrub, rock and abandoned terraces, with barrancos that run dry for most of the year and then flood in a night after a storm. The Barranco de la Cueva is one of those ravines, and the shelter in it belongs to the scatter of painted rocks found across the mountains of Alicante.

  2. 2. Cueva de las Arañas del Carabassi

    The Carabassí is one of the last stretches of wild-ish coast between Alicante and Santa Pola: a line of dunes held together by pine and marram grass, a long beach, and the sea glittering under the flight path of Alicante airport. In the low rocky ground behind it lies a small cave known for painted marks, the Cueva de las Arañas del Carabassí, close to the village of El Altet and a short hop from Santa Pola.

  3. 3. Barranc de les Coves- Abric I

    Ibi is the toy capital of Spain, a fact that surprises everyone who rides through this mountain town in the hills of Alicante. Since the early twentieth century, workshops here have produced dolls, tin toys and plastic figures, and the Valencian Toy Museum tells the story. The rock shelter named Barranc de les Coves Abric I lies in the hills between Ibi and Alcoy, in a ravine whose name simply means the gully of the caves.

  4. 4. Abric de Gontrán

    Head south-west from Xàtiva along the old road to Castile and you climb gently into a corridor between two mountain ranges, the Serra Grossa on one side and the Serra de Enguera on the other. The town of Moixent sits where the corridor narrows, with Vallada just downstream and the vineyards of Fontanars dels Alforins on the plateau beyond. The Abric de Gontran lies in the hills just outside Moixent.

  5. 5. Olula

    Caudete and Almansa sit in a corridor of land where Castile-La Mancha, Valencia and Murcia meet, and for centuries this was frontier country, where kingdoms and armies passed and fought. Olula is a point in the hills near Caudete, linked on the rides with rock-art shelters in the surrounding sierras, part of the extensive UNESCO listing of prehistoric art in Mediterranean Spain.
