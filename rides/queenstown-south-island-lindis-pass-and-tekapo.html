Why this ride

A proper escape from Queenstown with roads worth waking up for. 958 km round from Kingston - plan on about 14 h 12 of riding. The climbs: near Kingston, Scenic stretch near Te Anau and Kingston · Wanaka and Queenstown Loop. It tops out at about 1,100 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The roads

Milford Road (SH94)

67 km from Scenic stretch near Te Anau to Queenstown. A proper up-and-over: 310 m of climbing, 655 m at the top, 350 m down.

Rider note

Check the pass is open before you commit to it.

The stops

  1. 1. Fuel stop near Kingston

    Start here.

  2. 2. High point near Kingston

    58 km in. You top out here; the road stops climbing.

  3. 3. Scenic stretch near Te Anau

    174 km in. The ride passes about 10 km from it. This is where the road stops climbing.

  4. 4. Queenstown

    241 km in. The ride passes about 21 km from it. The high point of this section.

    The nearest way in is a walking track, the Hollyford, which runs for days through the valley to the sea at Martins Bay, and the nearest roads stop well short of it. The ride is named for the Kingston · Wanaka and Queenstown Loop, which lie a long way east, so take this stop as a nod to the wilderness behind the road.

    Read the complete Queenstown write-up.

  5. 5. Wanaka

    687 km in.

    Omarama sits in the Waitaki basin, a wide, high, dry valley in the lee of the Southern Alps, and the land around it has the stripped look of the South Island's high country, with tawny tussock slopes, braided rivers and a huge sky. Omarama is best known to pilots, since the wind that comes over the mountains makes it one of the best gliding sites in the world.

    Read the complete Wanaka write-up.

  6. 6. Food stop near Wanaka

    Same place as the previous stop. The ride passes about 10 km from it. Food stop near Wanaka is where this day stops to eat.

  7. 7. Near Queenstown

    871 km in.

    South of Queenstown, the road to Kingston follows the eastern shore of Lake Wakatipu, squeezed between deep, cold water and the jagged wall of the Remarkables. In winter they are white to their bases; in summer the bare rock glows gold and grey in the evening light.

    Read the complete Near Queenstown write-up.

  8. 8. Back to Kingston

    Back where you started, 958 km.

    Here the lake country gives way to northern Southland, a region of broad river flats, low mountains and farms that stretch to the horizon.

    Read the complete Kingston write-up.
