Why this ride

A proper escape from Queenstown with roads worth waking up for. 694 km round from Kingston - plan on about 14 h 28 of riding. The climbs: near Kingston, Scenic stretch near Te Anau and Lindis Pass and Tekapo. It tops out at about 1,050 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

  5. 5. Queenstown / South Island return

    Omarama sits in the Waitaki basin, a wide, high, dry valley in the lee of the Southern Alps, and the land around it has the stripped look of the South Island's high country, with tawny tussock slopes, braided rivers and a huge sky. Omarama is best known to pilots, since the wind that comes over the mountains makes it one of the best gliding sites in the world.
