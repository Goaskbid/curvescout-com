Why this ride

curated scenic road. vineyards, lake, UNESCO landscape. 17 km from Lausanne to Vevey, about 19 min of riding. The highest point is about 1,300 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Lausanne

    Lausanne tips down a steep hillside to Lake Geneva, and you feel it in your legs, your brakes and your bike's low gears. The city sprawls over three hills and a lake front, with the old town at the top and the harbour district of Ouchy at the bottom, joined by one of the world's smallest metro systems. The Gothic cathedral looks out over the roofs and the water toward the French Alps across the lake.

  2. 2. Lutry

    Lutry is where the Lavaux begins, if you come from Lausanne, and it announces the fact with vineyards that climb straight up behind the old town. The terraces, held by dry-stone walls that run for kilometres, rise from the shore of Lake Geneva to the ridge above, a staircase of green in summer and gold in autumn. They were first built by monks in the Middle Ages, and in 2007 UNESCO placed the whole landscape on its World Heritage list. Lutry itself is a lakeside town of nearly nine thousand people, with a medieval centre, a harbour and a promenade.

  4. 4. Near Vevey

    Vevey is a lakeside town where the Alps seem to drop straight into Lake Geneva, and the light on the water can be so clear and bright that the mountains on the far side, in France, look close enough to touch. It is the capital of the Riviera vaudoise, about sixteen thousand people strong, with a long promenade, a market square lined with arcades and an air of calm prosperity. The town is the home of Nestlé, which started here in the nineteenth century, and the company's headquarters and food museum are a fixture on the waterfront.
