Why this ride

Northern Spain: gorge road through the Desfiladero de los Beyos. A one-way ride of 17 km, Posada de Valdeón to Amieva - roughly 19 min in the saddle. The highest point is about 1,350 m. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

Picos de Europa Loop

Expect company on a summer weekend. Early is better than fast. For 8 km this is Picos de Europa Loop, a road with a name of its own and a reason for it.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Near Posada de Valdeón

    Here the high wall of the Picos de Europa closes the Liébana valley like a stage curtain. Around Fuente Dé, the road from Potes simply runs out against a cliff, an amphitheatre of grey limestone that rises some eight hundred metres almost straight up. A cable car, the Teleférico de Fuente Dé, climbs that face in a few minutes to the upper station at around 1,800 metres, and from the top you walk out onto a moonscape of karst, scree and old mine workings, with the peaks of the Central Massif all around. On a clear day it is one of the great views of northern Spain. On a cloudy one, you rise through grey fog and step out above a sea of cloud.

  2. 2. Near Posada de Valdeón

    Caín is the end of the road in the most literal sense: a handful of stone houses at the bottom of the Valdeón valley where the Cares river disappears into one of the deepest gorges in Europe. From here, the famous Ruta del Cares, a footpath cut into the cliff, runs about twelve kilometres downstream to Poncebos in Asturias, hacked out of the rock in the twentieth century to maintain a water channel for a hydroelectric plant. The path clings to the gorge walls with drops of hundreds of metres, passing through tunnels and over narrow bridges, and walking even an hour of it from Caín is worth every step.

  3. 3. Near Amieva

    The western massif of the Picos de Europa, the Cornión, rises in a wall of grey limestone above the green valleys of eastern Asturias, and the country around Amieva, Cangas de Onís and Covadonga is where the mountains first take hold. It is a land of deep gorges, steep meadows, stone villages and fast rivers, with cows and goats on the slopes and the peaks often lost in cloud.
