Why this ride

curated scenic road. tea estates, mountains, waterfalls. 101 km from Kandy to Wellawaya, about 1 h 50 of riding. On the way it passes Nuwara Eliya, Sri Lanka Hill Country Road and Ella Town. The highest point is about 1,350 m. The line favours views over speed.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Kandy

    Kandy is the heart of Sri Lanka's hill country, a city built around an artificial lake in a bowl of green hills at about five hundred metres. For over two hundred years it was the capital of the last Sinhalese kingdom, which held out against the Portuguese, Dutch and finally the British, who took it in 1815. Its great treasure is the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, the Sri Dalada Maligawa, which houses what is believed to be a tooth of the Buddha. The temple draws pilgrims every day, with drumming and offerings at set times, and the whole city is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Around 110,000 people live here, so fuel, supplies and hotels are easy.

  2. 2. Near Nuwara Eliya

    At about 1,900 metres, Nuwara Eliya is the highest town of any size in Sri Lanka, and it was built by the British as a place to escape the heat of the lowlands. They called it Little England, and the name still fits in a slightly surreal way: Tudor-style hotels and bungalows, a golf course, a racecourse, a park with flower beds, a post office in red brick, and a lake where people ride pedal boats. Around it all, the hills are covered with tea, and the mist rolls in most afternoons. It can be cold here, especially at night, and the locals wear woolly hats and jackets.

  3. 3. Sri Lanka Hill Country Road point 3

    Horton Plains is the high, wild heart of Sri Lanka's hill country, a rolling plateau of grassland and cloud forest at more than two thousand metres, and it feels nothing like the tropical island below. The grass is golden and tussocky, the trees are stunted and twisted, draped in lichen, and the air is cold and clear in the early morning before the clouds come in. Sambar deer graze in the open, unconcerned by visitors, and the cries of birds echo across the plains. It is a national park and a UNESCO World Heritage site, part of the central highlands of Sri Lanka.

  4. 4. Ella Town

    Ella has gone from a sleepy hill village to Sri Lanka's backpacker capital in a generation, and it shows: the main street is now lined with cafes, bars, guesthouses and yoga studios, and on a busy evening it can feel more like Bali than the hill country. But step a few minutes outside the town, and the landscape is still glorious: steep hills covered in tea, waterfalls tumbling down rocky cliffs, and the famous Ella Gap, a deep notch in the mountains through which you can see all the way to the southern plains.

  5. 5. Wellawaya

    Wellawaya is a junction town in the dry south-east of Sri Lanka, and its main claim to fame is that the road to the coast and the road to the hills part company here. Around ten thousand people live in the town, which sits on flat, scrubby land at the foot of the escarpment, with the Uva highlands rising behind. Ella and Haputale, both in the hills, are a short climb away, and the contrast is sharp: from cool tea terraces you drop through bends and waterfalls to a hot, flat plain dotted with palms, paddy and thorn scrub.
