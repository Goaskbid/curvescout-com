Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Tralee

    Tralee is the county town of Kerry and the gateway to the Dingle Peninsula, and for one week each August it turns into a festival town. The Rose of Tralee, an international beauty and talent contest founded in 1959 and based on a nineteenth-century love song, draws women of Irish descent from around the world, and the whole town fills with music and crowds. Outside festival time, it is a busy, practical market town of about twenty-three thousand people, a place for supplies and a pint.

  2. 2. Dingle

    Dingle is a small harbour town at the western end of County Kerry's Dingle Peninsula, with fewer than two thousand people but about as many pubs as you could want. The streets slope toward the water, shops and houses are painted in strong colours, and the harbour is full of fishing boats. This is a Gaeltacht area, where Irish is still the everyday language for many families, so you may see signs in Irish first, and the town's Irish name, An Daingean, appears on the road markers.
