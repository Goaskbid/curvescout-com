Why this ride

A lake mountain road from San Martin de los Andes, Neuquen to Villa La Angostura, Neuquen, via Lago Machonico. Key character: Andean lakes; forests; volcanic peaks. Surface: paved.

Scenic road chapters

Seven Lakes Route

Slow enough to look. The surface follows the shoreline, so the corners arrive where the water decided, not where an engineer did. Water arrives beside the road and stays for 43 km. The line follows the shore because there is nowhere else for it to go.

Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. San Martín de los Andes

    San Martín de los Andes lies at the eastern end of Lake Lácar, in a pocket of Patagonian forest, and the town itself feels as though it were assembled from the surrounding trees: steep-roofed wooden chalets, carved signs, the smell of woodsmoke in the cooler months. It began in 1898 as a military post, one of the forts established in the conquest of the Mapuche lands, and grew into a mountain resort of around twenty-three thousand people. The Mapuche presence is still strong in the region, and the Lanín volcano, which rises to 3,776 metres above the lake district, is sacred to them.

  2. 2. Route of the Seven Lakes point 2

    Somewhere on this part of the road you cross from the valley of San Martin into the lake district proper, and the first of the lakes appears among dark forest. The seven lakes along the route are glacial, scooped out by ice during the last ice age and filled with meltwater, and each has its own colour, from steel blue to green to a milky turquoise when the light is right. Machonico, Falkner and Villarino are among those you pass, and they line up like beads along the road.

  3. 3. Near Villa La Angostura

    Villa La Angostura is a small town of about seven and a half thousand people on the north shore of Lake Nahuel Huapi, in the Patagonian Andes of Neuquén province, and it has the air of a Swiss resort that was built with local timber and stone. The name means the narrow village, from the slim isthmus that joins the town's peninsula to the mainland. It was founded in the 1930s, and the log-and-stone chalets, chocolate shops and smokehouses give it a cosy character. To the east, the city of Bariloche lies about eighty kilometres away along the lake.
