Why this ride

423 km from Mérida to Mérida, about 5 h 43 of riding. On the way it passes Old Town of Cáceres, Royal Monastery of Santa María de Guadalup and back through Old Town of Cáceres. It was put together for the scenery rather than the shortest line.

The roads

Los Milagros Aqueduct → Old Town of Cáceres

74 km from Los Milagros Aqueduct to Old Town of Cáceres on Autovía Ruta de la Plata. Hardly any climbing; it runs between 210 and 436 m. Take it at your own pace.

The stops

  1. 1. Los Milagros Aqueduct

    Start here.

    Mérida was founded in 25 BC as Emerita Augusta, a retirement town for veterans of the legions, and it became the capital of the Roman province of Lusitania. It is still, for my money, the best place in Spain to feel the Roman world, because the monuments are not locked away in a museum but scattered through a living town: a theatre, an amphitheatre, a circus, a long stone bridge across the Guadiana, a temple and the great aqueduct of Los Milagros. UNESCO listed the whole ensemble in 1993.

    Read the complete Los Milagros Aqueduct write-up.

  2. 2. Old Town of Cáceres

    74 km in.

    Cáceres has an old town so complete that film crews treat it as a ready-made set, and walking its streets at dusk, when the stone glows amber and the crowds have gone, you can see why. Inside the walls, built largely by the Almohads in the twelfth century, are dozens of fortified mansions, towers, churches and palaces, many built by noble families who made their fortunes in the conquest of the Americas. The towers once had battlements, but Queen Isabella ordered most of them cut down to curb the nobles' power. UNESCO listed the old town in 1986.
