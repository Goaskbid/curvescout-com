Waypoint introductions and full write-ups

  1. 1. Ubud

    Ubud is the place where Bali's rice paddies meet its art. Set in the hills at the island's centre, the town grew from a royal village into a centre for painters, dancers and carvers in the 1920s and 1930s, when European artists such as Walter Spies, a German, and Rudolf Bonnet, a Dutchman, settled here and encouraged local painters to explore new styles. The old royal palace, the Puri Saren, still hosts dance performances in the evenings, with gamelan music and elaborate costumes.
