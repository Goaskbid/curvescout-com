Why this ride

Scenery first, traffic last, with checkpoints that make sense on a bike. 216 km round from Kraaifontein - plan on about 3 h 56 of riding. The stops in between: Atlantis, Cape Town and Constantia. The climbs: Bergvliet, Stellenbosch and Franschhoek Pass and Winelands. Much of it runs through woodland. The plan has 1 food stop and 1 fuel stop marked.
