Why this ride

Big sky, single-track discipline and one of Britain’s great rider days. The stops in between: Applecross coast and Torridon mountain view. The climb of the day is Bealach na Bà. The passes are the reason to go. The plan has 1 food stop and 1 fuel stop marked.
