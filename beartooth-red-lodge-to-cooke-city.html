Why this ride

Alpine lakes, high altitude and an official status check before coffee. The stops in between: Top of the World store, Cooke City and Yellowstone northeast. Beartooth Pass is the high point. It tops out at about 2,850 m. It is a high-mountain day. The plan has 1 food stop and 1 fuel stop marked.
